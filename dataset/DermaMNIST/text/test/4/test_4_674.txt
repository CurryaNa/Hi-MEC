1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays significant polychromia, with prominent areas of bright red, blue-black, dark brown, and tan.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular, indistinct, and poorly circumscribed, featuring notching and fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is complex, featuring a large, moist, ulcerated area superiorly and a nodular, irregular texture in the inferior blue-black component.
5. Structural Features: This multicomponent lesion presents with a superior erythematous, ulcerated nodule and an inferior blue-black nodule with a cobblestone pattern. There are also multiple peripheral pigmented macules and peppering, consistent with a polymorphous pattern.
