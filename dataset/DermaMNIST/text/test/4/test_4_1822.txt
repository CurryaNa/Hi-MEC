1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including dark brown, black, and a prominent blue-white veil in the central area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, with feathery, radial projections (pseudopods) creating a starburst pattern.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, although it is overlaid with fine, whitish, linear chrysalis structures.
5. Structural Features: The lesion displays an atypical pigment network, central blotches of dark pigment, and features of regression such as the blue-white veil.
