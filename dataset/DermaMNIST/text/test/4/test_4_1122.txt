1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, from light to dark, against a background of pinkish-white skin with some erythematous areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, gradually fading into the surrounding skin without a distinct edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth but is punctuated by prominent follicular openings (pores) across the entire field.
5. Structural Features: A distinct pseudonetwork (annular-granular) pattern is visible, characterized by perifollicular pigmentation, along with fine, branching telangiectasias at the periphery.
