1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits marked color variegation, with areas of dark brown, light brown, tan, pink, and erythematous red.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, featuring notching and areas where the pigment fades indistinctly into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is irregular, with areas of regression showing effacement of normal skin markings (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: The lesion presents a multi-component pattern, including atypical pigment network, dark blotches, and significant regression structures such as scar-like depigmentation and erythema.
