1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a central area of light brown pigmentation set against a prominent erythematous (red/pink) background.
2. Border Appearance: The borders of the pigmented component are irregular in contour and appear indistinct or blurred.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment.

5. Structural Features: The most notable features are prominent, branching telangiectatic vessels (arborizing vessels) surrounding a central focus of amorphous, structureless brown pigmentation.
