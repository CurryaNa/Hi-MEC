1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including dark brown, black, light brown, pink-red, and areas of white scar-like depigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly defined, with pigment fading indistinctly into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears heterogeneous, with smooth pigmented areas and a central atrophic, slightly scaly texture.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include atypical pigment globules and blotches, extensive areas of regression with scar-like fibrosis (chrysalis structures), and fine, linear, and arborizing vessels.
