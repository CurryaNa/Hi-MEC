1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a multicomponent color pattern, including light brown, dark brown, and pink-red areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, showing a disorganized and abrupt transition to the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: There is marked asymmetry in the distribution of both color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears uneven with fine white scaling and shiny white linear streaks (chrysalis structures).
5. Structural Features: Key structures include numerous whitish globules (milia-like cysts), a disrupted pigment network, and fine, branching telangiectatic vessels.
