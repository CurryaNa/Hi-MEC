1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including shades of pink-red, light brown, and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, characterized by feathery, radial streaming and pseudopod-like extensions.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with a distinct, raised, papular or nodular component in the central area.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central, structureless pink-red nodule and a peripheral, atypical pigmented network with streaks radiating outwards.
