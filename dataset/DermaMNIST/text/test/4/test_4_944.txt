1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents a variegated color pattern, including pink-red, a central whitish-blue area, and peripheral dark brown to black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is highly irregular in shape and is ill-defined, with notching and blurring in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears multifocal, with areas of fine scaling and superficial crusting, particularly along the pigmented periphery.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central zone of scar-like regression (fibrosis), irregularly distributed dark brown globules and blotches, and fine, arborizing telangiectatic vessels.
