1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits significant color variegation, presenting with multiple shades of light brown, dark brown, black, and a prominent pinkish-white area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, featuring both abrupt cut-offs and areas that fade gradually into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is largely smooth with visible skin lines (dermatoglyphics), but the central lighter area appears slightly atrophic and crinkled.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is observed, including an atypical, thickened pigment network, structureless brown/black blotches, and a significant area of regression with scar-like depigmentation.
