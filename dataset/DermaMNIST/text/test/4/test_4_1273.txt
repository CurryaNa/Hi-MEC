1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple shades of brown, from light tan to dark brown, set against a pinkish-red background with prominent vessels.
2. Border Appearance: The borders of the pigmented area are ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding tissue.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment.

5. Structural Features: Key features include a disorganized pigment network, blotchy pigmentation, and surrounding telangiectatic vessels; the overall pattern with pale lobules is suggestive of sebaceous hyperplasia with superimposed pigmentation.
