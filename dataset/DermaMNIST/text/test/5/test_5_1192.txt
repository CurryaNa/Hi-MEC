1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including dark brown, red, and pink, creating a variegated appearance.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, with areas of notching and a surrounding erythematous flare.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The central surface is raised and papular with a cerebriform (brain-like) texture, while the periphery is macular.
5. Structural Features: It features a complex pattern with a central nodular component, dark globules, and prominent, fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) in the surrounding halo.
