1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including varying shades of brown (light to dark) and prominent erythematous pink-red areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with some areas appearing blurred or fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with skin lines (dermatoglyphics) partially preserved and no signs of scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic examination reveals blotchy, non-uniform pigmentation, an atypical or broken pigment network, and fine, irregular, linear, and serpentine vascular structures.
