1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays color variegation, presenting with shades of dark brown, black, and a central violaceous-red hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with areas of blurring and faint streaks extending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with partial effacement of the natural skin lines (dermatoglyphics) over the central, more pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Internal structures include an atypical pigment network, central structureless black and brown blotches, and peripheral radial streaming.
