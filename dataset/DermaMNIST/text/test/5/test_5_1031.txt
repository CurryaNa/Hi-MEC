1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with color variegation, including shades of tan, dark brown, black, and an erythematous pink-red background.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and fuzzy, with an irregular perimeter that blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with some effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics), especially in the central, more heavily pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is observed, featuring a central dark blotch, atypical dots/globules, and a disorganized reticular network.
