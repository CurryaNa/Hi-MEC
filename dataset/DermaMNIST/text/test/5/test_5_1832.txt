1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with two primary shades of brown: a dark brown, almost black, central core and a lighter, reddish-brown periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and indistinct, featuring feathery, pseudopodal-like projections that radiate outwards from the central mass.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and vellus hairs visible across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: A key feature is the starburst pattern, characterized by a central, darker pigmented area with radial streaks and pseudopods extending into the surrounding skin.
