1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light to dark brown, with small, focal, hypopigmented or skin-colored areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is relatively well-defined but irregular, with a slightly scalloped contour rather than a smooth, uniform edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall round-to-oval shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with the natural skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible and preserved over the pigmented area.
5. Structural Features: A prominent reticular pigment network is the dominant feature, characterized by a mesh-like pattern of dark brown lines with multiple, small, round, hypopigmented "holes" within it.
