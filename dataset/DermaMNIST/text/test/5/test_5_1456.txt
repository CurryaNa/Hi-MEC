1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with variegated pigmentation, featuring shades of light brown and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are somewhat irregular and indistinct, particularly along the lateral margins where the pigment appears to fade.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical, with an elongated and irregular ovoid shape.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is flat (macular) and smooth, with the natural skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved and running uninterrupted across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Pigmentation is organized in a linear or fibrillar pattern that aligns with the parallel skin furrows, characteristic of acral skin.
