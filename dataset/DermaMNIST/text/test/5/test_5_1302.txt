1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a pinkish-red background (erythema) overlaid with shades of brown and reddish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, feathering out and blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with visible fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) traversing the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Prominent, fine, branching (arborizing) telangiectasias are a key feature, along with ill-defined brownish blotches and a subtle, fragmented pigment network.
