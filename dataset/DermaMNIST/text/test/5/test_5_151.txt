1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a spectrum of red, ranging from pinkish-red to dark red, with some areas appearing brownish-purple.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of its internal vascular and pigmented components.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with no evidence of scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A prominent network of fine, branching, and interconnected blood vessels (arborizing and reticular telangiectasias) is the dominant feature, interspersed with blotchy, darker pigmented areas.
