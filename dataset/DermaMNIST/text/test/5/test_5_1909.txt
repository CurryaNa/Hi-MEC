1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, featuring a dark brown to black central core and a lighter brown to reddish-brown periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, exhibiting a smudged or hazy appearance with pigment fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of pigment, lacking a mirror image across any axis.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and no visible scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A central, dark, globular area of hyperpigmentation is present, with subtle radial streaming or a starburst pattern extending into a lighter peripheral pigment network.
