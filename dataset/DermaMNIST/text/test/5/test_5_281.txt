1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, varying from light tan to a darker, more saturated brown.
2. Border Appearance: The overall border is highly irregular, indistinct, and scalloped, appearing as a cluster of smaller, partially coalesced lesions.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its pigmented components.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with normal skin markings effaced by the pigmented structures.
5. Structural Features: The lesion is characterized by a multicomponent pattern of aggregated brown globules and clods, creating a cobblestone or agminated appearance.
