1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors (polychromia), including shades of pink, reddish-brown, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular in shape and poorly demarcated, with a scalloped edge formed by multiple lobules.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is papular and mamillated, creating an uneven, cobblestone-like texture.
5. Structural Features: Multiple translucent papules and lobules are present, particularly at the periphery, along with scattered dark brown to black clods and a central area of reddish pigmentation.
