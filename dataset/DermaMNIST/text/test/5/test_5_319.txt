1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including varying shades of light to dark brown, with focal areas of black and hypopigmented (whitish) patches.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, exhibiting a starburst or feathery pattern with finger-like projections extending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, without significant scale, crust, or ulceration; fine vellus hairs are visible across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include an atypical, reticular pigment network that is thickened and blotchy, combined with radial streaming and pseudopods at the periphery.
