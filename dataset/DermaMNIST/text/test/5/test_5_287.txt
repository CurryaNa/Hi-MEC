1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from a light tan background to areas of dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular in shape and indistinct, fading into the surrounding skin with a reticular pattern at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of color and structures.

5. Structural Features: A prominent reticular (net-like) pigment network is observed, which is more pronounced at the periphery, along with scattered hypopigmented or structureless areas within the lesion.
