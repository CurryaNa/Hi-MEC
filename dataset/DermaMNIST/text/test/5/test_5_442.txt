1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including light brown, dark brown, black, and prominent pink-red areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly defined, with both blurred and angular (spiculated) segments that fade into the surrounding tissue.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with partial effacement of skin markings, lacking significant scale or crusting.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a disorganized pigment network, eccentric dark blotches, and prominent, irregular arborizing telangiectasias.
