1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including light brown at the periphery and areas of dark brown and black centrally.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined, with some areas appearing scalloped or moth-eaten and fading into the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface has a waxy, cerebriform (brain-like) appearance, characterized by multiple fissures and sulci.
5. Structural Features: Key dermoscopic features include numerous dark brown to black comedo-like openings and lighter milia-like cysts scattered throughout the lesion.
