1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown at the periphery, a central cluster of dark brown, and a significant erythematous (red/pink) hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and ill-defined, appearing blurred and fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with some effacement of the normal skin markings (dermatoglyphics) over the central area.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a central erythematous area containing fine, serpentine or arborizing telangiectasias (visible vessels) and a focal aggregation of brown globules.
