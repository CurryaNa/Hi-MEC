1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with variegation, displaying multiple shades including light brown, dark brown, and focal black areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and indistinct, with areas that blur and fade into the surrounding skin, particularly at the edges of the pigment network.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is clearly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved dermatoglyphics (skin lines) and fine vellus hairs visible across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic examination reveals an atypical pigment network that is irregular and thickened, a central dark blotch, and radial streaming/pseudopods at the periphery.
