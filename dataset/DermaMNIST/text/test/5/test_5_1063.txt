3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is largely symmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and macular (flat), with preserved normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible throughout.
5. Structural Features: The lesion presents with a central concentration of dark brown globules and a peripheral, lighter pigment network, creating a targetoid or "fried egg" pattern.
