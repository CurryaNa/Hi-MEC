1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a primary erythematous (pink-red) base, overlaid with areas of reddish-brown and brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly demarcated, with pigmented areas radiating outwards in a starburst or reticular pattern.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its color and structural components.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth, with a possible slight elevation or papular quality in the central portion.
5. Structural Features: A central erythematous papule is present, surrounded by a peripheral pattern of brown dots, globules, and faint streaks.
