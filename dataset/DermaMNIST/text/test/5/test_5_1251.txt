1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including a dark brown to black central core and a lighter brown, radiating periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, featuring a "starburst" pattern with fine streaks of pigment extending into the surrounding skin.
