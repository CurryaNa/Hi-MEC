1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from a dark brown to blackish central area to a lighter tan-brown at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with a smudged or feathery appearance where the pigment fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears relatively flat, with native skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible but partially effaced across the pigmented area.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a disorganized pattern with a central, dark, amorphous blotch and an irregular, fading pigment network towards the edges.
