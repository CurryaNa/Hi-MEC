1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple shades of brown, varying from light tan to dark brown and black.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular, poorly demarcated, and exhibit a scalloped or "moth-eaten" configuration.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal structures and color.

5. Structural Features: Distinct structural features include a network of gyri and sulci, comedo-like openings (dark plugs), and milia-like cysts (small white-yellow globules).
