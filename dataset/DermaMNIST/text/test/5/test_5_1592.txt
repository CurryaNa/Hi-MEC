1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with two main colors: a dark brown, homogenous central area and a lighter, tan-to-reddish brown periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, characterized by feathery, spiculated, or starburst-like projections radiating into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with preserved dermatoglyphics (skin lines) and no visible scaling or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A prominent structural feature is the pattern of radial streaming or pseudopods, where pigmented streaks extend outwards from a central, darker core.
