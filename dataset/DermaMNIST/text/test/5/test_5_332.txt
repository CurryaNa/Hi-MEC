1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a central dark brown to black area surrounded by a reddish-pink erythematous halo.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, with the central pigmentation appearing to fade or smudge into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and vellus hairs visible across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Structural features include a central, blotchy, and non-uniform area of dark pigmentation and a prominent peripheral erythematous flare.
