1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with color variegation, including shades of light brown, dark brown, black, and areas of erythematous pink.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, featuring notching and a gradual fading into the surrounding skin in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth with preserved, albeit slightly effaced, skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Internal structures are disorganized, featuring an atypical pigment network, blotches, structureless dark areas, and a subtle, irregular vascular pattern.
