1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and a prominent pinkish-red background, with scattered whitish areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, fading gradually into the surrounding erythematous skin without a sharp demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of pigment and vascular structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and is free of scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a blotchy, structureless brown area, an atypical reticular pigment network, and prominent, branching arborizing telangiectasias.
