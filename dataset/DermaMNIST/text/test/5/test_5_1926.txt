1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light and dark brown, pink-red, and prominent grey-blue areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, featuring scalloped and notched edges that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular and multi-lobulated, with a raised, somewhat waxy, and cobblestone-like texture.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include aggregated globules, arborizing telangiectasias (branching vessels), and grey-blue ovoid nests, with a distinct nodular component.
