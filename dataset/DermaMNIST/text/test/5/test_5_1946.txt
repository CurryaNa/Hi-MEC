1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a primary reddish-brown coloration, containing multiple darker, purplish-red globules and foci.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and somewhat irregular, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin without a sharp demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in its ovoid shape and in the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with preserved skin lines and fine vellus hairs traversing the lesion.
5. Structural Features: The most prominent feature is the presence of multiple, clustered, reddish-brown lacunae (vascular spaces) of varying sizes, particularly concentrated in the central area.
