1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a dominant dark brown to black central area and a lighter brown peripheral halo.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and indistinct, showing a hazy or blurred transition into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is clearly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears largely smooth, with some effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) by the pigment.
5. Structural Features: A central, dark, structureless blotch is the most prominent feature, with a less dense, homogeneous brownish pigmentation at the periphery.
