1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including dark brown to black centrally, surrounded by a reddish-brown hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and fuzzy, particularly at the inferior and right-hand aspects, with a generally round but irregular outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with some effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) over the most pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: There is a central, dark, structureless clod of pigment with fine, fibrillar streaks or pseudopods radiating from the superior border and a surrounding reddish, homogeneous blush.
