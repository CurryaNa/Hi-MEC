1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and black, on an erythematous (reddish) base.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with a feathery, spiculated, or starburst pattern extending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a disorganized reticular network and prominent radial streaming or pseudopods, especially at the periphery.
