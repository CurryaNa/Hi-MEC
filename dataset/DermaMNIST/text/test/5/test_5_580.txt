1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays variegated pigmentation, with shades of dark brown, black, and a subtle blue-gray coloration centrally.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with fine, stellate, and reticular projections radiating into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal pigment and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with the underlying skin texture visible through the pigment.
5. Structural Features: A prominent atypical pigment network is observed, featuring dark, branching, and reticular lines, sometimes described as pseudopods, especially at the periphery.
