1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light tan to dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with areas of smudging and fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears flat and smooth, with preserved fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and overlying vellus hairs.
5. Structural Features: Internal structures include a disorganized reticular pattern, central structureless brown blotches, and some peripheral dark brown dots/globules.
