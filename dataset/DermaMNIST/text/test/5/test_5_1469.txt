1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and a prominent erythematous (reddish-pink) area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with pigment fanning out in a reticular or starburst-like pattern at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with fine vellus hairs visibly growing through the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include an atypical pigment network, central blotches of dark pigment, and a large, structureless reddish area, possibly indicating regression or increased vascularity.
