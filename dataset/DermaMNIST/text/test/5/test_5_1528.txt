1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a central dark brown to black area, surrounded by lighter brown and a faint erythematous hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with some areas appearing smudged or blurred as they fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved and visible across the lesion; no scaling or crusting is present.
5. Structural Features: The lesion is characterized by a central, dark, structureless blotch and irregular, radial streaks or pseudopod-like extensions at the periphery.
