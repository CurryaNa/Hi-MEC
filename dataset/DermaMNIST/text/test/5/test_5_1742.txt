1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a multicomponent color pattern, featuring a dominant erythematous (red) to pink hue centrally, with areas of light brown pigmentation, especially towards the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and blurred, gradually blending into the surrounding skin with an irregular outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and contains several vellus hairs.
5. Structural Features: The lesion displays a blotchy, non-uniform pigmentation pattern and contains fine, linear vascular structures (telangiectasias) within the erythematous areas.
