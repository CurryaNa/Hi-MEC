1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including reddish-brown, dark brown, and focal black areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with blurring and radial streaks extending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both shape and in the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven, with evidence of slight crusting and small, whitish, scaly foci.
5. Structural Features: Distinct structural features include disorganized blotches of pigment and fine, branching, arborizing telangiectasias, particularly visible at the periphery.
