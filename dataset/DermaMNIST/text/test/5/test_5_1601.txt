1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and a surrounding pink-to-red erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, particularly on the right side where the pigment fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics), showing no signs of scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Internal features include a multicomponent pattern with a peripheral reticular network, central blotches of dark pigmentation, and scattered brown dots/globules.
