1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, with a dark brown to black central zone and a lighter brown to reddish-brown coloration at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and highly irregular, characterized by a starburst pattern of pigmented streaks radiating into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface shows preserved fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and has fine vellus hairs growing through the lesion.
5. Structural Features: There is a central, dark, structureless or homogenous blotch surrounded by prominent peripheral radial streaks and pseudopods.
