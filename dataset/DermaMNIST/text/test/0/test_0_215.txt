1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with significant color variegation, including multiple shades of brown, pink, and a central whitish-pink area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, with a feathery or moth-eaten appearance where the pigment fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally flat, although there are subtle textural changes and potential atrophy suggested by the central lighter zones.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a disorganized and blotchy pigment network, multiple dots/globules, and a central zone of hypopigmentation and erythema suggestive of regression.
