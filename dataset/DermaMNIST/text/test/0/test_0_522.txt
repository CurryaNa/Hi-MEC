1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including light brown, dark brown, and focal black areas on an erythematous pink base.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly defined, with pigment appearing in multiple non-contiguous blotches and dots.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with some effacement of the normal skin lines within the pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic examination reveals multiple brown to black globules and dots, structureless pigmented areas, and prominent, branching arborizing telangiectasias.
