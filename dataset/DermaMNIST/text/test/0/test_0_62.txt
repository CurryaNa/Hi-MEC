1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a predominant pink-to-red erythematous background, overlaid with significant white, crystalline areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending diffusely into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is heterogeneous, characterized by central white scale-crust and ulceration, surrounded by smoother, slightly translucent tissue.
5. Structural Features: Prominent structural features include central, white, chrysalis-like structures (shiny white structures) and fine, arborizing telangiectasias (tree-like vessels) within the erythematous portions.
