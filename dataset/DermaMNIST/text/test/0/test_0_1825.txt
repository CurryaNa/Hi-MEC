1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including pink-red, translucent white, and irregular patches of dark brown and black pigment.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with an asymmetrical, scalloped edge that fades into the surrounding skin in some areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is complex and uneven, featuring raised, smooth, pearly nodules combined with a central area of erosion or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key features include multiple translucent nodules, a central ulcerated area, and asymmetric, blotchy brown-black pigmentation, particularly at the periphery.
