1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a pink-to-violaceous (purplish) hue, accompanied by small, focal brown and black dots.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth and somewhat translucent, with evidence of small erosions or pits.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include fine, branching arborizing telangiectasias, particularly at the periphery, and small, focal ulcerations.
