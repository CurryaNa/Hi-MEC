1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple shades of brown, from light tan to dark brown, with small, interspersed yellowish-white globules.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, featuring a "moth-eaten" or scalloped edge that fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven and verrucous, with a waxy or greasy appearance and multiple elevations and depressions.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include numerous milia-like cysts (whitish globules) and comedo-like openings (dark plugs), along with cerebriform patterns of fissures and gyri.
