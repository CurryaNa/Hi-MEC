1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a background of erythema (red-pink), punctuated by multiple whitish-yellow structures and some areas of light brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, with the inflammatory component blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of its internal components.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven, appearing moist and characterized by multiple small, raised pustular elevations.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include numerous discrete, whitish-yellow, non-follicular pustules on an erythematous base, with some fine dotted vessels and small hemorrhagic crusts.
