1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a polychromatic pattern, featuring a central dark brown to black area, a surrounding pinkish-red (erythematous) background, and scattered white structures.
2. Border Appearance: The central pigmented component has a relatively defined but irregular border, while the overall lesion's periphery is ill-defined and merges with the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution, with the primary pigmented and crusted features located eccentrically.
4. Surface Texture: The surface shows a central, crusted plug of dark material and entrapped fine hairs, while the surrounding erythematous skin appears somewhat shiny.
5. Structural Features: Key structures include a central comedo-like opening containing a keratin plug and multiple vellus hairs, fine arborizing telangiectasias on the periphery, and multiple white perifollicular openings.
