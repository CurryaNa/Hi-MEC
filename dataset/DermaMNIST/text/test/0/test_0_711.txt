1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including pink, red, light brown, and focal dark brown-to-black areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and highly irregular, blending indistinctly into the surrounding skin in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears uneven, with evidence of fine scaling and focal areas of crusting.
5. Structural Features: Observed features include irregular dots and globules, structureless pink-red areas, and fine, short, linear or branching vessels (telangiectasias).
