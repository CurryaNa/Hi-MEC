1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, pink, and white scar-like areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and appear ill-defined or blurred in several segments.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven, featuring fine scaling and areas of central crusting or early ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include asymmetric blotches of pigment, multiple whitish circular regression structures (annular-granular pattern), and an erythematous blush.
