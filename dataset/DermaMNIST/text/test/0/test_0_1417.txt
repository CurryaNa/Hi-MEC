1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including pink, scar-like white, and varying shades of brown from light tan to dark brown/black.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, fading into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears slightly scaly with fine white keratinization and evidence of linear scratches or excoriations.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central, structureless, pinkish-white zone indicative of regression, surrounded by disorganized blotches and globules of pigment, along with shiny white lines (chrysalis structures).
