1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including dark brown, black, and prominent reddish-purple hues, with whitish-grey areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular in shape and appears somewhat indistinct, with a subtle erythematous halo at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is characterized by a scaly and crusted texture, with fine white lines indicating hyperkeratosis.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a multicomponent pattern with dark, structureless zones, irregular dots/globules, and multiple reddish-purple vascular lacunae.
