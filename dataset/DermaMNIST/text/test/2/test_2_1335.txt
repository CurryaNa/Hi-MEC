1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits significant polychromasia, presenting with shades of pink, red, brown, black, and a central blue-grey and whitish area.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly defined, featuring a scalloped and lobulated periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface has a shiny, translucent quality, particularly at the periphery, with a crusted and ulcerated central region.
5. Structural Features: Prominent structures include arborizing telangiectasias, leaf-like areas, blue-grey ovoid nests, and a large central ulceration.
