1. Primary Colors: The lesion is multicolored, exhibiting shades of pink, yellowish-tan, brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: The border is relatively well-demarcated but irregular with a scalloped outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal structures and colors.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is verrucous (wart-like) and keratotic, with areas appearing crusted and greasy.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include multiple yellowish comedo-like openings (horn pseudocysts) and milia-like cysts, with focal areas of dark brown-to-black pigmentation.
