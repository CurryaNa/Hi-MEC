1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors (polychromia), including light brown, dark brown, black, and numerous small white to yellowish-white dots.
2. Border Appearance: Its border is relatively well-demarcated from the surrounding skin but follows an irregular, somewhat scalloped outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven and appears waxy and slightly verrucous, with areas of fine crusting and keratin deposits.
5. Structural Features: Key structures include multiple, well-defined, whitish milia-like cysts and darker, plugged, comedo-like openings, characteristic of a cerebriform pattern.
