1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown/black, and a prominent grey-blue to violaceous hue centrally.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and focally ill-defined, with some segments appearing smudged or fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of pigment and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with a partial effacement of the normal skin markings (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Internal structures include an atypical, disorganized pigment network at the periphery and structureless, homogenous areas of dark and grey-blue pigmentation centrally.
