1. Primary Colors: The lesion is polychromatic, exhibiting shades of pink-red, light to dark brown, and areas with a grey-violaceous hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, gradually fading into the surrounding skin without a distinct edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) that are partially effaced or interrupted.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include fine, branching vessels (arborizing telangiectasias), scattered brown and grey dots/globules, and structureless pigmented areas.
