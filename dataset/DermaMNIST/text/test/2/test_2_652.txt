1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with two main colors, a light tan-brown and a darker brown concentrated in globules.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and scalloped, with some areas appearing indistinct and fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat (macular), with visible fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) passing through it.
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include aggregated brown globules forming a central cluster and a faint, disorganized reticular pattern at the periphery.
