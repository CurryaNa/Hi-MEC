1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light tan to dark brown, and subtle pinkish-red foci.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, with a feathered or streaky pattern where the pigment fades into the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with the natural skin lines (dermatoglyphics) being preserved and highlighted by the pigment.
5. Structural Features: A distinct parallel furrow pattern is observed, characterized by linear pigmentation concentrated in the sulci (furrows) of the skin markings.
