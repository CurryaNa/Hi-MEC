1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including dark brown, black, light brown, and a central pinkish-white area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and indistinct, with scalloped edges and areas that fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its axis and the distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears irregular, with a central area of ulceration or erosion that may have some fine crusting.
5. Structural Features: Prominent structural features include an asymmetric, dark brown to black blotch, a central zone of regression with visible dotted vessels, and an atypical peripheral pigment network.
