1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including light brown and tan, with some focal darker brown areas set against a pinkish-red (erythematous) background.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, gradually fading into the surrounding skin without a sharp demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears relatively flat with a fine, slightly dry or scaly texture, and visible skin lines (dermatoglyphics) are preserved.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a disorganized, faint reticular (net-like) pigment pattern and the presence of fine, linear, and branching telangiectasias (small blood vessels).
