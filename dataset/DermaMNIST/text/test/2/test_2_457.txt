1. Primary Colors: The lesion is polychromatic, displaying multiple colors including light brown, dark brown, black, red, and areas of blue-gray pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are markedly irregular and ill-defined, with notching and an indistinct transition into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven, featuring areas of crusting and erosion, particularly in the central and superior portions.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic structures include an atypical pigment network, irregular blotches, blue-gray structures, and signs of ulceration with associated red dots and globules (vascular patterns).
