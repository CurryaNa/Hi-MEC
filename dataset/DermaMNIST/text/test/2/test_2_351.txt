1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from a light tan background to areas of dark brown and black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is highly irregular and indistinct, characterized by pseudopodal extensions and areas where the pigment network abruptly fades.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly flat, with visible skin lines and follicular openings, and lacks significant scaling or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key features include an atypical and disorganized pigment network, radial streaming (streaks) at the periphery, and structureless blotches of pigment.
