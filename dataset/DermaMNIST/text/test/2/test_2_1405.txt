1. Primary Colors: The lesion is polychromatic, displaying shades of pink, red, violaceous, white, and dark brown to black.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and focally ill-defined, especially in the superior and left quadrants.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven and lobulated, with areas of fine white scale and dark crusting.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include dotted and glomerular vessels creating a strawberry-like pattern, asymmetric blotches of dark pigment, and white keratinous clods.
