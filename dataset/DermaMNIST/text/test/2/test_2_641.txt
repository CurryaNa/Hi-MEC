1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light tan to dark brown, with some yellowish-orange hues.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular with a scalloped edge and appears well-demarcated in most areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface has a waxy and slightly verrucous (wart-like) texture with accentuated skin lines and fine keratin deposits.
5. Structural Features: Distinct structural features include a cobblestone pattern of multiple globules, numerous milia-like cysts, and comedo-like openings.
