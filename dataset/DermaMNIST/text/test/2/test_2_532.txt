1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including black, dark brown, pink, and red, along with a whitish-grey veil over some areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, featuring notching and a radial streaming pattern that blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is complex and uneven, with a centrally raised, crusted, and possibly ulcerated component.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central disorganized nodule with black crusting, polymorphous vascular patterns (dotted and linear irregular vessels), and a surrounding erythematous halo.
