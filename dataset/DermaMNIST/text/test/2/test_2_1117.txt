1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a multicomponent color pattern, featuring light brown, pink-violaceous, and focal white areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending indistinctly with the adjacent normal skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with some loss of fine skin markings (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include perifollicular white halos, fine arborizing telangiectasias, and a subtle pseudoreticular light brown network, with preserved terminal hair follicles.
