1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light tan to a focal area of dark brown and black.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly defined, with pigment fading and smudging into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible across the pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Key features include an eccentric dark brown blotch and structureless light brown areas; some shiny white structures (chrysalis structures) are also visible.
