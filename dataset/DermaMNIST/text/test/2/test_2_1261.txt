1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits marked polychromasia, with prominent shades of dark brown, black, violaceous-gray, and areas of white-pink.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly defined, featuring a scalloped and leaf-like periphery that fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is complex and irregular, demonstrating areas of ulceration, dark crusting, and a somewhat glistening or moist appearance.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic structures include multiple large blue-gray ovoid nests, arborizing vessels, leaf-like areas at the periphery, and central ulceration with overlying hemorrhagic crust.
