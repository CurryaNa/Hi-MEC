1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays color variegation, with shades ranging from light brown and dark brown to focal areas of black and a surrounding pinkish-red erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, showing a blurred and indistinct transition into the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, without evidence of significant scaling, ulceration, or crusting.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic examination reveals an atypical pigment network, blotches of dark pigment, and prominent, irregularly branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias).
