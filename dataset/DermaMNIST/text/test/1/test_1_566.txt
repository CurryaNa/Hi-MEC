1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and pinkish-white, with prominent reddish-purple vascular structures.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, with feathery, radial extensions and spoke-wheel-like projections (pseudopods).
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of pigment and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally flat, with areas of subtle scale and focal ulceration or crusting over the darker pigmented foci.
5. Structural Features: Key dermatoscopic features include multiple dark brown to blue-gray globules, leaf-like areas at the periphery, and prominent arborizing telangiectasias.
