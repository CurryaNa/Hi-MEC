1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, black, and areas of pinkish-white.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, featuring areas where pigment fades and radiates into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of pigment and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is generally smooth with preserved skin lines, but it is interrupted by a focal area of crusting or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Observed features include an atypical, disrupted pigment network, asymmetrically distributed dark brown to black dots and globules, and a central dark, irregularly shaped ulceration or crusted pit.
