1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a primary pinkish-red (erythematous) coloration, interspersed with shiny whitish areas and subtle violaceous hues.
2. Border Appearance: Its borders are irregular and somewhat lobulated, yet remain well-demarcated from the surrounding hair-bearing skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears glistening, non-scaly, and has a mamillated or bumpy texture.
5. Structural Features: Distinct arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels) are prominent across this raised nodule, which also features a subtle central ulceration and shiny white structures.
