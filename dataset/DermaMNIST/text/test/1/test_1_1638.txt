1. Primary Colors: The lesion is polychromatic, presenting with pink-red, translucent white, light brown, and focal areas of dark brown to black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, particularly on the superior and right lateral aspects where the pigment blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth and waxy, with evidence of central ulceration or erosion.
5. Structural Features: Prominent arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels) are present, along with a central ulcer, blue-gray globules, and peripheral leaf-like areas.
