1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits marked polychromia, presenting with shades of pink, red, light brown, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, featuring notching and areas where the pigment fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears slightly elevated and has a focally crusted or scaly texture, particularly over the central pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include multiple irregular dark brown to black globules and dots, fine linear and branching vessels (telangiectasias), and an area of lighter pigmentation suggesting possible regression.
