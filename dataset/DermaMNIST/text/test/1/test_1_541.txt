1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a primary pink-to-red (erythematous) base, interspersed with whitish atrophic areas and scattered, pinpoint dark brown to black globules.
2. Border Appearance: The border is well-demarcated yet highly irregular, displaying both scalloped and angulated segments.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears uneven and slightly atrophic, with fine scaling and evidence of focal erosion or crusting.
5. Structural Features: Prominent, fine, serpentine and arborizing telangiectasias are visible across the surface, along with multiple small, focal ulcerations.
