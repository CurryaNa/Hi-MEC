1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a predominant pink-to-red (erythematous) coloration, interspersed with subtle whitish areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and indistinct, gradually blending into the surrounding skin with an irregular outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and slightly shiny, lacking any significant scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Prominent, fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) are clearly visible across the lesion's surface. Some shiny white lines (chrysalis structures) may also be present.
