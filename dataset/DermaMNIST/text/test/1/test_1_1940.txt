1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including a pink-red background, a central violaceous to reddish-brown pigmented area, and whitish scar-like zones.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, with the pigmented component blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface features a central linear ulceration with associated crusting, while surrounding areas have a somewhat shiny or pearly appearance.
5. Structural Features: Prominent, branching arborizing telangiectasias are a key feature, along with a focal pigmented area and central erosion.
