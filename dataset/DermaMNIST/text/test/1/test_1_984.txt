1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits marked color variegation, presenting with shades of light brown, dark brown, black, and areas of pinkish-violaceous hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly defined, featuring both abrupt cut-offs and areas that fade indistinctly into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth, traversed by fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and a single hair shaft.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic structures include an atypical and disorganized pigment network, multiple irregular blotches and clods of dark pigment, and features of regression such as scar-like depigmentation and gray-blue peppering.
