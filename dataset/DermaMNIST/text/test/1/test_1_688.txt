1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including a pink-erythematous background with focal areas of black, dark brown, and reddish-brown crust.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and irregular, particularly where the erythematous component blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular and disrupted, characterized by central erosion and hemorrhagic crusting.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central ulceration, fine arborizing telangiectasias (branching vessels) on the periphery, and scattered blue-gray globules and dots.
