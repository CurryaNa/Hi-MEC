1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, predominantly dark brown and black, with some areas of lighter brown.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and ill-defined, exhibiting a starburst or spidery pattern with indistinct projections.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, without evidence of scaling, crusting, or significant elevation.
5. Structural Features: Distinct structural features include peripherally arranged dark streaks (pseudopods) and globules radiating from a disorganized central pigmented area.
