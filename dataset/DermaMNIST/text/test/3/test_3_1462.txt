1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including pink, red, and light brown, with a prominent central whitish, scar-like area.
2. Border Appearance: The border is ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending into the surrounding skin without a sharp demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface shows central atrophy with fine wrinkling, creating a slightly depressed and scarred texture.
5. Structural Features: Notable features include a central atrophic scar-like zone, surrounding erythema, subtle peripheral telangiectasias, and patchy light brown pigmentation at the margins.
