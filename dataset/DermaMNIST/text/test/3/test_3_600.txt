1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a violaceous (purple-pink) hue, a central yellowish-tan area, and overlying white structures.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and fade into the surrounding skin, forming a roughly round to oval configuration.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly symmetrical in its overall circular shape but demonstrates asymmetry in its internal color and structural patterns.
4. Surface Texture: The surface displays fine, intersecting white lines, giving it a scaly or cracked appearance.
5. Structural Features: A prominent network of fine, reticular white lines (Wickham's striae) is visible across the surface, particularly over the central yellowish zone and the peripheral violaceous halo.
