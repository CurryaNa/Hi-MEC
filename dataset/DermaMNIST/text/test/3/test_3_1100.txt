1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents a multicolor pattern, featuring various shades of brown, prominent pink-red erythema, and central white scar-like depigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular in shape and indistinct, fading into the surrounding skin rather than having a sharp demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in its overall contour and in the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly flat, with a central area of atrophy or scarring that may be slightly depressed.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a reticular pigment network at the periphery and a large central area of regression with scar-like white structures and erythema.
