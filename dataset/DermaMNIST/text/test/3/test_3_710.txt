1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays marked color variegation, with areas of black, dark brown, light brown, pink-red, and whitish-blue hues.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, showing an indistinct transition into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is complex, featuring areas of ulceration with associated crusting, alongside smoother, slightly shiny regions.
5. Structural Features: Key features include structureless brown and black blotches, an atypical pigment network, and areas of regression with scar-like depigmentation and prominent erythema.
