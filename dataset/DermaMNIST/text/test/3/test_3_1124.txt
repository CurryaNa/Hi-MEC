1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a combination of pinkish-red and areas of light brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending into the surrounding normal skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) that are partially effaced in the center.
5. Structural Features: There is a central area of subtle depression or atrophy, with fine, linear vessels and a faint, patchy brown pigment network at the periphery.
