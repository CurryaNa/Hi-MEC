1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a prominent black nodule, dark red-purple (violaceous) areas, and dark brown blotches.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined, featuring scalloped edges and areas of pigment diffusion into the surrounding epidermis.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is complex, with a raised, smooth, globular central component and flatter surrounding areas.
5. Structural Features: An eccentric, structureless black nodule is present, surrounded by reddish-purple blotches and globules, suggestive of hemorrhage or vascular lacunae.
