1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits color variegation, presenting with multiple shades including light brown, dark brown, black, and areas of pinkish-white regression.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in contour and ill-defined, showing both sharp cut-offs and areas that fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven with distorted skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and fine vellus hairs present throughout.
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include a disorganized pigment network, structureless blotches of dark pigment, and a prominent central area of scar-like regression.
