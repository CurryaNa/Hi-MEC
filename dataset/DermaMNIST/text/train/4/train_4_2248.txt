1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a pearly pink periphery, erythematous red areas, focal brown-black pigmentation, and a central bluish-gray hue.
2. Border Appearance: The border is relatively well-demarcated but irregular, featuring a characteristic raised and rolled (pearly) edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears somewhat translucent and waxy, with evidence of central ulceration and fine crusting.
5. Structural Features: Distinct arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels) are prominent over the surface, and there is a central area of ulceration and pigmentation.
