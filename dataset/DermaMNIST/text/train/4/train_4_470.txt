1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors (polychromia), including shades of light and dark brown, pink-red, and whitish-gray areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with some segments appearing blurred and fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is varied, appearing mostly smooth but with subtle textural irregularities and possibly fine scaling in the erythematous areas.
5. Structural Features: Notable structures include an atypical pigment network at the periphery, a central zone of regression with scar-like depigmentation, and arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels).
