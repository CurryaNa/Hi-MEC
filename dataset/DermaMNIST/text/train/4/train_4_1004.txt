1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including various shades of light to medium brown, prominent pink-red (erythema), and focal whitish-yellow areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular in shape and generally ill-defined, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is papular and verrucous, presenting a "cobblestone" or mammillated appearance rather than being smooth or scaly.
5. Structural Features: Distinctive features include numerous aggregated, small, round, brownish globules and whitish milia-like cysts, forming a cerebriform pattern.
