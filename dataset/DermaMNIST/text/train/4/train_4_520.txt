1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits significant color variegation, presenting with multiple shades of dark brown, black, and a prominent pinkish-white area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly defined, featuring notched, scalloped edges and an abrupt, indistinct termination of the pigment pattern.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears largely flat but with textural irregularities, including a fine reticular pattern in some areas and structureless zones in others.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic features include an atypical pigment network, peripheral streaks (pseudopods), blotches of dark pigment, and a large, structureless pink-white area suggestive of regression.
