1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors (polychromia), including light brown, dark brown, and areas of black. A subtle pinkish hue is also noted, particularly towards the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with a feathery or starburst pattern where the pigment network fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with preserved fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) in some areas but effacement in others.
5. Structural Features: Key features include an atypical, disorganized pigment network at the periphery and a central, amorphous dark brown-to-black blotch.
