1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a pearly, translucent white and pink periphery, a central gray-white atrophic area, and a small focus of brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is well-demarcated, raised, and rolled with a regular, rounded contour.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly symmetrical in its overall round shape, but its internal structures and coloration are asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: The surface has a waxy and translucent quality at the border, with central erosion and slight crusting.
5. Structural Features: Distinctive features include a classic rolled border, fine arborizing telangiectasias (branching vessels) on the surface, and central ulceration.
