1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays significant color variegation, presenting with shades of dark brown, black, and a prominent reddish-pink hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with some areas appearing notched and others fading indistinctly into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface exhibits fine, whitish scaling or linear streaks, also known as chrysalis structures.
5. Structural Features: Internal structures include an atypical, thickened pigment network in one area and large, structureless blotches of dark and reddish pigment elsewhere.
