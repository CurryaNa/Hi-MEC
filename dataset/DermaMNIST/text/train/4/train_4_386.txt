1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays marked color variegation, with areas of black, dark brown, tan, and a peripheral erythematous (red/pink) halo, as well as whitish-gray zones.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly demarcated, featuring notching and fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is profoundly asymmetrical in both contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is heterogeneous, with a prominent crusted and possibly ulcerated area on one side, while other parts appear scaly and slightly elevated.
5. Structural Features: There is a nodular, crusted black component, atypical pigment network, structureless blotches, and evidence of regression (gray-white areas).
