1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits marked color variegation, with areas of light brown, dark brown, black, and hints of blue-gray.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, with prominent notching and an abrupt, jagged cut-off in some areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: This lesion is profoundly asymmetrical in both its outline and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears mostly flat, with some loss or effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) over the most heavily pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: It displays a disorganized, multicomponent pattern, including structureless blotches of dark pigment and areas suggestive of regression.
