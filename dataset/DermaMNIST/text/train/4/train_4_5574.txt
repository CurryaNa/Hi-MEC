1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors (polychromia), including light brown, dark brown, black, and a central area of white scar-like depigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with radial streaming and pseudopod-like extensions fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with evidence of central atrophy and loss of normal skin markings, a feature known as regression.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is present, characterized by a disorganized pigment network, peripheral streaks, dark blotches, and a central scar-like area with absent structures.
