1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including light brown, dark brown, black, and a central white-pink scar-like area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, with areas of notching and fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears varied, with some smooth areas and a central region that looks atrophic or scarred.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include disorganized, blotchy pigmentation, a fragmented peripheral pigment network, and a significant central area of regression with fibrosis.
