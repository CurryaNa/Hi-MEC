2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly demarcated, showing both scalloped edges and areas that blur into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.

5. Structural Features: Key features include a multicomponent pattern with a disorganized pigment network, central dark brown/black globules, white scar-like areas of regression, and fine, irregular vascular structures.
