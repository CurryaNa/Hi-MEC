1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple shades, including light brown, dark brown, and focal black areas, on an erythematous (pink) background.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, with pigmentation fading indistinctly into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: This lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with some effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Observed structures include a disorganized reticular network, scattered black dots and globules, and fine, linear telangiectatic vessels.
