4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth, with fine skin markings (dermatoglyphics) preserved and visible across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is observed, including a peripheral reticular network, central globules/dots, a scar-like area of regression, and an eccentric dark brown/black blotch.
