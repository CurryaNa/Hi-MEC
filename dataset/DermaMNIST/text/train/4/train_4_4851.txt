1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays significant color variegation, presenting with shades of light brown, dark brown, black, and a peripheral pinkish-red erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, featuring both sharp and blurred edges that fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of pigment and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, though there is some effacement of the normal skin lines over the most pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include an atypical pigment network, central blotches of dark brown and black pigment, and irregular dots and globules.
