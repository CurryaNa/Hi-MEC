3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.

5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is observed, including an atypical pigment network, dark blotches, and prominent blue-white structures suggestive of a veil and regression.
