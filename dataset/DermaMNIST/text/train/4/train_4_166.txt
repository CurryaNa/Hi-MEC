1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light tan to dark brown, against a pinkish-red background.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, with indistinct margins that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, although some areas show fine, whitish scaling.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a disorganized reticular pigment network, blotchy and clumpy areas of pigmentation, and prominent, fine, branching blood vessels (telangiectasias).
