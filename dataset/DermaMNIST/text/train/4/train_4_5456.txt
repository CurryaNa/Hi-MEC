1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including erythematous pink and red, translucent pearly-white areas, and small foci of brown-black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly defined, with some sections appearing slightly rolled and raised.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is varied, featuring shiny, atrophic areas combined with superficial erosion and crusting.
5. Structural Features: Prominent, fine, arborizing telangiectasias (blood vessels) are visible, particularly at the periphery, along with a central area of ulceration.
