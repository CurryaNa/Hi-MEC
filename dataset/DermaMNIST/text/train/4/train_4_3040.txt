1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including a prominent whitish-gray area, pink-red periphery, and a central blue-black pigmented region.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and focally indistinct, particularly along the superior and lateral edges.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface exhibits a whitish, crystalline/scaly texture in the upper portion and evidence of central ulceration or crusting.
5. Structural Features: Key features include prominent white shiny structures (chrysalis structures), polymorphous vessels (including linear-irregular and dotted vessels), and a large, structureless blue-black area with central ulceration.
