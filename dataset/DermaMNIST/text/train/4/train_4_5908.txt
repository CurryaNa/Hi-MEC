1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays polychromasia, with distinct areas of light brown, dark brown, black, and erythematous pink/red.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and highly irregular, featuring notching and areas where the pigment fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its contour and the distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is varied, featuring a central area of ulceration with hemorrhagic crusting and slight scaling at the periphery.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central ulcer, irregular blotches of pigment, and arborizing telangiectasias visible in the erythematous base.
