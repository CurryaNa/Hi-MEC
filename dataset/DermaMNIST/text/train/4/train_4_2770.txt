1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including light brown, dark brown, black, and a focal pink-red area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and appear indistinct and blurred, particularly along the superior and right-sided margins.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth, without evidence of significant scaling or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is observed, characterized by atypical pigment network, central dark blotches, and an erythematous area suggesting regression or increased vascularity.
