1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including dark brown, light brown, and areas with a pinkish to violaceous hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, fading indistinctly into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with some effacement of the normal skin markings (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Key features include central, dark brown blotches/clods and a disorganized, faint reticular pattern at the periphery.
