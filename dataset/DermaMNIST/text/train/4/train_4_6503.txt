1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including dark brown, light brown, and prominent pink-red areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly defined, featuring feathery, pseudopodal extensions into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment and vascular structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, characteristic of a dermoscopic view with immersion fluid, as indicated by small air bubbles.
5. Structural Features: Key features include an atypical pigment network, central dark brown blotches, radial streaming at the periphery, and polymorphous vessels (linear and dotted) within the erythematous zones.
