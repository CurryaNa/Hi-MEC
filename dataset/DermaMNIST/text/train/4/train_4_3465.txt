1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, black, and a focal pink-red area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and indistinct, with pigment fading and blending into the surrounding skin in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears slightly uneven with visible but focally disrupted skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Key features include a multicomponent pattern with disorganized pigment network, blotches, a central scar-like area of regression with fine linear vessels (telangiectasias), and eccentric hyperpigmentation.
