1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including shades of light and dark brown, with focal grey-black areas and a surrounding pinkish-red erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, appearing blurred as they merge with the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears slightly waxy and uneven, characterized by numerous small, whitish, pinpoint structures.
5. Structural Features: Prominent structural features include fine, branching arborizing telangiectasias, multiple white milia-like cysts, and areas of structureless brown-grey pigmentation.
