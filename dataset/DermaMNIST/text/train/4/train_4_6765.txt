1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, from tan to dark brown, with some focal blackish areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are relatively well-demarcated but have an irregular, scalloped outline rather than a smooth, uniform edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is not smooth; it has a finely mamillated or verrucous texture, creating a cerebriform (brain-like) or cobblestone appearance.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a slightly elevated surface with multiple dark, comedo-like openings and small, whitish milia-like cysts.
