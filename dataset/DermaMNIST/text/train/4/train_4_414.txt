1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a variegated color palette, including prominent pink-to-red erythema, central whitish atrophic regions, and subtle, patchy light-brown pigmentation at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: Its borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending indistinctly into the surrounding skin along the elongated axis.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its linear shape and the internal distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with evidence of atrophy, characterized by a slightly shiny and finely wrinkled texture.
5. Structural Features: Distinctive features include fine, linear, and branching telangiectasias, particularly within the erythematous zones, and central scar-like changes with loss of normal skin markings.
