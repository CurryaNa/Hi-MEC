2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly demarcated, featuring scalloped edges and areas that fade indistinctly into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth, with effacement of normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) in the more heavily pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Multiple dermoscopic features are observed, including an atypical pigment network at the periphery, as well as disorganized globules and blotches centrally and to the right.
