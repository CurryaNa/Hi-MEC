1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits polychromia, presenting with multiple shades of light brown, dark brown, black, and pink, along with whitish scar-like areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, with pigment fading and spreading unevenly into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture appears irregular and non-uniform, with visible and distorted skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include asymmetric blotches of dark pigment, areas of regression (whitish/pink zones), and the presence of fine, branching blood vessels (telangiectasias).
