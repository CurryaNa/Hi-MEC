1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including various shades of brown, black, pink, and areas of white scar-like depigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, with areas of notching and fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is heterogeneous, presenting with both smooth areas and a central atrophic, slightly shiny or scarred texture where skin markings are lost.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include an atypical pigment network, irregular blotches of dark pigment, and extensive central regression structures with peppering.
