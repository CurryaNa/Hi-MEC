3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.

5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic structures include a central amorphous dark blotch, peripheral streaks and pseudopods, and polymorphous vascular patterns in the erythematous zones.
