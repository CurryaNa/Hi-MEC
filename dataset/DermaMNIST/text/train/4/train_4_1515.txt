1. Primary Colors: The lesion demonstrates significant color variegation, including shades of dark brown, black, light brown, and erythematous pink/red.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, with areas of notching and blurring that fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular and appears slightly raised and nodular in some areas, with disruption or effacement of normal skin lines.
5. Structural Features: Key features include atypical pigment network, structureless blotches, polymorphous vessels, and evidence of regression such as scar-like depigmentation.
