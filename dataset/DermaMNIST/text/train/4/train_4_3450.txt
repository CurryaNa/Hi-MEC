1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a pinkish-red (erythematous) base, and mottled light to dark brown hyperpigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its color.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears uneven and textured, with prominent follicular openings giving it a stippled or peau d'orange appearance.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include patchy, reticular brown pigmentation superimposed on an erythematous background, with numerous small, pale, uniform papules corresponding to follicular ostia.
