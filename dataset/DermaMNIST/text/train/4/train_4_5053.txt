1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including dark brown, light brown, and reddish-pink, surrounded by a distinct whitish, hypopigmented halo.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, featuring a scalloped and starburst-like periphery that fades into the surrounding halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of pigment and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears slightly irregular with preserved skin markings (dermatoglyphics) visible, but without overt scaling or crusting.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a disorganized, atypical pigment network, central structureless brown and pinkish blotches, and a prominent peripheral depigmented halo, which may indicate regression.
