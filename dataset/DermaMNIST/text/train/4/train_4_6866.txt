1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits marked color variegation, with areas of light brown, dark brown, black, and a prominent erythematous (red/pink) central zone.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, featuring blurred and indistinct margins that fade into the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) that are partially effaced in the central region.
5. Structural Features: Internal structures include an atypical pigment network at the periphery, central blotches, and features of regression such as scar-like depigmentation and erythema.
