1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and numerous whitish-yellowish globules against a pink, erythematous background.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, featuring a moth-eaten pattern where the pigmentation abruptly stops or fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both color distribution and the arrangement of its structural components.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears uneven and waxy, with visible small papules and keratin-filled invaginations.
5. Structural Features: Key dermoscopic features include multiple milia-like cysts (whitish globules) and comedo-like openings (dark keratin plugs), arranged in a cerebriform or brain-like pattern.
