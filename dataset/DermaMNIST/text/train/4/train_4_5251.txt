1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with variegation in color, including light brown, dark brown, and areas of black.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and indistinct, with pigment fading into the surrounding skin in a starburst or reticular pattern.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears slightly elevated and subtly textured, with some disruption of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: A central, darker, and more raised (papular) area is surrounded by a peripheral network of lighter brown pigmentation.
