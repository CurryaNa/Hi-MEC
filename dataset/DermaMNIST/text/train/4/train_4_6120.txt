3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.

5. Structural Features: Observed features include a disorganized, multicomponent pattern with blue-gray globules, whitish scar-like areas indicative of regression, and fine, branching arborizing telangiectasias.
