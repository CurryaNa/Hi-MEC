1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, black, and prominent reddish-pink areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with notching and fading into the surrounding skin rather than a sharp demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears uneven, with fine scaling and a focal area of central crusting or early ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include structureless, blotchy pigmentation, particularly in the central dark brown/black area, and the presence of atypical, fine linear or dotted vessels in the erythematous regions.
