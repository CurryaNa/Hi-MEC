1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors (polychromia), including pink, light brown, dark brown, and focal black areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, particularly in the pigmented portions which blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface consists of a centrally raised, smooth-to-slightly-scaly papule, with the surrounding pigmented areas appearing flat (macular).
5. Structural Features: A prominent feature is a central pink, non-pigmented nodule surrounded by eccentric, blotchy, and reticular brown-to-black pigmentation.
