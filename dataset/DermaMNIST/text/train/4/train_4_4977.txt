1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including varying shades of light to dark brown, black, and areas of pinkish-white regression.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with a scalloped or notched outline that fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly flat and smooth, without evidence of significant scaling, ulceration, or crusting.
5. Structural Features: Notable features include an atypical, disorganized pigment network, blotchy pigmentation, central whitish scar-like areas (regression), and surrounding telangiectasias (fine red vessels).
