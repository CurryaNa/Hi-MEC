1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a pink-to-red erythematous background, interspersed with light brown pigmentation and yellowish-orange globules.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion's border is relatively well-circumscribed and circular, though it appears somewhat indistinct due to a surrounding erythematous halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is largely symmetrical and round, but there is asymmetry in the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is multicomponent and slightly papular, appearing uneven due to the presence of multiple small, smooth-surfaced globules.
5. Structural Features: Distinctive features include an aggregation of yellowish-orange globules in a "cobblestone" pattern, overlying a background of erythema and fine, granular brown pigmentation.
