1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a variegated color pattern, including shades of pink, light brown, and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a scalloped or lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is asymmetrical, both in contour and in the distribution of internal structures and colors.

5. Structural Features: Prominent features include multiple milia-like cysts (small, whitish-yellow globules) and comedo-like openings (dark keratin plugs), creating a cerebriform or brain-like pattern.
