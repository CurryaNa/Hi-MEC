1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including pink-red (erythematous), white, and focal areas of irregular brown and black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are poorly demarcated and irregular, blending indistinctly with the surrounding skin in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is varied, presenting with areas of ulceration, fine white scaling, and focal dark crusting.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include asymmetric ulceration, polymorphous vessels, a blotch of dark pigment, and white shiny streaks (chrysalis structures).
