2. Border Appearance: The visible border is ill-defined and irregular, lacking a sharp demarcation from the surrounding tissue.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in terms of its internal structure and color distribution.

5. Structural Features: The lesion is characterized by multiple, well-demarcated, rounded whitish clods (globules) surrounded by a delicate network of fine, serpentine, and linear-irregular vessels (crown vessels).
