1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits variegation with shades of light to dark brown, pink-erythema, and a central white-to-gray scarlike area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, featuring stellate or spidery projections of pigment that fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The central portion appears atrophic and scaly, with possible early crusting, while the peripheral pigmented areas are smoother.
5. Structural Features: There is a prominent central zone of regression, characterized by white scarring and atrophy, surrounded by irregular, blotchy peripheral pigmentation.
