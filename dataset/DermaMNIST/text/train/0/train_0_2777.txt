1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a pink-to-red (erythematous) base, a central pearly white-to-translucent area, and scattered brown-to-black pigmented dots.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are poorly demarcated and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal structures and colors.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears waxy and slightly raised in the center, with a bumpy or papular texture at the periphery and potential for fine crusting.
5. Structural Features: A central, translucent papule is present, surrounded by erythema and multiple small, dark pigmented globules.
