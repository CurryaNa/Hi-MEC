1. Primary Colors: The lesion is multicolored, exhibiting areas of dark brown, erythematous pink-red, and prominent milky-white.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of colors and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is uneven, with evidence of fine scaling and subtle crusting, particularly over the erythematous areas.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include a structureless brown area, multiple white clods (milia-like cysts), and polymorphous vessels within the erythematous zones.
