1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors including pinkish-red (erythema), whitish-gray, and irregular areas of light to dark brown and black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending indeterminately with the surrounding sun-damaged skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is characterized by central ulceration with hemorrhagic crusting and peripheral areas of fine, white scaling.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central erosion/ulceration, irregular blotches of brown pigment, and fine, branching telangiectatic vessels on the erythematous background.
