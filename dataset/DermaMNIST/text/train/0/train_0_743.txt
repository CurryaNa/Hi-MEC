1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and areas of pinkish-red erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and irregular, exhibiting a poorly defined, moth-eaten periphery that fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is predominantly flat and smooth, with preserved skin markings (dermatoglyphics) visible throughout.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is observed, featuring an atypical pigment network, scattered brown dots/globules, fine linear vessels (telangiectasias), and structureless pink-white areas.
