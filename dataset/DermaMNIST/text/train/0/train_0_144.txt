1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including pink-red, light brown, dark brown/black, and focal whitish areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, with pigmented areas fading indistinctly into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears uneven, with some smooth areas interspersed with subtle scaling and focal superficial erosions or crusting.
5. Structural Features: Prominent structural features include atypical, blotchy brown pigmentation, numerous arborizing (tree-like) telangiectasias, and shiny white crystalline structures.
