1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits significant color variegation (polychromasia), presenting with areas of black, blue-grey, brown, and a central whitish-grey zone.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, with notching and an indistinct transition into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular, featuring central ulceration and crusting over a whitish, possibly fibrotic, scar-like area.
5. Structural Features: Key features include multi-component patterning with asymmetric pigmentation, a blue-grey ovoid structure, central scar-like regression, and prominent, irregular telangiectasias at the periphery.
