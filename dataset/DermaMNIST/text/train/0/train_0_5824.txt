1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and a prominent pinkish-red erythema, most concentrated centrally.
2. Border Appearance: The borders of the pigmented and erythematous areas are ill-defined and irregular, with a diffuse transition between different zones.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its color distribution and the arrangement of its internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, without significant scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key features include multiple small, whitish, annular-granular structures (rosettes) and blotchy, structureless areas of brown pigmentation.
