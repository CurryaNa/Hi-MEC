1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including pink, light brown, dark brown, and focal purplish/violaceous dots.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, gradually fading into the surrounding skin rather than having a sharp demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface has an uneven, papillomatous, or cobblestone-like texture, composed of multiple small, rounded globules.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include multiple whitish milia-like cysts, a faint peripheral pigment network, and dotted vascular structures.
