1. Primary Colors: The lesion is polychromatic, displaying a combination of pink-red, light brown, dark brown, and focal blue-grey pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, featuring scalloped edges and areas where pigmentation fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: Its surface texture is variegated, with some areas appearing smooth and translucent while others are slightly raised and nodular.
5. Structural Features: Key features include arborizing telangiectasias (branching vessels), multiple blue-grey ovoid nests, irregular blotches of pigment, and several small, whitish, milia-like cysts.
