1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from tan to dark brown, along with a focal grey-white area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with feathery or streaky edges that blur into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: A fine, whitish scale or hyperkeratosis is visible on the surface, particularly over the central, darker portions.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a disorganized pigment network, pseudopods or radial streaming at the periphery, and a central grey-white veil obscuring a structureless dark brown blotch.
