1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including pearly white, pink-red, and areas of dark brown to black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined, with a leaf-like or scalloped shape that blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical with respect to both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface presents with central ulceration and crusting, while some areas, particularly at the periphery, appear shiny or translucent.
5. Structural Features: Key features include prominent arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels), central ulceration, and whitish, scar-like fibrotic areas.
