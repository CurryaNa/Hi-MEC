1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including a central erythematous (pink-red) patch, surrounded by areas of light and dark brown pigmentation, with scattered small white foci.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with areas of notching and fading, particularly where the pigmentation meets the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth but exhibits subtle textural irregularities and contains small, slightly raised or keratotic-appearing white dots.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a central erythematous blotch, an atypical peripheral pigment network, and multiple small, whitish structures suggestive of regression or keratosis.
