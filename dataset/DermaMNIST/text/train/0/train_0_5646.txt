1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and prominent pink-red areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, with pigmented areas appearing fragmented and indistinctly merging with the erythematous background.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth and slightly translucent, without significant scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key features include disorganized, blotchy brown pigmentation and prominent, fine, branching telangiectatic vessels (arborizing telangiectasias).
