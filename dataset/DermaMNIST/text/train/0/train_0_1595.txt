1. Primary Colors: The lesion is multicolored, exhibiting shades of light to dark brown, pink-red (erythema), and focal white areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, with pigmented structures showing a disorganized, feathery edge that blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears uneven with a slightly cobblestoned or lobulated texture, and contains shiny white linear structures (chrysalis structures).
5. Structural Features: Prominent arborizing telangiectasias are visible, along with brown clods, leaf-like areas, and a potential small central ulceration.
