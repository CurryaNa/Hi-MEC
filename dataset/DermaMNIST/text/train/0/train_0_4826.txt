1. Primary Colors: The lesion is predominantly erythematous (red) and violaceous (purplish-red), with multiple yellowish-white globules.
2. Border Appearance: The border is somewhat irregular and lobulated, but it is relatively well-demarcated from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is roughly round but it is asymmetrical in its internal structure, color, and vascular pattern.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is not smooth, presenting a multi-lobulated or "cobblestone" texture composed of multiple small papules.
5. Structural Features: Distinctive features include yellowish-white clods/globules, some with central follicular openings, and prominent, branching "crown" vessels that surround the lobules but do not cross the center.
