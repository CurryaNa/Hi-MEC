1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits polychromia, with a pink erythematous base, patches of dark and light brown pigmentation, and numerous whitish-yellow globules.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly demarcated, with a blotchy, moth-eaten appearance at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is verrucous and mammillated, characterized by a collection of small, raised, round papules.
5. Structural Features: Key features include numerous milia-like cysts (the whitish-yellow globules) and comedo-like openings, which are classic cerebriform or gyri/sulci-like patterns. Several terminal hairs are also seen emerging from the lesion's surface.
