1. Primary Colors: The lesion is polychromatic, presenting with a pink-to-red erythematous base and multiple areas of light and dark brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is relatively well-demarcated but irregular in shape, with a scalloped or lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of color and structures.

5. Structural Features: Key features include aggregated brown globules and numerous small, whitish, circular or ring-like structures, possibly related to follicular openings.
