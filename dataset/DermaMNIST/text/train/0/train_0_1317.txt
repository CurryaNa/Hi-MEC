1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including pink-red, a central whitish area, and peripheral light brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined, featuring stellate projections that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and atrophic centrally, with a loss of normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central whitish fibrotic/scar-like area, fine arborizing telangiectasias (blood vessels), and patchy peripheral pigmentation.
