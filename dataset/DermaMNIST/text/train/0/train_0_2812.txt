1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including a pink-to-red background with superimposed light brown, dark brown, and black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The pigmented areas have irregular, scalloped, and poorly demarcated borders that fade into the surrounding tissue.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its pigment and vascular structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface has a mamillated or cobblestone-like texture, composed of numerous small, soft-appearing papules.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include a cobblestone pattern with brown-to-black globules and dots concentrated in the sulci, as well as fine, arborizing telangiectasias throughout.
