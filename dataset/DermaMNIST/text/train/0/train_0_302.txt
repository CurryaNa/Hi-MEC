1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including various shades of brown (light to dark) and a prominent pink-to-red background erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending indistinctly into the surrounding skin with a moth-eaten appearance.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears relatively smooth, allowing the underlying skin texture and markings to be partially visible through the pigment.
5. Structural Features: Prominent structural features include an atypical, blotchy pigment network and fine, branching telangiectatic vessels. There are also areas of perifollicular hypopigmentation.
