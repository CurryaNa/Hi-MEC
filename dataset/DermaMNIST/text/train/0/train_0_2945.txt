1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a primary pink-to-red coloration, interspersed with whitish, translucent areas and focal light brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, lacking a sharp demarcation from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and slightly glossy, without evidence of significant scaling, crusting, or erosion.
5. Structural Features: Prominent, fine, branching arborizing telangiectasias are the most distinct feature, along with some shiny white structures and small, scattered pigment globules.
