1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a multicomponent color pattern, including pink-red, white, and areas of brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin, particularly in the erythematous zones.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears complex, with areas of erosion and fine scale-crust, particularly in the central, erythematous regions.
5. Structural Features: Multiple white clods (milia-like cysts) are present, along with polymorphous vessels (dotted and linear) and focal ulceration with hemorrhage.
