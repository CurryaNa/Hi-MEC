1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a pink to erythematous base, prominent central whitish areas, and subtle peripheral light brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is characterized by central scaling and keratosis, giving it a dry and slightly crusted appearance.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a central area of white scale and atrophy, surrounding erythema, and prominent perifollicular white halos.
