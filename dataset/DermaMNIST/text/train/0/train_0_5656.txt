1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including dark brown, black, pink-red, and focal white areas.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion's borders are irregular and indistinct, particularly where the pigmented and erythematous areas meet.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is heterogeneous, with a pebbly, reticulated texture in the pigmented portion and a smoother, raised surface in the pink nodular area.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include an atypical, thickened pigment network and an adjacent structureless pink nodule containing fine, linear/arborizing vessels and white scar-like streaks suggestive of regression.
