1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including dark brown, light brown, and prominent shiny white structures centrally.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly defined, with characteristic radial streaming and pseudopod-like extensions.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The central surface appears slightly raised and exhibits a scaly and crusted texture.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include a disorganized central pigmented area, shiny white crystalline structures (chrysalis structures), and peripheral radial streaks.
