1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a primary pink-to-red hue, interspersed with whitish, translucent areas and focal blue-grey and dark brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with the lesion's edge appearing blurred and blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is markedly asymmetrical in both contour and internal structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is shiny and appears somewhat translucent and waxy, with multiple smooth-surfaced nodules and areas of central erosion or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Distinctive structural features include prominent arborizing telangiectasias (branching vessels), multiple lobules, and blue-grey ovoid nests.
