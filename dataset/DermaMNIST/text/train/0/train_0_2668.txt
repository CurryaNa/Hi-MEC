1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays a multicomponent color pattern, with a dominant pink-red central area, peripheral light brown pigmentation, and numerous whitish halos.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending diffusely into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears subtly altered and slightly scaly, lacking the smooth, uniform texture of normal skin.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include dotted and glomerular (coiled) vessels, and distinct white keratotic halos surrounding follicular openings, creating a "strawberry-like" pattern.
