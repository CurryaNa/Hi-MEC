1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and yellowish-tan, with some subtle pinkish areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly demarcated in some areas, featuring a scalloped or lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven and papular, creating a cobblestone or cerebriform texture rather than being smooth or scaly.
5. Structural Features: Distinctive features include multiple aggregated yellowish globules, some with central umbilication or dark, comedo-like openings.
