1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including pink-red, light to dark brown, and prominent whitish-yellow areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and poorly demarcated, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is rough and uneven, characterized by significant scale-crust and areas of erosion.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include central ulceration covered by keratinous material, shiny white structures (chrysalis/crystalline structures), and irregular, blotchy pigmentation.
