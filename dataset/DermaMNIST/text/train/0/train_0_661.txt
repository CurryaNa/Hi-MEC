1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays a combination of white-gray, yellowish, and light to medium brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and slightly raised, forming a distinct, thread-like scaly ridge (collarette).
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is largely symmetrical, exhibiting a roughly circular to oval overall shape.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is markedly dry and hyperkeratotic, with a centrally crusted and scaly texture.
5. Structural Features: A central, atrophic, or keratotic plug is present, surrounded by concentric rings giving it a targetoid appearance, which is characteristic of a cornoid lamella.
