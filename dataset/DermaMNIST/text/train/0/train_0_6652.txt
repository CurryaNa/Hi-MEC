1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including a pinkish-red background, whitish-yellow circular structures, areas of light brown pigmentation, and distinct black plugs.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly demarcated, with the central pigmented and structured area blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular and bumpy, characterized by numerous openings and a slightly waxy or greasy appearance rather than being smooth or scaly.
5. Structural Features: Multiple prominent comedo-like openings are visible, many of which are filled with black, keratinous plugs. The lesion also features numerous milia-like cysts (whitish clods) and fissures, creating a cerebriform or brain-like pattern.
