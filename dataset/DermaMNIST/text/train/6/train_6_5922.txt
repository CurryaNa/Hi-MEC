1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a central black and dark red (hemorrhagic) core, accompanied by a distinct blue-white area on the left periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and indistinct in some areas, particularly where it transitions into the blue-white structure.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and somewhat globular, suggestive of a blood-filled or thrombosed structure, with possible central crusting.
5. Structural Features: A key structural feature is a central, dark, exophytic nodule, with a peripheral blue-white veil and a surrounding erythematous halo.
