1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays significant polychromia, presenting with black, dark brown, red, and violaceous hues.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are markedly irregular and ill-defined, featuring notching and areas where the pigment abruptly stops or fades.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular and exhibits prominent hemorrhagic crusting, indicative of ulceration and friability.
5. Structural Features: Structural features include large, structureless black areas, ulceration, red-black lacunae, and a distinct violaceous macule at the inferior-left periphery.
