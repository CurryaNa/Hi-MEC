1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a dark brown to black coloration, interspersed with reddish-brown hues and areas of whitish scale.
2. Border Appearance: The border is sharply demarcated and well-defined, with a regular, slightly scalloped edge, contributing to a "stuck-on" appearance.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is largely symmetrical, exhibiting a regular, oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is markedly dry and verrucous (wart-like), characterized by significant scaling and deep fissures.
5. Structural Features: Prominent cerebriform (brain-like) patterns are visible, consisting of distinct ridges (gyri) and furrows (sulci), with some keratin-filled, comedo-like openings.
