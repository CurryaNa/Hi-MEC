1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a polychromatic appearance, with translucent whitish-pink, red, and dark violaceous (purple) areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is well-demarcated from the surrounding skin but follows an irregular, lobulated contour.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its lobulated outline and the heterogeneous distribution of internal colors.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is glistening and smooth in parts, but predominantly bumpy and mamillated, resembling a cluster of small globules.
5. Structural Features: It is a raised, exophytic nodule composed of multiple, translucent lobules containing distinct, dark red-to-purple vascular lacunae.
