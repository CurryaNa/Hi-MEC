1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of red, from a bright red center to dark red and violaceous areas, surrounded by a pinkish-white halo.
2. Border Appearance: The central border is irregular and somewhat scalloped, while the peripheral halo's border is ill-defined and radiates outward.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal color patterns.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears moist and eroded, with evidence of hemorrhagic crusting and a fine collarette of scale at the periphery.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include multiple, well-defined red lacunae (globules), central ulceration, and fine, radiating telangiectatic vessels within the erythematous halo.
