1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, predominantly black, bright red, and a blue-white hue. Surrounding the lesion is a ring of erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, blending into the surrounding inflamed skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is ulcerated and features a prominent, friable hemorrhagic crust. Portions of the surface appear moist and exophytic.
5. Structural Features: Key features include central ulceration, polymorphous vascular patterns, and blue-white structureless areas, all within a raised, nodular configuration.
