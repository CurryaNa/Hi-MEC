1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including a central jet-black area, a blue-gray periphery, a surrounding white halo, and an outer erythematous ring.
2. Border Appearance: The border of the central pigmented structure is highly irregular, notched, and sharply demarcated from the surrounding white halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The central surface is ulcerated and heavily crusted, contrasting with the smoother, atrophic-appearing halo.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central ulcerated black nodule, a surrounding halo of depigmentation (regression), and an outer inflammatory erythematous ring.
