1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including dark brown, black, and areas of reddish-purple to violaceous hues.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are well-defined yet highly irregular, featuring a scalloped and lobulated contour.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is predominantly verrucous (wart-like) and crusted, giving it a rough and uneven texture.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include aggregated dark clods and globules creating a cobblestone pattern, a raised papular morphology, and evidence of hemorrhage or ulceration with crust at the superior aspect.
