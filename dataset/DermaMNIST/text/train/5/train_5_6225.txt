1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and a central violaceous (purplish-brown) blotch.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly demarcated, showing a starburst pattern with radial streaks and a fading reticular network at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible, though they are somewhat effaced over the central, denser area.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is observed, consisting of a central, structureless dark blotch and a peripheral pseudoreticular network with radial streaming.
