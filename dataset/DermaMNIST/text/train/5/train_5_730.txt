1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, including a light brown background and numerous dark brown to black foci.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined, with pigment fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with preserved skin markings and vellus hairs passing through it.
5. Structural Features: Distinctive features include multiple dark brown to black globules and dots clustered centrally upon a reticular or structureless light brown base.
