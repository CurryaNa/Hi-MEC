1. Primary Colors: The central lesion displays a dark brown to gray-blue color, surrounded by a subtle pinkish-violaceous halo, set against a background of light and dark brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The central nodular component has a well-defined, regular, ovoid border, while the surrounding background pigmentation is more diffuse and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: The central dark ovoid structure is largely symmetrical, but the overall lesion complex, including the background pigmented area, is asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with an intact epidermis and visible fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: A key feature is a well-circumscribed, pigmented nodule with central umbilication or a darker central depression, arising within a pre-existing blotchy pigmented macule.
