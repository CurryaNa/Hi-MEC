1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays marked polychromia, with colors ranging from dark brown and black to deep red, surrounded by a distinct pink erythematous halo.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, featuring a "smudged" or blurred appearance where the pigment diffuses into the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears irregular and crusted, particularly in the central, darker areas, suggestive of superficial ulceration or hemorrhage.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a chaotic, multi-component pattern with central dark blotches, evidence of crusting/bleeding, and a surrounding inflammatory erythema.
