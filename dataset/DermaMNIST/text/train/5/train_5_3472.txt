1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including shades of light and dark brown, as well as reddish-pink areas. A single, small whitish-yellow structure is noted centrally.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and blurred, with an irregular outline that fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved fine skin markings (dermatoglyphics), showing no signs of scaling or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Internal structures include a disorganized reticular network, brown dots/globules, and structureless blotches. A single, small, whitish milia-like cyst is a notable feature.
