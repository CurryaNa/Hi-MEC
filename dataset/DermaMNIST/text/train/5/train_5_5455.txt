1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including a central dark brown to black area and a lighter brown periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular in shape and appears indistinct and blurred in some segments, fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with evidence of effacement (loss) of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Key features include a prominent, eccentric dark blotch and an atypical, disorganized pigment network.
