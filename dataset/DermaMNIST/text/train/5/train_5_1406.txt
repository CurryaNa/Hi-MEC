1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a polychromatic pattern, dominated by an erythematous (red-pink) base, with areas of violaceous-brown pigmentation and numerous pinpoint purpuric dots.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and irregular, gradually fading into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, lacking any significant scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include numerous, well-defined hemorrhagic dots (petechiae) and blotchy, disorganized areas of pigmentation throughout the lesion.
