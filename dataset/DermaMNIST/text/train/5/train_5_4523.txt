1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with two primary colors: a dark, homogenous brown in the center and a lighter, reddish-brown at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and indistinct, characterized by blurred edges with fine, radial projections extending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall ovoid shape and the distribution of its internal pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved but somewhat distorted over the central, more pigmented area.
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include a central, structureless dark brown blotch and prominent radial streaming or pseudopods at the periphery, creating a starburst pattern.
