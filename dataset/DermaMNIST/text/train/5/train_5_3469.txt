1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays significant polychromasia, with prominent shades of red, pink, and dark brown to near-black.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, particularly on the right side where a pinkish hue fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is lobulated and mamillated, creating a cerebriform or cobblestone-like texture with distinct sulci (grooves).
5. Structural Features: The lesion is characterized by large, aggregated globules, a delicate pigment network overlying the lobules, and fine, arborizing telangiectasias visible in the grooves and pinkish areas.
