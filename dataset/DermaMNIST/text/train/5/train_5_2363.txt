1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays color variegation, featuring a dark brown to black central blotch that transitions to lighter brown and tan shades at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with a blurred or smudged appearance, particularly along the inferior and lateral margins.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with effacement of normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) in the most heavily pigmented areas; there is no visible scaling or crusting.
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include a large, structureless, darkly pigmented blotch and a surrounding faint, disorganized pigment network that fades into the normal skin.
