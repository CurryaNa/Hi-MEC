1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with varying shades of brown, ranging from light tan to a medium brown centrally.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and highly irregular, featuring feathery or starburst-like projections extending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with the overlying skin lines (dermatoglyphics) remaining visible and largely uninterrupted.
5. Structural Features: A prominent structural feature is the pattern of radial streaming, where lines of pigment radiate outwards from a more concentrated central area.
