1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light tan to dark brown, with subtle reddish and pinkish hues.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, exhibiting a smudged or 'moth-eaten' appearance where the pigment network fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth and slightly glossy, without evidence of significant scale, erosion, or crusting.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a disorganized pigment network, scattered dark brown dots/globules, and distinct, fine, branching arborizing telangiectasias, especially at the periphery.
