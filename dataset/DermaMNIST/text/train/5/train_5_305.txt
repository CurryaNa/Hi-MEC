1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), with a dominant deep red-violaceous blotch and peripheral areas of dark brown to black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, with some sections appearing blurred and others having an abrupt, notched edge.

4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) across the lesion's area.
5. Structural Features: The lesion presents with a large, structureless red-black area, consistent with hemorrhage or thrombosis, and eccentric, peripherally located brown-black globules and dots.
