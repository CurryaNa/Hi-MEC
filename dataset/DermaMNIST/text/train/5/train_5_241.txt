1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a predominant pink to reddish-violaceous coloration, interspersed with subtle whitish areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are well-demarcated but irregular, exhibiting a scalloped or lobulated contour.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the arrangement of its internal components.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is mamillated or cobblestone-like, composed of multiple fused papules or lobules.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include clustered, reddish-pink lobules and fine, arborizing telangiectasias coursing over the surface.
