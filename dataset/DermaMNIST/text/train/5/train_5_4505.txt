1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light tan to dark brown, with some pinkish-tan areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in contour and appear somewhat ill-defined, fading gradually into the surrounding skin in certain segments.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears largely smooth, characterized by prominent follicular openings from which numerous terminal hairs emerge.
5. Structural Features: Internal structures include a background pigment network, structureless brown areas, and distinct perifollicular hyperpigmentation (darker pigment concentrated around hair follicles).
