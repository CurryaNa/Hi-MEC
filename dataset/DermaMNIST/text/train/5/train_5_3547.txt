1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a predominant erythematous (red) to pink color, featuring a central whitish, atrophic-appearing area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, appearing indistinct and blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and internal structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and somewhat translucent or pearly, with evidence of central thinning or atrophy.
5. Structural Features: Distinct structural features include a centrally depressed, scar-like area and fine, branching (arborizing) telangiectasias visible across the surface.
