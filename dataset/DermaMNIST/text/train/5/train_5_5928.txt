1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including pink-red, yellow-white, and dark brown to black foci.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion is well-demarcated with a sharp, regular, and circular border.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is round and highly symmetrical in its outline.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is verrucous (wart-like) and lobulated, with areas of yellowish keratinous scale and crust.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include a cerebriform (brain-like) pattern, multiple comedo-like openings (dark plugs), and milia-like cysts (small white-yellow globules).
