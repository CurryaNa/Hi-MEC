1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including dark brown, black, and prominent red from vascular structures.
2. Border Appearance: Its borders are irregular and ill-defined, with a feathery or fuzzy transition into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, lacking any significant scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: There is a central, dark, atypical pigment network and prominent, fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) on the periphery.
