1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a central, deep reddish-brown coloration that fades to a lighter tan-brown at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The border is generally regular and round-to-oval in shape, but it is indistinct and fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly symmetrical in both shape and color distribution across its axes.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics), though superficial linear excoriations are visible.
5. Structural Features: A central, homogenous or structureless area of pigmentation is the dominant feature, with no visible pigment network, globules, or dots.
