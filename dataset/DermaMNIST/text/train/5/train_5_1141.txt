1. Primary Colors: The lesion is predominantly erythematous, exhibiting shades from pink to a deeper red-violaceous hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and indistinct, gradually fading into the surrounding normal skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is generally symmetrical in its roundish shape and distribution of color.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and terminal hairs passing through it.
5. Structural Features: Key features include diffuse erythema and fine, scattered punctate (dot-like) vessels throughout the lesion.
