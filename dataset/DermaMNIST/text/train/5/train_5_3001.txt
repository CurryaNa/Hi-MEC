1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a central dark brown core, a surrounding lighter brown area, and peripheral black dots.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and indistinct, with a "starburst" or "feathery" appearance due to radial streaks and stippling of pigment.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and macular (flat), with visible preservation of the natural skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: A central, darkly pigmented reticular network transitions into peripheral radial streaks and dots, a pattern often described as pseudopods or radial streaming.
