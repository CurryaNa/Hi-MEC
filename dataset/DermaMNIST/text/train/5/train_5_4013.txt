1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits significant color variegation, with areas of light brown, dark brown, black, and a prominent central blue-grey hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and indistinct, with pigment fading and poorly demarcated edges.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, lacking scale or ulceration, though skin markings are effaced over the most heavily pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Key dermoscopic features include asymmetric, structureless blotches of dark pigment and a large area of fine blue-grey dots (peppering/granularity), which is a feature of regression.
