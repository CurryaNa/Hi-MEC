1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with two primary colors, a dark brown, almost black, central area surrounded by a lighter, reddish-brown periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, characterized by fine, feathery, or fibrillar streaks radiating outwards from the lesion's body.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in its overall shape and pigment distribution, though the peripheral streaks exhibit a degree of radial symmetry.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, seeming to be flush with the surrounding epidermis without significant elevation or depression.
5. Structural Features: A distinct starburst pattern is the dominant feature, consisting of peripheral symmetric radial streaks (pseudopods) emanating from a central, darker, homogeneous to blotchy pigmented core.
