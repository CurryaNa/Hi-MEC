1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits variegation in color, presenting multiple shades of brown from light tan to dark brown, with focal areas of black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly demarcated, with a fading, reticular pattern that blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally flat (macular) with a visible, complex reticulated pattern under dermoscopic view.
5. Structural Features: An atypical pigment network is prominent, characterized by irregular mesh size and thickness, along with structureless brown blotches and an abrupt cutoff of the network at the periphery.
