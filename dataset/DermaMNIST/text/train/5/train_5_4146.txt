1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including a dark brown to black central area and a lighter brown periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, characterized by a feathery or starburst pattern of radial streaming at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and vellus hairs preserved across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a central, dark, structureless blotch and peripheral streaks (pseudopods) radiating outwards.
