1. Primary Colors: The lesion is multicolored, presenting with shades of light brown, dark brown/black, and prominent pink to red erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with pigment appearing to smudge and blend into the surrounding inflamed skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment and color.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, though normal skin markings are effaced, particularly in the erythematous areas.
5. Structural Features: Internal structures include disorganized, blotchy pigmentation centrally and a surrounding erythematous halo with possible fine vascular patterns.
