1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with two primary shades of brown: a dark brown, almost black, central area and a lighter tan-brown periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The central border is relatively defined but irregular, while the peripheral border is indistinct, smudged, and fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved across the entire lesion.
5. Structural Features: The lesion exhibits a multicomponent pattern, characterized by a central, structureless hyperpigmented blotch surrounded by a faint, peripheral reticular network or smudge.
