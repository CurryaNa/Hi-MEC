1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including dark brown, light brown, and a reddish-brown hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and ill-defined, with a blurred or fading appearance at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its axis and the distribution of its pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and relatively flat, with preservation of the natural skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: A reticular (net-like) pigment pattern is visible, especially at the edges, with a central, more homogenous blotch of darker pigmentation.
