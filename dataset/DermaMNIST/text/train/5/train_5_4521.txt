1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a variegated color pattern, predominantly reddish-brown and dark brown, with areas of lighter brown and violaceous hues.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular, ill-defined, and spiculated, featuring fine, feathery projections radiating outwards.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat (macular), without evidence of scaling, ulceration, or crusting.
5. Structural Features: Distinctive features include a disorganized, reticular pattern with prominent, symmetrically radiating streaks and pseudopods, creating a starburst or arborizing appearance.
