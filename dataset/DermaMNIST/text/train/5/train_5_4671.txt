1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a central dark brown to black pigmented area, which is surrounded by a distinct, uniform halo of white depigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border of the central pigmented component is irregular and indistinct, while the peripheral border of the white halo is relatively well-defined but fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The central pigmented structure is asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution, whereas the surrounding white halo is largely symmetrical and oval.
4. Surface Texture: The surface of the central pigmented lesion appears smooth to slightly mammillated (pebbled), with no visible scaling, ulceration, or crusting.
5. Structural Features: A key structural feature is the central, disorganized, globular pigmented nevus, which is completely encircled by a symmetric zone of leukoderma (depigmentation).
