1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors including light to dark brown, pink, and translucent, pearly white.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined, featuring a feathery, reticular pigment network at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is multi-lobulated and uneven, with a "cobblestone" or "bunch of grapes" appearance and a prominent smooth, glossy nodule.
5. Structural Features: The lesion presents with multiple aggregated, translucent nodules, fine arborizing telangiectasias, and an atypical peripheral pigment network.
