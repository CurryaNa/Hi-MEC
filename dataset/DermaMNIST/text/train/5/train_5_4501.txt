1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a bicolored pattern, featuring a dark brown to black central zone and a lighter tan-brown periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and indistinct, characterized by symmetric, feathery radial streaks (streaming) that blur into the surrounding skin.

4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, without evidence of scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: The dominant feature is a classic starburst pattern, consisting of a central dark, homogeneous blotch with peripheral pseudopods and streaks. A small, circular, hypopigmented area is noted eccentrically within the central dark region.
