1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a central dark brown to black area, surrounded by lighter brown and reddish-brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, featuring a blurred, scalloped edge that fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible but partially effaced in the most densely pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a central, structureless dark blotch and asymmetric peripheral streaks or pseudopods radiating outwards.
