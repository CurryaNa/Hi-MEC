1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, predominantly red due to vascularity, along with areas of light and dark brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, particularly on the left and inferior aspects, where the lesion appears to fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, without evidence of crusting or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Prominent, fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) are a key feature, especially in the superior half, along with blotchy, irregular pigmentation.
