2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular in outline and appears blurred or indistinct, fading into the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and no evidence of scaling or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: The lesion displays a central, dark, structureless blotch surrounded by a peripheral reticular or globular pattern.
