1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a pinkish-tan to light brown background with multiple dark red to purplish pinpoint macules distributed within it.
2. Border Appearance: The border is generally ill-defined and indistinct, gradually fading into the surrounding erythematous skin, especially along the left margin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of its internal color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat (macular), with fine vellus hairs and preserved skin lines passing through the lesion.
5. Structural Features: The predominant structural feature is the presence of numerous, regularly distributed dotted vessels or pinpoint petechiae across the surface.
