1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a central area of light to medium brown pigmentation, surrounded by a distinct, uniform white or hypopigmented halo.
2. Border Appearance: The border of the central pigmented component is irregular, while the outer edge of the hypopigmented halo is well-demarcated and smooth.
3. Shape Symmetry: The central pigmented portion is asymmetrical, while the surrounding halo is largely symmetrical and concentric.
4. Surface Texture: The surface of the entire lesion appears smooth, lacking any significant scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A key structural feature is the central pigmented nevus undergoing regression, evidenced by the concentric ring of depigmentation (halo phenomenon). The central nevus itself shows a somewhat globular pattern.
