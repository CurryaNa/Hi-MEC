1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including a central dark brown to black blotch, surrounded by a lighter brown and tan periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and blurred, featuring an irregular, starburst-like pattern with radial streaks extending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment, which is more concentrated and darker in the center.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, lacking any visible scaling, crusting, or ulceration, with normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) obscured centrally.
5. Structural Features: The lesion is characterized by a central, homogeneous, dark pigment blotch and peripheral radial streaming or pseudopods.
