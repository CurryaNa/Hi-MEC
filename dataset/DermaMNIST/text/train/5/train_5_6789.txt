1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors (polychromia), including dark brown, light brown, and a central whitish area, surrounded by a pinkish erythematous halo.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and irregular, with pigment fading and radiating into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears mostly smooth with a central area of fine whitish scale-crust.
5. Structural Features: There is a central whitish, scar-like structureless area indicative of regression, surrounded by a dark brown pigment blotch.
