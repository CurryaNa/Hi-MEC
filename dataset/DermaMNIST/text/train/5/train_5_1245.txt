1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a primary reddish-brown hue at the periphery and a central area of hypopigmentation or whitish coloration.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and indistinct, gradually blending into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is largely symmetrical in its overall round to oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with skin lines partially effaced in the central region.
5. Structural Features: A prominent central, scar-like white patch is observed, surrounded by a homogeneous reddish-brown halo; fine, unfocused vessels may be present peripherally.
