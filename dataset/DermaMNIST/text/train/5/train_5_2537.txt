1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including dark brown, black, and a lighter tan/brown, creating a variegated color pattern.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and ill-defined, exhibiting a starburst or spiculated pattern with radial streaming and pseudopods.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of its internal colors and patterns.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears largely flat with fine, overlying fibrillar structures and partially preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Prominent structural features include a central, disorganized, and atypical pigment network, structureless brown blotches, and distinct radial streaming at the periphery.
