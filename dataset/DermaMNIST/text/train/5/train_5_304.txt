1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, predominantly dark brown and black, with some lighter brown areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and indistinct, characterized by a reticular pattern that feathers into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of its internal pigmentation.

5. Structural Features: A prominent and atypical reticular (pigment network) pattern is visible, appearing dense and blotch-like centrally and becoming finer at the periphery.
