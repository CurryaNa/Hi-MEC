1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light to dark brown, pinkish-red, and translucent pearly-white areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly defined, featuring fine, radiating telangiectasias and wispy pigmented extensions.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is asymmetrical in terms of both color distribution and structural organization.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is multi-lobulated and uneven, composed of multiple smooth, shiny, globoid papules creating a cobblestone-like texture.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include multiple translucent and pigmented globules, a multicomponent pattern, and prominent, fine, branching arborizing telangiectasias on the surface and periphery.
