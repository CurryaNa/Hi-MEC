1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a primary erythematous (pink-red) base, overlaid with areas of darker violaceous (purplish-red) to brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, gradually fading into the surrounding skin without a distinct, sharp demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) appearing largely preserved.
5. Structural Features: There are blotchy, darker pigmented areas concentrated along the periphery, forming an incomplete annular (ring-like) pattern around a lighter erythematous center. Fine, branching telangiectatic vessels are visible, particularly within the redder portions.
