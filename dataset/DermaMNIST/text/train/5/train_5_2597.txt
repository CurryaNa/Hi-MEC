2. Border Appearance: The lesion's borders are irregular and ill-defined, particularly on the superior and right-hand aspects where they blend into the surrounding skin.

4. Surface Texture: The surface appears irregular and slightly elevated, with evidence of central scaling and crusting.
5. Structural Features: Key features include blotchy, asymmetric pigmentation, a central area of erosion or ulceration, and fine, short, linear irregular vessels, especially at the periphery.
