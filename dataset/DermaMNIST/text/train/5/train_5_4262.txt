1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light brown at the periphery to a dark, almost black-brown in the central area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, showing a starburst or feathery pattern with radial streaks fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of its pigment and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears flat and largely smooth, with preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible across the lesion, particularly in the center.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include an atypical reticular (net-like) pattern, a darker central blotch, and pseudopods or radial streaming at the periphery.
