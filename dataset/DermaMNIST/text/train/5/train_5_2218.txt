1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a prominent dark red and brown center with focal black clods. A surrounding halo of pinkish-red erythema is also visible.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and irregular, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin without a sharp demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface has a centrally raised, mamillated, or cobblestone-like texture, contrasting with the smoother surrounding skin.
5. Structural Features: Distinctive features include multiple red-to-black lacunae (vascular spaces) centrally and fine, branching telangiectasias at the periphery. A classic pigment network is absent.
