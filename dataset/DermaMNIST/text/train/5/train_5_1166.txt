1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from a lighter tan-brown at the periphery to a dark brown and blackish hue concentrated in the central and superior portions.
2. Border Appearance: The border is generally regular in its ovoid shape but appears blurred and ill-defined, gradually fading into the surrounding skin, especially on the right side.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both color distribution and, to a lesser degree, in shape, with a more concentrated and darker pigment in the upper half.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat (macular), with the natural skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved and visible across the pigmented area.
5. Structural Features: A fine reticular (net-like) pigment pattern is visible, particularly at the periphery, which coalesces into a more homogeneous, structureless dark blotch centrally.
