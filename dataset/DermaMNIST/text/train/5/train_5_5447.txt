1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from a dark, almost black-brown centrally to a lighter brown at the edges.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, featuring a starburst pattern with spiky, radial extensions of pigment.

5. Structural Features: Key structural features include a central, structureless dark brown blotch and prominent pseudopods or radial streaming at the periphery.
