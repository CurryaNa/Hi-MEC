1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a multi-component color pattern, including a pink-to-red background with areas of light and dark brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, gradually fading into the surrounding skin without a distinct demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with preserved and slightly accentuated skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Key features include a reticular pigment network, especially at the periphery, and central areas of hypopigmentation or possible regression. Scattered small, dark brown dots are also present.
