1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, varying from light tan to a focal area of dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are predominantly ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin in a blotchy pattern.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with intact and visible skin lines (dermatoglyphics) traversing the pigmented area.
5. Structural Features: A multi-component pattern is observed, featuring blotchy pigmentation, a darker central reticular network, and a small area of focal regression or hypopigmentation.
