1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and erythematous pink-red areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, showing a blurred or fuzzy transition into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears relatively smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include irregular blotches, scattered dark brown dots/globules, and fine, branching telangiectatic vessels (arborizing telangiectasias).
