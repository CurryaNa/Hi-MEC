1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits an erythematous (red) base with central purpuric (dark red to purple) and brownish-black areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with skin lines (dermatoglyphics) still visible across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a central cluster of petechial or purpuric macules on an erythematous, possibly infiltrated plaque.
