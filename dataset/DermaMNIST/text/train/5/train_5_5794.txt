1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a predominantly erythematous (red) to violaceous (purplish-red) color, with some areas appearing as a lighter pink.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, diffusely blending into the surrounding normal-colored skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall lesion is asymmetrical, composed of multiple, irregularly distributed erythematous patches.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is smooth with preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and visible vellus hairs, lacking any scale, crust, or erosion.
5. Structural Features: Prominent, fine, arborizing (branching) and linear telangiectasias are visible, interspersed with punctate red dots and blotchy erythema.
