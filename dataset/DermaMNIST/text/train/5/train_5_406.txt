1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays color variegation, with prominent shades of dark brown, reddish-brown, and pinkish-tan.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, with a radial streaming or pseudopod-like extension of pigment at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The central surface is elevated and has a rough, cobblestone or cerebriform (brain-like) texture with some crusting.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a central, raised papillomatous area with a distinct polygonal pattern, surrounded by an irregular pigmented network and peripheral dark globules.
