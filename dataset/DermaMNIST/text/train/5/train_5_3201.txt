1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with varying shades of brown, from light tan to dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are somewhat irregular and ill-defined, fading into the surrounding skin at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface follows the natural skin lines (dermatoglyphics), which are accentuated by the pigment.
5. Structural Features: A distinct parallel ridge pattern is observed, characterized by linear pigmentation located predominantly on the skin ridges.
