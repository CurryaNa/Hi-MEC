1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits significant color variegation, with shades of light brown, dark brown, black, and areas of pinkish-red.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular in shape and ill-defined, featuring areas that are blurred and fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, though normal skin markings (dermatoglyphics) are focally effaced or disrupted.
5. Structural Features: Key features include an atypical and disorganized pigment network, structureless blotches of dark pigment, and areas of regression with scar-like depigmentation.
