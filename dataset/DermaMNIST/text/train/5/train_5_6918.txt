1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a polychromatic display of light red, dark red, and violaceous (purple) hues.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are generally ill-defined and irregular, with a more distinct, scalloped edge on the right side.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with intact hair follicles from which vellus hairs emerge.
5. Structural Features: Multiple, discrete and confluent, dark red to purple lacunae (vascular spaces) are present, creating a cobblestone or saccular pattern over a lighter erythematous patch.
