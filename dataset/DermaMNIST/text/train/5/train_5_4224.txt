1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays significant polychromasia, with shades of dark brown, black, light brown, pink, and a pale whitish-pink area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly defined, featuring a feathery or blurred transition into the adjacent normal skin in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is heterogeneous, showing a combination of a fine reticular skin pattern, central atrophic scarring, and a slightly crusted appearance over the darkly pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: The lesion presents a multicomponent pattern, including an atypical pigment network, irregular blotches of dark pigment, and extensive areas of regression with scar-like depigmentation and peppering. Comedo-like openings are also noted in the inferior pale section.
