1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including pink, red (erythema), and various shades of brown ranging from light to dark.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly defined, appearing blurred and fading into the surrounding skin in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with some effacement and disruption of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is visible, characterized by irregular blotches and streaks of brown pigment and a central erythematous area suggesting increased vascularity or inflammation.
