1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, from light tan to dark brown, and a central skin-colored to hypopigmented area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with a blurred or smudged periphery where the pigment network fades out.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible across the pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: A prominent reticular (net-like) pigment network is visible, particularly at the edges, surrounding a central structureless, hypopigmented zone.
