1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including dark brown, light brown, and a reddish-brown hue. A central zone of lighter pigmentation is surrounded by darker peripheral pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, featuring a starburst or feathery pattern that blurs into the surrounding skin. The perimeter is poorly circumscribed.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures. It lacks both axial and rotational symmetry.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible but partially effaced by the underlying pigment. There is no evidence of scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic structures include an atypical, thickened pigment network and radial streaks or pseudopods at the periphery. A central area of blotchy, lighter pigmentation is also a prominent feature.
