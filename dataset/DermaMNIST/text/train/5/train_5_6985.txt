1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including a central erythematous (pink-red) area, peripheral light brown to dark brown/black pigmentation, and central whitish zones.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular in shape and ill-defined, with areas of notching and indistinct margins that fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: There is marked asymmetry in both the overall contour and the distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears uneven and non-uniform, with a pebbly or stippled texture in the erythematous central zone.
5. Structural Features: Key features include blotches of eccentric dark pigmentation, a central area of regression with erythema and scar-like depigmentation, and fine dotted vessels.
