1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a dark brown to black central area and a lighter brown periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with a feathery or "starburst" appearance at the edges.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with preservation of the natural skin lines (dermatoglyphics) across the pigmented area.
5. Structural Features: It displays a disorganized reticular pattern at the periphery which transitions into a central, structureless dark blotch.
