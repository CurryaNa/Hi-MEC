1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a pink-red erythematous background and a central area composed of dark brown and black dots.
2. Border Appearance: The borders of the central pigmented cluster are somewhat irregular but discernible, while the surrounding erythema is ill-defined and blends into the adjacent skin.
