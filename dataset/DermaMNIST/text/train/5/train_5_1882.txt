1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays color variegation, with areas of light brown, dark brown, black, and a reddish-brown hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, featuring notching and a fuzzy, indistinct periphery in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth, with some loss or effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Internal structures include an atypical pigment network, dark structureless blotches, and fine whitish lines (crystalline/chrysalis structures) centrally.
