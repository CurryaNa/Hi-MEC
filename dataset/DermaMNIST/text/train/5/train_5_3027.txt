1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from a light tan at the periphery to dark brown and near-black in the central region.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, characterized by a feathery, starburst-like pattern of radiating streaks extending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally flat, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and several vellus hairs preserved and visible across the pigmented area.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a central, darker, globular/amorphous area and prominent peripheral radial streaming or pseudopod-like projections.
