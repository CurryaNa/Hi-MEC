1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a light brown background, dark brown to black globules, and distinct pink to red areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, appearing blurred and fading into the surrounding skin in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, without evidence of significant crusting or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Distinct structural features include multiple dark brown dots and globules, an absence of a typical pigment network, and the presence of prominent, irregular, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias).
