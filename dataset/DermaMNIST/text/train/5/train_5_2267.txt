1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including pinkish-white, translucent, and brown, with prominent red vessels.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly defined, composed of multiple aggregated lobules that create a polycyclic outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall structure is markedly asymmetrical in both its shape and the distribution of its components.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is exophytic and multi-lobulated, with some smooth, pearly areas and evidence of central ulceration and crusting.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include multiple translucent lobules and prominent, branching arborizing telangiectasias. An underlying pigmented component is also visible.
