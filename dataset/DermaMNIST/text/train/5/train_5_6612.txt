1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light brown at the periphery to dark brown and black in the central, more disorganized areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with areas that are blurred and fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears relatively smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved in some areas but effaced in the more heavily pigmented zones.
5. Structural Features: An atypical and disorganized pigment network is present, characterized by blotches and structureless areas, particularly in the center of the lesion.
