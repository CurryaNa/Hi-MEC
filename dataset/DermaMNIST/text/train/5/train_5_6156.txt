1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, primarily dark brown and black in the center, with lighter tan and brown areas at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The border is markedly irregular and indistinct, characterized by prominent pseudopodal extensions and radial streaming, giving it a starburst appearance.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat (macular), without evidence of scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A key structural feature is a central, dark, homogeneous blotch with peripheral streaks and a pseudoreticular pattern extending outwards.
