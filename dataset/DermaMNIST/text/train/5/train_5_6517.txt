1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents multiple colors, including dark brown, black, and light brown, with a peripheral erythematous (reddish) halo.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined, featuring notching and areas where the pigment fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its outline and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears slightly elevated and has a pebbly or cobblestone-like texture in the most heavily pigmented central area.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a central cluster of dark brown to black globules and dots, with a faint, structureless brownish pigmentation at the inferior edge.
