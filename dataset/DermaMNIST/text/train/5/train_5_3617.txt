1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including reddish-brown and dark brown pigmentation, situated on a background of pink to red erythematous skin.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are partially well-demarcated and partially ill-defined, with an overall irregular, non-circular outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and somewhat translucent, with fine skin lines preserved across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include prominent, fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) on the surface and periphery, along with blotchy brown-pigmented areas.
