1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a primary red-purple (violaceous) background and contains dark brown to black linear and branching structures.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and somewhat ill-defined, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its contour and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with vellus hairs emerging from within the lesion area.
5. Structural Features: Distinct, fine, branching telangiectasias (arborizing vessels) are the most prominent feature, overlying a translucent, reddish base.
