1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including various shades of brown (light to dark) and pinkish-tan. Small, whitish, hypopigmented foci are also visible, particularly in the central region.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are predominantly irregular and indistinct, with some areas appearing blurred and fading into the surrounding skin. The overall perimeter is not uniform or sharply demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of color and structural components.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is not smooth, presenting with a subtly papular or cobblestone-like texture. A slightly dry quality is also suggested.
5. Structural Features: Structural features include a multicomponent pattern with a peripheral reticular network, blotchy pigmentation, and a central area showing features suggestive of regression, such as scarring, hypopigmentation, and multiple milia-like cysts.
