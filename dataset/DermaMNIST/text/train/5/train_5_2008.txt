1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, from a light tan at the periphery to a dark brown-to-black coloration in the central zone.
2. Border Appearance: The border is highly irregular and indistinct, featuring fine, feathery, pseudopodal projections that radiate outwards, creating a starburst pattern.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its pigment, lacking any clear axis of symmetry.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with the overlying skin markings (dermatoglyphics) largely preserved and no evidence of scaling, ulceration, or crusting.
5. Structural Features: A key feature is the presence of peripheral radial streaming and pseudopods emanating from a more disorganized, darker pigmented area in the center.
