1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a predominant pink-to-red erythematous base, with areas of light brown and dark brown/black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and irregular, blurring into the surrounding skin without a well-defined edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and slightly glossy, with some loss of normal skin texture (effacement).
5. Structural Features: Distinct structural features include blotchy, asymmetric pigmentation and prominent, fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) across the surface.
