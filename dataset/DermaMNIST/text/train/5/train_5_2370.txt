1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays a polychromatic pattern, with prominent pink-to-violaceous hues, interspersed with areas of brown pigmentation and red from visible vasculature.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly defined, characterized by a multi-lobulated periphery that does not form a cohesive edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is mammillated and glistening, composed of multiple smooth, rounded nodules creating a cobblestone-like texture.
5. Structural Features: The most distinct features are large, ovoid, blue-gray to pinkish nodules and prominent, sharply focused, branching (arborizing) telangiectasias on the surface. Structureless brown areas are also present.
