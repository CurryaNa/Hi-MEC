1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays a heterogeneous color pattern, with a central area of light brown to dark brown pigmentation, surrounded by prominent erythema (redness) and violaceous hues.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and indistinct, blending gradually and irregularly into the surrounding inflamed skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution, with the brown pigmentation being eccentrically located within a larger, irregularly shaped erythematous patch.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with visible fine vellus hairs and preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: The lesion is a macular patch with central brown pigmentation and surrounding erythema. Fine, blurry telangiectatic vessels may be present in the peripheral erythematous areas.
