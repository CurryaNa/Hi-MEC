1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including a central dark brown area transitioning to light brown and reddish-brown at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, with a feathery or starburst-like pattern of pigment radiating into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, without any visible scaling, crusting, or significant elevation.
5. Structural Features: A central, darker pigmented blotch is present, from which a disorganized reticular network and peripheral streaks (pseudopods) radiate outwards.
