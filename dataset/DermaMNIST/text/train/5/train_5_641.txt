1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits polychromasia, displaying multiple shades including light brown, dark brown, black, and a subtle reddish-purple hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and highly irregular, with areas of notching and a scalloped or starburst-like periphery that fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is topographically irregular with slight elevation, and there is effacement of normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) over the central portion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a disorganized, atypical pigment network at the periphery, central black dots and globules, and structureless dark brown to black areas.
