1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from a light tan background to focal areas of dark brown and black.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and scalloped, with some areas appearing sharply demarcated while others fade more gradually into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal pigmentation.

5. Structural Features: Internal structures include a delicate, light brown reticular (net-like) pattern, interspersed with multiple dark brown to black dots and globules, and a central, more homogeneous area of pigmentation.
