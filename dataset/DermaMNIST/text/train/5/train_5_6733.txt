1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a polychromatic pattern, displaying shades of reddish-pink (erythematous), violaceous, and darker brownish-red.
2. Border Appearance: Its borders are ill-defined and irregular, gradually fading into the surrounding skin rather than having a sharp demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with the overlying skin lines (dermatoglyphics) being partially preserved but somewhat effaced.
5. Structural Features: Distinct structural features include numerous fine, reddish-purple punctate dots (globules/vessels) and a larger, structureless brownish-red blotch.
