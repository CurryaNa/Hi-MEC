1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including shades of light and dark brown, a central whitish/hypopigmented area, and peripheral pinkish hues.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, featuring a feathery or spiculated edge where the pigmentation meets the central pale zone.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with skin lines (dermatoglyphics) partially preserved in some areas.
5. Structural Features: Key features include an arcuate or annular pattern of peripheral pigmentation composed of dots and lines, surrounding a central structureless, scar-like white area suggestive of regression.
