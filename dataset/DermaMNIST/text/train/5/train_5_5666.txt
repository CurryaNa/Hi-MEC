1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a primary pinkish-red coloration, with some light brown areas and scattered dark brown-to-black dots.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending into the surrounding skin without a sharp demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly symmetrical in its overall roundish shape, but the internal distribution of color and vascular structures is asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture appears smooth and slightly shiny or waxy, with no evidence of scaling, ulceration, or crusting.
5. Structural Features: Key dermoscopic features include fine, branching vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) and multiple small, well-defined whitish globules (milia-like cysts).
