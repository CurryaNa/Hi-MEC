1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a central dark brown to black coloration, transitioning to a lighter brown at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The border is highly irregular and indistinct, exhibiting a stellate or starburst pattern with fine, feathery projections.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its structural patterns.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with the underlying skin lines (dermatoglyphics) slightly effaced by the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Distinct structural features include a central, homogeneous dark blotch and peripheral radial streaming or pseudopods.
