1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, predominantly light brown with areas of darker brown and reddish-brown dots centrally.
2. Border Appearance: The border is generally regular in its round shape but appears indistinct and blurred, gradually fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is largely symmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is smooth with preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and fine vellus hairs emerging from within the lesion.
5. Structural Features: The lesion displays a central reticular (net-like) pattern composed of darker pigmented globules, with a more homogeneous, lighter pigmentation at the periphery.
