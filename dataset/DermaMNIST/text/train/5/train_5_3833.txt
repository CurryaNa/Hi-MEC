1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light tan to dark brown and focal black areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly defined, with some areas appearing notched and others fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with an effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) over the pigmented area.
5. Structural Features: An atypical pigment network is present, characterized by irregular lines, blotches, and dark globules.
