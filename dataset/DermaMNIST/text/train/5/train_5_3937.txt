1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits marked polychromasia, presenting with dark brown, black, a reddish-brown peripheral halo, and central grey-white areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with a radial or "starburst" pattern of pigmentation fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The shape is highly asymmetrical in both contour and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The central area appears elevated and crusted, contrasting with the flatter, smoother texture of the peripheral halo.
5. Structural Features: A central dark blotch is overlaid with shiny white reticular lines (regression structures), and the entire lesion is surrounded by a prominent erythematous or reddish-brown structureless halo.
