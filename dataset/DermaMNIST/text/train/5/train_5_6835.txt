1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits significant color variegation, presenting with multiple shades of light brown, dark brown, black, and a focal reddish-pink area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, with notching and areas where the pigment fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth under dermoscopy, though the topography is irregular, reflecting the varied underlying structures.
5. Structural Features: The lesion displays multiple atypical structures, including a disorganized pigment network, streaks (pseudopods) at the periphery, and blotches of dark pigment. Structureless areas and features suggestive of regression are also present.
