1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a bichromatic pattern, consisting of dark brown and black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular, with a feathery or streaky appearance where the pigment extends along the skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and pigment distribution, with a denser, more nodular component on one side.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth and integrated with the skin, with a slightly raised, papular texture in the central, darker area.
5. Structural Features: The lesion exhibits a central cluster of dark globules and a peripheral linear pigmentation that follows the furrows of the skin, characteristic of a parallel furrow pattern.
