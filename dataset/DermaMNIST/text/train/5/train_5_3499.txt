1. Primary Colors: The lesion is polychromatic, displaying shades of brown, black, blue-grey, and pink.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly defined, featuring a lobulated or scalloped periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is verrucous (wart-like) and exophytic, with areas of heavy crusting and scale.
5. Structural Features: The lesion presents as a multicomponent nodule with central, heavily pigmented, blue-black clods and peripheral, pink, fleshy, papillomatous projections. Fine, linear vessels are visible in the pinkish areas.
