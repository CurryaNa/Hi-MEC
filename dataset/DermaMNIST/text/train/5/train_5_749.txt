1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a background of diffuse pink-red erythema, overlaid with numerous small, darker red to purpuric dots.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending indistinctly with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is smooth with preserved skin markings, lacking any significant scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key features include multiple, regularly distributed red dots, consistent with dotted or glomerular vessels, scattered across an erythematous base.
