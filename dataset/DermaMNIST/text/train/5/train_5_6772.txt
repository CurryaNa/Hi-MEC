1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, varying from light tan at the periphery to dark brown and black in the central and scattered areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with a "moth-eaten" or fading edge that blurs into the surrounding skin, punctuated by peripheral globules.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, without any visible scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic structures include a disorganized pigment network, blotches of dark pigment, and multiple dots/globules, particularly at the periphery, indicating a multicomponent pattern.
