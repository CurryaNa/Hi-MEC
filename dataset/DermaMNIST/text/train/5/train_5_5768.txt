1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, including a tan central papule and a surrounding dark brown to light brown macular area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and blurred, with irregular, feathery extensions and radial streaming at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall ovoid shape and the distribution of its pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and slightly raised in the center, with a flat texture in the surrounding pigmented region.
5. Structural Features: A central, well-demarcated, raised papule is surrounded by a darker, flat (macular) pigmented area, creating a distinct biphasic or targetoid pattern.
