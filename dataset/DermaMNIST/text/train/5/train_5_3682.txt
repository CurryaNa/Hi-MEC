1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays significant color variegation, including light brown, dark brown, black, pink-red, and whitish/flesh-colored areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, featuring a fuzzy, smudged, and radial pattern that blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is complex, with smooth, raised (nodular) components in the center and a flatter, slightly textured periphery.
5. Structural Features: Key features include central exophytic, hypopigmented nodules, a surrounding multicomponent pattern with globules and atypical pigment network, and radial streaming at the periphery.
