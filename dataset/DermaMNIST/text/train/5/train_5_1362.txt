1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and prominent erythematous (red/pink) areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, showing areas of fading and scalloping rather than a sharp demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible, showing no significant scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key features include an atypical, disorganized pigment network, particularly at the periphery, and a central erythematous blush suggesting increased vascularity.
