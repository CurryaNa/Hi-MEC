2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, featuring a blurred or smudged transition into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of color and internal structures.

5. Structural Features: Internal structures include an atypical, disorganized pigment network, blotches, and a central area with features of regression, including scar-like depigmentation and peppering.
