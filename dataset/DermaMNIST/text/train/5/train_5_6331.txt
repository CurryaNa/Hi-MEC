1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown/black, and a central erythematous (pink/red) area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with areas that appear blurred and fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, without significant scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: It displays an atypical, blotchy pigment network at the periphery, with a central area of hypopigmentation and erythema, suggestive of regression.
