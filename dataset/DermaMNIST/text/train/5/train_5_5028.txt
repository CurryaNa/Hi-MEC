1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a central, dark brown to black homogeneous pigmentation, surrounded by a faint, lighter brown peripheral halo.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are generally regular and circular, though they appear somewhat blurred or ill-defined upon close inspection.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is largely symmetrical in its round to oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and uniform, without evidence of scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a central, dark, structureless (homogeneous) pigmented zone, with a lack of other distinct dermoscopic structures like a pigment network or globules.
