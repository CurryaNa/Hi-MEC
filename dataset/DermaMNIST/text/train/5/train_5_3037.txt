1. Primary Colors: The lesion is multi-colored (polychromatic), displaying shades of light brown, dark brown, black, red, and a central violaceous hue.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and indistinct, with areas that are blurred and fade into the surrounding skin, and evidence of notching.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include an atypical pigment network, peripheral dots/globules, and a central structureless blotch with darker pigmentation.
