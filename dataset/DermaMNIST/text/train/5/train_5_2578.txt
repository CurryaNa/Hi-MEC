1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a dominant erythematous (red) and pink hue. Subtle purplish or violaceous tones are visible at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The border is ill-defined and indistinct, gradually fading into the surrounding normal skin. Its overall shape is regular and ovoid.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is generally symmetrical in its round-to-oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and is devoid of scale, crust, or ulceration. The normal skin lines are preserved but slightly effaced.
5. Structural Features: The primary feature is diffuse erythema, which appears somewhat more intense peripherally. There are no prominent pigment networks, but subtle dotted vascular structures may be present.
