1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and black, with a paler, hypopigmented central zone.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and irregular, featuring a smudged or moth-eaten appearance where the pigment fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth with visible, though slightly effaced, skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Key features include a disorganized pigment network, irregularly distributed dots and globules, and blotches, creating a multicomponent pattern.
