1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and black, along with some subtle pink-red areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with feathery and spiculated margins that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with intact skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and several emerging vellus hairs.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is observed, characterized by a disorganized reticular network, dark blotches, and prominent perifollicular hyperpigmentation.
