1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a deep red to maroon base, interspersed with areas of dark brown and black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and blurred, featuring an irregular shape that fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of colors.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with intact skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and vellus hairs passing through it.
5. Structural Features: Internal structures include dark, blotchy lacunae (blood-filled spaces) and some small, coiled vascular structures, particularly in the upper portion.
