1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits marked color variegation, with shades ranging from light brown to dark brown and black.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, featuring notches and pseudopods with an abrupt, smudged cut-off in some areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly flat, with effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) over the most heavily pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include a multicomponent pattern with an atypical pigment network, central structureless blotches, and radial streaks at the periphery.
