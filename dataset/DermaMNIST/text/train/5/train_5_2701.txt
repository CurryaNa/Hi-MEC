1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a polychromatic appearance, primarily consisting of an erythematous (pink-red) base with superimposed purpuric (deep red/purple) and yellowish-brown areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, diffusely blending into the surrounding skin without a distinct demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall ovoid shape and the distribution of its internal colors and patterns.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat (macular), with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include focal collections of punctate red dots and small, irregular vascular structures, consistent with petechiae and hemorrhage, set within a background of diffuse erythema.
