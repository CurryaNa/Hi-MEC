1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a dark reddish-brown to black central core, surrounded by a lighter red vascular periphery.
2. Border Appearance: Its borders are irregular and ill-defined, demonstrating a stellate or feathery pattern at the edges.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of its internal structures and colors.
