1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including a dark brown to black central component, a surrounding violaceous (purple-red) halo, and a separate bright red area.
2. Border Appearance: The overall border is irregular and poorly demarcated, with the erythematous component fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its structural components and colors.
4. Surface Texture: The surface of the large, dark component is smooth, tense, and dome-shaped, consistent with a fluid-filled bulla (blister).
5. Structural Features: The primary structural feature is a large, raised, hemorrhagic or heavily pigmented bulla, with an adjacent smaller, erythematous macule or papule.
