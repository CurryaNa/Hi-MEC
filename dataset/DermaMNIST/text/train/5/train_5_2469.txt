1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors (polychromasia), including light brown, dark brown, black, and a prominent reddish-pink hue.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and indistinct in some areas, showing a gradual fade and not a sharp demarcation from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, without any visible scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include a multicomponent pattern with an atypical pigment network, multiple dark brown to black globules/clods, and structureless reddish-pink areas.
