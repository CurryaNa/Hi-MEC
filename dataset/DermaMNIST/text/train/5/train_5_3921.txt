1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a prominent erythematous to violaceous peripheral hue, surrounding a central area of pallor and hypopigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and indistinct, blending gradually into the surrounding normal skin in an irregularly round configuration.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and texture.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with evidence of slight central atrophy and fine wrinkling, but lacks significant scaling or crusting.
5. Structural Features: A key feature is the biphasic pattern, consisting of a raised, inflammatory rim and a depressed, atrophic central zone. Several terminal hair shafts are seen emerging from within the lesion.
