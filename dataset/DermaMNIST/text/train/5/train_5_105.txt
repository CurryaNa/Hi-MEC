1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including dark brown, black, and a surrounding pink-violaceous (purplish) hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are markedly irregular and ill-defined, with significant notching and areas where the pigment fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears irregular and is not smooth, with some areas, particularly the darkest parts, appearing slightly raised and possibly crusted.
5. Structural Features: The lesion is characterized by disorganized, fragmented clods of dark pigment and structureless brown-black areas, with surrounding scar-like depigmentation and erythema.
