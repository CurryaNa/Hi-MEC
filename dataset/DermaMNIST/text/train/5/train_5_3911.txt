1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including light brown, dark brown, and areas of black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly demarcated, with spiculated and starburst-like projections fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with skin lines (dermatoglyphics) partially visible.
5. Structural Features: An atypical pigment network is observed, characterized by radial streaming, pseudopods at the periphery, and central blotches of darker pigment.
