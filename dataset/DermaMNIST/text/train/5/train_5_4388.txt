1. Primary Colors: The lesion is polychromatic, displaying shades of red, reddish-brown, and focal areas of dark brown to black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with some areas appearing blurred and fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, although normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) are partially effaced or obscured by the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include blotchy, disorganized pigmentation and prominent, fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias), especially in the superior portion.
