1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including various shades of brown, tan, and focal areas of black pigment.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly defined, with areas of notching and fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with preserved fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible in some areas.
5. Structural Features: It exhibits a multicomponent pattern, including a reticular (net-like) pigment network, structureless blotches, and dark brown globules.
