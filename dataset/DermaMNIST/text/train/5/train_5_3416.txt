1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a uniform light to medium brown pigmentation, interspersed with multiple discrete, whitish, hypopigmented circular areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is relatively regular in its overall contour but appears slightly indistinct and fades gradually into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly symmetrical in its roundish shape, although there is some asymmetry in the distribution of internal hypopigmented structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and macular (flat), with no visible scaling, crusting, or erosion.
5. Structural Features: The pathognomonic feature is a distinct pattern of perifollicular hypopigmentation, creating a "cobblestone" or "honeycomb" appearance with pale circles within the pigmented lesion.
