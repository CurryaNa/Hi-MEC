1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including light brown, dark brown, and areas of black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and indistinct, with a gradual fading or smudging of pigment into the surrounding skin at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved across the pigmented area.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is visible, characterized by a reticular (net-like) pattern at the periphery and a central, dark, structureless blotch.
