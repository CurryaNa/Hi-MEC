1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including light and dark brown, black, and prominent areas of red/pink erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly defined, with stellate (star-shaped) projections and indistinct margins.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its contour and the internal distribution of pigment and vascularity.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears slightly irregular with potential for subtle central ulceration or crusting.
5. Structural Features: There are blotchy, eccentric areas of dark pigmentation and prominent, fine, linear, and dotted vessels (telangiectasias) within the central and peripheral erythematous zones.
