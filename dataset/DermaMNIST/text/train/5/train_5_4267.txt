1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with significant color variegation, displaying shades of light brown, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are markedly irregular and indistinct, with blurred, notched, and scalloped edges that fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth, with effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) in the darker, more pigmented regions.
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include an atypical pigment network, structureless blotches of dark pigment, and radial streaming or pseudopods at the periphery.
