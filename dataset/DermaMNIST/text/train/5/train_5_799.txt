1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, including a dark brown to black central area and a lighter tan/brown peripheral halo.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and indistinct, characterized by sharp, finger-like projections (pseudopods) radiating outwards from the center, creating a starburst or stellate appearance.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its axis and its pattern, with an eccentric and disorganized distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat (macular), with normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible throughout the lesion, suggesting no significant scale or crust.
5. Structural Features: The most prominent structural feature is a central, dark, disorganized globule with peripheral streaks and pseudopods radiating symmetrically around the lesion, a pattern often described as "starburst."
