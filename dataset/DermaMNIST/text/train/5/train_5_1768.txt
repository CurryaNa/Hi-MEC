1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from a light tan at the periphery to a dark brown in the central and satellite areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and indistinct, with feathery edges and pseudopodal extensions, particularly on the right side.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved, fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible throughout the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include a peripheral reticular (net-like) pattern, central brown globules (clods), and a small, separate satellite pigmented macule to the left.
