1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a dark brown to black central area and a lighter brown peripheral zone.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and indistinct, with areas that appear blurred and fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth, with some effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) over the central, darker portion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a central, structureless black blotch and a disorganized, lighter brown reticular pattern at the periphery.
