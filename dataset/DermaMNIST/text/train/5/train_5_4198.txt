1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a light brown to tan base color, interspersed with prominent red and pink hues corresponding to vascular structures.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, featuring leaf-like or scalloped projections that fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of its internal pigmentation and vascular patterns.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and slightly translucent, with no evidence of scaling, crusting, or significant elevation.
5. Structural Features: Distinct, sharply focused, branching (arborizing) telangiectasias are the most prominent feature, overlying a subtle and disorganized light brown pigment network.
