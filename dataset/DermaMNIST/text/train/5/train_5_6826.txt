2. Border Appearance: The periphery is ill-defined and highly irregular, characterized by numerous fine, pigmented streaks and pseudopods radiating outwards in a stellate or starburst fashion.

4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved dermatoglyphics (skin lines) and lacks any significant scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: The most prominent feature is a multicomponent pattern consisting of central, structureless blotches and prominent, symmetrically and asymmetrically radiating streaks, creating a classic "starburst" appearance.
