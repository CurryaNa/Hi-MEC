1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and areas with a reddish-pink hue.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly defined, with some sections appearing blurred and fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.

5. Structural Features: Internal structures include a reticular (net-like) pigment network, asymmetric blotches of darker pigment, and irregular dots/globules.
