1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including various shades of brown, pink-red, and central whitish or hypopigmented areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with a blurred, fading transition into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is clearly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth, without evidence of significant scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: There are disorganized pigment patterns, fine, branching vessels (arborizing telangiectasias), and central areas suggestive of structural regression.
