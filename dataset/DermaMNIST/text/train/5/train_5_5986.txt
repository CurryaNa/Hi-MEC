1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, including light tan, medium brown, and areas of dark brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, featuring a scalloped or starburst-like edge that fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with the natural skin lines (dermatoglyphics) partially visible through the pigmentation.
5. Structural Features: An atypical reticular (net-like) pigment network is observed, along with structureless blotches of darker pigment, particularly in the central portion.
