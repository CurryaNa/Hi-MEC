1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits significant color variegation, with areas of dark brown, black, tan, and a central erythematous (red/pink) zone.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly defined, with pigment diffusely fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is heterogeneous, featuring a crusted, slightly raised area on the upper left and a central area of ulceration with a small crust.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central erythematous halo surrounding a focal ulceration, disorganized pigment blotches, and a small, white, milia-like cyst inferior to the center.
