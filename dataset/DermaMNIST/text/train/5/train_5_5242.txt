1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a light brown to reddish-brown background and distinct, fine red linear patterns.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, showing a gradual fading of pigmentation into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal structures and color.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with the normal epidermal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved.
5. Structural Features: The most prominent feature is the presence of fine, branching, tree-like blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) overlying a subtle pigmented pseudonetwork.
