1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with shades of pink and red, displaying a more pronounced erythema in the central area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and indistinct, gradually blending into the surrounding skin with a somewhat irregular, ovoid outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly symmetrical in its overall shape, but the internal distribution of color and vascular structures is asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with overlying fine hairs remaining intact.
5. Structural Features: Fine, branching, arborizing telangiectasias are the most prominent feature, concentrated centrally upon an erythematous base.
