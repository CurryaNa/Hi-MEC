1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a primary background of pink to erythematous red, interspersed with small, irregular areas of reddish-brown and dark brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending indistinctly with the surrounding normal skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved dermatoglyphics (skin lines) and is devoid of any significant scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key features include fine, branching telangiectatic vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) and scattered, small, reddish-brown globules and blotches.
