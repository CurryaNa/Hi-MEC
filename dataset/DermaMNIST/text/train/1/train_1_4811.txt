1. Primary Colors: The lesion is polychromatic, displaying shades of bright red, dark red, black, and brown against a background of erythematous pink skin.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly defined, particularly on the left side where it blends with an erythematous halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is ulcerated and features hemorrhagic crusting in the central and left portions.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central ulceration with hemorrhage, and peripherally located, irregularly shaped clods and globules of black and brown pigment.
