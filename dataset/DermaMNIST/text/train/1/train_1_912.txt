1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including light brown, pink-red, and a focal area of dark brown/black pigment.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly defined, particularly in the erythematous portions where they blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth and has a slightly translucent or pearly quality, without overt scaling or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) are prominent across the surface, accompanied by an asymmetrically located, dark pigment blotch.
