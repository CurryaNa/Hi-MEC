1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including a pink-red background, dark brown to black pigmented areas, and yellowish hemorrhagic crust.
2. Border Appearance: Its borders are highly irregular and ill-defined, with some sections showing a scalloped or notched configuration.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven and ulcerated, with significant areas covered by serosanguinous and hemorrhagic crust.
5. Structural Features: Prominent, large-caliber arborizing telangiectasias (branching vessels), irregular dark pigment blotches, and central ulceration are key features.
