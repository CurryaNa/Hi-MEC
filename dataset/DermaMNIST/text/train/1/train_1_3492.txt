1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors (polychromasia), including pink-red, dark brown, and black, with areas of white-yellowish scale and crust.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and poorly demarcated, blending indistinctly with the surrounding skin in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular, featuring prominent scaling, thick crusting, and evidence of erosion or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key structures include a raised (papulonodular) morphology, blotchy dark pigmentation, and central ulceration covered by hemorrhagic crust.
