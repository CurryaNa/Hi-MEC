1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays marked polychromia, with prominent areas of blue-gray, black, red-pink, and whitish scar-like coloration.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and ill-defined, featuring both indistinct margins that blend with the skin and sharply demarcated, jagged edges.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is profoundly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is variegated, showing significant central ulceration with hemorrhagic crusting, contrasted with smoother, atrophic areas.
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include large, confluent blue-gray ovoid nests, central ulceration, and distinct, branching arborizing telangiectasias at the periphery.
