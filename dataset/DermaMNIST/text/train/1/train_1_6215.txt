1. Primary Colors: The lesion is polychromatic, presenting with dark brown to black central pigmentation, a surrounding light brown reticular network, and a pinkish erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The border of the central pigmented component is irregular and stellate, with pigment extending in a starburst pattern into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with preserved dermatoglyphics (skin lines).
5. Structural Features: Key features include prominent radial streaming or pseudopods emanating from a central pigmented hub, along with fine, linear, and serpentine vessels.
