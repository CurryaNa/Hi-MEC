1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including prominent erythema (red), pink, and focal areas of black and grey pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin without a distinct demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface shows evidence of central erosion and fine crusting, along with some shiny, structureless areas.
5. Structural Features: Key features include polymorphous vessels (linear-irregular and dotted), blotches of dark pigment, and a central reddish, ulcerated zone.
