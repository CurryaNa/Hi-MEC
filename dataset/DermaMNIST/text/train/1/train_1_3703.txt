1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including pink-red (erythematous), translucent pearly white, and a focal area of dark red to black hemorrhagic crusting.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular, with a combination of central crusting and ulceration, as well as smoother, waxy or shiny areas.
5. Structural Features: Prominent, fine, branching telangiectasias (arborizing vessels) are present, especially at the periphery, along with central ulceration and a raised, rolled border.
