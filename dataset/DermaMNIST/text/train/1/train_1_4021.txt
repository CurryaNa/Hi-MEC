1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including dark brown, black, red, and pink-tan.
2. Border Appearance: Its borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, appearing blurred and indistinct in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is irregular, featuring a central area of erosion or ulceration with some crusting.
5. Structural Features: Key features include disorganized, blotchy pigmentation, a central dark clod, and fine, irregular, branching vessels (telangiectasias) primarily in the periphery.
