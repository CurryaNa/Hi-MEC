1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a polychromatic pattern, with a predominant pink-to-red base, interspersed with areas of dark brown-to-black pigmentation and fine white structures.
2. Border Appearance: The border is relatively well-demarcated but is distinctly irregular and scalloped, lacking a smooth, continuous outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and patterns.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven and nodular, presenting with fine white scaling and focal areas of dark, hemorrhagic crusting.
5. Structural Features: Distinctive features include a raised (nodular) conformation, prominent, branching and arborizing telangiectasias, and multiple irregular, dark brown globules and clods.
