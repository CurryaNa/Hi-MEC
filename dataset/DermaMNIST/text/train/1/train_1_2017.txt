1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), with a dominant pink-to-red (erythematous) area and a central focus of dark brown-to-black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion's borders are irregular in shape and indistinct, appearing blurred and poorly demarcated from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears slightly elevated and irregular, with evidence of central crusting or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central, dark, ulcerated or crusted clod, surrounded by a pink, structureless (amelanotic) area.
