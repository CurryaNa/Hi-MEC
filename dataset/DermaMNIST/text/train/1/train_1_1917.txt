1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including an erythematous pink halo, blotchy light brown pigmentation, and a central focus of red consistent with hemorrhage.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, particularly where the erythematous component fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its color and structural components.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is characterized by central ulceration and crusting, while the surrounding area appears slightly raised and smooth.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central ulcer, asymmetric peripheral pigmentation, and a surrounding erythematous halo with fine, dotted vessels.
