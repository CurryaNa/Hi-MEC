1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a polychromatic pattern, primarily featuring shades of pink and red, with scattered areas of light brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Its borders are ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and vascular structures.
