1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including light brown, dark brown-to-black, and distinct red-pink hues.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular in outline and focally ill-defined, particularly at the periphery where it blends with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and in the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears somewhat shiny and lobulated, with a slightly raised and uneven topography.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include prominent, branching arborizing telangiectasias, multiple blue-gray globules/ovoid nests, and a central pigmented, clod-like structure.
