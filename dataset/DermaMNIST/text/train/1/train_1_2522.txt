1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a polychromatic pattern, with a dominant erythematous red-pink background, an eccentric dark brown-black pigmented area, and subtle whitish zones.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, particularly the erythematous component which fades diffusely into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its color and structural components.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, though there may be subtle, fine scaling or early crust formation in some areas.
5. Structural Features: Prominent structural features include polymorphous vessels (dotted and linear), an eccentric structureless brown-black pigmented zone, and several terminal hairs traversing the lesion.
