1. Primary Colors: The lesion is polychromatic, exhibiting pinkish-white, dark brown, black, and prominent blue-gray to violaceous areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and highly irregular, blending indistinctly with the surrounding tissue.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is complex and irregular, featuring areas of ulceration, crusting, and a shiny, uneven topography.
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include large, blue-gray ovoid nests, ulceration, and prominent, irregularly branching (arborizing) telangiectasias.
