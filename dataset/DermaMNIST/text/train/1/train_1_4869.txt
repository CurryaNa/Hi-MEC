1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including erythematous pink/red, light brown, and focal blue-gray to black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, particularly at the periphery, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The central surface is ulcerated and crusted, while the surrounding erythematous halo is relatively smooth.
5. Structural Features: Prominent arborizing telangiectasias are visible at the periphery, with central ulceration and scattered blue-gray globules.
