1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a prominent pink-red central area, scattered light brown pigmentation, and whitish scar-like regions.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, with the erythematous and pigmented components blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth but shows signs of slight atrophy and a small, focal area of erosion or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Distinct structural features include fine, linear, and branching telangiectasias (arborizing vessels), a small central ulceration, and patchy, unstructured brown pigmentation.
