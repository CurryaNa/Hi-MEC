1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a multi-component color pattern, including pink-red, pearly white, and focal dark brown/black areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, lacking a sharp demarcation from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.

5. Structural Features: Key structures include fine, branching arborizing telangiectasias, central ulceration, and scattered blue-gray pigmented globules.
