1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors including pink-red, light brown, dark brown, and blue-gray areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth but with focal erosion and loss of fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Distinct structures include prominent, branching (arborizing) telangiectasias, multiple blue-gray ovoid nests, and small areas of ulceration.
