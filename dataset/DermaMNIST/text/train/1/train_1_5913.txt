1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a pink-to-erythematous base and a focal area of dark brown to black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears slightly uneven with evidence of focal erosion or early ulceration in the pigmented area.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central, darkly pigmented, clod-like structure and fine, unfocused telangiectasias (small blood vessels) throughout the pinkish body of the lesion.
