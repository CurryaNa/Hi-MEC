1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, black, and pinkish-red, indicating polychromasia.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined, with some areas appearing blurred and blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular, exhibiting areas of atrophy (scar-like depression) and slight central crusting.
5. Structural Features: Key features include disorganized pigmentation with asymmetric blotches and globules, central atrophy with possible early ulceration, and a lack of a recognizable benign pattern.
