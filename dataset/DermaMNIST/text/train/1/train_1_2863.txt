1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including pink, red (hemorrhagic), dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, gradually fading into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The central surface is irregular, featuring areas of crusting and ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key features include central ulceration with hemorrhage, surrounding erythema (inflammation), and some small, scattered dark globules.
