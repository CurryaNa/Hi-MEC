1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including a central dark brown to black area, surrounded by a lighter brown and a peripheral pinkish-violaceous halo.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and blurred, gradually fading into the surrounding skin, with a slightly irregular but generally round shape.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth, although there may be subtle textural changes or slight crusting in the central, darker portion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a central, heavily pigmented, possibly elevated or nodular component, with surrounding radial pigmentation and an erythematous flare.
