1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including a central pink-to-red area, peripheral light brown pigmentation, and a focal dark brown/black dot.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and irregular, blurring into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears slightly scaly and crusted, particularly over the central erythematous area which shows some disruption.
5. Structural Features: There is a central area of erosion or early ulceration, surrounded by a disorganized, blotchy pigment pattern and a small, eccentric black globule.
