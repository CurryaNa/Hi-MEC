1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a pink-to-red (erythematous) base, multiple dark brown to black pigmented dots and blotches, and areas of white scale.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, making it difficult to clearly demarcate the lesion from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal pigmentation and scaling.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is dry and exhibits fine, superficial white scaling (desquamation) across the erythematous areas.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include terminal hairs emerging from the lesion, perifollicular black dots and globules, and a background of diffuse erythema.
