1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including prominent pink and red (erythematous) areas, as well as scattered black and dark brown specks.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, lacking a clear demarcation and blending into the surrounding epidermis.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is disrupted, showing areas of erosion and central ulceration, with some focal crusting.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include central ulceration, fine, branching telangiectatic vessels (arborizing telangiectasias), and multiple blue-gray to black dots and globules.
