1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a predominant pink-to-red background erythema, with a central area containing light brown, dark brown, and black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, gradually merging with the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with fine, slightly effaced skin markings (dermatoglyphics) and visible vellus hairs.
5. Structural Features: Structures include central, irregularly distributed brown-to-black globules and dots, surrounded by fine, linear, and serpentine vessels (telangiectasias) on an erythematous base.
