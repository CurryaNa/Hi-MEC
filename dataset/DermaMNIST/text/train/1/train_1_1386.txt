1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including erythematous pink, a translucent whitish hue, focal dark brown-to-black pigmentation, and areas of bright red hemorrhage.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and ill-defined, blending into the surrounding inflamed skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular, featuring central ulceration covered by a hemorrhagic crust.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include central ulceration, fine, branching arborizing telangiectasias at the periphery, and scattered dark pigmented clods.
