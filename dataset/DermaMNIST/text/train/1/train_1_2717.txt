1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including pink, red, light brown, dark brown, and a central deep red to black area.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly defined, with fuzzy or blurred margins that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular, featuring a central area of crusting and possible early ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include a central dark clod, blotchy pigmentation, and fine, irregular, linear and arborizing blood vessels, particularly at the periphery.
