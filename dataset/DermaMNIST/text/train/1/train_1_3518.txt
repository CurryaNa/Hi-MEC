1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors (polychromasia), including pink-red, light brown, and focal areas of dark brown to black.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears relatively smooth, with some effacement of normal skin lines, particularly in the central pink area.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include blotches of dark pigmentation, structureless pink areas, and prominent fine, branching telangiectatic vessels (arborizing vessels).
