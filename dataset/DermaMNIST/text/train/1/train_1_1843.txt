1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including shades of brown, black, red/pink, and a focal blue-grey dot.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, with pigment fading indistinctly into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular, featuring a focal area of dark hemorrhagic crust and a slightly moist or glistening texture overall.
5. Structural Features: Notable features include blotchy, non-uniform pigmentation, fine arborizing telangiectasias (small blood vessels), and a focal point of ulceration or crusting.
