1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays significant polychromasia, with prominent areas of black, dark red, and pinkish-violaceous hues.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly demarcated, featuring notching and scalloping with adjacent erythema.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular, combining areas of hemorrhagic crusting and ulceration with smooth, shiny nodular components.
5. Structural Features: Key features include multiple translucent, raised nodules, particularly at the periphery, and a central, ulcerated plaque covered by dark, thrombosed crust.
