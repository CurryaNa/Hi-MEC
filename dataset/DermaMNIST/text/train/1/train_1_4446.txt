1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits marked color variegation, with areas of erythematous pink, light brown, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly defined, featuring significant notching and areas where the lesion blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is complex and non-uniform, presenting with areas of ulceration covered by hemorrhagic crust, as well as atrophic, scar-like zones.
5. Structural Features: Key features include extensive ulceration and crusting, alongside prominent signs of regression such as whitish-pink scar-like areas and scattered pigmented globules.
