1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including light brown, dark brown, and a focal black area centrally.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and blurred, showing an irregular, feathery, or starburst-like pattern.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal pigmentation.

5. Structural Features: Key features include an atypical pigment network, a central disorganized blotch, and peripheral radial streaming or pseudopods.
