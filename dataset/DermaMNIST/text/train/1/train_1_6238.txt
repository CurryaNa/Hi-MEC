1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including prominent erythema (red), dark brown to black pigmentation, and whitish, scar-like areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, with pigmented areas showing a blotchy, indistinct edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of its color and structural components.
4. Surface Texture: The surface shows evidence of fine scaling and a central area with features suggestive of erosion or early ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Polymorphous vessels are visible within the erythematous zones, accompanied by eccentric blotches of dark pigment and central white, structureless areas consistent with regression.
