1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including pink-red, light brown, and focal areas of dark brown to black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending indistinctly with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface features a central area of erosion and subtle crusting, with a somewhat shiny or waxy appearance in the pink regions.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include central ulceration, fine, branching telangiectatic vessels (arborizing telangiectasias), and disorganized, blotchy brown pigmentation.
