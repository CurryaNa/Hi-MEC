1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a multi-component color pattern, including large blue-gray and violaceous areas, peripheral erythema (redness), and scattered whitish specks.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with the blue coloration blending into the surrounding skin, particularly on the superior aspect.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and slightly translucent, with fine vellus hairs present across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Distinct structures include large, lobulated blue-gray ovoid nests, prominent arborizing telangiectasias (branching vessels), and multiple small, white, milia-like cysts.
