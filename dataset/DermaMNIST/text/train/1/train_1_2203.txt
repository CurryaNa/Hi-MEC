1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and focal black areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined, with pigment streaks radiating into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of pigment.

5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic structures include an atypical pigment network, peripheral streaks (pseudopods), and central, structureless blotches of dark pigment.
