1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays polychromia, with areas of pink, red, light brown, dark brown, black, and yellowish-tan.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly demarcated, with some sections appearing scalloped and others fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is variegated, presenting with areas of crusting, waxy scale, and a central, slightly eroded or ulcerated texture.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include multiple dark brown to black clods and globules, a central pink structureless area suggesting ulceration, and yellowish keratinous crusts.
