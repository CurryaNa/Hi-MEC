1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors (polychromasia), including pink-red erythema, light brown, dark brown, and focal black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with the pigmented component showing notching and the erythematous halo being poorly demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears irregular, with a combination of smoother inflamed areas and a slightly raised, possibly crusted or scaly central component.
5. Structural Features: Prominent arborizing telangiectasias are visible, along with disorganized pigmentation, multiple globules/dots, and evidence of central ulceration or crusting.
