1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a pink-red erythematous base, light brown areas, and multiple dark brown to black pigmented foci.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending indistinctly with the surrounding skin in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth and slightly translucent, without evidence of significant scaling or crusting.
5. Structural Features: Key features include prominent, branching (arborizing) telangiectasias, multiple dark brown/blue-gray globules, and several small, focal ulcerations or erosions.
