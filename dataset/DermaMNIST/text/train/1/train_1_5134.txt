1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including pearly white, pink-red, a focal area of dark red-to-black hemorrhage, and subtle blue-gray pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Its borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, featuring a raised, translucent, and rolled periphery in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is varied, with a central ulcerated and crusted area adjacent to surfaces that appear smooth, shiny, and waxy.
5. Structural Features: Distinct structures include a central ulceration with hemorrhage, prominent, fine, branching arborizing telangiectasias, and pearly, translucent nodules.
