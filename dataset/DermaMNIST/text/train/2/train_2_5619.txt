1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of light to medium brown, tan, and pink, with scattered small, dark brown to black specks.
2. Border Appearance: The border is relatively well-demarcated but irregular, exhibiting a slightly scalloped and indistinct edge in some areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall round-to-oval shape and in the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is verrucous and papillomatous, creating a pronounced cobblestone or cerebriform (brain-like) texture.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include numerous milia-like cysts (small white dots), comedo-like openings (dark plugs), and fissures creating a pattern of gyri and sulci.
