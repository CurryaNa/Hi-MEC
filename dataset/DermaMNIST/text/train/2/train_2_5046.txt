1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including shades of brown, black, and red, surrounded by a pink erythematous halo.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined, with a distinct peripheral collarette of white scale.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is predominantly scaly at the periphery and centrally crusted, with evidence of erosion or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: The lesion presents with central hemorrhagic crusting and ulceration, asymmetric dark brown and black pigmented blotches, and a peripheral ring of scale.
