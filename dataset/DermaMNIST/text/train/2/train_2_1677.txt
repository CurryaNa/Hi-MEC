1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a mottled appearance, combining erythematous pink, reticulated light brown pigmentation, and scattered whitish or hypopigmented areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending indistinctly with the adjacent normal skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its color and structural components.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears atrophic and dry, exhibiting fine, superficial scaling over the pigmented and erythematous areas.
5. Structural Features: Key features include prominent, fine, branching telangiectasias and a net-like or reticular pattern of pigmentation.
