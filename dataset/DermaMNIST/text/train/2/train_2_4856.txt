1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including dark brown, black, and tan, with focal grey-white areas centrally.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly defined, featuring a scalloped periphery where the pigment abruptly stops or fades.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is verrucous (wart-like) and cerebriform, with a dry, crusted, and finely fissured appearance.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a multicomponent pattern with dark globules, a disorganized pigment network, and central scale-crust.
