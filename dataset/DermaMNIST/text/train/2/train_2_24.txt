1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors (polychromasia), including various shades of brown, black, and distinct blue-gray areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and poorly demarcated, showing scalloping and notching with an abrupt cutoff in some areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth, though with subtle topographical variations and effacement of normal skin lines in some regions.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a multicomponent pattern with an atypical pigment network, multiple black clods, blue-gray structureless zones, and several milia-like cysts/comedo-like openings.
