1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including varying shades of light to dark brown and areas of pink-red erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with pigmentation that fades and blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved dermatoglyphics (skin lines) and is not scaly, crusted, or ulcerated.
5. Structural Features: Prominent structural features include blotchy, non-uniform pigmentation and numerous fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) on and around the lesion.
