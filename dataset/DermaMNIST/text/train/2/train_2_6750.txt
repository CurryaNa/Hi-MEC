1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with shades of light brown and tan, surrounded by diffuse pink and red erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and indistinct, gradually blending into the adjacent skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears dry with fine, subtle scaling, and the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) are partially effaced.
5. Structural Features: Fine, branching telangiectasias (red vessels) are present, along with a faint, reticular pattern of light brown pigmentation.
