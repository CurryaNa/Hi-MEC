1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits color variegation, presenting multiple shades of brown, from tan to dark brown, along with significant whitish-pink areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined, with some areas fading into the surrounding skin and other sections showing a scalloped, indistinct edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with some loss of fine skin lines (effacement), and is devoid of scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key dermoscopic features include a disorganized reticular pattern, structureless brown areas, and extensive whitish regression structures. Prominent, branching (arborizing) telangiectasias are also visible over the lesion and in the surrounding skin.
