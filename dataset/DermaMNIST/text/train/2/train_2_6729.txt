1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with two main shades of brown: a darker, more concentrated brown centrally and a lighter tan at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, characterized by feathery, pseudopod-like projections and streaks radiating outwards.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat (macular), with no evidence of scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: An atypical reticular pigment network is present, which breaks up into radial streaming at the periphery; fine telangiectasias are also noted around the lesion.
