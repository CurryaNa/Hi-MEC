1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light tan to dark brown, along with areas of hypopigmentation and erythema (pinkness).
2. Border Appearance: The border is ill-defined and irregular, featuring a scalloped or moth-eaten appearance where the pigmentation fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat (macular), with preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible throughout the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a patchy, reticular (net-like) pigment pattern, most prominent at the periphery, and a central structureless, hypopigmented area suggestive of regression.
