1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including pink-red (erythema), light brown, whitish-yellow, and focal dark brown/black areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular in shape and indistinct in several areas, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is heterogeneous, featuring a focal crusted area and a dry, scaly, reticulated pattern over the majority of the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a focal ulceration with an overlying hemorrhagic crust, a fine whitish-yellow network or scale, and scattered dark brown dots/globules.
