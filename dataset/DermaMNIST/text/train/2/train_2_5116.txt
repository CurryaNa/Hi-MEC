1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light tan to darker brown, interspersed with pinkish and skin-colored areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with pigmentation that fades into the surrounding skin in a blotchy, starburst-like pattern.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved and visible across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: A reticular (net-like) pigment network is present, which is disorganized and blotchy, along with some small whitish dots (milia-like cysts or regressive structures).
