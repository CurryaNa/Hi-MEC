1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (multicolor variegation), including pink, red, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly defined, with a scalloped contour in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal structures and colors.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is nodular and uneven, with evidence of central crusting and possible ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key features include disorganized pigmentation, multiple black dots and globules, and fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) at the periphery.
