1. Primary Colors: The lesion is multi-colored, exhibiting a pink-to-red background with areas of light brown and dark brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The pigmented components have irregular and ill-defined borders that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its structure and the distribution of its colors.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with visible fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and is devoid of scale or crusting.
5. Structural Features: Key features include prominent, branching, serpentine vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) and an atypical pigment network with blotchy and leaf-like areas.
