1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a predominant whitish-yellow color, interspersed with small brown-black dots, and is surrounded by a faint pinkish erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The border is well-demarcated from the surrounding skin, with a slightly irregular but non-infiltrative edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is roughly round to oval and is largely symmetrical along its axes.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is dry and keratotic, exhibiting a cerebriform or brain-like pattern with fine fissures.
5. Structural Features: Prominent structural features include multiple milia-like cysts (small white globules) and comedo-like openings (dark keratin plugs).
