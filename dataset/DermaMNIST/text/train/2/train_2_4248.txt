1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including shades of brown, black, and red centrally, surrounded by a pink to erythematous halo.
2. Border Appearance: The overall border is irregular and poorly defined, with the central pigmented and crusted component appearing more demarcated than the peripheral erythema.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and in the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is characterized by central hemorrhagic crusting and ulceration, transitioning to a fine, whitish scale at the periphery.
5. Structural Features: A central, ulcerated plaque with variegated pigmentation and hemorrhagic crust is present, surrounded by an erythematous zone featuring a collarette of scale.
