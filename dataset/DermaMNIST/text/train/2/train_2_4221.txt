2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined, fading into the surrounding skin, especially within the erythematous component.

5. Structural Features: Structural features include a disorganized reticular pattern, scattered brown and black dots/globules, and peripheral erythema, suggesting an inflammatory component.
