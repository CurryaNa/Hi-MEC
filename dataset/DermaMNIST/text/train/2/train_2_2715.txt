1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including various shades of light brown, dark brown, and a pinkish-tan background.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and irregular, showing a gradual, blurred transition into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and visible vellus hairs.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a reticular (net-like) pattern, central blotches of darker pigment, and prominent annular-granular structures, specifically pigmentation around hair follicle openings (perifollicular pigmentation).
