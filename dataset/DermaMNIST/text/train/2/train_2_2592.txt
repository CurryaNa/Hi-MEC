1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple shades of brown, ranging from light tan to dark brown, with some near-black areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with pigment gradually fading into the surrounding skin, creating a moth-eaten appearance.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally flat, characterized by numerous prominent adnexal (follicular) openings.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopy reveals asymmetric perifollicular pigmentation, creating an annular-granular pattern and rhomboidal structures, with areas of more confluent, structureless brown pigment.
