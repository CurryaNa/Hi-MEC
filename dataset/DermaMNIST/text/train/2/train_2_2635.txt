1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light tan to a dark, concentrated brown in the center.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, characterized by a feathery or starburst pattern of pigment extending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of its internal pigmentation.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and fine vellus hairs visible across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: The predominant structural feature is a pattern of radial streaming and pseudopods, creating a distinct starburst appearance, particularly at the periphery.
