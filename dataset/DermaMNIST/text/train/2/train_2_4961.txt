1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays significant polychromasia, with prominent areas of pink, red, dark brown, and black, along with a whitish surface scale.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, with some areas showing an abrupt, blotchy edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall ovoid shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven and papular, exhibiting areas of fine white scaling and peripheral dark crusting.
5. Structural Features: Key features include irregular dark brown/black clods at the periphery, a central pink-red structureless area, and an overlying fine white reticular network (negative pigment network).
