1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays color variegation, with prominent areas of pink, light brown, and dark brown, as well as focal red dots.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly defined, with the pigmented component feathering indistinctly into the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of its structural and color components.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is predominantly verrucous (wart-like) and irregular, with some areas of crusting and erosion over the central raised portion.
5. Structural Features: A central, exophytic pink nodule with fine vascular structures is observed arising from a larger, heterogeneously pigmented patch.
