1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with two main shades: a light brown background and areas of darker brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and focally ill-defined, exhibiting a scalloped or moth-eaten appearance in some areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with preserved fine skin lines and no significant scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A prominent pseudoreticular pattern is observed, characterized by a brown pigment network surrounding numerous, regularly spaced, pale adnexal (follicular) openings.
