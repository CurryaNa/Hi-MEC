1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, predominantly light brown and dark brown, on an erythematous (pink) background.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, showing a gradual fading into the surrounding skin rather than a sharp demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of pigment within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears flat and smooth, with preserved dermatoglyphics (skin lines) visible across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: A reticular (net-like) pigment network is the dominant feature, accompanied by fine, branching telangiectasias (visible blood vessels).
