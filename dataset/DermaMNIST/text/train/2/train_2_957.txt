1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with color variegation, including light brown, dark brown/black, and prominent purplish-violaceous hues.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with notching and areas that fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth, without visible scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic structures include an atypical, disorganized pigment network, structureless blotches of dark pigment, and some peripheral telangiectasias.
