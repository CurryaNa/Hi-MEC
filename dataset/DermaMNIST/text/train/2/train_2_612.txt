1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors including tan-yellow, light brown, dark brown/black specks, and a peripheral pinkish erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular, with some segments appearing well-demarcated while others are ill-defined and blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is predominantly crusted and verrucous (wart-like), with a somewhat waxy appearance in the central area.
5. Structural Features: Notable structures include a central, raised keratinous mass containing multiple dark, comedo-like openings or horn cysts.
