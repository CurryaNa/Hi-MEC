1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits polychromasia, with dominant violaceous and purplish hues, interspersed with brown, grey, and whitish areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and ill-defined, gradually fading into the surrounding pigmented skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in terms of both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is complex, featuring smooth, slightly atrophic-appearing patches alongside areas with a fine, overlying whitish network.
5. Structural Features: Distinct structures include a delicate, reticular white pattern (Wickham's striae), numerous brown-to-violaceous dots and globules, and areas of regression with grey-white coloration.
