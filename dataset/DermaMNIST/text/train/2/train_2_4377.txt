1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and numerous small white dots. A surrounding pinkish erythema is also visible.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, featuring a reticular pattern that fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears uneven with a subtly mammillated or cobblestone-like texture.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is observed, characterized by a disorganized pigment network, blotchy brown pigmentation, and prominent white dots/globules creating an inverse network appearance.
