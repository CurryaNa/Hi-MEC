1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a central dark brown to black area, a prominent white-grey scale-crust, and a peripheral erythematous (pink-red) halo.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion has a well-demarcated border with a regular, oval outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is broadly symmetrical along its longitudinal axis, but the internal distribution of color and scale is asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: The central surface is rough and verrucous (warty), covered by a thick, adherent scale-crust.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include central hyperkeratotic scale-crust over a pigmented base and a peripheral crown of radially arranged dotted and hairpin blood vessels.
