1. Primary Colors: The lesion is predominantly skin-colored to light tan, with multiple scattered dark brown to black specks and mild erythema at the base.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are well-demarcated and distinct from the surrounding skin, with some irregularity and crusting along the inferior margin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly symmetrical in its overall dome shape, but asymmetrical in its surface features and color distribution.

5. Structural Features: This is an exophytic, pedunculated papule with a "stuck-on" quality, featuring small, dark, comedo-like openings or horn cysts.
