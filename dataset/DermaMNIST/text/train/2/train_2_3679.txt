1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including a central pink-red area, surrounded by zones of gray-blue, light brown, and whitish pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly defined, with a hazy and blurred transition into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears mostly smooth, but with subtle scaling and possible early erosion or crusting in the central erythematous region.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a multicomponent pattern with a central erythematous blotch, a surrounding blue-white veil, and peripheral scar-like areas of regression.
