1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits significant color variegation, presenting with shades of dark brown, black, light brown, pink-red, and areas of white-blue/grey.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly demarcated, featuring notching, scalloping, and areas where the pigment fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is heterogeneous, with some areas appearing smooth and globular, while others show fine scaling and slight crusting.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a multicomponent pattern with atypical globules, dark blotches, and signs of regression such as a grey-blue veil and peppering; atypical vascular patterns are also present in the erythematous zones.
