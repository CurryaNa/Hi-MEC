1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including a dark brown to black central area, a lighter brown periphery, and numerous small, well-defined whitish-yellow circles.
2. Border Appearance: The border is somewhat irregular and scalloped, appearing well-demarcated in some areas and slightly indistinct in others.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical regarding both its color distribution and the arrangement of its internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface has a slightly raised, verrucous, and cerebriform (brain-like) texture, especially in the darker central region.
5. Structural Features: Key features include multiple milia-like cysts (whitish clods) and comedo-like openings scattered throughout the lesion, characteristic of a seborrheic keratosis.
