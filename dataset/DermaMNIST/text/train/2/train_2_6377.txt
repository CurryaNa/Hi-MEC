1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light tan to dark brown, with subtle pink and skin-colored areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with the pigment appearing to fade and smudge into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly flat, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) disrupted and slightly effaced in the central region.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a disorganized reticular pattern at the periphery, central blotchy and confluent pigmentation, and a small, lighter-colored, atrophic-appearing area suggestive of regression or scarring.
