1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays a variegated pigmentation, with colors ranging from light tan and brown to dark brown, along with whitish-yellow keratotic material.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are well-demarcated and sharply defined from the surrounding skin, exhibiting an irregular, scalloped, or lobulated contour.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is asymmetrical, composed of multiple fused, rounded lobules of varying sizes.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is verrucous (wart-like) and keratotic, appearing rough, dry, and crusted.
5. Structural Features: The lesion is exophytic (raised) and papillomatous, featuring multiple distinct, dark, plugged follicular openings known as comedo-like openings or horny pseudocysts.
