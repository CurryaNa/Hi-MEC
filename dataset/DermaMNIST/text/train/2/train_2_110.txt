1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits polychromasia, with prominent areas of brown, black, blue-gray, and yellowish-white crust.
2. Border Appearance: The border is generally well-demarcated but highly irregular and scalloped, with a "stuck-on" appearance in some sections.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is predominantly verrucous and crusted, with significant areas of ulceration and exudate.
5. Structural Features: Key features include multiple comedo-like openings, milia-like cysts, and a chaotic, multi-component pattern with structureless blue-gray areas.
