1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, predominantly light to medium brown, with focal areas of darker brown and grey-blue peppering.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregularly shaped and somewhat ill-defined, with the pigment network fading out towards the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears dry and scaly, characterized by fine, superficial fissures and a wrinkled texture.
5. Structural Features: A prominent, fine reticular (net-like) pigment network is visible throughout, with superimposed focal dots and globules, particularly in the central and superior-central areas.
