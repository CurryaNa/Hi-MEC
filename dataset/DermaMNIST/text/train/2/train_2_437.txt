1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors, including a central area of dark brown, black, and reddish-brown clods suggestive of hemorrhage, surrounded by a pink to erythematous halo.
2. Border Appearance: The central crusted component has a relatively defined but irregular border, which is sharply demarcated by a collarette of scale from the surrounding ill-defined erythematous zone.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape of the lesion is grossly symmetrical and round to oval, but the internal distribution of color and structure is markedly asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is characterized by central hemorrhagic crusting and focal ulceration, with a distinct peripheral ring of fine, whitish scale.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a central, raised, crusted, and ulcerated papule with dark globules and a prominent peripheral collarette of scale.
