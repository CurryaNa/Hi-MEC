1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays polychromia, with notable areas of black, dark brown, light brown, and a prominent central blue-white veil.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined, featuring notching and areas of abrupt termination of pigment.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven, with significant areas of crusting and ulceration, particularly at the periphery and in the central-superior aspect.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic structures include a large, central blue-white structureless area, multiple irregular black and brown dots and clods (globules), and evidence of regression.
