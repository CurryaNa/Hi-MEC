1. Primary Colors: The lesion is predominantly skin-toned to light brown, with a focal area of darker grey-brown pigmentation centrally.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are well-demarcated but highly irregular and scalloped, formed by the aggregation of multiple small papules.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in its overall contour and in the distribution of color.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is verrucous and papillomatous, exhibiting a cobblestone or cerebriform (brain-like) texture.
5. Structural Features: Key features include multiple exophytic, digitiform projections, some with central keratin plugging (comedo-like openings), and fine vellus hairs emerging from the surface.
