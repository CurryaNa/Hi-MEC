1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of light to medium brown, along with a central area of hypopigmentation or whitish discoloration.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, exhibiting a feathery or moth-eaten pattern, particularly at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and macular (flat), with preserved dermatoglyphics (skin lines) visible across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a delicate, atypical pigment network that is more prominent at the edges, and a central zone of regression or scar-like depigmentation.
