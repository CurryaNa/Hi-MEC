1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors (polychromasia), including dark brown, black, tan, and focal white-gray areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is highly irregular and ill-defined, with a spiculated or starburst pattern of pigmentation extending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven, featuring a centrally raised, crusted, and ulcerated area.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic structures include a central ulcerated core, an atypical pigment network, and prominent radial streaming and pseudopods at the periphery.
