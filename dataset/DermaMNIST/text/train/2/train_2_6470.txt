1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a polychromatic pattern, consisting of a pink-violaceous background, numerous bright white to yellowish-white globules, and focal brown-to-black crusted areas.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion possesses a well-demarcated and geographically regular border, forming a generally round-to-oval shape.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape of the lesion is largely symmetrical, though the distribution of internal colors and structures is asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven and complex, featuring a combination of smooth areas and multiple raised, crusted, and keratotic plugs.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include numerous, sharply demarcated, white milia-like cysts, brown comedo-like openings, and fine, branching arborizing telangiectasias visible across the pinkish base.
