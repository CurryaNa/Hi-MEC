1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including various shades of brown (light to dark) and a central area of pinkish-white hypopigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is highly irregular and scalloped, with some areas appearing sharply demarcated and others fading indistinctly into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears dry with fine, whitish scaling, giving it a crinkled or wrinkled texture over the pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a peripheral reticular (net-like) pigment network and significant central scar-like regression, characterized by atrophy and loss of pigment.
