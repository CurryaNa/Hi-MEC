1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including various shades of light to dark brown and areas of pinkish-red.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with the pigment network fading out into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears slightly rough and scaly, with preserved fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include a reticular pigment network at the periphery and multiple whitish, cerebriform or crypt-like openings in the central area.
