1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a prominent pink-to-red nodular area, yellowish-tan crusted sections, and focal dark brown-black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion's borders are highly irregular, poorly defined, and exhibit a scalloped or multi-lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is markedly asymmetrical in both contour and internal structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is heterogeneous, combining a waxy, crusted, and verrucous appearance with a more fleshy, nodular component.
5. Structural Features: Distinct structures include a multi-component pattern, prominent arborizing (tree-like) vessels within the pink nodule, and small areas of ulceration.
