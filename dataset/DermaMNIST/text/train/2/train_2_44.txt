1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, black, and a violaceous-pink background.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, gradually fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears slightly waxy and verrucous, with numerous prominent follicular openings.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include multiple black, comedo-like openings (keratin plugs), small whitish milia-like cysts, and areas of reticular (network-like) pigmentation.
