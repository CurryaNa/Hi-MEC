1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors (polychromia), including light brown, dark brown, black, and gray-blue areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and focally ill-defined, with some areas appearing scalloped or notched.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is verrucous (wart-like) and scaly, with a prominent, centrally located, crusted and fissured area.
5. Structural Features: Distinct structures include multiple dark, comedo-like openings and small, whitish milia-like cysts, characteristic of a seborrheic keratosis.
