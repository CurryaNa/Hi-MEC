1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and areas of pinkish-red erythema.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and irregular, with pigmentation that fades gradually into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth with preserved fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and emerging vellus hairs.
5. Structural Features: Internal structures include a disorganized reticular network, scattered brown dots and globules, and some perifollicular pigmentation.
