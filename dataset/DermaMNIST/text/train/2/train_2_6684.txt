1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a central brown pigmented area and a surrounding prominent pink-to-red erythematous halo.
2. Border Appearance: The borders of the central pigmented component are irregular and indistinct, blending into the surrounding erythematous patch.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment and vascularity.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with no evidence of significant crusting or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Distinct structural features include a central, blotchy brown pigmentation and prominent, branching arborizing telangiectasias on the surface.
