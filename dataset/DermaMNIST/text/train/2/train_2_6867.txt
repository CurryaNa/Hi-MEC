1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with varying shades of light brown and tan pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, with a feathery or fibrillar pattern that blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with preservation of the natural skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: A fine, reticular (net-like) pigment network is visible, which becomes more diffuse and radiates outwards at the periphery.
