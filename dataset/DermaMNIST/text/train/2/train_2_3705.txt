1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a pink-red erythematous background, areas of light to dark brown pigmentation, and yellowish-tan crusted sections.
2. Border Appearance: The overall border is ill-defined and irregular, appearing scalloped and composed of multiple coalescing smaller lesions.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular and varied, featuring areas of central ulceration and crusting, as well as some shiny, waxy-appearing surfaces.
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include multiple ulcerated nodules, fine, branching arborizing telangiectasias, and shiny white structures within a multicomponent pattern.
