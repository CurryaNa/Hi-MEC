1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromasia), including black, dark brown, light brown, and pinkish-red areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and are focally indistinct and blurred.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and internal color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular and elevated (papular/nodular), with evidence of focal crusting and possible ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key features include irregular blotches of dark pigment, structureless areas, and a polymorphous vascular pattern with some signs of erosion.
