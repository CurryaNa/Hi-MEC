1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits marked color variegation, presenting with shades of light and dark brown, black, and a prominent pink-red erythematous area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and ill-defined, with some areas appearing blurred and fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface has a dry, crinkled texture with fine white scaling, particularly noticeable over the central erythematous region.
5. Structural Features: Multiple features are observed, including irregular blotches of black and dark brown pigment, fine branching vessels (arborizing telangiectasias), and a chaotic, multi-component pattern.
