1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a central white scar-like area, shades of light to dark brown, and prominent black/blue-grey pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with pigmented structures appearing to fade or break up at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth, with evidence of central atrophy or scarring and visible hair follicles.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include irregularly distributed dots and globules, a chaotic pigment network, a central white regression zone, and prominent arborizing telangiectasias at the periphery.
