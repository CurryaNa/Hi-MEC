1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits three distinct zones of color: a central white, scar-like area, a surrounding pinkish-red (erythematous) halo, and a peripheral light brown (hyperpigmented) rim.
2. Border Appearance: The border is generally regular in its circular contour but is somewhat ill-defined and feathered where the peripheral brown pigmentation blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is symmetrical, presenting as a well-proportioned, roughly circular macule/papule.
4. Surface Texture: The central surface appears atrophic, smooth, and slightly depressed, with a loss of normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: The most prominent feature is a central fibrotic, atrophic depression surrounded by a rim of erythema and a delicate peripheral pigment network. Terminal hairs are observed growing through the lesion.
