1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a central, achromic to porcelain-white area, surrounded by a pinkish-violaceous background.
2. Border Appearance: The border of the central white patch is somewhat ill-defined, featuring fine, fibrillar, radiating projections that create a starburst or stellate appearance.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is generally round and demonstrates a high degree of symmetry in both its shape and internal pattern.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and slightly atrophic, without evidence of scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: The most prominent feature is the presence of shiny, white, radially arranged lines (chrysalis structures) emanating from a central, structureless, fibrotic-appearing white patch.
