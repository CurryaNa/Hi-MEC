1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays a spectrum of pink and red (erythema), with a central zone that is paler, whitish-pink.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending into the adjacent skin without a distinct demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is elongated and asymmetrical along both its long and short axes.
4. Surface Texture: The central surface appears atrophic and finely wrinkled or crinkled, with some subtle whitish scaling.
5. Structural Features: Key features include central atrophy, a linear depression, and visible fine, linear blood vessels (telangiectasias) primarily in the peripheral erythematous areas.
