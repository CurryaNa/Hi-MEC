1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a central pink-to-violaceous area, surrounded by a distinct peripheral rim of tan-to-brown hyperpigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion has a generally round, well-demarcated border, although the outer pigmented edge fades somewhat gradually into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is largely symmetrical in its circular shape and concentric color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: The central surface appears atrophic, shiny, and slightly crinkled, with a loss of normal dermatoglyphics, suggestive of scarring.
5. Structural Features: It is characterized by a centrally depressed, scar-like white patch with fine, radiating striae, encircled by a uniform halo of pigmentation.
