1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a prominent pink-to-violaceous hue, central whitish areas, and peripheral light brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, particularly where the pigmented component fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is slightly elevated and appears waxy or shiny, without significant scaling or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Key features include shiny white crystalline structures (chrysalis structures) and fine, short telangiectasias, with an absence of a classic pigment network.
