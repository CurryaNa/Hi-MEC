1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a central whitish-pale zone, surrounded by a pinkish-erythematous ring and a faint, peripheral light brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The inner border of the erythematous ring is well-demarcated, while the outer border of the lesion is ill-defined and gradually fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly symmetrical, exhibiting a regular, circular to oval shape with concentric color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and slightly waxy, lacking any significant scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A central, slightly depressed or atrophic area is the dominant feature, encircled by a subtly raised annular rim that may contain very fine, short telangiectatic vessels.
