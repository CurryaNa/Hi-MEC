1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a central pink to violaceous (reddish-purple) hue, with a subtle light brown pigmentation at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and blend into the surrounding skin, forming a roughly circular to oval shape.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly symmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is characterized by fine white scaling and superficial cracking, giving it a dry appearance.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a central erythematous-violaceous area with prominent, intersecting white lines (chrysalis-like structures) and scattered whitish dots.
