1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a tri-color pattern, with a central whitish-atrophic area, a surrounding pink-to-red (erythematous) rim, and a peripheral light brown halo.
2. Border Appearance: The border is regular and relatively well-demarcated, forming an oval shape, although the peripheral brown pigmentation fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is generally symmetrical in its round-to-oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: The central surface is atrophic and depressed, showing fine wrinkling and some minute, adherent crusts.
5. Structural Features: A key feature is the central depression (atrophy/scarring) surrounded by a slightly raised, firm-appearing rim and a peripheral network of tan pigmentation.
