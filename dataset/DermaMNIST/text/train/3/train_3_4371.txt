1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including violaceous (reddish-purple), brown, and prominent white, scar-like areas of hypopigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined in several areas, exhibiting a scalloped or lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical with respect to both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is predominantly smooth but shows fine, whitish scaling and wrinkling, with effacement of skin lines in the central white area.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is evident, characterized by a peripheral reticular pigment network and extensive areas of regression appearing as structureless white zones.
