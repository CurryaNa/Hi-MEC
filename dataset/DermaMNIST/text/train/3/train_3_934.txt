1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits significant color variegation, presenting with shades of pink-red, light and dark brown, black, and a prominent blue-gray ovoid area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly demarcated, with notching and areas where the pigment fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is complex, featuring areas of ulceration, hemorrhagic crusting, and atrophic, shiny-white patches.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include central ulceration, blue-gray globules/ovoid structures, and features of regression such as scar-like white areas. Shiny white lines (chrysalis structures) are also visible.
