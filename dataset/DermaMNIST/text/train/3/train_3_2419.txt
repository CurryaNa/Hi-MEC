1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a central erythematous-pink area, a surrounding whitish-to-silvery scale, and focal tan-to-brown hyperpigmentation at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The border is well-demarcated and sharply defined from the surrounding skin, with a slightly irregular, scalloped outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of its color and structural components.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is characterized by a central, slightly atrophic and fissured texture, encircled by a dry, peeling collarette of scale.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central, eroded-appearing erythematous patch and a distinct peripheral collarette of scale lifting at the inner edge.
