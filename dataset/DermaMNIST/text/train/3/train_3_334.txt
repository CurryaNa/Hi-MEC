1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a polychromatic pattern, featuring a pink-to-violaceous peripheral ring and a hypopigmented, whitish-pink central area.
2. Border Appearance: The external border is somewhat indistinct and fades into the surrounding skin, while the inner margin of the raised ring is more clearly demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is largely symmetrical in its overall annular (ring-like) shape.
4. Surface Texture: The central zone appears slightly atrophic with a fine, wrinkled or subtly scaly texture, whereas the peripheral ring is smoother.
5. Structural Features: A distinct annular configuration is present, with a raised, erythematous border and central clearing or regression. Fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) are partially effaced in the center.
