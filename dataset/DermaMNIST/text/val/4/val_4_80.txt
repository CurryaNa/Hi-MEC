1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors (polychromia), including various shades of brown, from light to dark, and a prominent whitish-pink nodular component.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, featuring areas of notching and fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical with respect to both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is complex, with a raised, exophytic, and papillomatous (wart-like) texture in the central nodule, contrasting with the flatter pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: A key structural feature is a multicomponent pattern, consisting of a raised, hypopigmented nodule superimposed on a flat, macular area with an atypical, irregular pigment network.
