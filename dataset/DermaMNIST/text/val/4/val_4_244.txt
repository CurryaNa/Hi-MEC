1. Primary Colors: The lesion is polychromatic, displaying a central pink and red nodule surrounded by areas of light brown, dark brown, and black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is highly irregular and poorly defined, featuring areas of blotchy, streaky, and smudged pigmentation that fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is complex, with a raised, fleshy, and slightly glistening nodular component centrally, contrasted with the flatter peripheral pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central exophytic pink nodule with fine linear and arborizing vessels, surrounded by an asymmetric, multicomponent pattern of dark globules, blotches, and a disorganized pigment network.
