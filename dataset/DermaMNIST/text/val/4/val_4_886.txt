1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors (polychromasia), including light brown, dark brown, and foci of black, along with a central whitish-grey area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, with feathery, pseudopodal extensions on the periphery that fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is irregular, with disruption of normal skin lines and subtle variations in elevation, rather than being smooth or uniformly scaly.
5. Structural Features: It displays a multicomponent pattern, featuring an atypical pigment network, dark blotches, peripheral black dots (peppering), and a central scar-like area of regression.
