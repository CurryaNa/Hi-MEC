1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including shades of brown, black, and a prominent central reddish-violaceous area.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular in shape and ill-defined, with fuzzy and notched margins.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears relatively smooth but shows fine wrinkling and effacement of normal skin lines, especially over the central portion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a disorganized, multi-component pattern with peripheral blotches of dark pigment and a central, structureless reddish zone containing fine, whitish, scar-like streaks (regression structures).
