1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits variegation of color, including shades of light brown, dark brown, black, and areas of pinkish-red and whitish depigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, featuring notching and areas where the pigment fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is predominantly smooth, with effacement and distortion of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics), especially over the darker portions.
5. Structural Features: Key features include a multicomponent pattern with disorganized, blotchy pigmentation, a central dark blotch, and whitish areas consistent with structural regression.
