1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a central, homogenous, bright erythematous (red) area, surrounded by a distinct whitish-pink peripheral ring.
2. Border Appearance: The border is sharply demarcated and regular, forming a well-defined circular shape with a fine collarette of scale at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly symmetrical, exhibiting a circular or round morphology.
4. Surface Texture: The central erythematous surface appears smooth and possibly exophytic or moist, while the surrounding ring has a dry, scaly texture.
5. Structural Features: A key structural feature is a central, raised erythematous papule or nodule, which is characteristically encircled by a whitish collarette of scale.
