1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a dominant purplish-red (violaceous) and magenta coloration, with a central whitish-pink area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are fairly well-demarcated but irregular, exhibiting a slightly scalloped or lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is asymmetrical, particularly concerning the distribution of color and the irregularity of its border.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved, especially in the central portion.
5. Structural Features: Key features include multiple reddish-purple, well-defined globular structures (lacunae) and a central, semi-translucent whitish veil or fibrotic-like area.
