1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a polychromatic pattern with pink-red, light brown, grey-brown, and multiple distinct whitish circular areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending diffusely into the adjacent normal skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and in the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears uneven and slightly atrophic, with some areas of fine scale, particularly over the pigmented regions.
5. Structural Features: Key structures include numerous whitish, circular halos or rosettes around follicular openings, prominent linear and branching telangiectasias, and structureless brown-grey pigmented areas.
