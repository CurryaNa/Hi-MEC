1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including light to dark brown, black, pink, and scar-like white.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly defined, with pigment fading into the surrounding skin in a blotchy pattern.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular, with a combination of raised (papular) areas, atrophic (scarred) regions, and central crusting or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include extensive central regression with fibrosis, residual dark brown globules and dots, ulceration, and fine arborizing telangiectasias.
