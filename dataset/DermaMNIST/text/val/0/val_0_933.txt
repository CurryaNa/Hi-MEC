1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including pink-red erythema, pearly white areas, and light brown pigmentation at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and poorly demarcated, blending into the surrounding skin in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is uneven and rough, characterized by central crusting and scaling over what appears to be an eroded or ulcerated base.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include central ulceration, multiple shiny or pearly papules, and fine, short, linear telangiectatic vessels.
