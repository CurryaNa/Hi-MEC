1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including light brown, dark brown, and pinkish-white areas.
2. Border Appearance: The visible borders are ill-defined and irregular, showing a gradual fading of pigment into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both color distribution and overall structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is mamillated and uneven, creating a cobblestone or cerebriform (brain-like) appearance.
5. Structural Features: Distinctive features include numerous pale, rounded papules separated by pigmented furrows (sulci), forming a prominent gyri-and-sulci pattern.
