1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from light tan to dark brown, against a pinkish background.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and irregular, with pigment fading and streaking into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved and running through the pigmented area.
5. Structural Features: A disorganized pigment network with linear and radial streaks (pseudopods) is present, along with blotchy, non-uniform pigmentation.
