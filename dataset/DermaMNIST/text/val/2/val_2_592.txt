1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple shades, including light brown, dark brown, and focal whitish or skin-colored hypopigmented areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular in shape and indistinct, with a moth-eaten appearance where the pigment network fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with visible but focally disrupted or effaced skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Key features include a multicomponent pattern with an atypical, irregular, and broken pigment network, structureless brown blotches, and areas of regression.
