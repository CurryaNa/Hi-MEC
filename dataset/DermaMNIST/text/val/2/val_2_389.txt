1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors (polychromia), including light brown, dark brown, black, focal pink-red areas, and a central whitish-grey veil.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and indistinct, fading into the surrounding skin in several areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The central surface is characterized by scaling and fine crusting, indicating surface disruption.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include central ulceration, an asymmetric and disorganized multi-component pattern, and areas of structureless whitish-grey discoloration suggestive of regression.
