1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits color variegation, presenting multiple shades including light brown, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, with areas of notching and abrupt pigment cutoff.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is predominantly flat, with effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) in the more heavily pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include an atypical pigment network, peripheral streaks (pseudopods), and central, dark, structureless blotches.
