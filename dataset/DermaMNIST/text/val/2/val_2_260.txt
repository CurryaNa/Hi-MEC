1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with shades of light to dark brown and a prominent pinkish background.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, with a stellate or starburst pattern of pigmentation at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical with regard to both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is characterized by a mammillated or cobblestone texture, composed of numerous small, rounded papules.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include aggregated globules/papules and a patchy, reticular network of brown pigmentation overlying the cobblestoned surface.
