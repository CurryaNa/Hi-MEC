1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays a polychromatic pattern, with dominant pink and violaceous hues, along with whitish scar-like areas and subtle light brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is predominantly smooth, with focal areas of atrophy and a small central indentation suggesting early ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Distinctive features include fine, linear, and branching (arborizing) telangiectasias, shiny white structures (chrysalis structures), and areas of regression.
