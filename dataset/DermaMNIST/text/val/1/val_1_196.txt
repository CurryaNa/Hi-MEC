1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits polychromasia, with areas of pink, white, tan-brown, black, and a focal point of red.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, blending into the surrounding skin in some areas and showing abrupt, pigmented edges in others.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is varied, with a smooth, atrophic and slightly depressed central zone and a small area of focal erosion or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: The lesion presents with a central scar-like area of regression, peripherally scattered irregular brown/black globules, and atypical linear vessels.
