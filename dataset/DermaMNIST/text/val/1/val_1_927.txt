1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a predominant pink-red erythema, scattered black and blue-gray dots, and a central whitish, scar-like area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears scaly and crusted, with evidence of central erosion or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include fine, branching arborizing telangiectasias, multiple pigmented globules, and a central ulcerated region.
