1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors (polychromia), including light brown, dark brown, black, and a central whitish-grey area on a pink, erythematous background.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and stellate, with ill-defined, finger-like projections of pigment radiating outwards.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears to have central fine scaling or crusting over a slightly atrophic or depressed area.
5. Structural Features: Key structures include a central fibrotic or scar-like zone with peripheral streaks and globules of dark pigment in a starburst pattern, and prominent telangiectatic vessels in the surrounding skin.
