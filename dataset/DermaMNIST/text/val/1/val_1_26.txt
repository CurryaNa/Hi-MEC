1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple colors, including erythematous pink, red, and focal areas of tan-brown and dark brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, with a slightly raised, translucent, and rolled quality in some sections.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is partially shiny (pearly) and translucent, featuring a central area of ulceration with some fine crusting.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include prominent, branching arborizing telangiectasias, central ulceration, and irregularly distributed brown pigmented globules.
