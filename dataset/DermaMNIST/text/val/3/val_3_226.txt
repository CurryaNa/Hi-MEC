1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays a polychromatic pattern, with a prominent erythematous to violaceous peripheral rim and a central atrophic area that is whitish-yellow.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, gradually fading into the surrounding healthy skin without a distinct demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is asymmetrical and roughly ovoid, lacking any clear axis of symmetry.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is characterized by central atrophy, appearing thinned, shiny, and slightly depressed with a scarred texture.
5. Structural Features: There is significant central atrophy and fibrosis appearing as whitish, scar-like tissue. This is encircled by an erythematous, slightly raised border, and some fine, linear vessels (telangiectasias) are visible.
