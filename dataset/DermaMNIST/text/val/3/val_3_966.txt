1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a polychromatic pattern, with areas of pink-red, light brown, dark brown, and focal white.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, gradually fading into the adjacent normal skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, but is characterized by multiple small, atrophic or slightly depressed circular zones.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include multiple whitish, scar-like circular macules (regression structures), asymmetric blotches of brown pigment, and fine, irregular dotted and linear vessels.
