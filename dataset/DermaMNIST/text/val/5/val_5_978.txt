1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with color variegation, including shades of light brown, dark brown, and black, surrounded by an erythematous (red) halo.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, appearing blurred and fading into the adjacent skin, especially at the superior aspect.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of pigment within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears uneven with a slightly crusted or scaly texture, most prominently over the darkest pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Key features include blotchy, multi-component pigmentation, a more concentrated and possibly nodular area inferiorly, and smaller satellite pigmented macules.
