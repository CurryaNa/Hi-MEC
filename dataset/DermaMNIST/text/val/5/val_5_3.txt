1. Primary Colors: The lesion is multicolored, presenting with shades of light brown, dark brown, and a surrounding erythematous pink-red hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, featuring areas of fading and notching around the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of internal pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with some effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Key features include an atypical, disorganized pigment network with blotches of darker pigmentation and a subtle peripheral erythematous halo.
