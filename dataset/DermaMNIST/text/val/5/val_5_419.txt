1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, predominantly dark brown and black, with subtle areas of lighter brown at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular in shape and appears focally indistinct or notched, transitioning abruptly to the surrounding skin in most areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment within it.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and non-scaly, with the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) being effaced over the lesion.
5. Structural Features: A central, dark, structureless blotch is the most prominent feature, with a disorganized pigment pattern at the edges; multiple terminal hairs are observed emerging from the lesion's surface.
