1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light and dark brown, black, pink-red (erythematous), and a central whitish, hypopigmented zone.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and poorly demarcated, with areas of notching and fading into the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface texture is heterogeneous, featuring a central smooth, atrophic, and slightly depressed area, while the pigmented portions appear relatively flat.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include a central scar-like area suggestive of regression, surrounded by peripheral and scattered islands of atypical pigment network, dark brown globules, and irregular blotches.
