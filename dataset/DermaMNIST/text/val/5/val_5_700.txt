1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits a central area of light and dark brown pigmentation, surrounded by a prominent, uniform white or hypopigmented halo.
2. Border Appearance: The border of the central pigmented component is well-defined and regular, while the outer border of the halo is less distinct and blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall lesion, including the halo, is largely symmetrical and round-to-oval in shape; however, the internal pigmentation is slightly asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible across both the pigmented center and the surrounding halo.
5. Structural Features: The lesion is characterized by a central pigmented nevus surrounded by a distinct, symmetrical ring of depigmentation, a classic presentation of a halo nevus (Sutton's nevus).
