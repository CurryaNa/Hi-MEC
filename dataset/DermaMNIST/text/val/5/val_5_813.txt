1. Primary Colors: The lesion is polychromatic, exhibiting shades of dark brown, black, and a surrounding erythematous (red) halo.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, with a blotchy and indistinct transition into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the internal distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears crusted and dry, with fine white linear scaling or excoriation lines visible across the pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Key features include central, dark, structureless blotches and clods of pigment, all encompassed by an erythematous flare.
