1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a central black and dark brown area, transitioning to a lighter brown at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The border is highly irregular and spiculated, with sharp, well-demarcated streaks and pseudopods radiating outwards.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth, with skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible at the periphery but obscured centrally by dense pigmentation.
5. Structural Features: The lesion displays a classic starburst pattern, characterized by radial streaming and peripheral pseudopods emanating from a central, dark, homogeneous blotch.
