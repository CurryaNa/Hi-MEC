2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and indistinct, featuring a feathery or blurred transition into the surrounding skin.

5. Structural Features: A disorganized reticular (net-like) pattern is present at the periphery, while the central zone shows features of regression, including loss of pigment and scar-like changes.
