1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with a primary pinkish-yellow and whitish coloration, interspersed with small, focal brown specks.
2. Border Appearance: The border is well-demarcated from the surrounding scalp but is highly irregular and lobulated in shape.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is asymmetrical, composed of a cluster of variously sized, rounded nodules.
4. Surface Texture: The surface has a waxy, bumpy, and uneven texture, creating a cobblestone or cerebriform (brain-like) appearance.
5. Structural Features: The lesion is characterized by multiple, exophytic, translucent lobules grouped together, with some displaying small yellowish-brown comedo-like openings or keratinous plugs.
