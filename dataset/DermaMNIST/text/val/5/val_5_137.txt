1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with color variegation, including light brown, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and indistinct, with a blotchy and stippled periphery that fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both shape and in the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears macroscopically smooth, though dermoscopy reveals a granular texture due to pigment aggregates.
5. Structural Features: Key structural features include multiple globules and dots, particularly at the periphery, and central clods of dark, amorphous pigment.
