1. Primary Colors: The lesion displays multiple shades, including light brown, dark brown, and areas of black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and ill-defined, with spiculated (starburst-like) projections and areas where the pigment fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears predominantly smooth, with effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) across the pigmented areas.
5. Structural Features: Key features include an atypical pigment network, streaks and pseudopods at the periphery, and central, structureless blotches of dark pigment.
