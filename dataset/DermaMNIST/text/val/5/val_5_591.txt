1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits multiple colors (polychromasia), including a dark brown to black central area, a prominent pink to erythematous halo, and foci of yellowish-white crust.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion's borders are irregular and ill-defined, particularly where the erythematous component merges with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape and internal structures are markedly asymmetrical across all axes.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular and disrupted, characterized by central ulceration, adherent serosanguineous crusting, and peripheral fine scaling.
5. Structural Features: A central, darkly pigmented, and ulcerated papule is surrounded by an inflamed, erythematous zone with erosions and hemorrhagic crusts, indicating a chaotic and disorganized structure.
