1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown at the periphery and dark brown to black in the central and radiating areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and highly irregular, featuring poorly defined, feathery edges and starburst-like projections.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth and flat, without significant scale, crust, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Dermoscopic structures include an atypical pigment network that breaks up at the periphery, central blotches, and prominent radial streaks or pseudopods.
