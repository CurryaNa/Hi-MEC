1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, including a dark brown to black central zone and a lighter, reddish-brown periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, characterized by feathery, radiating streaks of pigment extending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution, with an irregular, non-circular outline.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and no signs of scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: The lesion displays a "starburst" pattern, with a central, darkly pigmented reticular or globular area and prominent radial streaming or pseudopods at the periphery.
