1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including a dark brown to near-black central zone and a lighter tan to reddish-brown periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, with a stippled or "peppery" edge that gradually fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in its overall outline and in the distribution of its color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears macular (flat) and smooth, without evidence of scaling, ulceration, or crusting.
5. Structural Features: A key feature is the peripheral pattern of pigmented globules and streaks radiating outwards from a more homogeneous central area, consistent with a starburst or spitzoid pattern.
