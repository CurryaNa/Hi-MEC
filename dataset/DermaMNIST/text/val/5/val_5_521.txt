1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and focal areas of black and grey-blue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and indistinct, featuring areas of abrupt cutoff and finger-like projections (pseudopods) extending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible but focally effaced or interrupted by the pigment.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include an atypical, reticular (net-like) pattern with thickened, irregular lines and variable mesh sizes, as well as structureless blotches and radial streaming at the periphery.
