1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with varying shades of brown, from light tan to a darker, more concentrated brown.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are highly irregular and poorly defined, appearing stippled or "peppery" with pigment fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The shape is markedly asymmetrical and irregular, lacking any discernible axis of symmetry.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and macular (flat), with the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics) visible throughout the lesion.
5. Structural Features: The lesion is composed of multiple discrete, punctate (dot-like) and globular macules of pigment that coalesce in some areas.
