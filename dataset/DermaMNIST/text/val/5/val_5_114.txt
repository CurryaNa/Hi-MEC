1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including a dark brown to black central area and lighter brown pigmentation at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular and ill-defined, exhibiting a starburst or spiculated pattern with streaks and pseudopods radiating into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears generally smooth and flat, with skin markings (dermatoglyphics) that are present but distorted over the lesion.
5. Structural Features: A central, dark, structureless blotch is present, from which an atypical, disorganized pigment network and peripheral streaks emanate.
