1. Primary Colors: The lesion predominantly displays shades of red and pink, with some areas showing a deeper, dark red or violaceous hue.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are ill-defined and irregular, featuring a feathery or stellate pattern where the vascular structures blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of its internal vascular patterns.
4. Surface Texture: The surface of the skin appears smooth and intact, without evidence of scaling, crusting, or ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Distinct, fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) are clearly visible, radiating outwards from a more concentrated central erythematous area.
