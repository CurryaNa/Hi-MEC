1. Primary Colors: The lesion exhibits polychromia, presenting with shades of light brown, dark brown, and pink-red.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are well-demarcated but highly irregular and scalloped in shape.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its contour and the distribution of internal structures and color.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular, featuring multiple smooth, raised, translucent papules or vesicles.
5. Structural Features: Distinct features include multiple, large, bubble-like translucent globules and a blotchy, non-uniform pigment pattern.
