1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including a pink-to-red erythematous background, light brown patches, and scattered dark brown to black dots.
2. Border Appearance: The overall border is irregular and poorly demarcated, blending into the surrounding skin, especially the erythematous portions.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is highly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the internal distribution of pigment and color.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) and vellus hairs visible; no scaling or crusting is observed.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include multiple, asymmetrically distributed dark brown dots and globules (peppering) over an erythematous and lightly pigmented base.
