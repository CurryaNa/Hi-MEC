1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades, including a dark brown to black central area and a lighter brown periphery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are indistinct and blurred, with an irregular contour that fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth, with partial effacement of the normal cutaneous markings (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: A central, dark, homogeneous blotch is present, with an irregular, reticular pattern and fading pigment at the periphery.
