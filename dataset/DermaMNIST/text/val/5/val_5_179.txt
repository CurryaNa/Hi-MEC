1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple shades of brown, ranging from a light brown at the periphery to a dark brown/black color concentrated centrally.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are irregular in shape and indistinct, with the pigment network fading out into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both its overall contour and the distribution of its internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and flat, with the preservation of fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) across the lesion.
5. Structural Features: A prominent atypical reticular (net-like) pigment network is observed, which is thicker and darker in the center and becomes finer and fades peripherally.
