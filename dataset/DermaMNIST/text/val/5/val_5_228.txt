1. Primary Colors: The lesion presents with multiple colors, including light brown, dark brown, and a central area of whitish-pink hypopigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and indistinct, characterized by radiating streaks and scattered dark globules at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is markedly asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of pigment.

5. Structural Features: The lesion displays a multicomponent pattern, featuring a central reticular network, a focal area of regression, and peripheral dots/globules and radial streaming.
