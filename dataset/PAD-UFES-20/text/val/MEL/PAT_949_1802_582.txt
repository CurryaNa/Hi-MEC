1. Primary Colors: Black, blue-gray, and dark brown, with a subtle peripheral violaceous (purplish) hue.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with some areas of pigment fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both contour and pigment distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Irregularly raised, nodular, and somewhat verrucous (wart-like) with areas of light crusting.
5. Structural Features: A papulonodular lesion characterized by significant multicoloration, a lobulated or cobblestone-like surface, and a darker, more elevated central component.
