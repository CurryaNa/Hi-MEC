1. Primary Colors: Reddish-brown (central) and chalk-white (peripheral).
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular; the outer border of the white halo is ovoid and distinct from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Highly symmetrical; the central pigmented lesion is centered within the surrounding depigmented halo.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, with preserved skin lines (dermatoglyphics) across both the central lesion and the white halo.
5. Structural Features: A central, uniformly pigmented papule is surrounded by a distinct, symmetrical halo of depigmentation, creating a classic targetoid or bull's-eye pattern.
