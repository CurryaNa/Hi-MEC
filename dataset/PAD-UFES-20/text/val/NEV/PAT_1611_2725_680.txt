1. Primary Colors: Uniform dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical; ovate to circular.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and cerebriform (brain-like), creating a bumpy, uneven surface.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic papule with a lobulated surface and a 'stuck-on' appearance.
