1. Primary Colors: Grayish-brown, dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, scalloped, and well-demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the plaques are arranged in an irregular, somewhat linear configuration.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (warty) and papillomatous, giving it a cobblestone-like or pebbly feel.
5. Structural Features: Lesion consists of multiple, elevated, coalescing papules that form irregular plaques.
