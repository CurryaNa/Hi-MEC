1. Primary Colors: Flesh-toned to pinkish-white.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular.
3. Shape Symmetry: The largest lesion is mildly asymmetrical, with a reniform (kidney-like) or ovoid shape.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and non-scaly, with a slightly waxy appearance.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, dome-shaped papule with a prominent central umbilication (indentation).
