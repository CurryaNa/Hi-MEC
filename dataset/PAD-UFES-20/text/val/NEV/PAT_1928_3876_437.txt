1. Primary Colors: Pink, flesh-toned, with scattered brown and dark brown-to-black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated but highly irregular and lobulated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Highly asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Multinodular and botryoid (grape-like), with a somewhat smooth and glistening surface on individual nodules.
5. Structural Features: An exophytic, cerebriform mass composed of multiple coalescing, variably sized papules and nodules. Coarse, dark hairs are present, emerging from the lesion. Pigmentation is heterogeneous, appearing as dark macules on the surface of nodules and within crevices.
