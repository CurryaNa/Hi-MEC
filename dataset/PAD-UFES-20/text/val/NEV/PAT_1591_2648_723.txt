1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, light brown, and a focal area of dark brown to black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and lobulated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous (cobblestone-like) and glistening, with some peripheral yellowish scale-crust.
5. Structural Features: Elevated (papular) lesion with multiple components, including polychromia and a distinct dark brown/black clod.
