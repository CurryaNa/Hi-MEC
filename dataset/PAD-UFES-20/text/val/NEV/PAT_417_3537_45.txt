1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with areas of pinkish-tan, violaceous-brown, and subtle whitish tones.
2. Border Appearance: Generally regular oval outline, but with indistinct and somewhat blurred margins that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is largely symmetrical in its overall oval shape, but internal color and texture are asymmetrically distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic and crinkled, with a slightly scaly surface and multiple small depressions.
5. Structural Features: A slightly raised papule with central dells or pits corresponding to follicular openings, and heterogeneous pigmentation. Hairs are seen growing through the lesion.
