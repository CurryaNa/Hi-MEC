1. Primary Colors: Black, dark brown, and light brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, with fading and satellite pigmented macules at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion has an elongated, irregular shape.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous and cerebriform (brain-like), particularly in the central, raised portion.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is present, with a central, heavily pigmented plaque showing a cobblestone-like surface. The periphery exhibits a flatter, reticular and globular pattern with color variegation.
