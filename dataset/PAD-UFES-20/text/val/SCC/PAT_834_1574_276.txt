1. Primary Colors: Whitish-gray, tan, and light brown, with subtle pink erythema at the base.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and irregular with a scalloped or lobulated edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like), hyperkeratotic, and craggy with a dry, "stuck-on" quality.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic papule composed of multiple aggregated lobules, with visible keratin plugging within surface fissures and crypts.
