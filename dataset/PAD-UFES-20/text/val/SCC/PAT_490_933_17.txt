1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-tan, erythematous (red), and white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted with an adherent, greasy-appearing scale-crust; surrounding skin is dry and finely scaly.
5. Structural Features: Raised erythematous papule with a prominent, central, keratotic, yellowish crust.
