1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), yellowish-tan, and dark brown to black specks.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat indistinct, with a pink halo that blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and slightly ulcerated, with a surrounding waxy or pearly appearance.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule with central umbilication (depression) filled with small, dark hemorrhagic crusts ("peppering") and a translucent quality to the surrounding rim.
