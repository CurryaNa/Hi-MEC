1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous) at the periphery with a central whitish-yellow core.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated with a regular, round border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Generally symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic, crusted, and centrally fissured.
