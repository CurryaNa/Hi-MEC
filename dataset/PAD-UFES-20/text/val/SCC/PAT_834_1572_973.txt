1. Primary Colors: Grayish-white, pink, red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and not sharply demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and hyperkeratotic (scaly/crusted).
5. Structural Features: A raised papule with a thick, adherent, grayish-white crust covering the majority of the lesion, with an adjacent focus of erythema and possible erosion at the superior pole.
