1. Primary Colors: Dark brown, black, and tan.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, with both well-demarcated and slightly indistinct or smudged margins.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the individual lesions are irregularly ovoid and the overall cluster lacks symmetry.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and flat, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved across the surface.
5. Structural Features: Multiple, discrete and coalescing, non-palpable (macular) pigmented lesions of varying sizes. Pigmentation is variegated. Hairs are observed growing through the lesions.
