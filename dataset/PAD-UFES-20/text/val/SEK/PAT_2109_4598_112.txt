1. Primary Colors: Tan, brown, and greyish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated from the surrounding skin with an irregular, lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of surface features.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (warty) and papillomatous, with a cerebriform (brain-like) and somewhat greasy appearance.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, raised lesion composed of multiple rounded lobules separated by deep fissures, giving it a characteristic "stuck-on" appearance.
