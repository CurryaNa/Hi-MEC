1. Primary Colors: Variegated brown (from tan to dark brown) and a fine, whitish-silver scale.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with pigment fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and scale.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, rough, and scaly (keratotic) with a fine, adherent scale.
5. Structural Features: A flat or minimally elevated lesion (macule/thin plaque) with central hyperpigmentation and a frosted or "stuck-on" appearance due to the overlying scale that accentuates the skin lines.
