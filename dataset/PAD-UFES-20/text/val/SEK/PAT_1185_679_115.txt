1. Primary Colors: Dark brown, gray-black, and a peripheral halo of light brown.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated but irregular and slightly indistinct in some areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and finely verrucous (wart-like), with a cerebriform (brain-like) pattern in the center.
5. Structural Features: A central, darker, slightly elevated papule surrounded by a flatter, lighter brown macular area, exhibiting color variegation.
