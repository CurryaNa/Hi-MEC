1. Primary Colors: Erythematous pink, violaceous (purple-blue), and dark gray/black.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with indistinct margins that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven and slightly rough, with some areas of fine whitish scale.
5. Structural Features: A multi-component pattern consisting of an erythematous patch with scattered, irregular, dot-like and clod-like foci of dark violaceous and black pigmentation.
