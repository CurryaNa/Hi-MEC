1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red, white, and hypopigmented pink.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and poorly demarcated, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and irregular in its distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly with fine, adherent, whitish scales.
5. Structural Features: Diffuse erythema, perifollicular scaling (scales accumulating around the base of hair shafts), and possible atrophic changes evidenced by skin shininess.
