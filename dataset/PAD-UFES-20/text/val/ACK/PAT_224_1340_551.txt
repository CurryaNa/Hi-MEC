1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), with a focal dark brown to black area and regions of white scale.
2. Border Appearance: The overall erythematous patch has ill-defined and irregular borders, blending with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is an irregular patch that follows the curvature of the ear's antihelix.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly (desquamative), with a focal area of firm hemorrhagic crust.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous patch with overlying fine scale and a superimposed, centrally located, crusted papule or small ulceration.
