1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), white, and light brown, with a focal dark red hemorrhagic crust.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular borders on individual lesions.
3. Shape Symmetry: The individual lesions are asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Varied; some lesions are atrophic and smooth, while one prominent lesion has central crusting.
5. Structural Features: Multiple scattered macules and papules on a background of sun-damaged skin. Several lesions exhibit central atrophy and hypopigmentation, creating a stellate (star-like) scar appearance. One lesion displays central erosion with crusting. Faint peripheral telangiectasias are visible on some lesions.
