1. Primary Colors: A combination of mottled light to dark brown, white/hypopigmented patches, and pinkish-red areas.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined; the lesion is a diffuse field of change that merges indistinctly with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the distribution of pigmentary and vascular changes is irregular.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic, with fine wrinkling and some slightly scaly areas over a background of thinned skin.
5. Structural Features: A combination of mottled hyperpigmentation (lentigines) and hypopigmentation, skin atrophy, and visible fine, linear red blood vessels (telangiectasias).
