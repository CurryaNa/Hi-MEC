1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, violaceous, and white/silvery.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted with a central area of erosion.
5. Structural Features: An eroded plaque with adherent, whitish hyperkeratotic scale at the periphery and a central erythematous, ulcerated base. A violaceous hue is present at the superior aspect of the lesion.
