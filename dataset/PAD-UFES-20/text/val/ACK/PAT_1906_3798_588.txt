1. Primary Colors: Light brown, white, and a focal greyish-white nodule.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and fairly well-demarcated, though slightly indistinct in some segments.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, scaly, and crusted with fine, reticulated fissures giving a verrucous (warty) appearance.
5. Structural Features: A slightly elevated plaque with a cerebriform (brain-like) surface pattern and a distinct, exophytic, greyish-white papule on the left aspect.
