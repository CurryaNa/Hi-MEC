1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), pearly white, and a focal yellowish-white central area.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and slightly waxy or pearly at the periphery, with a centrally crusted and slightly eroded surface.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule with a slightly rolled border, central ulceration or erosion, and fine, branching telangiectasias (small blood vessels) visible on the surface. Hairs are seen emerging from within and around the lesion.
