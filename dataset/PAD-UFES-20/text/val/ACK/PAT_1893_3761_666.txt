1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink) base with a central, prominent white to yellowish-white area.
2. Border Appearance: The borders of the underlying papule are relatively well-demarcated but slightly irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly symmetrical in its overall round shape, though the central white material is asymmetrically distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic, scaly, and crusted centrally; the surface is dry and rough.
5. Structural Features: A central, exophytic, horn-like keratotic plug or scale-crust is the dominant feature, arising from a small papular base.
