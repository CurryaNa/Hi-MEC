1. Primary Colors: Multiple shades of brown (tan to dark brown), pinkish-red, and areas of hypopigmentation (white).
2. Border Appearance: The area contains multiple lesions; some hyperpigmented macules have relatively distinct but irregular borders, while the pinkish papule and background mottling have ill-defined, blurred borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall pattern is asymmetrical, and individual lesions within the field are also asymmetrical and irregular in shape.
4. Surface Texture: Heterogeneous; predominantly smooth and flat (macular) with some slightly raised (papular) areas and a background of atrophic, finely wrinkled skin.
5. Structural Features: A field of dermatoheliosis characterized by multiple hyperpigmented macules (solar lentigines), at least one pinkish, erythematous papule, and scattered areas of hypopigmentation and atrophy.
