1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), white (hypopigmented), and yellowish-tan.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Varied; predominantly smooth, shiny, and atrophic (thin and wrinkled) with some slightly raised, roughened erythematous patches.
5. Structural Features: Prominent atrophy, fine, thread-like telangiectasias (visible blood vessels), and patchy erythema. The lesion is primarily a polychromatic patch with no significant scaling or crusting.
