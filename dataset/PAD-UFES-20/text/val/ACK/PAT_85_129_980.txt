1. Primary Colors: Skin-colored to pinkish-brown at the base, with a yellowish-white to tan keratotic projection.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming a circular interface with the surrounding skin at its base.
3. Shape Symmetry: Radially symmetrical, exhibiting a conical or cylindrical shape.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic, dry, and rough, with a crusted and compacted appearance.
5. Structural Features: A solitary, exophytic, horn-like projection (cornu cutaneum) composed of compacted keratin, arising from a firm papular base.
