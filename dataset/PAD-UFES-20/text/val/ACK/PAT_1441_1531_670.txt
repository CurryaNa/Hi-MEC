1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (reddish) and a central whitish-yellow area.
2. Border Appearance: The border is somewhat ill-defined and slightly irregular, with the erythema fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is largely symmetrical in its roundish overall shape, but asymmetrical in color and structure due to the off-center crust.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and crusted centrally, with a rough, scaly appearance.
5. Structural Features: A raised erythematous papule with a central, adherent, keratotic scale-crust.
