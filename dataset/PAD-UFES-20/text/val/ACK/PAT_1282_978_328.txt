1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish-yellow, and a focal area of dark brown-to-black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: Ulcerated and crusted, with adherent serosanguinous (yellowish and dark) crust.
5. Structural Features: A central erosion or ulceration is the main feature, covered by a multi-colored crust. The lesion is situated on a background of sun-damaged skin with mottled pigmentation.
