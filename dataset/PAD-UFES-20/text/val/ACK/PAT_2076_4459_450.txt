1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), with some lighter, skin-toned areas creating a mottled appearance.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and indistinct, gradually blending into the surrounding skin with an irregular outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of erythema.
4. Surface Texture: Slightly rough with very fine, subtle scaling; skin lines (dermatoglyphics) are partially preserved but somewhat effaced.
5. Structural Features: The lesion appears as a patch (flat or minimally raised) with non-uniform erythema and scattered, tiny, pinpoint reddish-brown dots (puncta).
