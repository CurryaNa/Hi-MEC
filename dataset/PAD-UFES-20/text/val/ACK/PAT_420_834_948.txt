1. Primary Colors: Pink, brown-black, and whitish.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and mostly regular in its overall circular shape, with a slightly lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its overall shape, but with asymmetrically distributed surface features.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and papillomatous, with focal areas of hemorrhagic crusting and ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic papule with a lobulated surface, a distinct area of ulceration, and multiple dark brown to black dots consistent with thrombosed capillaries or hemorrhagic crusts.
