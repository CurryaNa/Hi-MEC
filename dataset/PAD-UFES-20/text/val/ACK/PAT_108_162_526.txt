1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-white, translucent white, with minute black specks.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion's base is well-defined and circumscribed; the overall contour is irregular and lobulated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous and hyperkeratotic, with a crusted and scaly appearance, particularly on the distal aspect.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, pedunculated papule with filiform (finger-like) projections. Small black dots, suggestive of thrombosed capillaries, are visible within the whitish, crusted areas.
