1. Primary Colors: White, pink/erythematous.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with fine, feathery scaling at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic and scaly, with thick, adherent, white scales that appear dry.
5. Structural Features: A central plaque of thick, white hyperkeratosis overlying an erythematous base, with a small central brownish crust or erosion visible within the scale.
