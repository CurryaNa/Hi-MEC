1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), whitish-yellow, and light brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with indistinct margins that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and the distribution of color and vascular patterns.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth with evidence of slight atrophy; no significant scale, crust, or ulceration is visible.
5. Structural Features: Prominent, fine, arborizing (branching) telangiectasias are present across the surface, along with mottled dyspigmentation (both hypo- and hyperpigmentation) and patchy erythema.
