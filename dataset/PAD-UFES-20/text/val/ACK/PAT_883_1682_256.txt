1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, pink, reddish-brown, and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and indistinct, blending into the surrounding vermilion border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular and crusted, with some scaly and nodular areas.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic (outward-growing) papulonodule with central ulceration, adherent hemorrhagic crust, and a slightly translucent, pearly quality on the superior aspect.
