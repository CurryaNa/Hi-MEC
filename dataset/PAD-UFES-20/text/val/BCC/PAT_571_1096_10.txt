1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish-yellow, and dark grey-to-black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with a surrounding erythematous halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted and scaly; appears rough and uneven.
5. Structural Features: An elevated (papulonodular) lesion with central ulceration covered by a thick, adherent, multi-colored crust (hemorrhagic and keratinous).
