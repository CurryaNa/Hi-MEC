1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, with areas of white-to-yellow scale and dark brown/black crusting.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, with some sections appearing slightly raised or rolled.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
