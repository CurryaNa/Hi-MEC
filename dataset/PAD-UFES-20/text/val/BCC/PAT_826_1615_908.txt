1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish, and focal light brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Heterogeneous, with areas of fine, whitish scale and other areas appearing smooth and slightly atrophic.
5. Structural Features: Prominent telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels), diffuse erythema, and possible slight atrophy in the central, lighter-colored region.
