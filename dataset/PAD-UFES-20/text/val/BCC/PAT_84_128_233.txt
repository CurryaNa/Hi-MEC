1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), translucent pearly-white, and a focal area of bright red.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated, raised, and rolled with a slightly irregular, scalloped outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated and moist with minimal crusting; the peripheral border appears smooth with a waxy or pearly sheen.
5. Structural Features: A central depression consistent with ulceration, surrounded by a raised, translucent, papulonodular border. Fine, arborizing telangiectasias may be present on the rolled edge.
