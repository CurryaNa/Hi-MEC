1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), violaceous (purplish), and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and arcuate (curved), with a well-demarcated, raised border that blends into surrounding erythema.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted, with a fine, adherent, whitish scale particularly on the border.
5. Structural Features: A plaque with a raised, annular (ring-like) border and a slightly depressed or atrophic center. The lesion is situated in a hair-bearing area.
