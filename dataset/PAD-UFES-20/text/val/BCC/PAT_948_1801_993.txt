1. Primary Colors: Black, dark brown, and peripheral erythema (redness) with some yellowish crust.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a slightly raised, rolled quality.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is roughly oval but not symmetrical across any axis.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted, rough, and firm-appearing.
5. Structural Features: An elevated nodule featuring a central, dark, hemorrhagic/necrotic crust. A small area of ulceration is visible superiorly, and there is a surrounding erythematous halo.
