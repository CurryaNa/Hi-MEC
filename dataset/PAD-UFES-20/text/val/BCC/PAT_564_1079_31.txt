1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous, with focal dark brown to black hemorrhagic crusting.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined borders, particularly along the superior and inferior aspects.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical along both the long and short axes.
4. Surface Texture: Glistening, translucent, and uneven, with areas of ulceration and crusting.
5. Structural Features: An elevated, lobulated (composed of multiple small nodules) lesion with a pearly, waxy appearance. There are multiple foci of ulceration with associated hemorrhagic crusts. Fine telangiectasias are visible on the translucent surface.
