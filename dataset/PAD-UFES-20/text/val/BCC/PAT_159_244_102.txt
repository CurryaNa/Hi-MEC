1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, and a central yellowish-white atrophic area.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with some areas blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic and slightly waxy, with areas of superficial erosion and a smooth, translucent quality in other parts.
5. Structural Features: Arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels) are visible, particularly on the periphery. There is central atrophy and a small focus of ulceration. The overall appearance has a subtle pearly or translucent quality.
