1. Primary Colors: Pearly white, translucent, and erythematous (pink-red).
2. Border Appearance: Poorly demarcated with a slightly raised, pearly, and rolled border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Generally symmetrical, appearing as a roughly circular papule.
4. Surface Texture: Waxy and pearly, with a non-scaly, slightly uneven surface due to a central depression.
5. Structural Features: Prominent arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels) are visible across the surface. The lesion has a translucent quality and exhibits central umbilication (indentation).
