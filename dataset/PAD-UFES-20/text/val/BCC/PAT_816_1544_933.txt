1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), translucent pearly-white, and flesh-toned.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat indistinct, with a slightly raised, rolled quality.
3. Shape Symmetry: Generally symmetrical in its overall ovoid shape.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and waxy with a pearlescent sheen.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule with a translucent periphery, central erythema, and a minute central erosion or ulceration. Fine, delicate blood vessels (telangiectasias) are suggested.
