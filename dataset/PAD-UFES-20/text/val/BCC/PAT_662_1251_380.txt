1. Primary Colors: Black, dark brown, dark red, and peripheral erythema (pink).
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly at the inferior pole.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Exudative (glistening) and heavily crusted, with matted hairs embedded on the surface.
5. Structural Features: A central, raised, dark, necrotic-appearing eschar or thick hemorrhagic crust is the dominant feature. The lesion is surrounded by an erythematous halo and shows signs of erosion.
