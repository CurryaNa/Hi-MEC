1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, with central yellowish serous exudate and black/dark brown hemorrhagic crusts.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with a slightly raised or rolled translucent edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Glistening and moist with central crusting and ulceration.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration, a pearly and slightly elevated border, and prominent telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels) on the surrounding skin and at the lesion's periphery.
