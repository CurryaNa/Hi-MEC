1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-white, and tan-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Rough, scaly, and crusted (hyperkeratotic).
5. Structural Features: An erythematous base covered by thick, adherent, yellowish-white scale and crust; the lesion appears slightly elevated.
