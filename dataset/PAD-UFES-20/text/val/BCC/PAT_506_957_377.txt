1. Primary Colors: Erythematous pink and red, with focal areas of white and a central dark brown/black crust.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Heterogeneous, with areas of scaling (hyperkeratosis), crusting, and erosion.
