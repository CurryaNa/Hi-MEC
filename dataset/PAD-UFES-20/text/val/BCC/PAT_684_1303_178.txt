1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellow-green, white, and focal dark red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted, scaly, and verrucous (wart-like).
5. Structural Features: A central, thick, adherent, multi-colored crust overlying an eroded base with focal hemorrhage; the lesion is a slightly elevated plaque.
