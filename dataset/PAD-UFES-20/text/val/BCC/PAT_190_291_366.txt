1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, and focal areas of hypopigmentation (whitish) on a background of tan skin.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with margins that are blurred and blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Moist, with areas of erosion and hemorrhagic crusting.
5. Structural Features: Multiple foci of ulceration and erosion, some of which appear as open sores with active bleeding. The central area is erythematous and appears slightly atrophic. The surrounding skin shows mottled pigmentation.
