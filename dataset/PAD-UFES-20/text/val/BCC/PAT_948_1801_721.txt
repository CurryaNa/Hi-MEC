1. Primary Colors: Black, dark brown, white-grey, and peripheral pink-red (erythema).
2. Border Appearance: The lesion has a raised, rolled, and somewhat irregular border that is not sharply demarcated from the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in shape, color, and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated with a firm, adherent necrotic crust (eschar); the peripheral rim exhibits a fine, whitish scale-crust.
5. Structural Features: A distinct crateriform nodule featuring central umbilication filled with a dark, keratinaceous/necrotic plug and a raised, firm, "volcano-like" border.
