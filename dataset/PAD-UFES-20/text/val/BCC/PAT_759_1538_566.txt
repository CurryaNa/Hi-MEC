1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red (erythematous), translucent pearly-white.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-defined but slightly indistinct, with a regular, round to oval outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and waxy, non-scaly.
5. Structural Features: Raised (papular) lesion with a pearly, translucent quality, prominent arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels), and a small, whitish, milia-like cyst.
