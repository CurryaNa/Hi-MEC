1. Primary Colors: Pink, translucent white, blue-grey, and yellow-white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated with a surrounding smooth, pearly surface.
5. Structural Features: Raised, rolled border, foci of pigmentation, and arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels).
