1. Primary Colors: Pink, violaceous, with a central reddish-brown area and translucent whitish/pearly regions.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly at the superior and inferior margins.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in terms of color, structure, and border definition.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth with a waxy or pearly sheen, especially over the raised portions.
5. Structural Features: A raised, papulonodular lesion with a translucent, rolled-appearing border and a central area of depression or early ulceration. Fine, thread-like vessels (telangiectasias) may be present on the surface.
