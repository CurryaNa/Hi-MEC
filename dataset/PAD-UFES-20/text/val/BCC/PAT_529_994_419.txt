1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, with a central area of deeper red, and small brown/black foci.
2. Border Appearance: The border of the central ulceration is well-demarcated but irregular in shape. The surrounding erythema has a more diffuse, ill-defined border.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: The central area is moist, ulcerated, and partially crusted. The surrounding skin appears shiny and taut.
5. Structural Features: There is a central, crater-like ulceration with a fleshy, erythematous base. The rim of the ulcer appears slightly raised or rolled. The surrounding skin is erythematous and appears edematous.
