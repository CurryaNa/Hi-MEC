1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red) and translucent white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with a scalloped or lobulated contour.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Glistening, smooth, and papular (composed of small bumps).
5. Structural Features: Multiple fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) are present on the surface. The lesion exhibits small, coalescing, pearly or translucent papules, particularly at the periphery, suggesting a rolled border.
