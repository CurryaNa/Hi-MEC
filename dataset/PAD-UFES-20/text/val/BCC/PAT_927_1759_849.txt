1. Primary Colors: Pearly white, violaceous-red, and central brown crust.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and partially ill-defined, with a distinct raised, rolled border on the superior aspect.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in shape, color, and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed; the superior portion is smooth and waxy, while the center is crusted and eroded.
5. Structural Features: A translucent, pearly nodule with a central ulceration and a prominent, rolled border. A fine, hair-like structure or telangiectasia is visible superior to the central ulceration.
