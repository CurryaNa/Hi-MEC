1. Primary Colors: Red, pink, violaceous.
2. Border Appearance: The central papule has a well-demarcated, regular, circular border, surrounded by an ill-defined, erythematous halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: Symmetrical; the lesion is circular.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, dome-shaped, and glistening.
5. Structural Features: A raised, translucent, dome-shaped papule with fine, arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels) on the surface.
