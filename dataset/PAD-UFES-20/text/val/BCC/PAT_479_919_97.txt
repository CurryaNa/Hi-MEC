1. Primary Colors: Violaceous (purple), erythematous (red), and pink, with scattered black specks.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with the color fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed; the violaceous area appears smooth while the red area is eroded, moist, and has fine crusting.
5. Structural Features: A focal erosion is present on the right side of the lesion. The lesion exhibits polychromia (multiple colors). Superimposed blue-purple linear markings, consistent with surgical ink, are visible along skin creases.
