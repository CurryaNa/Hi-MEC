1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish-translucent, dark red, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined; the outer erythematous halo blends into the surrounding skin, while the inner border of the ulceration is distinct but jagged.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, particularly in the distribution of the central ulceration and crusting.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated and crusted; the surrounding rim appears somewhat shiny and smooth.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration filled with hemorrhagic crust. The ulcer is encircled by a raised, rolled, and translucent (pearly) border. The entire lesion is surrounded by an erythematous halo.
