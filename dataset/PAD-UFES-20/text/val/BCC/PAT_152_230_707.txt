1. Primary Colors: Translucent erythematous (red-pink) center with a surrounding zone of erythema on tan skin.
2. Border Appearance: The central dome-shaped lesion has a sharply demarcated, regular, and circular border; the surrounding erythematous patch has ill-defined borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: The central lesion is symmetrical and round.
4. Surface Texture: Glistening, smooth, and taut.
5. Structural Features: A solitary, translucent, dome-shaped, bulla-like nodule with slight central umbilication, arising from an erythematous plaque.
