1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous-red, and pearly-white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a slightly raised, rolled appearance on the superior aspect.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Slightly scaly and waxy, with small, whitish, keratotic-appearing foci.
5. Structural Features: Raised papule with a subtle central depression and possible early ulceration or crusting.
