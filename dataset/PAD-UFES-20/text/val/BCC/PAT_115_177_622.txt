1. Primary Colors: Erythematous pink, bright red (hemorrhage), whitish/pearly, and dark brown/black.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Ulcerated, crusted (hemorrhagic), and friable.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration and crusting, surrounding pearly/translucent areas, and prominent, irregular telangiectasias (arborizing vessels). There are multiple points of active bleeding.
