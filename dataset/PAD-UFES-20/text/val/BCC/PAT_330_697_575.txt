1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) with central dark red to black hemorrhagic crusting.
2. Border Appearance: Poorly demarcated and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated, with a surrounding slightly raised, smooth-to-pearly rim.
