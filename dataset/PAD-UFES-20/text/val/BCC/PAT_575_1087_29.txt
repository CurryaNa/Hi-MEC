1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), black, and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, raised, and rolled with a pearly, translucent quality.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated with a rough, hemorrhagic crust; the peripheral border is smooth and waxy.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration covered by a dark crust, a raised and rolled border, and arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels) on the surface.
