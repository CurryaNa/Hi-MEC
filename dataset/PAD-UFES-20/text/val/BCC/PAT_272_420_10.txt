1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellow-brown, dark brown, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and verrucous; peripherally scaly.
5. Structural Features: A central, thick, adherent, yellowish-brown and hemorrhagic crust overlying an ulcerated base, surrounded by an erythematous, slightly indurated plaque with peripheral white scale.
