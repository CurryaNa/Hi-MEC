1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with areas of light brown, reddish-brown, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with notched and blurred margins that fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both contour and pigment distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Appears generally smooth, with some effacement of the normal skin lines, particularly in the darker areas.
5. Structural Features: Multicomponent pattern with heterogeneous pigmentation, including a lighter macular area and a darker, possibly nodular, component with dark globules and dots.
