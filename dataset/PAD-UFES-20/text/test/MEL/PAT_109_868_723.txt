1. Primary Colors: Variegated shades of brown and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with areas of notching and fading.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: Dry with accentuated skin lines (dermatoglyphics) creating a furrowed appearance.
5. Structural Features: Heterogeneous pigmentation with a multicomponent pattern, featuring darker brown to black areas interspersed with lighter brown zones.
