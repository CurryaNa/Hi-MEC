1. Primary Colors: Black, dark brown, light brown, and pinkish-white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, with scalloped and notched edges; some areas are ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and wrinkled, with exaggerated skin lines (dermatoglyphics) across the surface.
5. Structural Features: A large, central, black patch, surrounded by variegated brown pigmentation, and a superior pinkish-white area suggestive of regression.
