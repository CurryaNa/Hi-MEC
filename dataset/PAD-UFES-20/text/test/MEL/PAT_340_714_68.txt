1. Primary Colors: Black, blue-grey, and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and notched, with a multi-lobulated, sharply demarcated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and papillomatous, with a cerebriform (brain-like) and slightly bristly surface.
5. Structural Features: Raised (exophytic) lesion composed of multiple lobules, exhibiting heterogeneous pigmentation, and featuring comedo-like openings or keratin-filled pits.
