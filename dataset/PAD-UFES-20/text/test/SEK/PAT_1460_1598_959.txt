1. Primary Colors: Light brown, grey-black, and whitish-yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and fairly well-demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in shape and pattern.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (warty) and keratotic with a crusted, "stuck-on" appearance.
5. Structural Features: Multiple whitish-yellow keratotic globules (milia-like cysts) and comedo-like openings are visible on the surface, creating a cerebriform pattern.
