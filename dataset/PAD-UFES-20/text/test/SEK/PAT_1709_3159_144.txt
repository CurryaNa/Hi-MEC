1. Primary Colors: Tan, light brown, grayish-brown, and skin-colored.
2. Border Appearance: Generally well-demarcated but not sharply defined, with rounded to slightly irregular outlines.
3. Shape Symmetry: The individual papules are largely symmetrical, appearing round to oval.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and verrucous (warty) with a fine, fissured, and cerebriform (brain-like) surface, giving a "stuck-on" appearance.
5. Structural Features: Multiple, slightly elevated papules of varying sizes, some appearing to coalesce. The surface has a dull, waxy quality with exaggerated skin markings (dermatoglyphics).
