1. Primary Colors: Whitish-tan, light brown, with black specks.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its round to oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like), crusted, and keratotic.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic papule with a "stuck-on" appearance, featuring small pits and dark keratotic plugs (comedo-like openings).
