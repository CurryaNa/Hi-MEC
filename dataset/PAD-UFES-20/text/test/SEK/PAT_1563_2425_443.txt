1. Primary Colors: Dark brown, black, and grey.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular with an ovoid contour.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (warty) and cerebriform (brain-like) with a dry, fissured surface.
5. Structural Features: Raised (papular) with multiple furrows and ridges, giving it a "stuck-on" appearance, and exhibiting comedo-like openings (keratin plugs).
