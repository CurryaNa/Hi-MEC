1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with areas of light brown, dark brown, black, pinkish-red, and a distinct white/hypopigmented patch.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with some sections fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both contour and internal color/structure distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Mostly smooth and flat (macular), with partial effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Features of regression are present as a white, scar-like area; there are multiple dark brown/black dots and globules (peppering); and structureless blotches of pinkish-brown pigment are visible centrally.
