1. Primary Colors: Translucent skin-tone, pearly-white, brown, and focal black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, composed of coalescing papules creating a scalloped periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: Lobulated and waxy, with a pearly sheen and areas of superficial crusting.
5. Structural Features: A multi-lobulated plaque composed of clustered, translucent papules. There are distinct foci of dark brown/black pigmentation and a small central crusted area, suggestive of ulceration.
