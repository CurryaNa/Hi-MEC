1. Primary Colors: Black, dark brown, with some grayish-white areas.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat scalloped, generally well-demarcated but not sharply defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both contour and distribution of color.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and papillomatous, appearing rough, dry, and slightly scaly.
5. Structural Features: Raised (papular) lesion with a cerebriform (brain-like) or cobblestone surface pattern, dark pigmentation, and foci of gray-white hyperkeratosis (scaling).
