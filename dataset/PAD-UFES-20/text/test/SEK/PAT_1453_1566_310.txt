1. Primary Colors: Variegated shades of brown, including light brown, tan, and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, with some areas appearing blurred and others having stellate or jagged projections.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and slightly scaly, with some effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: Flat (macular) lesion with a mottled, non-uniform pigment pattern and a geographic, star-like outline.
