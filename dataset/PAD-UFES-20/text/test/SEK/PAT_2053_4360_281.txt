1. Primary Colors: Brown, black, pink, and whitish-grey.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and indistinct, particularly where the two components of the lesion meet.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, composed of at least two distinct morphological parts.
4. Surface Texture: Rough and scaly, with adherent, dry, whitish scale (hyperkeratosis) on the larger component and a crusted, verrucous texture on the smaller, darker component.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent lesion featuring a lower, flatter, scaly plaque adjacent to a more raised, pigmented, and nodular component with variegated coloration.
