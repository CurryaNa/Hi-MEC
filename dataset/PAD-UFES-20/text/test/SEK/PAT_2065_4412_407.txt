1. Primary Colors: Whitish-yellow, pearly.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated with an irregular, slightly scalloped outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like), scaly, and dry with a "stuck-on" appearance.
5. Structural Features: Raised papule with a cerebriform (brain-like) surface composed of multiple small lobules and fissures, with evidence of keratin plugging within the crevices.
