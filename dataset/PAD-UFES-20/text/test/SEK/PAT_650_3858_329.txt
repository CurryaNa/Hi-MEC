1. Primary Colors: Grey-brown, tan, and off-white.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, appearing sharply defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical with a round to oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and hyperkeratotic, with a dry, fissured, and crumbly surface.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic papule with a cerebriform (brain-like) surface, prominent furrows, and a "stuck-on" appearance.
