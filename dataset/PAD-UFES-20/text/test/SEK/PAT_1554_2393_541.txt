1. Primary Colors: Brown, gray, and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like), hyperkeratotic, and crusted.
5. Structural Features: Raised, exophytic papule with a cerebriform surface and a "stuck-on" appearance.
