1. Primary Colors: Dark brown to black, with some violaceous (purplish) and erythematous (red) undertones.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and distinct, with generally round to oval but slightly irregular margins.
3. Shape Symmetry: The individual papules are largely symmetrical in their overall shape.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (warty) and mammillated (bumpy), with a dry, slightly crusted, "stuck-on" appearance.
5. Structural Features: Multiple discrete, exophytic (raised) papules. Some lesions exhibit a cerebriform (brain-like) surface pattern and comedo-like openings (horn cysts). One lesion shows a small area of hemorrhagic crusting.
