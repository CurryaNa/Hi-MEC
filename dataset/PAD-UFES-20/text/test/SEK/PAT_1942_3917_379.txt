1. Primary Colors: Dark brown and black.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming a distinct oval shape.
3. Shape Symmetry: Symmetrical along its longitudinal and transverse axes.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and cerebriform (brain-like), with multiple small lobules, fissures, and sulci.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic (raised) papule with a mammillated surface; some dark, comedo-like openings are visible within the crevices.
