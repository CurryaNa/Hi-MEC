1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with shades of grey-brown, dark brown, and black, containing multiple whitish-yellow specks.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat lobulated, appearing sharply demarcated in most areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and uneven, with areas of scale and crust.
5. Structural Features: Elevated papule/plaque with a cerebriform (brain-like) surface, exhibiting keratin-filled pits (comedo-like openings) and horn cysts, giving it a "stuck-on" appearance.
