1. Primary Colors: Brown, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Sharply demarcated and well-circumscribed with regular, round to oval borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: Generally symmetrical, presenting as round or oval shapes.
4. Surface Texture: Waxy, slightly verrucous (wart-like), and lobulated.
5. Structural Features: Multiple exophytic (raised) papules and nodules of varying sizes with a characteristic "stuck-on" appearance.
