1. Primary Colors: Brown, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Generally well-demarcated with irregular, scalloped borders, creating a "stuck-on" appearance.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like), dry, and scaly.
5. Structural Features: Multiple pigmented papules and plaques of various sizes, some with a cerebriform (brain-like) surface and visible comedo-like openings or horn pseudocysts.
