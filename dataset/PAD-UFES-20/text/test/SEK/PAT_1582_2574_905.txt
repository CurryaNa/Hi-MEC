1. Primary Colors: Dark brown, black.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated but irregular with lobulated contours.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and papillomatous, with a cerebriform (brain-like) pattern.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic (raised) papule with a "stuck-on" quality, featuring fissures and multiple small, whitish milia-like cysts (horn pseudocysts).
