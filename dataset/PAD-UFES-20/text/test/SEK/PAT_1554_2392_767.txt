1. Primary Colors: Whitish-grey, light brown, and skin-tone.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated at the base; the overall perimeter is highly irregular and lobulated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Rough, dry, hyperkeratotic, and verrucous (wart-like).
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, pedunculated papule with a papillomatous (cauliflower-like) surface composed of multiple finger-like projections. Hairs are seen emerging from the surrounding skin.
