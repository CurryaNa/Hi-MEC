1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with shades of light brown, dark brown, and gray-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat notched, but relatively well-demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and uneven, with a cerebriform (brain-like) topography.
5. Structural Features: A raised (nodular) lesion with multiple lobules, fissures, and clefts. There are foci of darker, more concentrated pigmentation throughout the lesion.
