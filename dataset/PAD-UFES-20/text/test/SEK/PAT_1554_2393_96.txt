1. Primary Colors: Dark grey, black, and white, with a subtle violaceous hue at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined in areas, though generally demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like), hyperkeratotic, and crusted.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic (outward-growing) papule with a central, heavily crusted, and keratotic core and multiple areas of pigmentation.
