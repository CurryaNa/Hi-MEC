1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, tan, various shades of brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like), crusted, and focally waxy.
5. Structural Features: A raised plaque composed of coalescing, somewhat translucent papules with areas of dark pigmentation and overlying crust.
