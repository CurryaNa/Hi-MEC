1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) with areas of white and translucent scale.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, blending into the surrounding sun-damaged skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and texture.
4. Surface Texture: Thin, finely scaly, and atrophic with a slightly waxy or pearly sheen in some areas.
5. Structural Features: A flat to slightly raised plaque with diffuse erythema and surface scale; skin markings are partially effaced.
