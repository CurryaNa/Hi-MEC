1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, white, and yellowish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with blurring and effacement of the vermilion border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Heterogeneous, with areas of crusting, scaling, and erosion.
5. Structural Features: Multiple shallow erosions and ulcerations, some covered by hemorrhagic crusts, interspersed with whitish, atrophic, and scaly patches.
