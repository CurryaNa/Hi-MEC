1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), yellowish-green, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular and crusted, with areas of moist, sero-purulent exudate and darker, hemorrhagic crust (eschar).
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration covered by a polymorphic crust, surrounded by an erythematous halo. Some fine telangiectasias are visible on the adjacent skin.
