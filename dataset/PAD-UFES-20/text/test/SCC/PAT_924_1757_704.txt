1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), brown, and yellowish-white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined due to a prominent peripheral scale.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted and hyperkeratotic, with a notable adherent white scale surrounding the lesion.
5. Structural Features: A centrally raised papule or nodule with features of ulceration covered by a hemorrhagic and serous crust. A distinct collarette of scale is present at the base.
