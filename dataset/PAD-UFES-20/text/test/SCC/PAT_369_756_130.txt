1. Primary Colors: Whitish-yellow, pink-red (erythematous), with subtle tan/brown areas.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated at the base, with the exophytic component having a jagged, uneven edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic, verrucous (wart-like), and crusted; appears dry and rough.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic (outward growing) nodule with a prominent, firm-appearing, irregular, central keratotic horn/crust. The base of the lesion is erythematous.
