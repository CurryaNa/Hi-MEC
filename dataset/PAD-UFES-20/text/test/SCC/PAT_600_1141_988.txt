1. Primary Colors: Reddish-pink (erythematous), brownish-grey, and yellowish-tan.
2. Border Appearance: Well-defined and regular, with a raised, rolled character.
3. Shape Symmetry: Symmetrical; the lesion is approximately circular.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated and covered with a firm, multi-colored crust; the peripheral border is smooth and appears translucent or pearly.
5. Structural Features: A central depression filled with eschar (crust) surrounded by a distinct, elevated, and rolled border.
