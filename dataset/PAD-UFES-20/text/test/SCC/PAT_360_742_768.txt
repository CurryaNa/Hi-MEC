1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-tan with central white-to-yellow keratotic debris.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming a circular outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Symmetrical; the lesion is dome-shaped and roughly circular.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) with a centrally crusted and scaly apex.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic papule with a central keratotic plug or core; the lesion is raised and interrupts the surrounding field of vellus hairs.
