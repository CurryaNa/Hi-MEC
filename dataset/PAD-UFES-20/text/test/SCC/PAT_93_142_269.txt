1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous (red), and white.
2. Border Appearance: The border is relatively well-defined and regular, forming a round to oval shape.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is largely symmetrical in its overall shape.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is predominantly scaly and crusted, particularly in the central area.
5. Structural Features: A raised (papular or nodular) lesion with a central area of whitish-yellow, adherent scale or keratotic crust. The base of the lesion is pink to red.
