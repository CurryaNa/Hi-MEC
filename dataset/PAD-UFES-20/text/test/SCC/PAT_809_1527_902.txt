1. Primary Colors: Pink, violaceous, tan, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular, verrucous, with central scale-crust.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, firm-appearing papule with a central, whitish hyperkeratotic core or plug and an erythematous to violaceous base.
