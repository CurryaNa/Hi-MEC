1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, dark brown, black, and yellow-green.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular, crusted, and ulcerated.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration covered by a dark brown to black necrotic eschar (crust), surrounded by an erythematous, slightly elevated rim, with some areas of yellowish serous crusting.
