1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), white, and a pale pink to hypopigmented central zone.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and arcuate, with a raised, moderately well-demarcated outer edge that is more distinct than the inner border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly, with fine, adherent white scales concentrated on the raised border, and some small central crusts.
5. Structural Features: An annular patch characterized by a raised, erythematous border and central clearing. The central area appears slightly atrophic with evidence of small erosions or crusting.
