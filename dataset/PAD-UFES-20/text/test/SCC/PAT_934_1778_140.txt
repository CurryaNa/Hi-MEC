1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous (red), and pearly white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic and waxy with central fine crusting and erosion.
5. Structural Features: A slightly depressed (atrophic) plaque with central erythema and what appears to be early ulceration, a pearly or translucent quality at the periphery, and possible fine telangiectasias (small, dilated blood vessels).
