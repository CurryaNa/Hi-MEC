1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), skin-toned, with prominent yellowish-white and tan scale-crust.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are relatively well-demarcated, appearing round to ovoid, with some surrounding erythema that slightly blurs the edges.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Markedly hyperkeratotic and crusted, with thick, dry, adherent scale.
5. Structural Features: A raised, exophytic papule or nodule with a central, thick, keratotic crust, suggestive of underlying erosion.
