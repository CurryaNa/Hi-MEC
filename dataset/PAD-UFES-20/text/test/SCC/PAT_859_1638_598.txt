1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular; the main nodule has a well-demarcated, raised border, while an adjacent patch has a less-defined, scalloped edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated with a surrounding smooth, somewhat waxy-appearing rim.
5. Structural Features: A central, raised papulonodule with a pearly, rolled border. There are multiple foci of dark brown to black crusting and evidence of central ulceration.
