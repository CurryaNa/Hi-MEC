1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), black, and whitish-yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and lobulated, with a fairly well-demarcated base.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: Glistening and moist with areas of ulceration and adherent black hemorrhagic crust and white fibrinous material.
5. Structural Features: An exophytic, multi-lobulated nodule with central ulceration, thrombosed capillaries (black dots), and fine telangiectasias on the erythematous surface.
