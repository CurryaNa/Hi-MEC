1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, white, and focal black/brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed, with atrophic (thinned) areas, small crusted erosions, and a slightly scaly surface.
5. Structural Features: Central scar-like atrophy, multiple foci of ulceration with hemorrhagic crusting, subtle telangiectasias (fine red vessels), and surrounding erythema on a background of sun-damaged skin.
