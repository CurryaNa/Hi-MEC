1. Primary Colors: Pink-to-red (erythematous) base with overlying white and yellowish scale.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly, crusted, and rough (hyperkeratotic).
5. Structural Features: Raised papule or small plaque with a central, adherent, yellowish-white scale and an erythematous periphery.
