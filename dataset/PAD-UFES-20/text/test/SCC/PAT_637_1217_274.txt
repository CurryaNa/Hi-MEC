1. Primary Colors: Violaceous, black, dark brown, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated with a scalloped outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated with a fine, white, scaly collarette at the periphery.
5. Structural Features: A raised (papulonodular) lesion featuring central dark, hemorrhagic crusting and ulceration, surrounded by a violaceous rim and a peripheral collarette of scale. A smaller satellite papule with similar features is present superior-right to the main lesion.
