1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) with a central white and yellowish crusted area.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular with a distinct, circular border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Symmetrical and generally round.
4. Surface Texture: Rough with a scaly and crusted surface, particularly in the center.
5. Structural Features: A raised, firm-appearing, dome-shaped papule/nodule with central umbilication (depression) containing a keratotic plug/crust.
