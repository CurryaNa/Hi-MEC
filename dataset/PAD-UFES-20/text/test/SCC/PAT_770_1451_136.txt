1. Primary Colors: Dark red, black, and erythematous pink.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin within a skin fold.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and linear, following the orientation of a skin crease.
4. Surface Texture: Ulcerated and covered with a prominent hemorrhagic crust.
