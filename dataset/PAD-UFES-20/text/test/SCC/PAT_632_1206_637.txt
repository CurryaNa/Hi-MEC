1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pinkish-red) with multiple small, whitish-yellow foci.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and uneven with a cobblestone-like appearance.
5. Structural Features: Raised, multi-lobulated papule or nodule with numerous small, whitish-yellow pustules or milia-like cysts scattered across the surface.
