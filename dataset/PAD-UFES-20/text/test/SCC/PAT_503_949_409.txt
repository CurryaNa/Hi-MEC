1. Primary Colors: Pearly pink-white, translucent, erythematous (red), with a central yellowish hue and surrounding mottled brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated, raised, rolled, and slightly irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is roughly ovoid but not uniform.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and waxy at the periphery with a centrally crusted and slightly depressed surface.
5. Structural Features: A prominent, raised, translucent, rolled border with central umbilication or ulceration. Fine, arborizing telangiectasias (blood vessels) are visible over the lesion's surface and surrounding skin.
