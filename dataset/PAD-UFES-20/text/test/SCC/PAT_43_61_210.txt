1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-white, and small dark brown/black foci.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly; the surrounding skin appears dry and slightly wrinkled.
5. Structural Features: A central, shallow depression or ulceration is covered by an adherent, yellowish scale-crust. The lesion is surrounded by an erythematous halo.
