1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, brown, and black with a surrounding erythematous to violaceous halo and a fine white scale at the periphery.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with some raised and rolled segments.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and distribution of surface features.
4. Surface Texture: Irregularly nodular and ulcerated, covered with hemorrhagic and serous crusting.
5. Structural Features: A centrally ulcerated, exophytic nodule with a lobulated or cobblestone-like surface composed of smaller papules and nodules.
