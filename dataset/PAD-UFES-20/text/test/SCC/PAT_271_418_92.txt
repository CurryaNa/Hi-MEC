1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), with central white and yellow-tan areas and a focal point of bright red.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, with a distinct, rolled-appearing edge where the lesion rises from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical; the lesion is dome-shaped and roughly circular.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular and complex, featuring a combination of scaling, hyperkeratotic crusting, and a small area of moist erosion/ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A firm, exophytic nodule characterized by a central crateriform depression filled with a keratin plug and crust.
