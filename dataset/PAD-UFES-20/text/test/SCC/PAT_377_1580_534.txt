1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), brown, black, and pearly white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with the erythematous base blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in shape, color, and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Polymorphous, featuring areas of erosion, crusting, and a smooth, slightly raised papular component.
5. Structural Features: A complex lesion with an erythematous base, scattered foci of ulceration with dark brown/black hemorrhagic crust, and a small, somewhat translucent, pearly papule in the lower central area.
