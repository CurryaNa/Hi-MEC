1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), white, and a central dark brown/black area.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and moderately defined, demarcated by a fine, scaly rim.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of surface features.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted; the surface has fine, white, adherent scale, particularly at the periphery, with a central crusted area.
5. Structural Features: A central, dark, crusted erosion or ulceration on an erythematous base. A distinct peripheral collarette of scale is visible. The lesion appears slightly atrophic or depressed.
