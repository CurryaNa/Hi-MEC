1. Primary Colors: Pink, whitish-yellow, and tan, with a small superior violaceous area.
2. Border Appearance: The primary lesion has a well-defined but irregular border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Keratotic and crusted, with a friable, verrucous (wart-like) appearance.
5. Structural Features: An exophytic papule with a distinct, slightly rolled border and a central area of ulceration covered by keratinous debris and crust.
