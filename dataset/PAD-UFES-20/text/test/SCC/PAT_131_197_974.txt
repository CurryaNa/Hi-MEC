1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, with focal areas of yellow-orange crust and dark red to black specks.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, with some areas appearing slightly raised and translucent.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is composed of several coalescing nodules of varying sizes.
4. Surface Texture: Nodular and irregular, with a glistening or moist appearance, and areas of erosion and crusting.
5. Structural Features: A multi-lobulated papulonodular plaque with a shiny surface, focal ulceration, and hemorrhagic crusting. There is a suggestion of a rolled border in some segments.
