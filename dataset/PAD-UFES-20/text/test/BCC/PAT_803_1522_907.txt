1. Primary Colors: Pearly pink, erythematous (red), and a focal dark red-brown hemorrhagic area.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with a raised, rolled quality.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed; partially smooth and waxy, with a central area of ulceration and crusting.
5. Structural Features: A raised, translucent/pearly border with a central depression (ulceration) covered by a hemorrhagic crust. Fine, branching blood vessels (telangiectasias) may be present on the surface.
