1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red-pink), white, and yellow.
2. Border Appearance: The border is relatively defined but irregular, with a subtle erythematous halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is largely symmetrical in its overall round shape, but asymmetrical in color and internal structure.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted with an underlying ulceration; the surrounding rim is raised (papular) and slightly scaly.
5. Structural Features: A raised erythematous papule with central ulceration covered by an adherent, whitish-yellow crust. Pinpoint hemorrhages are visible within the central crusted area.
