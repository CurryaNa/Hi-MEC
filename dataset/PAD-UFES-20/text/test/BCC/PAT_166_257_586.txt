1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pinkish-red) center with a surrounding flesh-toned to whitish-pink halo.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and indistinct, gradually blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical with a roughly circular to oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: Generally smooth with effacement of normal skin lines; not scaly, crusted, or ulcerated.
5. Structural Features: A raised, firm-appearing papule or nodule with central erythema and a paler, slightly depressed or atrophic-appearing periphery. A subtle central indentation may be present.
