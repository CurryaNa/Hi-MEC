1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pinkish-red), pearly white, and focal dark brown/black crusting.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and internal structure.
4. Surface Texture: Shiny, waxy, and translucent with a centrally crusted and slightly eroded area.
5. Structural Features: Prominent arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels), a central ulceration, and a pearly, slightly translucent quality to the papule.
