1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red) base, with areas of brown, black, and a translucent whitish-yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Irregularly crusted and scaly; the crust appears thick and adherent.
5. Structural Features: A central, elevated, multi-component crust containing variegated pigmentation (black, brown) overlying an erythematous papule or plaque.
