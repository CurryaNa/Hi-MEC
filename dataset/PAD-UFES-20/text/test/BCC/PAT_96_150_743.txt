1. Primary Colors: Bright red, translucent pink, and pearly-white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, featuring a partially raised, rolled, and translucent edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Predominantly moist and glistening with areas of erosion; the peripheral raised border appears smooth and pearly.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration, a pearly and translucent rolled border, and fine, short linear blood vessels (telangiectasias).
