1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, with focal black and dark brown specks.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Glistening and moist, with areas of central crusting and erosion.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration, a translucent or "pearly" quality, and prominent surrounding telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels).
