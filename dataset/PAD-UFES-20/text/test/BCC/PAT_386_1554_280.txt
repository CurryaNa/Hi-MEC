1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), dark red to black, and yellowish-white.
2. Border Appearance: The overall lesion area is ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is a diffuse patch composed of multiple, irregularly distributed smaller lesions.
