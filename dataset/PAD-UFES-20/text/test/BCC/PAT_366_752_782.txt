1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, translucent yellow, and pearly white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a slightly raised, rolled, and translucent edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated, moist, and glistening with yellowish crusting; the surrounding skin is atrophic.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration, a raised and pearly border, and a glistening, eroded center.
