1. Primary Colors: Black, dark brown, yellowish-tan, and red/pink at the base.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with surrounding erythema.
3. Shape Symmetry: Markedly asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Heavily crusted, keratotic, and friable with a verrucous and ulcerated appearance.
5. Structural Features: An exophytic, fungating nodule with a prominent, adherent, necrotic, and hemorrhagic central crust. Ulceration is evident beneath the crust, and the base of the lesion appears erythematous and indurated.
