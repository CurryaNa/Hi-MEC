1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-white, tan, and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a surrounding erythematous halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Thickly crusted, scaly, and rough with an adherent, keratotic surface.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule with apparent central ulceration covered by a thick, multi-component (keratotic and hemorrhagic) crust.
