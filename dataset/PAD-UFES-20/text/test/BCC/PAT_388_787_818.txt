1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-brown, and areas of lighter pink/skin-tone.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with the central lesion's erythema fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
