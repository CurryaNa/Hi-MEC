1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink/red), dark brown, black, and whitish-pink.
2. Border Appearance: The border of the central ulceration is irregular, while the surrounding erythematous halo is ill-defined and fades into the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is elongated and irregular in its overall contour.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated; the surrounding rim is slightly raised and smooth.
5. Structural Features: A central, linear ulceration covered with a hemorrhagic and serous crust. The lesion is surrounded by a pale, erythematous rim. The adjacent skin shows signs of solar elastosis (mottled pigmentation and atrophy).
