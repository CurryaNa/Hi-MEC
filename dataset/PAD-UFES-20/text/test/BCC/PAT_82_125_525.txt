1. Primary Colors: Variegated with shades of reddish-pink, violaceous, dark brown to black, and areas of white-grey.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with notching and indistinct margins.
3. Shape Symmetry: Markedly asymmetrical in both contour and distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven and crusted, with areas of ulceration and scaling.
5. Structural Features: Elevated (nodular) lesion featuring multiple blotches of dark pigmentation, central ulceration, and fine, branching telangiectasias (visible vessels) at the periphery.
