1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), whitish/hypopigmented, and skin-colored.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and elongated.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic (thinned) and wrinkled, with small, pitted indentations.
5. Structural Features: A central scar-like, atrophic area with multiple small pits (cribriform scarring). The lesion appears slightly depressed with areas of both erythema and whitish sclerosis.
