1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish, and areas of hypopigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with stellate or branching extensions.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic (thinned) and slightly shiny, with loss of normal skin markings and a subtle central crust or erosion.
5. Structural Features: A central, slightly raised erythematous papule surrounded by scar-like changes and fine, visible telangiectasias (small blood vessels).
