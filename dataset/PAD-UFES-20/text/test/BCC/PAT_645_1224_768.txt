1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), dark red to brownish-black, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with the peripheral erythema gradually blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
