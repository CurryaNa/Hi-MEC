1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), violaceous, with focal dark brown to black pigmented specks.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat indistinct, with a non-uniform, slightly lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of features.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven and slightly waxy, with focal areas of fine crusting.
5. Structural Features: Raised (papular) lesion with prominent, fine, branching telangiectasias on the surface and small, dark pigmented globules.
