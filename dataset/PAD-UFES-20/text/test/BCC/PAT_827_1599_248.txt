1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red, translucent violaceous, with a focal pearly white area and a central yellowish-brown crust.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming a round-to-oval outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Mostly smooth and waxy, with a small, centrally located crusted and scaly area.
5. Structural Features: Raised, dome-shaped nodule with a translucent, pearly appearance and a small central ulceration or crust.
