1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with areas of black, dark brown, red (erythema), and grayish-tan.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Ulcerated and crusted, with a moist, hemorrhagic appearance centrally and some peripheral scaling.
5. Structural Features: A central, friable-appearing ulceration covered by a dark eschar and crust; surrounding erythema and disorganized pigmentation are present.
