1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with shades of dark brown, black, red-pink, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated from the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted, scaly, and friable with some waxy components.
5. Structural Features: Elevated (papulonodular) lesion with evidence of central ulceration covered by a hemorrhagic and serous crust, and areas of hyperkeratosis (white scale).
