1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, with a central focus of darker red and whitish-yellow crust.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and topography.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven, with a centrally crusted and ulcerated area, and a slightly scaly periphery.
