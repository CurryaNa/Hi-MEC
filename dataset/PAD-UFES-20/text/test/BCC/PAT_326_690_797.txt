1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous base with overlying white-to-silvery areas.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated but irregular in shape.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and keratotic, with fine, dry, adherent white scale on the surface.
5. Structural Features: Raised papule with fine telangiectasias (small, visible blood vessels) and a scaly central portion.
