1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red, translucent pearly white, and a focal spot of black pigment.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, blending with the surrounding skin in some areas, with a more distinct, raised border on the papular components.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and multiform.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and shiny (pearly) over the papular components, with an area of central crusting and ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A multi-lobulated lesion composed of raised papules, prominent arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels), central ulceration, and a focal area of dark pigmentation.
