1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink/red) base, yellowish-tan, brown, with scattered black foci.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic and crusted, with an adherent, rough scale.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic (outward-growing) papule with a central, horn-like keratotic core, an erythematous and possibly indurated base, and focal erosion.
