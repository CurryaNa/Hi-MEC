1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink/red), tan, brown, and black/grey.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, featuring a distinct, elevated, and rolled peripheral margin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and fissured; the surrounding border is raised and relatively smooth.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration covered by a thick, adherent, hemorrhagic crust (eschar), surrounded by a raised, pearly, and rolled border.
