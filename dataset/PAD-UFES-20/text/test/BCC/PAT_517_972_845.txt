1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red, reddish-brown, and amber.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted with a shiny, irregular, and friable appearance; surrounding erythematous rim is smoother.
5. Structural Features: A central focus of ulceration covered by a hemorrhagic and serosanguineous crust, surrounded by an erythematous halo. Extrinsic blue-black markings are visible on the adjacent skin.
