1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red), with focal areas of more intense red.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and indistinct, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall erythematous patch is roughly symmetrical (round), but the internal papules and erosions are asymmetrically distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Slightly shiny and atrophic with possible fine scaling; not uniformly smooth.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous patch with superimposed small erythematous papules and at least two focal erosions or ulcerations. Subtle telangiectasias may be present.
