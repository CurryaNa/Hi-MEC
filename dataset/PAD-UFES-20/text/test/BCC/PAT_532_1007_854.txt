1. Primary Colors: Pink, bright red, dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: The border of the overall erythematous lesion is ill-defined and irregular; the central ulcerated portion has a more distinct but still irregular margin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally moist and ulcerated with focal crusting; the surrounding skin appears erythematous and slightly atrophic.
5. Structural Features: A central, irregularly shaped ulceration with a glistening, friable-appearing red base and a small area of dark crust at its inferior aspect.
