1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), brown, black, and yellowish.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration with an adherent, variegated (yellow, brown, black) crust, surrounded by an erythematous halo and a slightly raised, somewhat translucent border.
