1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), skin-toned, with a focal area of bright red and dark red hemorrhagic crust.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of features.
4. Surface Texture: Waxy and slightly translucent at the periphery with a central area of erosion and crusting.
5. Structural Features: A slightly elevated, rolled border surrounding a central, shallow ulceration.
