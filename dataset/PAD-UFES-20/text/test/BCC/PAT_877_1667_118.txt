1. Primary Colors: Dark brown to black, erythematous (red/pink), and whitish.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and indistinct, with some areas poorly demarcated from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly, with some fine scale at the periphery.
5. Structural Features: A central, darkly pigmented, crusted plaque is present, surrounded by an erythematous halo and areas of whitish scale; there may be underlying ulceration.
