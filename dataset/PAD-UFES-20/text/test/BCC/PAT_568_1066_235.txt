1. Primary Colors: Pink, pearly white, and red.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated with a slightly raised, rolled, and regular round-to-oval outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, glistening, and waxy or translucent.
5. Structural Features: Raised papule with a central pearly-white area and fine, arborizing red blood vessels (telangiectasias) on the surface.
