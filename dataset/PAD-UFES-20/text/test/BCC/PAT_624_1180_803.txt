1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), dark brown to black, and yellowish-white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted and uneven, with areas of hemorrhagic and serous crust.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration or erosion with surrounding erythema, a superior dark hemorrhagic crust, and an inferior yellowish-white crusted papule.
