1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pinkish-red) with a central area of yellowish-brown and tan.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and texture.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly, with the surrounding lesion appearing slightly rough.
5. Structural Features: A central, shallow ulceration or erosion with adherent serous crust. The lesion is a papule with a slightly raised, erythematous rim. Fine telangiectasias are subtly visible at the periphery.
