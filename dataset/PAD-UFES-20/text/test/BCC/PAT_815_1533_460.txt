1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) base with focal areas of brown pigmentation and whitish scale.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted over a slightly atrophic (thinned) surface.
5. Structural Features: Central area of atrophy and possible superficial ulceration, with focal pigmentation and effacement of normal skin markings.
