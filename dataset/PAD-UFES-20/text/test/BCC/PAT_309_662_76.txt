1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), yellow-orange, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted and scaly; the central crust has a waxy appearance.
5. Structural Features: Ulcerated plaque with a central, multi-colored, serosanguineous crust and surrounding erythema.
