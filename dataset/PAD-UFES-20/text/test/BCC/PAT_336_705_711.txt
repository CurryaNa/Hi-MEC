1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, and focal dark brown to black dots.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly on the superior and lateral aspects.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth to slightly uneven with a somewhat waxy or translucent quality; skin lines are partially effaced.
5. Structural Features: A slightly elevated papule exhibiting multiple colors (polychromasia) and containing small, dark, dot-like pigmented structures.
