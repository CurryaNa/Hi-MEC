1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), focal blue-gray to black, and translucent pearly white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: Heterogeneous, with a shiny, slightly atrophic surface and a central area of subtle erosion.
5. Structural Features: Multiple fine, branching telangiectasias (arborizing vessels), focal blue-gray pigment globules, a slightly raised and pearly border, and central erosion.
