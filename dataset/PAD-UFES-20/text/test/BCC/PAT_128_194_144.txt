1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white (central), pink-red (surrounding halo), tan/light brown (background skin).
2. Border Appearance: The central keratotic component is well-demarcated, while the surrounding erythematous halo has an ill-defined border that fades into the adjacent skin.
