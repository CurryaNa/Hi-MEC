1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous) and pearly white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined with a raised, rolled quality.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and waxy/pearly, particularly at the periphery, with a central area of slight atrophy or erosion.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule with a translucent, pearly appearance and a central pink, atrophic depression.
