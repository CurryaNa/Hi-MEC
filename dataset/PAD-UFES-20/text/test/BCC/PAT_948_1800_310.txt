1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pinkish-red), red-brown, dark brown/black, and whitish-translucent.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated, with surrounding areas appearing slightly atrophic.
5. Structural Features: A central focus of ulceration with hemorrhagic crusting, surrounded by a zone of erythema and a paler, possibly atrophic halo. Small, scattered dark brown macules are present at the periphery.
