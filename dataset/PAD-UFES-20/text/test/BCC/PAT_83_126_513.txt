1. Primary Colors: Red, dark red, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and slightly ill-defined, with an erythematous halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular, with a central, dark, hemorrhagic crust and a moist, glistening surface.
5. Structural Features: A raised (papulonodular) lesion with central ulceration, hemorrhagic crusting, and variegated pigmentation.
