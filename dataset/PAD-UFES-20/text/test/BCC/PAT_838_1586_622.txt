1. Primary Colors: Pearly white, translucent pink, and light red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly at the inferior and lateral margins.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth with a subtle waxy sheen and effacement of normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: A slightly elevated papule with a pinkish periphery and a central, whitish, slightly atrophic or depressed scar-like area.
