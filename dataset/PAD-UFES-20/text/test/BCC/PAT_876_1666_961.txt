1. Primary Colors: Red, pink, yellowish-brown, and white.
2. Border Appearance: The central ulcerated area has a slightly irregular but discernible border, surrounded by an erythematous halo with an indistinct, fading edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is roughly circular but lacks symmetry in its color distribution and surface features.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated and crusted with a moist, friable appearance, surrounded by a rim of fine white scale (collarette).
5. Structural Features: An erythematous papule with central ulceration and hemorrhagic-serous crusting is the dominant feature. A subtle, raised, pearly border might be present at the edge of the ulceration, along with a peripheral collarette of scale.
