1. Primary Colors: Bright red, dark red, black, and yellowish-white against a pink erythematous background.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly where it merges with the surrounding inflamed skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Ulcerated, moist, and friable with overlying hemorrhagic crust.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration covered by hemorrhagic crust and yellowish fibrinous exudate; the lesion appears somewhat raised or nodular.
