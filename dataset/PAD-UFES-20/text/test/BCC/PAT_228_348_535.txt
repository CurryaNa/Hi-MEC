1. Primary Colors: Violaceous (purplish-red), pink, and focal areas of black and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated but with an irregular, non-circular outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of internal colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: Predominantly smooth and glistening, with a focal area of dark brown/black crust.
5. Structural Features: Raised, nodular lesion exhibiting color variegation, translucent violaceous areas, and distinct black clods of pigment.
