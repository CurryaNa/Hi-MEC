1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), translucent pearly-white, and a central reddish-brown crust.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a rolled quality.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and internal structure.
4. Surface Texture: Waxy and smooth along the periphery, with a central area of crusting and slight atrophy.
5. Structural Features: A raised, translucent border, central ulceration with crusting, and fine, arborizing telangiectasias (small blood vessels) visible on the surface.
