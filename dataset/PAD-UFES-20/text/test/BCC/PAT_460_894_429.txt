1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, dark brown, black, and off-white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Markedly asymmetrical in shape, color, and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Variegated, featuring ulcerated/eroded areas and adherent, hard crusts.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration, multiple foci of irregular brown-black pigmentation and crusting, and prominent telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels) on the surface and periphery.
