1. Primary Colors: Translucent pinkish-red (erythematous) with a small, focal area of dark brown/black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, with a smooth, rounded perimeter.
3. Shape Symmetry: Symmetrical; the lesion is generally round to oval.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, shiny, and pearly.
5. Structural Features: Raised, dome-shaped (nodular) papule with a focal area of pigmentation on its superior-right aspect.
