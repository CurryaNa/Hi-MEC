1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), yellowish/amber, and dark red/brown.
2. Border Appearance: The central erosion has irregular borders, while the surrounding erythema is ill-defined and blends into the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Moist and glistening centrally, with serous and hemorrhagic crusting.
5. Structural Features: A central, shallow ulceration with a yellowish, exudative base, surrounded by an erythematous halo. A trail of dried, serosanguinous material extends inferiorly.
