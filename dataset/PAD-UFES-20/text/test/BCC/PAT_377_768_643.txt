1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), tan-brown, and a small area of white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly the erythematous component which blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and internal structure.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and eroded with some fine, whitish scale at the edges of the central depression.
5. Structural Features: A central shallow ulceration with crusting, surrounded by a translucent, erythematous halo. Fine, branching telangiectasias are visible within the pink areas. Faint blue ink markings are present adjacent to the lesion.
