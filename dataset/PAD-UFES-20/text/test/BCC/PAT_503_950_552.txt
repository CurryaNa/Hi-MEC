1. Primary Colors: Black, dark brown, yellowish-green, and erythematous red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with a notched appearance in some areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular and heavily crusted, with areas of both necrotic eschar and serous/purulent crust.
5. Structural Features: A multi-component pattern featuring central ulceration, variegated pigmentation, and a thick, adherent scale-crust. An erythematous halo surrounds the lesion base, and a small satellite papule is present on the left border.
