1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous) and a central whitish-yellow area.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly the erythematous component which blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly, with the surrounding skin appearing slightly atrophic.
5. Structural Features: A central, adherent, whitish-yellow crust overlying a subtle erosion; surrounding erythema; and fine, thread-like telangiectasias visible at the periphery.
