1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, light to dark brown, and tan, with some translucent, pearly areas.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and not sharply demarcated, with a slightly raised, rolled edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
