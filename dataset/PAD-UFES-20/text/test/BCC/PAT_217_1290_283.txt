1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-brown, and a small dark brown punctum.
2. Border Appearance: The peripheral border is ill-defined and fades into the surrounding skin; the central component has a more distinct but irregular border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated and covered with a serosanguineous and hemorrhagic crust; the surrounding skin appears slightly atrophic.
5. Structural Features: A central, crusted ulceration is the dominant feature, surrounded by a pink, erythematous halo. There is a subtle, slightly raised, translucent quality to the border immediately surrounding the ulcer.
