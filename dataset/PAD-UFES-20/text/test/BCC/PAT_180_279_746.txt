1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red (erythematous), with a central focus of dark red and brownish-black crust.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming a distinct, rounded edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its overall oval contour.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, shiny, and somewhat translucent (pearly) over most of the surface, with a central area of rough hemorrhagic crusting.
5. Structural Features: A raised, solid nodule featuring central ulceration, hemorrhagic crust, and fine, arborizing telangiectasias (small blood vessels) on the surface.
