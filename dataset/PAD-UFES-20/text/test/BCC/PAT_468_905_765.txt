1. Primary Colors: Black, dark brown, tan, and pink/red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly defined, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated, with a slightly waxy or pearly appearance at the periphery.
5. Structural Features: A central, dark, hemorrhagic crust over an ulcerated base, a subtle raised peripheral rim, and fine telangiectasias on the surrounding skin.
