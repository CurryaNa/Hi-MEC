1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink/red), yellowish-brown, and red.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding inflamed skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and internal color pattern.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly, with some surrounding dryness.
5. Structural Features: A central erosion or ulceration covered by a yellowish-brown crust, surrounded by a raised, erythematous papular border.
