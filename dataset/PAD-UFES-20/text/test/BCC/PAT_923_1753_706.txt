1. Primary Colors: Brown, black, pink-erythematous, and pearly/translucent.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, blending with the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly; the peripheral papular component appears somewhat waxy and smooth.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule with a central, focal ulceration covered by a dark brown to black hemorrhagic crust. Prominent, branching telangiectasias are visible on the surface of the lesion.
