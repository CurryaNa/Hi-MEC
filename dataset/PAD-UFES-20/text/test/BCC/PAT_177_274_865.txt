1. Primary Colors: Pearly white, translucent, and pink/erythematous.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and glossy over the nodular components.
5. Structural Features: Two raised, coalescing, translucent nodules with a surrounding erythematous halo and subtle central atrophic or scar-like changes. Possible fine telangiectasias are visible on the surface of the nodules.
