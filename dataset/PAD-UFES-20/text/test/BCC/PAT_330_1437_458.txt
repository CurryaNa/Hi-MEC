1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), dark brown, black, and whitish-silver.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with the erythematous halo fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted and scaly; the central portion is covered with a thick, adherent hemorrhagic crust, while the periphery exhibits fine, whitish scale.
5. Structural Features: A central, ulcerated plaque with variegated hemorrhagic crusting, surrounded by an erythematous and scaly halo.
