1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), dark red to brown-black (hemorrhagic), and focal translucent yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a slightly raised, pearly, and rolled inner border surrounding the central defect.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and internal structure.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated with hemorrhagic crusting; the surrounding rim appears smooth and slightly waxy.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration is the dominant feature, accompanied by a raised "rolled" border and fine, branching telangiectasias at the periphery.
