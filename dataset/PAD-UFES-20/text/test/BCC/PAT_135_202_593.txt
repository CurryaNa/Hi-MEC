1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink/red), black, and dark blue.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated, with a smoother, slightly shiny periphery.
5. Structural Features: A raised erythematous papule with central ulceration, hemorrhagic crusting, and focal pigmentation. The border has a somewhat translucent or pearly appearance.
