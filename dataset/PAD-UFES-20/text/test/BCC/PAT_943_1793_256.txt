1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), dark brown, black, and yellowish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: The peripheral erythematous border is irregular and somewhat ill-defined, while the central crusted area has a more distinct but irregular border.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted, rough, and uneven, suggesting ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration covered by a dark, hemorrhagic-appearing eschar, surrounded by a raised erythematous halo.
