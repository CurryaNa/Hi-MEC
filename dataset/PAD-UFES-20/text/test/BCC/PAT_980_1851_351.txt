1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink), pearly white/translucent, and a central focus of brown and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, with a raised, rolled, and somewhat ill-defined edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the overall shape is roughly ovoid but lacks symmetry in its border and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: The peripheral border is smooth and waxy/pearly, while the center is ulcerated and covered with a hemorrhagic crust.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration with crusting is the dominant feature. The lesion is a nodule with a raised, pearly border. Telangiectasias (fine blood vessels) are visible on the surrounding skin.
