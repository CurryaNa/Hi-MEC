1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, and translucent white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, particularly on the inferior and right margins.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical with a round to oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, glistening, and somewhat waxy or pearly.
5. Structural Features: Raised papule with fine, arborizing telangiectasias (small, branching blood vessels) visible across the surface.
