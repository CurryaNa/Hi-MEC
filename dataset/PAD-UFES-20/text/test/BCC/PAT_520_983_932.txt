1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), translucent pearly white, and central dark brown to black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a raised, rolled edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: Shiny and waxy at the periphery with a centrally crusted and ulcerated area.
5. Structural Features: A raised, pearly border with visible fine, branching blood vessels (telangiectasias) surrounding a central ulceration with hemorrhagic crusting.
