1. Primary Colors: Red (erythema), yellow (serous crust), black (hemorrhagic eschar), and fine red lines (telangiectasias).
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated, with a surrounding shiny and inflamed surface.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration covered by a dark, hemorrhagic crust, surrounded by a subtle, translucent, rolled border with prominent, branching telangiectasias on the surface.
