1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pinkish-red), skin-colored, with a central dark brown to black eschar and a superior red punctum.
2. Border Appearance: The border of the papule is somewhat ill-defined and blends with the surrounding erythema, while the central ulceration has a sharply demarcated edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall lesion is roughly round and largely symmetrical, but it is asymmetrical in its internal structure due to the off-center ulceration.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is centrally ulcerated with a hemorrhagic crust; the peripheral rim appears smooth and slightly waxy.
5. Structural Features: A pink papule with a central, crater-like ulceration filled with a dark eschar. The border of the papule appears slightly raised and translucent ("rolled" appearance). A small pinpoint hemorrhage is present superior to the ulcer.
