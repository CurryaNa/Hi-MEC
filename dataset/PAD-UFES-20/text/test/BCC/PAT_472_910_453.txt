1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish-yellow, and pale pink.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and indistinct, with the lesion gradually blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly symmetrical in its round shape, but the internal features are asymmetrically distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and rough with fine, adherent, whitish scales (keratotic).
5. Structural Features: An erythematous patch with evidence of central atrophy, superficial scaling, and a small, focal erythematous papule near the center.
