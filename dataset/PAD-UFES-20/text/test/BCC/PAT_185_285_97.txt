1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), with areas of white scale and focal black/dark red hemorrhagic crusts.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin in some areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of surface features.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven and rough, characterized by crusting, scaling, and ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration or erosion is present, along with small foci of hemorrhagic crusting. The lesion appears as a firm, infiltrated papule.
