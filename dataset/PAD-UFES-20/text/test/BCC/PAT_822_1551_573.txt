1. Primary Colors: Erythematous pink, dark red-to-black, and foci of yellowish-white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly the erythematous component which blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Heterogeneous, featuring central hemorrhagic crusting and ulceration, with some overlying fine scale.
5. Structural Features: A central, crusted ulceration with a surrounding, subtly raised, erythematous border.
