1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), porcelain-white (hypopigmented/atrophic), and subtle light brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, diffusely blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, finely scaly (xerotic), and atrophic with fine wrinkling, creating a "cracked" appearance.
5. Structural Features: A mottled pattern with interspersed patches of erythema and atrophy. The atrophic areas appear thin, shiny, and whitish. Skin markings are effaced in some areas and accentuated in others. No overt ulceration or prominent vessels are visible.
