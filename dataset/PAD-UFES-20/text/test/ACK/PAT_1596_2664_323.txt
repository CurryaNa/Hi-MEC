1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), white, and tan-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly, with areas of fine, adherent scale.
5. Structural Features: Multiple erythematous papules and small plaques, some coalescing. A fine, whitish, reticulated scale is visible on the surface of the larger plaques. The surrounding skin shows signs of photodamage, including solar lentigines.
