1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red (erythematous), translucent pearly-white, with scattered dark red and brown specks.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
