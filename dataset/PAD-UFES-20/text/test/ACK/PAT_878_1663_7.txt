1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, erythematous (red), and white.
2. Border Appearance: The central component is well-demarcated with an irregular border; the surrounding erythematous base is less defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally hyperkeratotic and crusted; peripherally there is fine, adherent scaling.
5. Structural Features: A central, exophytic, yellowish-white keratinous plug or horn arising from an erythematous papulonodular base, with a surrounding collarette of scale.
