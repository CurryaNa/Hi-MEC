1. Primary Colors: Yellow, pink, red.
2. Border Appearance: Roughly circular with a slightly raised, rolled, and somewhat ill-defined border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Generally symmetrical and round.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly; peripherally smooth but slightly elevated.
5. Structural Features: Central shallow ulceration covered by a yellowish, adherent crust, surrounded by a distinct, pinkish, translucent, rolled peripheral border.
