1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous, with a central dark brown to black crust.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated but irregular, with a raised, rolled edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and the placement of central features.
4. Surface Texture: Pearly or waxy at the periphery with a centrally crusted and roughened area.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule with a central ulceration covered by a hemorrhagic crust.
