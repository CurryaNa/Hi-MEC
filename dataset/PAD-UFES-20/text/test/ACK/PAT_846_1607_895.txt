1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink), white/pearly, and dark brown/black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, with a scalloped, flaky edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated, with surrounding fine white scale.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration with hemorrhagic crusting, a surrounding slightly raised, pearly, and erythematous rim, and overlying hyperkeratotic scale.
