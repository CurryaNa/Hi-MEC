1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), translucent white/pearly, and focal dark red to black (hemorrhagic crust).
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with a slightly elevated, somewhat rolled quality.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated, surrounded by a smooth, slightly waxy or pearly surface.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration with evidence of recent bleeding (hemorrhagic crust), a raised, pearly-appearing border, and fine, branching telangiectasias (arborizing vessels) on the surface of the lesion and surrounding skin.
