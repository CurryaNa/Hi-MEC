1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), purpuric (violet/purple), and pink.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with multiple lesions coalescing into larger patches.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the overall pattern and individual lesions are irregularly shaped.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and non-scaly, with fine skin lines apparent.
5. Structural Features: Multiple flat (macular) lesions, including pinpoint petechiae and larger, confluent ecchymotic patches.
