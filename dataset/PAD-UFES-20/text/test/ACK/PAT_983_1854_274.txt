1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-brown, tan, and dark brown on the central growth, with a surrounding erythematous (pink-red) base and adjacent white scale.
2. Border Appearance: The base of the central lesion is irregular and somewhat circumscribed, arising from a larger, ill-defined erythematous plaque.
3. Shape Symmetry: Highly asymmetrical, presenting as an irregular, conical, exophytic mass.
4. Surface Texture: Markedly hyperkeratotic, verrucous (wart-like), and laminated (layered); the surrounding skin is dry and scaly (desquamating).
5. Structural Features: A prominent, indurated, exophytic, horn-like projection (cutaneous horn) is the central feature, arising from an erythematous, scaly base. The surface of the horn appears compacted and layered.
