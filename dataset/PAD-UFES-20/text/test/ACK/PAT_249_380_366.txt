1. Primary Colors: Central yellowish-white; peripheral erythematous (pink-red) rim.
2. Border Appearance: The peripheral border is relatively well-defined but slightly irregular; the inner border of the erythematous rim is distinct.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall lesion is largely symmetrical in its roundish shape.
4. Surface Texture: A central, firm, keratotic crust is present; the surrounding rim appears smooth.
5. Structural Features: The lesion is a papulonodule characterized by a central, crateriform depression filled with a prominent yellowish-white keratin plug, surrounded by a raised, rolled, erythematous border.
