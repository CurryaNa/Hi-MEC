1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red), light brown, dark brown, and hypopigmented (pale white).
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular; the changes represent a diffuse field with indistinct borders that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the various components (patches of color, texture changes) are irregularly shaped and distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, atrophic, and finely wrinkled with areas of fine, adherent, whitish scale.
5. Structural Features: Mottled dyspigmentation with scattered hyperpigmented macules and hypopigmented patches, blotchy erythema, and significant skin atrophy. Some erythematous areas show slight scaling, consistent with actinic keratoses on a background of severe photodamage.
