1. Primary Colors: Multiple shades of brown and tan, with areas of pinkish-red and pale skin tones.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular; the pigmented areas have a mottled, "moth-eaten" appearance, with indistinct borders that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall pattern of pigmentation is highly asymmetrical, composed of numerous irregularly shaped and sized macules.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic and dry, with prominent fine lines and wrinkles (rhytides).
5. Structural Features: A diffuse, mottled, and reticulated pattern of hyperpigmentation with confluent brown macules on a background of sun-damaged skin (solar elastosis).
