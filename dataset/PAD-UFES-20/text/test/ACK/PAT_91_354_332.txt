1. Primary Colors: Erythematous pink-red, hypopigmented white, and scattered brown hyperpigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, with an irregular distribution of color and texture changes.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven and atrophic, appearing thinned and slightly depressed in some areas, with a largely smooth surface.
5. Structural Features: Mottled pigmentation, areas of atrophy, patches of erythema, and visible follicular ostia with hair shafts. A small, slightly raised papule is noted in the superior aspect.
