1. Primary Colors: Heterogeneous, with shades of light brown, dark brown, and grey-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and indistinct, with some areas poorly demarcated and fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and slightly crusted, with a dry, matte finish.
5. Structural Features: A disorganized, reticular pattern is present, particularly at the periphery. Centrally, there are clods/globules of darker pigment and fine, whitish, crystalline-like scaling.
