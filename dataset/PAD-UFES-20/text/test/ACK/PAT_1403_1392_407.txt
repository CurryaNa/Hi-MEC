1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red) with overlying fine, whitish-silvery scale and scattered flesh-toned areas.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, diffusely blending into the surrounding skin without a distinct margin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the affected area is a diffuse, irregularly shaped patch.
4. Surface Texture: Rough, dry, and uneven with a fine, adherent, scaly, and slightly granular texture.
5. Structural Features: Diffuse background erythema, prominent follicular openings, and fine, linear and arborizing telangiectasias (visible small blood vessels). The skin appears slightly thickened in some areas.
