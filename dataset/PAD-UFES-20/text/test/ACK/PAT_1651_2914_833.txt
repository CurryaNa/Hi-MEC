1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous (red), and areas of whitish atrophy on a yellowish-tan skin background.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and poorly demarcated; the lesions are multiple and coalesce, forming an irregularly shaped field of change.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in its overall distribution and shape.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic and slightly shiny with fine, adherent scale giving it a rough or sandpaper-like feel.
5. Structural Features: Multiple, scattered erythematous papules and small plaques are present. Subtle telangiectasias (fine, dilated blood vessels) are visible within the erythematous areas. The lesion is on hair-bearing skin.
