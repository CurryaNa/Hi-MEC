1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red) base with white and yellowish-white scale.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, rough, and scaly with adherent, fine to thick hyperkeratotic scale and some crusting.
5. Structural Features: Multiple, discrete erythematous macules and papules with overlying scale-crust. The lesions are situated on a background of photodamaged skin with prominent rhytides (wrinkles). Some areas exhibit slight erosion.
