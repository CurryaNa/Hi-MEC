1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) base with a central yellowish-white exudate or plug.
2. Border Appearance: The erythematous papule has somewhat ill-defined borders, while the central white component is more sharply demarcated but irregular in shape.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, primarily due to the irregular shape and placement of the central white material.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and firm-appearing; the surrounding erythematous skin appears slightly raised and smooth.
5. Structural Features: An inflamed papule featuring a central, hard, yellowish-white keratinous or calcified plug extruding from the surface.
