1. Primary Colors: Mottled brown, tan, pink-red, and areas of hypopigmentation (white).
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined; the area of altered pigmentation blends diffusely into the surrounding skin without a clear demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall pattern of discoloration is highly asymmetrical and irregular.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and atrophic with fine wrinkling and accentuated skin lines.
5. Structural Features: A reticulated pattern of hyperpigmented and hypopigmented macules (poikiloderma), scattered erythematous macules, and fine telangiectasias. A small, dark blue-black linear mark is visible near the center.
