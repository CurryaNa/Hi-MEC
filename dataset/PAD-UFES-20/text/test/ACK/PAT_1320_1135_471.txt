1. Primary Colors: Pink-erythematous, pearly white, and translucent.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and raised, composed of confluent, small, pearly papules, creating a "rolled" or beaded edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Grossly symmetrical in its overall round shape, but with fine asymmetry in the nodular border.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and waxy with a pearly or translucent sheen, particularly at the periphery.
5. Structural Features: A distinct raised, pearly, rolled border surrounds a slightly atrophic or depressed pink center. Fine, arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels) are visible on the surface.
