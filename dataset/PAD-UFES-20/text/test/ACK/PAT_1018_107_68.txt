1. Primary Colors: Light brown, dark brown, and off-white.
2. Border Appearance: The overall area is ill-defined; individual pigmented and hypopigmented macules have slightly irregular but relatively distinct borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and random distribution of macules across the affected area.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth with fine, visible skin lines (dermatoglyphics); no scaling or elevation.
5. Structural Features: A mottled pattern consisting of multiple, small, flat macules of both hyperpigmentation (lentigines) and hypopigmentation.
