1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, multiple shades of brown (tan to dark brown), and pearly white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding sun-damaged skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Highly asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Heterogeneous, featuring atrophic (thinned) areas, fine scaling, and a raised, smooth, waxy papule.
5. Structural Features: A large field of mottled, irregular pigmentation with a superimposed translucent, pearly papule, fine telangiectasias (visible small blood vessels), and focal areas of superficial erosion and crusting.
