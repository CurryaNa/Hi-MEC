1. Primary Colors: Black, reddish-pink, and yellowish-tan.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated and circular, with a raised, slightly rolled, and subtly irregular edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its overall circular shape, but asymmetrical regarding the distribution of color and texture.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated and covered with a thick, adherent, multi-colored (hemorrhagic and sero-keratotic) crust.
5. Structural Features: A central depression/ulceration is filled with a thick eschar showing black, yellow, and reddish components. The lesion is surrounded by a raised, somewhat pearly, and erythematous rim.
