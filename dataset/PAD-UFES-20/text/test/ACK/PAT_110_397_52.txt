1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), hyperpigmented (brown), and hypopigmented (white/pale).
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with multiple lesions coalescing into larger, poorly demarcated patches.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the overall distribution and individual components are irregular and lack symmetry.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly, with a fine, whitish, adherent scale creating a rough and slightly crinkled appearance.
5. Structural Features: A mottled pattern of inflammation (erythema) and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation is present. The lesions are predominantly flat (macules and patches) with overlying scale. Some areas may exhibit slight atrophy.
