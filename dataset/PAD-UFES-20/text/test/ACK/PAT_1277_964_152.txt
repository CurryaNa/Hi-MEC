1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, brown, black, and whitish-atrophic areas on a background of light tan skin.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion is annular with a moderately defined, slightly irregular erythematous outer border.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall round shape is largely symmetrical, but the internal features are asymmetrically distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and atrophic (thinned); the peripheral rim appears slightly raised and dry.
5. Structural Features: A prominent annular (ring-like) pattern with a peripheral erythematous rim. The central area is atrophic and contains two distinct, crusted, umbilicated puncta (small, indented ulcerations).
