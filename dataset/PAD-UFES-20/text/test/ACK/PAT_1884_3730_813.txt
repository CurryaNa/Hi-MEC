1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), flesh-toned, whitish-yellow, and dark brown/black points.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and diffuse, with indistinct borders blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall process is broadly symmetrical in its distribution on the nose, but the specific inflammatory and scarring components are asymmetrically distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven, bumpy, and oily with areas of fine scaling and atrophic scarring.
5. Structural Features: Prominent dilated follicular openings (patulous pores) with some open comedones, erythematous papules, phymatous thickening causing a bulbous contour, and areas of whitish atrophic scarring.
