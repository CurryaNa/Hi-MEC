1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, and white, with a small central reddish-brown crust.
2. Border Appearance: The primary lesion has a well-defined but irregular border; surrounding lesions are more ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and rough with fine, adherent white scale, particularly at the periphery, and a central crusted area.
5. Structural Features: Raised erythematous papule with evidence of central erosion or ulceration, fine surface telangiectasias (visible vessels), and surrounding atrophic, sun-damaged skin.
