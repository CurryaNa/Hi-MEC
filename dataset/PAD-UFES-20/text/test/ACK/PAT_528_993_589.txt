1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous, with a central area of dark brown, black, and yellowish-white.
2. Border Appearance: The erythematous base has a well-demarcated and regular circular border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its overall circular shape, though the central crust is asymmetrically distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted, verrucous, and keratotic; the peripheral base appears smoother and slightly scaly.
5. Structural Features: A raised, dome-shaped (exophytic) papule featuring a central, well-defined hyperkeratotic plug or crust, surrounded by an erythematous collar.
