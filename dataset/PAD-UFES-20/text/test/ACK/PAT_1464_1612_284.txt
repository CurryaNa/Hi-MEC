1. Primary Colors: Light brown and tan, with mottled areas of hypopigmentation (pale tan/white) and hyperpigmentation (darker brown), and scattered reddish-brown macules.
2. Border Appearance: The area of change is diffuse; borders are ill-defined and blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Not applicable as it is not a discrete lesion; the pattern of pigmentation and textural change is heterogeneous and asymmetrical across the field.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, finely wrinkled (rhytidosis), and atrophic with prominent skin lines and fine scaling.
5. Structural Features: Mottled pigmentation, skin atrophy (thin, slightly shiny appearance), and scattered, small, erythematous-to-brown macules, some with fine crust.
