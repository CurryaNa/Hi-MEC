1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), black, and white.
2. Border Appearance: The outer border of the erythematous halo is ill-defined, while the border of the central component is sharply demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall lesion is largely symmetrical and round.
4. Surface Texture: The erythematous rim appears smooth and slightly raised; a fine white scale or crust is present at the margin of the central opening.
5. Structural Features: A raised, dome-shaped papule with central umbilication or crater filled with a black keratotic plug or eschar; fine telangiectasias are visible on the surface of the erythematous component.
