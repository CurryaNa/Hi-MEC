1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) with a focal area of yellowish-white.
2. Border Appearance: The border is relatively well-demarcated but slightly irregular and not sharply defined, with a generally round to oval outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is predominantly scaly with a focal area of adherent crust.
5. Structural Features: The lesion presents as an erythematous plaque with fine surface scale. There is a distinct small, central-inferior focus of ulceration covered by a yellowish crust.
