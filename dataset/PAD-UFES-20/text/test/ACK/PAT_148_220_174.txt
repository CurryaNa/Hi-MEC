1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) and white/off-white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly with a focal area of thickened, adherent scale (hyperkeratotic).
5. Structural Features: A raised plaque with an erythematous base covered by fine scale and a more prominent, focal, adherent, whitish, hyperkeratotic crust.
