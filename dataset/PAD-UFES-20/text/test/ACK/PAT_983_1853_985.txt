1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-brown, tan, with central dark brown and black specks, on an erythematous (pink-red) base.
2. Border Appearance: The border is well-demarcated but irregular in shape.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic, heavily crusted, and scaly with a hard, rough surface.
5. Structural Features: A prominent, exophytic, conical mass composed of concentric, laminated layers of keratin, consistent with a cutaneous horn.
