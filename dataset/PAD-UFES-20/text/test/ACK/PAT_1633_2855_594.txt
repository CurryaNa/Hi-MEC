1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red), light brown, with mottled areas of hyperpigmentation (brown) and hypopigmentation (whitish-pink).
2. Border Appearance: The overall area of change has indistinct and poorly defined borders, representing a field change rather than a discrete lesion.
3. Shape Symmetry: The affected area is large and asymmetrical; multiple smaller macules and patches within it are also irregularly shaped and asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Rough, dry, and xerotic, with fine, adherent, whitish scales creating a crinkled texture.
5. Structural Features: Multiple, ill-defined erythematous macules and thin papules are scattered across a background of photodamaged skin (dermatoheliosis). Fine telangiectasias are present, along with mottled pigmentation and skin atrophy.
