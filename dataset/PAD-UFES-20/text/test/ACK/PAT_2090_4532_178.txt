1. Primary Colors: Skin-colored, pinkish-white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually merging with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, forming an irregular patch.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, rough, with fine, whitish, adherent scales.
5. Structural Features: A slightly elevated plaque characterized by accentuation of the skin lines (lichenification). A small, skin-colored papule is noted superior to the main plaque.
