1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous) base with overlying white to silvery-white areas.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.

5. Structural Features: Erythematous papule or small plaque with adherent, whitish scale.
