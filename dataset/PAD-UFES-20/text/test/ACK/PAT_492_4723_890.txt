1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pinkish-red), pearly white, and a central yellowish-brown crust.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly where it merges with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and waxy with a central, crusted, and slightly depressed area.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration covered by a crust, a slightly elevated and rolled border, and fine, arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels) on the surface. The lesion is situated on atrophic, sun-damaged skin.
