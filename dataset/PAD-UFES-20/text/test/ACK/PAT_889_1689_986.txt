1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, brown, black, and pink-red (erythematous).
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a peripheral collarette of fine white scale.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Markedly hyperkeratotic, crusted, and verrucous (wart-like) with a friable, crumbly appearance.
5. Structural Features: A central, exophytic, keratotic nodule with areas of dark crusting, situated on an erythematous base.
