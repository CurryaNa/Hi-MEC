1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink), whitish, and a central area of dark brown to black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with the erythematous halo blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly; the surrounding pinkish area appears dry and slightly atrophic.
5. Structural Features: A central, adherent, dark hyperkeratotic crust and scale, suggestive of ulceration, surrounded by an erythematous to whitish plaque.
