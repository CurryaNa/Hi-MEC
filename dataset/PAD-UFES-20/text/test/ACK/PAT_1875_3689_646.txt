1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, violaceous, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with a slightly scalloped, whitish, atrophic-appearing rim.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed, featuring central fine white scale or crystalline-like deposits over a slightly depressed surface.
5. Structural Features: A pinkish-red papule with central violaceous discoloration and whitish hyperkeratotic or crystalline structures. There are signs of central atrophy.
