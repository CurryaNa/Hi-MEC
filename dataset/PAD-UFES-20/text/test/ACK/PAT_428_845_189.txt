1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) base with a central white to yellowish-white adherent scale.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated but with a slightly irregular and indistinct outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Roughly symmetrical in its overall circular shape, but asymmetrical in the fine details of the border and central scaling.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly, featuring a rough, adherent, keratotic crust in the center.
5. Structural Features: A slightly elevated papule with a central, crusted erosion on an erythematous base; prominent hyperkeratosis is the main feature.
