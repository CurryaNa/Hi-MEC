1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red) with overlying whitish-yellow scale and crust.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted with central erosion.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration covered by adherent scale-crust; fine telangiectasias are visible on the surrounding skin.
