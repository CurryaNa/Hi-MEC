1. Primary Colors: Pink to red (erythematous) base with overlying white and yellowish scale-crust.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, rough, and scaly with areas of adherent crusting.
5. Structural Features: Erythematous, slightly elevated plaque with central hyperkeratosis (scaling) and crust formation.
