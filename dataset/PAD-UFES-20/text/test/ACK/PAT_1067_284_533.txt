1. Primary Colors: Pearly white, yellowish, and erythematous (pink-red).
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and not sharply demarcated, blending into surrounding erythema.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly with a rough, uneven surface.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration covered by a yellowish crust, a slightly raised and rolled periphery, and fine telangiectasias (small blood vessels) visible on and around the lesion.
