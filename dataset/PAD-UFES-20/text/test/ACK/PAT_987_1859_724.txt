1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish-yellow, and light brown.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated, raised, and arcuate (curved), forming an incomplete ring.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly, with some fine crusting observed along the border.
5. Structural Features: Annular plaque characterized by a prominent, raised, erythematous border with central clearing. The central area appears slightly atrophic and less erythematous than the periphery.
