1. Primary Colors: Grayish-white and light green central body with a brown peripheral base.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming a distinct circular boundary with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Symmetrical; the lesion is roughly circular in its overall shape.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and hyperkeratotic (scaly/thickened), with a dry, crusted, and cerebriform (brain-like) surface.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic (outward-growing) papule with a sessile (broad) base. The surface is composed of multiple fused lobules and deep clefts.
