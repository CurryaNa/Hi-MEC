1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, with surrounding mottled hyperpigmentation (brown) and areas of hypopigmentation (whitish).
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic (thinned) and smooth with fine wrinkling, with minimal to no visible scale.
5. Structural Features: A patch characterized by erythema, fine telangiectasias (visible small blood vessels), and poikilodermatous changes including atrophy and pigmentary alteration, all on a background of sun-damaged skin (dermatoheliosis).
