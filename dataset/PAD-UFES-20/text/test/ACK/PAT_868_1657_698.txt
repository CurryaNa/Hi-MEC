1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, pearly white, and focal gray-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending with the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: Shiny and waxy with fine surface scale and wrinkling.
5. Structural Features: Arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching vessels), a central translucent papule, and scattered gray-brown pigmented globules.
