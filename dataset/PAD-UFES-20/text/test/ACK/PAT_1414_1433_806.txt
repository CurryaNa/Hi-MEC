1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, pink-red (erythematous), skin-toned.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined, irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Keratotic, crusted, and scaly on an atrophic and wrinkled base.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous papule with a central, firm, yellowish keratotic plug or cutaneous horn. The adjacent skin exhibits signs of significant solar damage (solar elastosis).
