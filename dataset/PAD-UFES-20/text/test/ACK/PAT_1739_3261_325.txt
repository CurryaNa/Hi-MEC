1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red) with areas of fine, whitish scale.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; appears as an irregularly shaped patch or a confluence of smaller lesions.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and finely scaly.
