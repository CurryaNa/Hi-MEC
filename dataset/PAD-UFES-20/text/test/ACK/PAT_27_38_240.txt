1. Primary Colors: Black, yellowish-brown, and peripheral pink-red (erythema).
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Heavily crusted, hyperkeratotic, and friable.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration with a dark, necrotic/hemorrhagic crust, surrounded by a thick, adherent, yellowish scale-crust and a subtle erythematous halo.
