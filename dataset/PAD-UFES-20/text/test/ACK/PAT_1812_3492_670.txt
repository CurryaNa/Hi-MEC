1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, with surrounding light brown skin and adjacent purpuric linear markings.
2. Border Appearance: Indistinct and poorly demarcated, blending into the surrounding tissue; the overall shape is roughly ovoid.
3. Shape Symmetry: The central papule is largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, with effacement of the normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: A solitary, slightly elevated papule. Adjacent linear, atrophic-appearing areas contain fine, branching telangiectasias.
