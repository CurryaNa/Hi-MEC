1. Primary Colors: Variegated, including dark brown, black, pink, and a central hypopigmented white/pinkish area.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, with some areas appearing blurred and blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular, with fine wrinkling and an atrophic appearance, particularly in the center.
5. Structural Features: A central scar-like, regressive area that is hypopigmented and pink, surrounded by irregular, blotchy, dark pigmentation. Fine telangiectasias may be present in the central atrophic zone.
