1. Primary Colors: White-yellow, pink, and red (erythematous).
2. Border Appearance: The lesion is roughly circular with an ill-defined outer erythematous border that blends into the surrounding skin; the central white-yellow component is more sharply demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its overall round shape.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally hyperkeratotic, crusted, and scaly; the surrounding erythematous rim appears relatively smooth.
5. Structural Features: A central crater-like depression (umbilication) filled with a dense, whitish-yellow keratin plug, surrounded by a raised, firm-appearing erythematous border.
