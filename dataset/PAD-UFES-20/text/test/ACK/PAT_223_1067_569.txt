1. Primary Colors: Translucent, pearly, skin-colored, and pink-erythematous.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat scalloped, formed by the coalescence of smaller papules.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Bumpy and lobulated with a glistening, non-scaly surface.
5. Structural Features: Composed of multiple, aggregated, translucent papules forming a larger plaque; fine, thread-like telangiectasias are visible on the surface.
