1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), silvery-white, and yellowish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined; the lesion appears as a diffuse patch without a distinct margin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the affected area is a large, irregular patch.
4. Surface Texture: Markedly dry, scaly (desquamating), and hyperkeratotic with areas of adherent crusting.
5. Structural Features: Thick, adherent, silvery-white scales overlying an erythematous base, with focal areas of yellowish-brown crust. Prominent skin lines (lichenification) and fine fissures are visible. Hairs are present throughout the lesion.
