1. Primary Colors: Violaceous (purplish-brown) with some darker brown and black foci.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming an oval outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical along the longitudinal axis.
4. Surface Texture: Mammillated or cobblestone-like, appearing slightly bumpy but not scaly or crusted.
5. Structural Features: An elevated papule with multiple terminal hairs emerging from its surface and subtle surface clefts.
