1. Primary Colors: Uniformly pink to flesh-colored.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and distinct from the surrounding scalp, with a lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Grossly symmetrical in its overall round to oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and lobulated, with a soft-appearing, cobblestone or cerebriform (brain-like) surface.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, sessile nodule composed of multiple, tightly clustered, rounded lobules. Hair shafts are visible emerging from the base and between the lobules.
