1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with purple-brown, dark brown, and yellowish-green areas.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and lobulated, with a focal, indistinct macular extension.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and verrucous (warty) with overlying crusting.
5. Structural Features: Raised, exophytic nodule with a cerebriform (brain-like) surface, multiple colors, and keratotic debris.
