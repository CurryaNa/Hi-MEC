1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with shades of pink, light brown, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and generally well-demarcated, though not sharply defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and distribution of color.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and uneven, with a cobblestone-like or warty appearance.
5. Structural Features: A raised (nodular) lesion with multiple components, including a central, darkly pigmented (dark brown/black) area with a cerebriform surface, surrounded by a pinkish-brown periphery.
