1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with prominent shades of gray-blue, violaceous, and whitish-pink.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and lobulated, yet well-demarcated from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Highly asymmetrical in both shape and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and verrucous (wart-like), with multiple folds and clefts creating a rough, uneven surface.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic (outwardly growing) papule or nodule with a cerebriform (brain-like) and multi-lobulated morphology.
