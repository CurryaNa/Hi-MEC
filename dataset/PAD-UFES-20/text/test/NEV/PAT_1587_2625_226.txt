1. Primary Colors: Violaceous, dark blue-brown, and a peripheral pinkish-red (erythematous) halo.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Slightly wrinkled with partial effacement of superficial skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: A central, darker, slightly elevated (papular) violaceous component surrounded by a peripheral erythematous patch.
