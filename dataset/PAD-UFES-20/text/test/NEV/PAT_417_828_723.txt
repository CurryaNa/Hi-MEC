1. Primary Colors: Violaceous, light brown, and dark brown foci.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly at the superior and lateral margins.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both contour and distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven and slightly glossy, with a mamillated or cobblestone-like surface.
5. Structural Features: A slightly elevated papule with multiple dark brown puncta and small, crusted indentations. Hair follicles are seen emerging from the lesion.
