1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with multiple shades of brown (light to dark) and areas of black, plus a central whitish-pink scar-like area.
2. Border Appearance: Highly irregular, with notching and indistinct, fading margins in multiple locations.
3. Shape Symmetry: Markedly asymmetrical in both contour and distribution of color and internal structures.
4. Surface Texture: Predominantly flat (macular), with fine textural changes and effacement of skin lines in the more heavily pigmented zones.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern is present, characterized by irregular blotches of dark pigment, and a prominent central zone of hypopigmentation suggesting regression.
