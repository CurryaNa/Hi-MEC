1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with multiple shades including light brown, dark brown, black, and small focal areas of hypopigmentation (whitish specks).
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly defined, featuring scalloped, notched, and indistinct margins that fade into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Markedly asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color.
4. Surface Texture: Largely smooth with visible skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved across the surface; no significant scaling or crusting is apparent.
5. Structural Features: A multi-component pattern with disorganized structures. Key features include blotches and clods of dark brown/black pigment, particularly in the central and lower portions, and scattered areas of regression (hypopigmentation).
