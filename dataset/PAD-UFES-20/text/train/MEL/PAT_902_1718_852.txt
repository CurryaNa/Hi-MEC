1. Primary Colors: Variegated; dark brown, black, and reddish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and scalloped, with some areas being poorly demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Mammillated (bumpy) with fine fissures and a slightly crusted or scaly quality.
5. Structural Features: A raised, multi-lobed papule with central dark pigmentation and a smaller, lighter brown nodular component at the inferior border.
