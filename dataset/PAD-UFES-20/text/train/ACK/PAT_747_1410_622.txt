1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), white, and dark brown/black.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly.
5. Structural Features: Raised papule with central ulceration, hemorrhagic crusting, and a surrounding erythematous halo.
