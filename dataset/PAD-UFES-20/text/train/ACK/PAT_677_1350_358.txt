1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-brown, grayish-green, and dark brown centrally, with a peripheral erythematous (pink-red) halo.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming a distinct, round, slightly elevated border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its round to oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic, crusted, and rough, with a verrucous (wart-like) quality.
5. Structural Features: An exophytic papule featuring a central, adherent keratotic crust or cutaneous horn on an erythematous base.
