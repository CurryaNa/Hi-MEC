1. Primary Colors: Mottled erythema (pink to red), tan-brown hyperpigmentation, and areas of white hypopigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and indistinct, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is a diffuse, irregularly shaped patch.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and rough with fine, whitish scaling and accentuated skin lines (wrinkling).
5. Structural Features: A reticulated pattern of mixed pigmentation and erythema, consistent with atrophy; a focal, more erythematous papule is noted superiorly.
