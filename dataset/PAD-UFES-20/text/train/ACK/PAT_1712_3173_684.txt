1. Primary Colors: Whitish-yellow and peripheral pink/erythematous.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming a circular to slightly oval outline, partially obscured by an external ink marking.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in both shape and internal structure.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally hyperkeratotic with an adherent, dry, scaly crust; the peripheral rim is smoother but appears slightly raised.
5. Structural Features: A central, firm, keratin-filled plug or core surrounded by a distinct, raised, erythematous collarette, giving the lesion a crateriform (volcano-like) appearance.
