1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, yellow-tan, black, and white.
2. Border Appearance: The overall field of change has ill-defined borders; individual papules within this area have more distinct but irregular borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Multifocal areas of scale and crust on a background of dry, rough skin; the scale varies from fine and white to a thick, adherent yellow-tan crust.
5. Structural Features: A field of sun-damaged skin presents with multiple lesion types: a superior keratotic papule with a thick, yellowish crust (marked in ink), an inferior small papule with a black, crusted center and an erythematous halo, and scattered ill-defined erythematous patches with fine scale.
