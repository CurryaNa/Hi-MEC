1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous) and white/hypopigmented.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in shape, color, and texture.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and rough with fine, adherent white scale (keratotic).
5. Structural Features: An erythematous patch with overlying fine scaling, giving it a sandpaper-like texture. Some areas appear slightly atrophic.
