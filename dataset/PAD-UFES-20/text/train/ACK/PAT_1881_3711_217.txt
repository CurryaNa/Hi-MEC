1. Primary Colors: Pink, light brown, and white/hypopigmented areas.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, presenting as an irregular patch.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic and slightly scaly, with small keratotic papules.
5. Structural Features: Background of mottled hyperpigmentation and hypopigmentation; a central, ill-defined erythematous patch contains small, yellowish-white papules and foci of crust.
