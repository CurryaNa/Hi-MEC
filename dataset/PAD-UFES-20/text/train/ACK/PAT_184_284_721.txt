1. Primary Colors: White, pinkish-red (erythematous).
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated but with an irregular outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and hyperkeratotic (scaly/crusted).
5. Structural Features: Elevated papule with a central, thick, white, craggy scale surrounded by a subtle erythematous halo. A small erosion is present on one edge.
