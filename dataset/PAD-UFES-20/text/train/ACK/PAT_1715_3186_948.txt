1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, white, and skin-toned, set against a background of mottled light brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and blurred, with individual lesions blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The individual papules are roughly symmetrical (round to oval), while the larger confluent areas are asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic, characterized by fine wrinkling and a slightly shiny, dry, and subtly scaly surface.
5. Structural Features: Multiple discrete and confluent papules and small plaques, with some showing erythema and others exhibiting central atrophy and hypopigmentation.
