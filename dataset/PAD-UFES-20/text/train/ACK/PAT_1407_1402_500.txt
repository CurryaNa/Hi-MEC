1. Primary Colors: Mottled light brown and purplish-brown with multiple scattered, small, white to off-white patches.
2. Border Appearance: The overall affected area is diffuse with ill-defined borders; individual patches within the area are irregular and indistinct.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the condition presents as a mottled pattern of multiple, irregularly shaped macules and patches.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and atrophic (thinned) with areas of fine, whitish scale.
5. Structural Features: The skin exhibits signs of chronic sun damage (dermatoheliosis), including mottled hyperpigmentation, purpuric macules (purplish patches), and small, white, atrophic, stellate (star-shaped) patches. Fine vellus hairs are present.
