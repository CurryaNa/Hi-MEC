1. Primary Colors: Erythematous pink, off-white, dark red, and black.
2. Border Appearance: The peripheral erythematous border is ill-defined; the central ulcerated border is distinct but highly irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and internal structure.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated with a surrounding smooth erythematous ring.
5. Structural Features: A central crater-like depression containing whitish crust, a dark red hemorrhagic punctum, and a central black dot/plug, all surrounded by an inflammatory erythematous halo.
