1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red (erythematous), skin-colored, and a central yellowish-white area.
2. Border Appearance: Well-defined and regular, forming a round to oval shape.
3. Shape Symmetry: Symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth at the periphery with a central, rough, star-shaped crust.
5. Structural Features: A raised, dome-shaped papule with central umbilication containing a keratinous plug or crust. The peripheral rim has a slightly translucent or pearly appearance.
