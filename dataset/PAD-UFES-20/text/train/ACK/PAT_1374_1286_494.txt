1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, violaceous, white, and tan-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Markedly asymmetrical in color, shape, and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic and scarred, with areas of fine scale and slight crusting.
5. Structural Features: A central erythematous-to-violaceous papule, surrounding atrophic scarring, mottled hyper- and hypopigmentation, and fine linear scars.
