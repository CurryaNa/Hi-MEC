1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish, and yellowish-white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with the erythematous base gradually blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both color and topography.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally covered with an adherent, firm, hyperkeratotic crust and scale; the surrounding erythematous area appears dry and slightly scaly.
5. Structural Features: A central, irregular, whitish-yellow keratotic plaque/crust sits atop an erythematous base; there may be underlying erosion or ulceration beneath the crust. Hairs are seen growing through the lesion.
