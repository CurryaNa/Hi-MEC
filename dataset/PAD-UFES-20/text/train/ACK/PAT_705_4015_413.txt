1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with areas of pink-red (erythema), white (hypopigmentation), light brown (hyperpigmentation), and central violaceous linear markings.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and indistinct, blending diffusely into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, presenting as an irregular, patchy area of change rather than a discrete, shaped lesion.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic and finely wrinkled, with a dry appearance and presence of fine vellus hairs.
5. Structural Features: Prominent features include patchy atrophy giving a thinned, "cigarette paper" appearance, fine linear and branching telangiectasias, and mottled dyspigmentation (both hyper- and hypopigmentation). Some areas exhibit scar-like changes.
