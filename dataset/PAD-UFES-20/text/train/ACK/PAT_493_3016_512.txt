1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, pink, and erythematous (red).
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, with a circular to oval outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated with a friable, keratotic, and crusted surface; the peripheral rim is raised and firm.
5. Structural Features: An elevated papule or nodule featuring a central crateriform ulceration filled with a prominent yellowish-white keratotic plug, surrounded by a distinct erythematous, rolled-appearing border.
