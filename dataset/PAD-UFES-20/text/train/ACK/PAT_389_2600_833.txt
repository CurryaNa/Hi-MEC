1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red), light brown, and whitish.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion presents as a diffuse patch with multiple focal points rather than a single, discrete shape.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and rough with fine, adherent, whitish scales and areas of subtle crusting.
5. Structural Features: The lesion is a patch with multiple erythematous macules and papules on a background of mottled hyperpigmentation and skin atrophy (fine wrinkling). Some focal areas exhibit more prominent scale and scale-crust.
