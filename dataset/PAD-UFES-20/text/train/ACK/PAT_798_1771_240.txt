1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, light brown, dark brown, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and indistinct, presenting as a large, diffuse patch with mottled borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical due to the irregular and haphazard distribution of pigment and vascular changes.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and atrophic with fine, delicate scaling and effacement of normal skin markings.
5. Structural Features: A poikilodermatous pattern consisting of reticulated hyperpigmentation, patchy hypopigmentation, skin atrophy, and diffuse erythema with fine, thread-like telangiectasias.
