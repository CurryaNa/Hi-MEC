1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, cream-colored, and pinkish-red (erythematous).
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with the central crustaceous area having a crumbling periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of surface features.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally hyperkeratotic and crusted, with a thick, waxy, and friable scale.
5. Structural Features: A raised (exophytic) lesion characterized by a central, adherent, yellowish scale-crust overlying an erythematous papular base.
