1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), yellowish-green, and flesh-toned.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with the erythematous halo blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and the distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated and moist with a crusted or sloughy surface; the peripheral erythematous skin appears slightly edematous.
5. Structural Features: A central, shallow ulceration filled with yellowish-green fibrinous material or crust, surrounded by an erythematous rim.
