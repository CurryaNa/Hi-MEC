1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), violaceous (purplish-brown), and scattered light brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending diffusely into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven, with areas of fine scale and a slightly rough, papular texture, particularly in the most erythematous portion.
5. Structural Features: Variegated pigmentation with an inflamed, erythematous plaque-like area on one side and a flatter, more violaceous-brown patch on the other; fine hair follicles are visible within the lesion.
