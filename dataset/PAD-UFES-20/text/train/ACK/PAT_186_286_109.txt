1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red (erythematous), with a central area of white scale.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming an oval shape.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Predominantly smooth and dome-shaped with a central, rough, scaly-crusted patch.
