1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, and focal areas of white and yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Glistening and eroded with areas of fine scale and crusting.
5. Structural Features: Central erosion and ulceration, surrounded by erythema and prominent, fine, branching telangiectasias.
