1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink) and white/silvery-white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, rough, and scaly (hyperkeratotic).
