1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (ranging from pale pink to a more prominent red), with some areas of light brown pigmentation and small, dark brown to black crusted specks.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with multiple smaller lesions coalescing into larger, polycyclic patches.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the overall cluster of lesions is highly irregular in shape.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and slightly scaly, with a finely roughened surface and some punctate crusting.
5. Structural Features: Multiple, slightly elevated erythematous papules and plaques. Some larger plaques show a hint of central clearing or atrophy. Small, crusted erosions are visible within the lesions.
