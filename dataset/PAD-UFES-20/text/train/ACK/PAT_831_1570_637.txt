1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, pink-erythematous.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and fairly well-demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic, verrucous (wart-like), and crusted with a dry, rough appearance.
5. Structural Features: Elevated papule with a central, adherent, yellowish keratotic core surrounded by an erythematous rim. There is a small central depression or erosion within the keratotic material.
