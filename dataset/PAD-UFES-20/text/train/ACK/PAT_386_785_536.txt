1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), dark brown, black, and yellowish-tan.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, particularly at the erythematous base.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted and scaly; a thick, adherent, yellowish-tan scale/crust overlies a darker, more friable central area.
5. Structural Features: A central, darkly pigmented, crusted ulceration is visible. There is a prominent, adherent keratotic scale, surrounding erythema, and fine telangiectasias on the adjacent skin. Fine vellus hairs are also present.
