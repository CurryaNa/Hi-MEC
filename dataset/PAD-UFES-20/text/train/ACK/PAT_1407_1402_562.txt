1. Primary Colors: Mottled pink, porcelain white, and violaceous-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined; the affected area blends diffusely with no distinct margin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the distribution of color and texture is irregular across the field.
