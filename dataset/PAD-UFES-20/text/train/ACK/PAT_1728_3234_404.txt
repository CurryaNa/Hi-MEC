1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), light brown, dark brown, and a central yellowish-white crust.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and atrophic with a focal area of firm, adherent crusting.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration covered by a serosanguineous crust, visible telangiectasias (fine blood vessels), and surrounding mottled hyperpigmentation.
