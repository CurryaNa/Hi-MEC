1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), white, and a focal area of dark red to brown-black.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly with fine, adherent, white scales (desquamation) and a focal, firm crust.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous patch with prominent superficial scaling. There is a central focus of erosion covered by a hemorrhagic crust. Fine hairs are visible throughout the lesion.
