1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, cream, with a subtle peripheral erythematous (red) halo.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated with an irregular border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is roughly ovoid but irregular.
4. Surface Texture: Crumbly, keratotic, and crusted.
5. Structural Features: A central, raised (exophytic) core composed of yellowish-white, friable-appearing keratinous material.
