1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red/pink), tan, brown, with areas of hypopigmentation (white) and scattered dark brown macules.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined; the affected area is a diffuse patch with indistinct borders that merge with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is a large, irregularly shaped field of change.
4. Surface Texture: Rough, dry, and scaly with fine, adherent scale.
5. Structural Features: Mottled pigmentation, multiple erythematous scaly papules, and a central, linear, atrophic hypopigmented scar.
