1. Primary Colors: A mixture of light brown, pale pink (erythema), and whitish (hypopigmented) areas on a background of light skin tone.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular; the various colored patches merge into one another, creating a mottled pattern without a distinct, single border.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall pattern of discoloration is highly asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and finely wrinkled (atrophic), with some very fine, subtle scaling.
5. Structural Features: A mottled pattern of intermingled hyperpigmentation, hypopigmentation, and erythema (poikiloderma), with visible fine telangiectasias (small, red vessels) and scattered fine vellus hairs.
