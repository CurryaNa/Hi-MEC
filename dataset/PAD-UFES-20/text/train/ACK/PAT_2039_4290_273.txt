1. Primary Colors: Violaceous, erythematous, and brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic and dry with fine scaling and areas of superficial erosion/crusting.
5. Structural Features: The lesions appear as purpuric macules and patches on a background of mottled hyperpigmentation (dermatoheliosis); the superior lesion demonstrates superficial erosion.
