1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-tan, and patches of white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and indistinct, diffusely blending with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is a large, irregularly shaped patch.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and rough with fine, adherent, whitish-yellow scale.
5. Structural Features: Mottled pigmentation with prominent skin lines (exaggerated dermatoglyphics), areas of atrophy (thin, wrinkled appearance), and adherent scale-crust.
