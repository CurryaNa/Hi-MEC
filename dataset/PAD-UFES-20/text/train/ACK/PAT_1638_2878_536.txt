1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) base with mottled light brown and tan hyperpigmentation, overlaid with fine white-to-yellowish scale.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; presents as a poorly demarcated patch rather than a distinct shape.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, rough, and scaly (keratotic) with fine, adherent scale.
5. Structural Features: A diffuse patch of erythema and pigmentary alteration with overlying fine keratosis; skin lines (dermatoglyphics) are present but partially obscured by the scale.
