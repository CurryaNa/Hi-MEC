1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, erythematous (red), and a focal dark blue/black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with the erythematous component blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in shape, color, and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly; peripherally smooth.
5. Structural Features: A multi-component lesion consisting of a central yellowish-white keratotic crust, a surrounding erythematous halo, and an eccentric dark blue/black macule.
