1. Primary Colors: Dark brown, black, tan, and white, with a surrounding pinkish hue.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and well-demarcated from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Heterogeneous; the raised nodule is crusted and lobulated, while the adjacent flatter area is scaly and verrucous (wart-like).
5. Structural Features: A complex, biphasic lesion composed of an exophytic, pigmented nodule and an adjacent, flatter, hypopigmented plaque.
