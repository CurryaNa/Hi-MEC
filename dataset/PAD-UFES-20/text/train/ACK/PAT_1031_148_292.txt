1. Primary Colors: Flesh-toned, whitish-yellow, pink-red, and light brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular for the overall affected area; some individual papules have more discrete, rounded borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; composed of multiple, variably sized and shaped lesions scattered irregularly.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed; some areas are smooth and waxy, others are atrophic and shiny, with some patches of fine scale and crusting.
5. Structural Features: Multiple small, translucent papules, focal superficial erosions, areas of atrophy, and fine scaling on a background of sun-damaged, hairy skin.
