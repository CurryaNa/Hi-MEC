1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, white, and a central reddish-brown area.
2. Border Appearance: Irregularly shaped with well-defined but not sharply demarcated borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both contour and distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven, with fine whitish scaling and a centrally located crusted area.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration with a hemorrhagic crust, fine, short, linear and branching telangiectasias (arborizing vessels), and translucent to white scaling across the surface.
