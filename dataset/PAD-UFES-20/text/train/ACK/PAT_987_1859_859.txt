1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous (red), and pearly white/translucent.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and irregular, with a distinctly raised, rolled edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: The central area is atrophic with fine scaling and some minor crusting; the peripheral border is relatively smooth.
5. Structural Features: A prominent, raised, translucent, "pearly" border is the most notable feature. The center of the lesion appears depressed or ulcerated. Fine, thread-like vessels (telangiectasias) are subtly visible on the surface.
