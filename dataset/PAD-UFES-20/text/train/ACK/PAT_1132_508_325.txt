1. Primary Colors: Variegated; light brown, dark brown, erythematous (pink-red), and whitish.
2. Border Appearance: The overall area of change is ill-defined and merges with the surrounding skin; individual macules and papules are poorly circumscribed.
3. Shape Symmetry: The pattern is asymmetrical, with irregularly shaped and sized components distributed unevenly.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and rough with areas of fine, adherent scale, particularly over the erythematous and whitish papules.
5. Structural Features: Background of mottled hyperpigmentation and hypopigmentation (solar lentigines). Multiple scattered, slightly elevated, erythematous papules with a scaly surface are visible.
