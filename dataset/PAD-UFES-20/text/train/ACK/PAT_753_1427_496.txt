1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, light brown, dark brown, and whitish-yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, blending into the surrounding mottled skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Heterogeneous; partially flat with an area that is raised, scaly, and focally crusted.
5. Structural Features: Multicomponent lesion with an irregular pigmented macule underlying an erythematous papule, which is topped by an adherent, whitish-yellow hyperkeratotic crust.
