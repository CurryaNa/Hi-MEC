1. Primary Colors: Pink, red (erythematous), white, and yellowish-tan.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted, with adherent, flaky white and yellowish scale.
5. Structural Features: Erythematous plaque with central erosion covered by a yellowish-tan crust and a surrounding collar of scale (hyperkeratosis). The purple marks are extrinsic.
