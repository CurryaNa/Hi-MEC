1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Shiny with fine, whitish scale and a slightly crusted or erosive center.
5. Structural Features: Erythematous plaque with fine, arborizing telangiectasias, particularly at the periphery, and a central area of scale-crust suggesting early ulceration.
