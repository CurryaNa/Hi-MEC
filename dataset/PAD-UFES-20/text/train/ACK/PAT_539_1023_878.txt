1. Primary Colors: Translucent whitish-yellow, with black puncta.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with a frayed, feathery periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted, scaly, and friable.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic papule with a central, waxy-appearing scale-crust containing multiple small, black dots (puncta), arising from an erythematous base.
