1. Primary Colors: White (hypopigmented), erythematous (red), violaceous, and light brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular; comprised of multiple coalescing patches and macules, with some individual white areas showing stellate (star-like) borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the overall affected area and its individual components are irregular in shape and distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic, thinned, and finely wrinkled; appears generally smooth and dry.
5. Structural Features: Multiple, irregularly shaped, white, atrophic scars (atrophie blanche); interspersed erythematous to violaceous macules; visible skin thinning; and scattered small hyperpigmented macules.
