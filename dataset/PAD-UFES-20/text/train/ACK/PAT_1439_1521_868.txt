1. Primary Colors: Pearly white, yellowish, and skin-toned at the base.
2. Border Appearance: The base is well-demarcated; the overall outline of the projection is irregular and lobulated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, with an elongated, horn-like or finger-like projection.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like), waxy, and keratotic.
5. Structural Features: An exophytic (outward-growing), filiform projection, appearing as a cutaneous horn-like structure arising from the skin surface.
