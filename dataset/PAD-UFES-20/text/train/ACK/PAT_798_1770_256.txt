1. Primary Colors: Skin-toned, pink, red, and light brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding sun-damaged skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its overall oval shape, though internal features are asymmetrically distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven, with atrophic (thinned) areas and a focal point of erosion with slight crusting.
5. Structural Features: Central atrophy and scarring, a small central erosion/ulceration, and fine, branching telangiectasias (small, visible blood vessels).
