1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, tan, and erythematous (pink/red) at the base.
2. Border Appearance: The base of the lesion is well-demarcated with a slightly irregular circumferential border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is a conical, curved projection.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic, rough, dry, and laminated with a crusted appearance.
