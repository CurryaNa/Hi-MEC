1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, pale yellow, with a pinkish erythematous base.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated at the base; the edges of the projecting structure are irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and elongated.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic, crusted, dry, and rough.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, columnar, horn-like projection (cutaneous horn) composed of compacted keratin.
