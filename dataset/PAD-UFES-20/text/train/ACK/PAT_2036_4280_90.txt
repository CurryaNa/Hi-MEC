1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) base with overlying white to yellowish-white scales and scattered small brown macules.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and poorly demarcated, diffusely involving the skin of the ear helix.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is irregular and conforms to the anatomical curve of the ear helix.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, rough, and scaly with fine, adherent scales covering the surface.
5. Structural Features: A diffuse erythematous plaque with overlying hyperkeratosis (scaling). Some areas show thicker, more focal scaling. Fine vellus hairs are visible throughout the lesion.
