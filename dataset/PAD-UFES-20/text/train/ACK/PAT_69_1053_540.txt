1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) and white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted, with areas of fine, adherent white scale.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous plaque with overlying white, reticular (lace-like) striae and focal areas of thicker, whitish hyperkeratosis.
