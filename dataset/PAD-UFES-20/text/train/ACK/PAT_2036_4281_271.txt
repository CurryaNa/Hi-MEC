1. Primary Colors: Pink, red (erythematous), whitish, and yellowish-white on a background of mottled brown and pink skin.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding sun-damaged skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Varied, with areas of dryness, atrophy (thinning), and focal, adherent, yellowish-white keratotic scale and crust.
5. Structural Features: Multiple erythematous patches with overlying scale-crust, areas of atrophy, and follicular plugging, all situated within a field of significant solar damage (dermatoheliosis).
