1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, tan, and light brown, with some small white macules.
2. Border Appearance: The overall area represents a field change with no discrete border; individual erythematous patches are ill-defined and blend with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The distribution of changes is asymmetrical; individual macules and patches are irregularly shaped.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and rough with fine, adherent scale.
5. Structural Features: Multiple, scattered, ill-defined erythematous macules and thin papules are present on a background of mottled hyperpigmentation (solar lentigines) and atrophy.
