1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pinkish-red) with some whitish, scaly areas.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Slightly rough and dry with fine, adherent scale.
5. Structural Features: Central erythema, fine telangiectasias (small, branching blood vessels), and a slightly atrophic or superficially eroded center.
