1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red), violaceous, and yellowish-tan.
2. Border Appearance: Multiple ill-defined patches with indistinct borders that blend into the surrounding tissue.
3. Shape Symmetry: The patches are irregularly shaped and asymmetrically distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Markedly atrophic and thin, with fine, crinkled, "cigarette paper-like" wrinkling and a translucent, adherent scale.
5. Structural Features: Underlying erythematous to violaceous patches are visible through the atrophic epidermis, with some areas showing central pallor. Purpuric macules are present, suggesting fragility.
