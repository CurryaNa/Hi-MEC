1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), yellowish-white, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, particularly the erythematous component.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted, keratotic, and ulcerated.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration partially covered by an adherent, friable, yellowish-white crust.
