1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), whitish/hypopigmented, and violaceous (purple-red).
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined; the affected area blends diffusely into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the changes are irregularly distributed across the field.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic, shiny, and finely wrinkled ("cigarette paper" texture) with areas of subtle, fine scaling.
5. Structural Features: Prominent telangiectasias (fine, linear blood vessels) are visible, particularly within multiple erythematous to violaceous macules and patches. There is significant background solar elastosis and central scar-like atrophy.
