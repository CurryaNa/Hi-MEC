1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), with subtle violaceous (purplish) undertones and scattered pinpoint white-to-yellow foci.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with the erythema diffusely blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven and bumpy with prominent, dilated pores. The surface appears moist or oily with some areas of fine hemorrhagic crusting.
5. Structural Features: Diffuse background erythema, prominent follicular ostia (pores), and fine, linear telangiectasias (tiny, broken blood vessels).
