1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) with slight violaceous tones and a whitish, scaly center.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated but with an irregular, slightly serpiginous outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and internal structure.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, with a fine, adherent, whitish scale, particularly over the central portion.
5. Structural Features: A thin plaque with a slightly raised border and evidence of central atrophy (thinning and slight depression of the skin). Vellus hairs are present throughout the lesion.
