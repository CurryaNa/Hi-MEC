1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red) and pink, with areas of whitish scale and darker red erosions.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and diffuse, irregularly blending into the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall distribution of the lesion is asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Rough and uneven, with fine scaling, small erosions, and some slight crusting.
5. Structural Features: Diffuse erythema with scattered erythematous micropapules and possible microvesicles. There are confluent patches with erosions, particularly in the skin fold. Fine telangiectasias are visible.
