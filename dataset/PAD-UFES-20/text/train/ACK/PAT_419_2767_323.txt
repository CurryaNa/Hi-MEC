1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), violaceous (purple), and lighter pink tones.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic, slightly shiny, and wrinkled, with fine surface changes but no significant scaling or crusting.
5. Structural Features: An infiltrated plaque with subtle central atrophy, fine telangiectasias (small, visible blood vessels), and a somewhat translucent quality, set within a background of sun-damaged skin (solar elastosis).
