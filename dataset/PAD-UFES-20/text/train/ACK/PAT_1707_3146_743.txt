1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-white, and brown to black.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with some lesions appearing to coalesce into larger patches.
3. Shape Symmetry: The individual lesions are asymmetrically shaped and vary in size.
4. Surface Texture: Rough, dry, and scaly, with areas of adherent keratotic crust and focal erosions.
5. Structural Features: Multiple erythematous papules and thin plaques with overlying scale-crust. Some lesions exhibit central erosion and hemorrhagic crusting. The surrounding skin displays mottled pigmentation, indicative of chronic sun damage.
