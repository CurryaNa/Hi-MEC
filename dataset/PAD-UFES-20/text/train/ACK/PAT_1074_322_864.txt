1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish-yellow, and scattered brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Rough, dry, and scaly with some adherent yellowish crusting.
5. Structural Features: A poorly demarcated plaque with overlying hyperkeratosis (scale). There are a few scattered, irregular brown macules and a focal, more intensely erythematous papule that may represent an early erosion.
