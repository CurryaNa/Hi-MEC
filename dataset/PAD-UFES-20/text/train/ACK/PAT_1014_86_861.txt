1. Primary Colors: Pink, translucent white, and red.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion has a raised, pearly, and somewhat irregular border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Broadly symmetrical in its ovoid shape.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and waxy with a central area of fine scaling and slight depression.
5. Structural Features: Prominent fine arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels) are visible across the surface, particularly at the periphery. There is a subtle central atrophy or early ulceration.
