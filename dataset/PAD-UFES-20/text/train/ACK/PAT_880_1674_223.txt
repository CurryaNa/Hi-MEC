1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish-yellow, and light brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with some components showing an arcuate or serpiginous edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Varied, with areas of dry, adherent scale and other areas appearing smooth and atrophic (thin and shiny).
5. Structural Features: An erythematous plaque with prominent whitish-yellow scaling, particularly at the edges, and significant central atrophy. Some areas exhibit an annular (ring-like) configuration.
