1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, translucent, pink-red (erythema).
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, with stellate or dendritic-like projections; poorly circumscribed.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Waxy and slightly verrucous, with fine central crusting.
5. Structural Features: A central cluster of small, coalescing, yellowish-white papules surrounded by a patch of erythema.
