1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-white, grayish.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and moderately defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Rough, dry, and hyperkeratotic with adherent, thick, yellowish-white scale and crust.
5. Structural Features: Raised papules and plaques with prominent central, warty (verrucous) scale and crust on an erythematous base.
