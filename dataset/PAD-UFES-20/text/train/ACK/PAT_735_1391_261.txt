1. Primary Colors: Whitish-yellow, pink, and red (erythematous).
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with the erythematous halo blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and keratotic, with a surrounding smooth to slightly atrophic texture.
5. Structural Features: A central, shallow ulceration covered by a firm, whitish-yellow adherent crust, surrounded by an erythematous halo.
