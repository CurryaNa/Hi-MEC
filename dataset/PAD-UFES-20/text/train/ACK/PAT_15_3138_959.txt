1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with shades of brown, pink, red, and white/hypopigmented areas.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with indistinct margins that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in overall shape and in the distribution of color and texture.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed, with areas of fine scaling, smoothness associated with atrophy, and a slightly waxy or pearly sheen.
5. Structural Features: The lesion exhibits multiple components, including atrophic, white, scar-like patches, erythematous (red) areas with fine telangiectasias (small, dilated blood vessels), and scattered, irregular brown pigmentation. A slightly raised, translucent plaque is also present.
