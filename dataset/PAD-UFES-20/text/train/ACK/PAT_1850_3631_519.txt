1. Primary Colors: White, various shades of brown (light to dark), and pink.
2. Border Appearance: The overall affected area is large with ill-defined borders; internal white macules have irregular but relatively distinct borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic, with fine wrinkling and areas of slight scaling.
5. Structural Features: A mottled pattern of reticulated hyperpigmentation mixed with confluent and punctate macules of depigmentation and areas of erythema, consistent with poikiloderma.
