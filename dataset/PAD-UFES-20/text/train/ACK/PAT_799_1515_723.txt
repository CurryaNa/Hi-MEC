1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, pink, and red.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming a circular boundary.
3. Shape Symmetry: Generally symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic, crusted, and verrucous (wart-like) with dry, adherent scale.
5. Structural Features: Raised (exophytic) papule featuring a central, firm, keratotic core and a surrounding erythematous halo.
