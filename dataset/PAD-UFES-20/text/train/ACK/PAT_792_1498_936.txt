1. Primary Colors: White, grey, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat indistinct, with a feathery or stellate pattern of scaling at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, both in its overall shape and the distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly, with a central area of adherent crusting or keratotic material.
5. Structural Features: A central, slightly depressed (umbilicated) area containing grey-to-black keratotic plugging, surrounded by a whitish, scaly, slightly elevated rim.
