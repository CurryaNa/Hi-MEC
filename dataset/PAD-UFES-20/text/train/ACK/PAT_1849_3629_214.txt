1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), violaceous, white (hypopigmentation and scale), and mottled brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Highly asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Heterogeneous, with atrophic (thin, shiny) areas, patches of fine white adherent scale (keratotic), and focal crusting.
5. Structural Features: Multiple erythematous papules and ill-defined plaques on a background of poikiloderma (atrophy, telangiectasias, and pigmentary changes). Some papules are topped with scale, and there are small erosions present.
