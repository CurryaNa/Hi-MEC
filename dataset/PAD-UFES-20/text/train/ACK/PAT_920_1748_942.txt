1. Primary Colors: Erythematous pink, red, with areas of white and yellowish-white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and focally crusted, with dry, adherent white scale.
