1. Primary Colors: White, pinkish-violaceous, and a focal area of dark blue.
2. Border Appearance: The central whitish patch has ill-defined and irregular borders. The adjacent blue macule has well-defined borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall lesion complex is asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: The central area is atrophic and finely wrinkled with effacement of skin lines. The blue area is smooth and macular.
5. Structural Features: A central atrophic, hypopigmented plaque is present. Adjacent to this is a small, distinct, homogenous dark blue macule.
