1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pinkish-red), white to yellowish scale, and a central area of beefy red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly the erythematous component which blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted at the periphery, with a central area of erosion or ulceration that appears moist.
5. Structural Features: A central, irregularly shaped ulceration with an overlying crust, surrounded by a slightly raised, erythematous rim.
