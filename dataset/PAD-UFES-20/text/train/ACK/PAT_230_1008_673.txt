1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish-yellow, and tan-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin. The lesion is outlined by an external black ink marking.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and internal color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and dry, with a firm, adherent, whitish-yellow hyperkeratotic crust in the center.
5. Structural Features: A central, crusted papule with surrounding erythema and fine scale. The central crust may represent ulceration or hyperkeratosis.
