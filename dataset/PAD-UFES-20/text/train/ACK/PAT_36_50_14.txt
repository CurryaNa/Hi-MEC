1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pinkish-red) with overlying white, flaky scale.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly, with fine, lamellar desquamation.
5. Structural Features: A slightly raised plaque with prominent, loosely adherent scales and partial effacement of skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
