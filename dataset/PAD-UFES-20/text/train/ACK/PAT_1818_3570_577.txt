1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red, pale white, and areas of hypopigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding sun-damaged skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and elongated in shape.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic, smooth, and finely wrinkled, with loss of normal skin markings (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: A slightly depressed patch characterized by atrophy and erythema, with some fine, visible telangiectasias (small blood vessels).
