1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), violaceous (purple-blue), and whitish-gray.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven and slightly atrophic, with areas of fine scale and prominent follicular openings.
5. Structural Features: Polychromia (multiple colors), scattered fine telangiectasias, and distinct violaceous patches within an erythematous plaque.
