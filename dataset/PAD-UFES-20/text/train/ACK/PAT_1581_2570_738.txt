1. Primary Colors: Variegated with pink-red (erythema), mottled light to dark brown hyperpigmentation, and white to pale pink atrophic areas.
2. Border Appearance: The overall area has ill-defined, irregular borders that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Highly asymmetrical in both overall shape and the internal distribution of color and texture.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed texture, predominantly atrophic (thin, shiny, finely wrinkled) with areas of fine scaling and focal crusting.
5. Structural Features: Displays features of severe photodamage including atrophy, mottled dyspigmentation, scattered fine telangiectasias, and several small, crusted erosions or ulcerations.
