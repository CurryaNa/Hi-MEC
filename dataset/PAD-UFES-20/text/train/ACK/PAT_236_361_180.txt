1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-tan with an erythematous (pink) base.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated at its base, clearly distinguished from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is a vertically projecting, conical structure that is largely symmetrical along its central axis.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic and firm, with a somewhat waxy appearance.
5. Structural Features: An exophytic, conical, horn-like papule composed of compacted keratin.
