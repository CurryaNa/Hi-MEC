1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, and skin-toned to slightly hypopigmented.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is a diffuse patch without a distinct, uniform shape.
4. Surface Texture: Dry with a fine, subtle scale, and a slightly shiny, atrophic quality in some areas.
5. Structural Features: A broad erythematous patch containing scattered, fine telangiectasias (small, dilated blood vessels) and a few small erythematous papules. There is some effacement of normal skin markings.
