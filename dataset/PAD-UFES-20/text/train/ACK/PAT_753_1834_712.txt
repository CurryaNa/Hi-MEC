1. Primary Colors: Multiple shades of brown, tan, black, and focal areas of white to flesh-colored hypopigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: The pigmented areas have ill-defined, irregular borders that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall distribution of pigment and texture is asymmetrical, and individual components are irregularly shaped.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic, dry, and finely wrinkled with areas of fine scaling and focal adherent crusting.
5. Structural Features: A background of mottled hyperpigmentation (solar lentigines), scattered dark brown to black macules, focal hypopigmented atrophic patches, and small, slightly elevated papules with surface scale and crust.
