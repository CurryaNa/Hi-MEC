1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), yellowish-white, and violaceous-tan.
2. Border Appearance: The central plaque has a relatively well-demarcated, irregular border; the surrounding erythema has ill-defined borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.

5. Structural Features: A central, round, yellowish-white, crusted plaque, suggestive of ulceration or hyperkeratosis, is surrounded by an erythematous halo. The adjacent skin shows signs of atrophy.
