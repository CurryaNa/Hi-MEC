1. Primary Colors: Variegated, including pearly white, pink-red, light brown, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with indistinct margins that blend into the surrounding sun-damaged skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and structure.

5. Structural Features: A multicomponent lesion featuring a central, translucent pearly papule, focal erosion/ulceration with crust, and irregular, blotchy pigmentation. Dark linear markings are also present.
