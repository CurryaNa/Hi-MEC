1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, brown, gray-black, and yellow.
2. Border Appearance: The overall lesion is a patch with ill-defined and irregular borders that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic and scaly with areas of crusting and a focal smooth papule.
5. Structural Features: An ill-defined erythematous patch containing a distinct pigmented papule, focal scale-crust, and multiple areas of mottled pigmentation on a background of severe solar elastosis (wrinkling and atrophy).
