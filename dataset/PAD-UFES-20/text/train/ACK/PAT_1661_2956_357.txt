1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), with whitish-silver and translucent areas.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is a diffuse patch without a distinct, regular shape.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and slightly rough with a fine, shiny scale.
5. Structural Features: Diffuse erythema, multiple small pearly papules, and fine arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels).
