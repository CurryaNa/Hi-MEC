1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, light brown, dark brown, and whitish-yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with multiple lesions blending into the surrounding skin, characteristic of a field change.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the area consists of multiple, irregularly shaped and randomly distributed lesions.
4. Surface Texture: Varied, featuring areas of fine, whitish, adherent scale over a rough and atrophic-appearing background.
5. Structural Features: Multiple erythematous (pink-red) macules and patches with overlying scale. Mottled hyperpigmentation (brown macules) is present throughout. The overall appearance is consistent with chronic sun damage (dermatoheliosis).
