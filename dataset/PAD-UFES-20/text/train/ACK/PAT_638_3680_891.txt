1. Primary Colors: Variegated, featuring shades of pink, tan, brown, focal grey-black pigmentation, and areas of white scale.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with margins that are difficult to distinguish from the surrounding photodamaged skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and internal structures.

5. Structural Features: A complex pattern of variegated pigmentation, focal scaling, and areas of apparent atrophy (shiny, thinned skin). A darker pigmented focus is noted near the center.
