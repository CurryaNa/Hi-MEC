1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink), hypopigmented (whitish), and hyperpigmented (light brown) mottling.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined, with changes blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The affected area is an irregularly shaped patch, making it asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic and finely wrinkled with a slightly dry, subtly scaly appearance.
5. Structural Features: Presence of poikiloderma, characterized by a combination of atrophy, fine telangiectasias (visible small vessels), and mottled dyspigmentation (both hyper- and hypopigmentation). Small, depressed scars are also noted.
