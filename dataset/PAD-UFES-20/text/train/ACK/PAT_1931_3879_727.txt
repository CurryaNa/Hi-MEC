1. Primary Colors: Violaceous, pink, and whitish, with scattered small brown-to-black specks.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, particularly on the lateral aspects where it blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, with fine, whitish, adherent scaling and areas of slight crusting.
5. Structural Features: A slightly atrophic patch with central whitening and scaling, and multifocal, small, dark specks of pigment/crust.
