1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red (erythematous) base with a central yellowish-white, pearly component.
2. Border Appearance: The erythematous base has an ill-defined border, while the central keratotic component is more distinctly demarcated but irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and keratotic (horny/scaly) with a rough appearance.
5. Structural Features: Erythematous papule with a central, firm-appearing, yellowish-white keratotic plug or crust.
