1. Primary Colors: Dark brown, black, pink, and red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, with notching and fading of pigment into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven, with a central atrophic (thinned) and slightly wrinkled appearance.
5. Structural Features: A central pink, scar-like area suggestive of regression, surrounded by irregular dark brown to black pigmentation. Fine, linear telangiectasias (small blood vessels) are present in the central area.
