1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), hyperpigmented (light to dark brown), and white/silvery.
2. Border Appearance: The borders of the individual papules and plaques are ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall affected area and the individual lesions are asymmetrical and irregularly shaped.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is rough, dry, and scaly with adherent, fine, whitish scales and areas of crusting.
5. Structural Features: Multiple, discrete erythematous papules and plaques are present on a background of mottled hyperpigmentation. There are overlying adherent scales and numerous linear and arcuate excoriations, some with dark crusts.
