1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), white, and dark brown/black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a raised, slightly rolled edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Roughly symmetrical in its overall round shape, but asymmetrical in color and structural distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally scaly (hyperkeratotic) and crusted over a rough, uneven base.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule or nodule with central ulceration covered by adherent white scale and dark, hemorrhagic crust. An erythematous halo surrounds the central lesion.
