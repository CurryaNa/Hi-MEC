1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, white, and light brown, presenting as mottled pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; presents as a diffuse field change rather than a discrete, symmetrical lesion.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic, slightly scaly, and rough with fine wrinkling.
5. Structural Features: Mottled pigmentation (dyschromia), fine telangiectasias, and signs of skin atrophy.
