1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red), light brown, and focal whitish areas.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, forming an irregularly shaped patch.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly, with some areas of fine crusting and slight atrophy.
5. Structural Features: Multiple coalescing erythematous macules and papules on a background of mottled hyperpigmentation. A central papule exhibits a whitish scale and possible early ulceration. The surrounding skin shows signs of solar elastosis (sun damage).
