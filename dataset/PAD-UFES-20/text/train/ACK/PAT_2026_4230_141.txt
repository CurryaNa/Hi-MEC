1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red) with areas of faint brown hyperpigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular borders; individual lesions are poorly demarcated and tend to coalesce.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; consists of multiple, irregularly shaped macules and patches distributed across the forehead.
4. Surface Texture: Dry with a fine, subtle scale, superimposed on chronically sun-damaged, wrinkled skin.
5. Structural Features: Multiple erythematous macules and patches, some of which may be slightly raised (papular), without obvious ulceration or prominent telangiectasias. The lesions are present within a field of solar elastosis (prominent wrinkles).
