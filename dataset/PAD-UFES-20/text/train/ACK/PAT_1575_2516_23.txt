1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, and light brown on a background of fair skin.
2. Border Appearance: The borders of the individual erythematous macules are ill-defined and blend into the surrounding skin; where lesions coalesce, they form larger patches with irregular, indistinct borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall distribution of lesions is asymmetrical; individual lesions are variably round to oval but form asymmetrical patches when confluent.
4. Surface Texture: Predominantly dry with areas of fine scale and slight atrophy. Some more inflamed papules exhibit central erosion and crusting.
5. Structural Features: Multiple erythematous macules and papules, some of which are confluent. Scattered light brown macules consistent with post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation are present. A few papules show central erosion or a small crusted dell.
