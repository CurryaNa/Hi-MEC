1. Primary Colors: White, yellow, and erythematous (red).
2. Border Appearance: The peripheral scale creates a well-demarcated, slightly irregular border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical, presenting as a round lesion.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and hyperkeratotic with adherent white scale and a central crusted area.
5. Structural Features: A central crateriform depression filled with a yellowish crust, surrounded by a raised, firm border with an adherent white collarette of scale.
