1. Primary Colors: Light brown, dark brown, pink, and red.
2. Border Appearance: The individual pigmented macules have ill-defined and irregular borders, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall pattern is asymmetrical, composed of multiple small, variably-sized, round to oval macules.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and finely wrinkled with evident skin lines, suggesting slight atrophy.
5. Structural Features: Multiple hyperpigmented macules (lentigines), scattered erythematous areas, and fine, linear telangiectasias (visible vessels) are present across the surface, characteristic of dermatoheliosis (sun damage).
