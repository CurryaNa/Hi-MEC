1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous) base with an overlying white to off-white scale.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated, with a slightly irregular, round-to-oval outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, rough, and scaly (squamous) with adherent, somewhat crusted scale.
5. Structural Features: Erythematous papule or plaque characterized by significant hyperkeratosis (thickened scale) on its surface.
