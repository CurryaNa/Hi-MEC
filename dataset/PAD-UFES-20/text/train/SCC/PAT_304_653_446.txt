1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-tan, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular yet fairly well-demarcated, characterized by a raised, rolled, and pearly edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Approximately symmetrical in its overall round shape.
4. Surface Texture: A combination of a smooth, waxy peripheral rim and a central area with adherent yellowish crust and fine white scale.
5. Structural Features: A central depression or ulceration covered by crust and scale, surrounded by a distinct, elevated, rolled border.
