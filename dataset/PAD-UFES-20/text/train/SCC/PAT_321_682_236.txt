1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), brown, and yellow.
2. Border Appearance: The central ulcer has a defined, slightly irregular border; the peripheral erythema has an ill-defined border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical, presenting as a round-to-oval lesion.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted with an adherent eschar; the surrounding erythematous rim appears slightly scaly.
5. Structural Features: A central, crateriform ulceration covered by a hemorrhagic crust, with a raised, rolled border and surrounding erythema.
