1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, translucent white, and a focal dark brown crust.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with indistinct boundaries that merge with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color pattern.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular, with atrophic (thinned and shiny) areas, fine scaling, and a small central crust.
5. Structural Features: Pearly, translucent papules, fine arborizing telangiectasias (delicate, branching blood vessels), and a central focal erosion.
