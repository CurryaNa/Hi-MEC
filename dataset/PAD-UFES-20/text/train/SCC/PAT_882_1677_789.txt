1. Primary Colors: White, yellow, brown, and black, with surrounding erythema (redness).
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic and crusted with adherent, friable scale.
5. Structural Features: An elevated papule with central ulceration covered by a dark eschar/crust and surrounded by thick, whitish-yellow scale.
