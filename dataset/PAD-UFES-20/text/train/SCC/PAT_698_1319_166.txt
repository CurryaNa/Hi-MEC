1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) base with central whitish-yellow deposits.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming a circular outline with a subtly raised, rolled edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical and dome-shaped.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic and crusted, with a rough, verrucous, and friable-appearing central scale.
5. Structural Features: A raised, firm-appearing nodule with a central crateriform depression filled with a prominent keratin plug.
