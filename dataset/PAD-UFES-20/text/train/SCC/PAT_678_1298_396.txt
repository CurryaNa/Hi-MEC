1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous) and pearly white/translucent.
2. Border Appearance: Irregularly ovoid with a raised, rolled, and somewhat ill-defined border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, waxy, and slightly uneven.
5. Structural Features: A central pearly papule with fine, branching telangiectasias (blood vessels) on the surface and a suggestion of central depression.
