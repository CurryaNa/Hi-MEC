1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, brown, and red, with a surrounding erythematous (pink-red) halo.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming a distinct circular boundary with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Symmetrical, presenting as a round plaque.
