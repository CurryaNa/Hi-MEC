1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, and focal areas of yellow-orange.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with the central ulcerated component having a more distinct, raised border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Moist, glistening, ulcerated, and partially crusted.
