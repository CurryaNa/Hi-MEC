1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), white, and focal dark red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and raised, with some areas appearing well-demarcated and others ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in shape, color, and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Exophytic, verrucous (wart-like), and friable with areas of ulceration and crust.
5. Structural Features: A fungating, nodular mass with central ulceration, focal hemorrhage, and multiple white, keratotic papules on the surface.
