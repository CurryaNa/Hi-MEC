1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) with overlying yellowish-tan scale-crust.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, polycyclic, and moderately well-demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted, with dry, adherent, flaky scale on an erythematous base.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous plaque with prominent, patchy, yellowish scale-crust. Some areas exhibit an arcuate or annular configuration. Fine hairs traverse the lesion.
