1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-green, white-yellow, and surrounding erythema (redness).
2. Border Appearance: The border of the central yellow component is irregular and blurred, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical with an elongated, somewhat oval shape.
