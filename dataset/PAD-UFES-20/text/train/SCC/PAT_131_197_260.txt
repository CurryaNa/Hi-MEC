1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, pearly-white, and focal yellow-orange.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, with a raised, rolled quality in some sections.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven and lobulated with areas of erosion and serous crusting.
5. Structural Features: A multi-nodular lesion with a translucent, pearly appearance, prominent fine, branching telangiectasias on the surface of the nodules, and central ulceration.
