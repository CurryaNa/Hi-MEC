1. Primary Colors: White, erythematous (pink-red), dark red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally scaly and crusted with a dry, keratotic appearance.
5. Structural Features: A central, adherent white scale with an adjacent small focus of erosion and hemorrhagic crusting, surrounded by a subtle erythematous halo.
