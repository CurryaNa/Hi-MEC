1. Primary Colors: Violaceous, pink, and red with overlying white scale.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and relatively defined, with a fine collarette of scale at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and roughly ovoid.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly, with fine, adherent whitish scales over the surface.
5. Structural Features: A raised, firm-appearing nodule with a central, more erythematous papule and subtle central erosion.
