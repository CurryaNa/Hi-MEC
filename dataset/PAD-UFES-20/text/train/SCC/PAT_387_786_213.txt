1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, and a central tan-brown crust.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and slightly irregular, with a distinctly raised, rolled quality.
3. Shape Symmetry: Roughly symmetrical in its overall round to oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted centrally with fine, white scaling (desquamation) along the peripheral border.
5. Structural Features: A prominent, elevated annular (ring-like) border surrounding a central depression or ulceration.
