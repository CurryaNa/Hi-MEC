1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, and reddish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated with an irregular, somewhat lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and distribution of surface features.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular and bumpy with a focal area of erosion covered by hemorrhagic crust.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, firm-appearing nodule with a pearly, translucent quality at the periphery and central ulceration.
