1. Primary Colors: Violaceous, pink, yellowish-brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and moderately defined, with some borders blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of color and crust.
4. Surface Texture: Rough and uneven, dominated by a thick, adherent, hyperkeratotic central crust.
5. Structural Features: A raised (nodular) lesion with central ulceration covered by a prominent, exophytic, keratotic, and hemorrhagic crust.
