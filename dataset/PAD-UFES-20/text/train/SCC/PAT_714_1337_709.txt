1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), white, and yellowish-white, with a focal area of brighter red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and relatively well-demarcated, though partially obscured by overlying scale and surgical markings.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of surface features.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and rough, characterized by adherent, flaky, white-to-yellowish scales (hyperkeratotic) and focal crusting.
5. Structural Features: Raised, firm-appearing papule or plaque with prominent surface scaling and a small central erosion.
