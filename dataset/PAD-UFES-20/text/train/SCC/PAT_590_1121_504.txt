1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, whitish-yellow, and dark red-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly at the periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly (keratotic) and centrally crusted.
5. Structural Features: A central area of hemorrhagic crusting and erosion, surrounded by a ring of white hyperkeratosis and a peripheral erythematous halo.
