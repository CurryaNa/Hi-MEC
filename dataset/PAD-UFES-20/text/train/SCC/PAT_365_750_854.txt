1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-white, and a focal dark red-brown area.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with some scalloped edges.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Rough with adherent, hyperkeratotic (scaly) and crusted areas.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous plaque with central yellowish scale-crust, a small focus of erosion or ulceration, and surrounding subtle atrophy.
