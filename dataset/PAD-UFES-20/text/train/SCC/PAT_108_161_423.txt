1. Primary Colors: Red, white/pearly, yellowish-brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, with some sections appearing raised and rolled, while other sections are obscured by crust.
3. Shape Symmetry: Highly asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Polymorphous, featuring a moist, glistening, eroded area (ulceration) adjacent to a heavily crusted, verrucous (wart-like), and hyperkeratotic component.
5. Structural Features: A complex lesion composed of a central, non-healing ulcer with a raised, pearly, rolled border, and an adjacent large, exophytic, keratotic growth consistent with a cutaneous horn.
