1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish-pale, and a central tan/brown focus.
2. Border Appearance: Well-defined, raised, and mostly regular, forming a distinct annular rim.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical, presenting as a roughly circular lesion.
4. Surface Texture: The peripheral rim appears smooth and somewhat pearly; the central area is slightly atrophic with a small point of central crusting.
5. Structural Features: Annular lesion with a raised, rolled, erythematous border and a paler, slightly depressed (atrophic) center containing a focal dark crust.
