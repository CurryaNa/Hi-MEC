1. Primary Colors: Red, white, yellow-green, and small dark blue/black foci.
2. Border Appearance: The border is irregular and somewhat indistinct, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both color distribution and overall shape.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is heterogeneous, featuring a moist, glistening, ulcerated area and an adjacent thick, friable, crusted area.
5. Structural Features: This is an exophytic lesion characterized by a large, red, ulcerated base and a superior, adherent yellow-green crust. Within the crust, there are small dark blue/black specks. A whitish, macerated-appearing collar is present at the interface between the ulceration and the crust.
