1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (reddish-pink), white, and yellowish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated but irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is largely symmetrical in its overall circular shape, but the central crusting and ulceration are asymmetrically distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated and crusted, surrounded by a peripheral collarette of scale.
5. Structural Features: An exophytic (raised) papule with a central crater containing keratinous debris and crust, surrounded by an erythematous, firm-appearing rim.
