1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-violaceous, yellowish-brown, and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated, ovoid, with a slightly irregular outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, primarily due to the irregularly shaped and distributed central crust.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted, hyperkeratotic, and scaly; appears dry and friable.
5. Structural Features: A raised papulonodule with a prominent, centrally located, adherent keratotic crust.
