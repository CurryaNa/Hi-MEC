1. Primary Colors: Whitish-gray, yellowish-white, and a pink erythematous base.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and sharply defined, with an irregular and lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and surface topography.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and hyperkeratotic, giving it a rough, scaly, and crusted appearance.
5. Structural Features: Raised (exophytic) papule composed of multiple small lobules, creating a papillomatous surface with visible keratin-filled crevices.
