1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), deep red, with central whitish-yellow and dark brown/black specks.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with a more distinct, raised inner border surrounding the central defect.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in overall shape, color, and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed; the center is ulcerated and crusted, while the surrounding rim appears smooth and somewhat pearly or waxy.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration with overlying serosanguinous crust is the dominant feature, surrounded by a raised, rolled, translucent border with visible fine, branching blood vessels (telangiectasias).
