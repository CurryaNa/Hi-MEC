1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), white/silvery, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with scale blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Predominantly scaly (hyperkeratotic) and dry, with a centrally crusted and eroded area.
5. Structural Features: A plaque with prominent, adherent, silvery-white scale surrounding a central area of erosion, hemorrhagic crusting, and a small black eschar.
