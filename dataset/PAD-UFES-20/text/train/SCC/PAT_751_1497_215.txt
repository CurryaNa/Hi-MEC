1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) and violaceous, with a central dark brown to black focus.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-circumscribed but with indistinct, blurred borders that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Grossly symmetrical with a round to oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth over the raised periphery with a central crusted and depressed area.
5. Structural Features: A raised, dome-shaped papule/nodule with prominent central umbilication or a crateriform center containing a keratotic plug or crust.
