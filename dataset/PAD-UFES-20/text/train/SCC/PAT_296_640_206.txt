1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), pearly white, and yellowish-brown, with a central hemorrhagic (dark red/brown) crust.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated, raised, and slightly irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Grossly symmetrical in its overall circular shape.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated and crusted; the peripheral border is smooth and waxy.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration covered by a hemorrhagic crust, a raised and translucent ("pearly") rolled border, and fine telangiectasias (small, visible blood vessels) on the border.
