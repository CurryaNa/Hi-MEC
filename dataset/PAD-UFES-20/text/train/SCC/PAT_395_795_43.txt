1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) base, whitish-gray scale, and a focal reddish-brown central point.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with the periphery partially obscured by overlying scale.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion has an irregular, non-uniform shape.
4. Surface Texture: Markedly scaly, dry, and rough with adherent, thick, whitish hyperkeratotic scale.
5. Structural Features: Raised erythematous plaque with prominent scaling and a small central erosion with hemorrhagic crusting.
