1. Primary Colors: Pink-violaceous, white, and a focal dark brown spot.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated with a slightly irregular, rounded outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is roughly symmetrical, but the surface features (scale and central dark spot) are asymmetrically distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic and scaly, with an adherent, dry, white scale that appears fissured.
5. Structural Features: An elevated papule with a pinkish-violaceous base, partially covered by a thick keratotic scale, and a small central dark crust or erosion.
