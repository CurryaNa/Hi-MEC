1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pinkish-red), yellowish-brown, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular in shape, but relatively well-demarcated from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and rough, with a central, adherent crust and peripheral fine scaling.
5. Structural Features: A slightly raised papule/plaque with a central, yellowish-brown, crusted area surrounded by an erythematous rim and a whitish, scaly collarette.
