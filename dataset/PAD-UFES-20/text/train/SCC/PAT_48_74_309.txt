1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-tan (central), erythematous/pink (peripheral).
2. Border Appearance: The central component has a well-defined, regular, round border; the surrounding erythematous halo has an ill-defined outer border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its overall round shape.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted with a thick, waxy, and adherent crust.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule featuring a central, firm, yellowish crust surrounded by a distinct erythematous halo.
