1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), white, and yellowish-tan, with a small focus of black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and relatively well-demarcated from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted, with a rough, verrucous appearance.
5. Structural Features: A raised (papular) lesion with central adherent yellowish crusting and surrounding white scale on an erythematous base, suggestive of hyperkeratosis and possible underlying ulceration.
