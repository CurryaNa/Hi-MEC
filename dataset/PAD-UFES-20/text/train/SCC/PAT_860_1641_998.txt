1. Primary Colors: Reddish-brown centrally, surrounded by an erythematous pink halo, with a fine whitish rim at the edge of the central erosion.
2. Border Appearance: The central erosion has a well-defined, irregular border. The surrounding erythematous halo has an ill-defined, indistinct border that blends into the normal skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; both the central feature and the surrounding erythema are irregular in shape and distribution.
4. Surface Texture: The central area is moist and appears eroded or ulcerated with an adherent crust. The surrounding skin is smooth but inflamed, with some fine scaling visible in the periphery.
5. Structural Features: A central, shallow, "punched-out" erosion with a hemorrhagic crust, encircled by a prominent inflammatory halo (erythema).
