1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), dark brown to black, and yellowish-white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and not sharply demarcated, with some sections appearing rolled.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Ulcerated, friable, and crusted, with some glistening, moist areas.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic nodule with central ulceration, hemorrhagic crusting (black/dark brown areas), serous or purulent exudate (yellowish-white crust), and visible fine, arborizing telangiectasias on the surface.
