1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) base with overlying white and a central focal point of dark brown/black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin in some areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and the distribution of surface features.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly, characterized by adherent white flakes (hyperkeratosis).
5. Structural Features: A slightly raised erythematous plaque with central hyperkeratotic scale and a small, dark, crusted focus suggesting erosion or ulceration.
