1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), yellowish-tan, and scattered dark brown to black specks.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is roughly round and largely symmetrical, but the internal color and texture are asymmetrically distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally rough, scaly, and crusted.
5. Structural Features: Raised papule with central yellowish scale-crust, fine telangiectasias at the periphery, and small dark crusted foci. Hairs are seen growing through the lesion.
