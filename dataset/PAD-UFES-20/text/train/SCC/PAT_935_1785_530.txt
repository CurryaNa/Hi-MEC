1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), brown, black, and yellowish-white.
2. Border Appearance: The overall border is ill-defined and irregular; the central crusted components have more sharply demarcated but irregular edges.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and the distribution of color and texture.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted, with adherent, rough, and heaped-up scale-crust.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous plaque with central, irregular scale-crust, foci of brown and black pigmentation, and fine telangiectasias visible on the surrounding erythematous base.
