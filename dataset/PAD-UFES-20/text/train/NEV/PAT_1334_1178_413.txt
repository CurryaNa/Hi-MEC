1. Primary Colors: Variegated with multiple shades of dark brown and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and notched, with some areas being sharply demarcated and others slightly less defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Lobulated and papillomatous, creating a cerebriform (brain-like) or cobblestone appearance.
5. Structural Features: Raised, exophytic nodule with a complex, convoluted surface containing fissures and a multi-component pigment pattern.
