1. Primary Colors: Light brown and tan, with scattered, minute dark brown specks.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and lobulated, but well-demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and mamillated, creating a bumpy, cobblestone-like surface.
5. Structural Features: Elevated (exophytic) lesion composed of multiple coalescing, soft-appearing globules. A small, distinct papule is noted at the inferior-lateral aspect.
