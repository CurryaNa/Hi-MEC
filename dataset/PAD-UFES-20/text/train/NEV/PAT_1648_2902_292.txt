1. Primary Colors: Skin-colored, pinkish-tan, with subtle light brown areas.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated but with an irregular, scalloped outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical due to multiple lobules of varying sizes.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and papillomatous, with a dry, slightly bumpy surface.
5. Structural Features: Raised, exophytic papule with a cerebriform (brain-like) or cobblestone surface composed of multiple coalescing lobules.
