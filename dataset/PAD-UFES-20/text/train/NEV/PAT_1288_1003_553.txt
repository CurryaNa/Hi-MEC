1. Primary Colors: Violaceous (purplish-brown), pink, and focal dark brown to black areas.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and lobulated, creating a bumpy, "mulberry-like" surface.
5. Structural Features: Raised (exophytic) papule with a multi-lobulated surface and small, dark punctate foci within the lesion.
