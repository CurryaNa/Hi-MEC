1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, light brown, and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with some segments appearing more distinct than others.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Varied; the erythematous areas exhibit a fine scale, while the pigmented portion appears slightly rough and possibly crusted.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent lesion composed of an erythematous patch, an eccentrically located, irregularly pigmented nodular area, and a smaller, satellite erythematous papule.
