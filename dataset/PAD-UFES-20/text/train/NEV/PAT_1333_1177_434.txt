1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with shades of pinkish-brown, dark brown, and gray-blue.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical with a round to oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: Mammillated (bumpy) and cerebriform (brain-like), with a lobulated, cobblestone appearance.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic papules/nodules with distinct grooves between lobules; the larger lesion displays heterogeneous pigmentation with darker central areas.
