1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with prominent shades of red, pink, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and not sharply defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and verrucous, with areas of crusting.
5. Structural Features: Raised (exophytic) nodule with significant color variegation, including dark brown/black blotches and globules. A small, possibly pedunculated, structure is present at the superior aspect.
