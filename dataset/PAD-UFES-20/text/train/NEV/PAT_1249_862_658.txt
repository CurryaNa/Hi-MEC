1. Primary Colors: Gray, light brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated but irregular with a lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and cerebriform (brain-like) with fissures.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, polypoid papule with a lobulated surface and multiple dark, comedo-like openings (keratin-filled pits).
