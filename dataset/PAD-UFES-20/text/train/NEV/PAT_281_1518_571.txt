1. Primary Colors: Pink and flesh-toned.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and clearly defined, with a slightly irregular, scalloped outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, composed of multiple unequal, fused lobules.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and mammillated, with a cobblestone-like surface created by numerous rounded projections.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic (raised) nodule with a distinct cerebriform (brain-like) pattern, characterized by deep furrows and clefts separating the individual lobules.
