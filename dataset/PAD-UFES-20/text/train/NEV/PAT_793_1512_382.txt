1. Primary Colors: A central dark brown pigment surrounded by a lighter reddish-brown and pinkish halo.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and feathery, with irregular margins that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is not uniform in shape or color distribution if bisected.
4. Surface Texture: Appears smooth and flat (macular), with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved across the surface.
5. Structural Features: A central, more concentrated area of pigmentation with a peripheral, less dense, erythematous flare.
