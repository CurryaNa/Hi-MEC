1. Primary Colors: Pink, light brown.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated, irregular with a lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and mammillated (bumpy with small protuberances).
5. Structural Features: Exophytic papule with a cerebriform (brain-like) surface, featuring multiple coalescing lobules and fine sulci (grooves).
