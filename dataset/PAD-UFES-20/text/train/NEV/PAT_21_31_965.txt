1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous, and translucent pearly-white.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular with a rounded contour.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and waxy with a slight sheen.
5. Structural Features: Raised, dome-shaped papule with a translucent, pearly quality and a subtle, slightly raised or rolled border. Fine, thread-like vessels (telangiectasias) may be present on the surface.
