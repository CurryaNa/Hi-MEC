1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-brown (honey-colored).
2. Border Appearance: The border of the central crusted area is irregular but discernible; the surrounding erythema has ill-defined borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted; an adherent, yellowish-brown crust covers a central erosion.
5. Structural Features: A central shallow erosion is covered by a honey-colored crust, located within a skin fold, and surrounded by a rim of erythema.
