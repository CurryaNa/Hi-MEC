1. Primary Colors: Pink, flesh-toned.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated, irregular, and lobulated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; comprised of at least two distinct, irregularly shaped, coalescing nodules.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and verrucous with a cerebriform (brain-like) or cauliflower-like surface.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, multinodular growth with deep sulci (grooves) separating the individual lobules. Terminal hairs are observed growing through the lesion.
