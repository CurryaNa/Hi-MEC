1. Primary Colors: Light brown, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and partially indistinct, especially at the inferior aspect.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Waxy, crusted, and slightly mammillated (bumpy).
5. Structural Features: A raised (papular) lesion with multiple dark brown to black globules and clods, resembling comedo-like openings, concentrated in the superior half.
