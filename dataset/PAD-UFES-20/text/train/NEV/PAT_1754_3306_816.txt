1. Primary Colors: Light brown, dark brown, and reddish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with some areas fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, with fine skin lines (dermatoglyphics) preserved across the surface.
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent pattern with a central, darker, more heavily pigmented blotch surrounded by a lighter brown, more uniform patch.
