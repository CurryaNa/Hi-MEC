1. Primary Colors: Brown, dark brown, black, and pinkish-red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly defined, with some areas appearing smudged or blurred.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both contour and distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular and complex, featuring both macular (flat) components and raised, papulonodular (bumpy) areas.
5. Structural Features: Multicomponent pattern with marked polychromia, raised verrucous/papular central elements, and multiple black globules and dots, particularly at the periphery.
