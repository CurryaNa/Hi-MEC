1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red to violaceous with subtle brown undertones.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated with a slightly irregular, scalloped or lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is irregularly ovoid.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and mammillated, creating a bumpy, cobblestone-like surface.
5. Structural Features: Raised (exophytic) papule with a lobulated surface structure. A single, minute dark speck is visible on the right periphery.
