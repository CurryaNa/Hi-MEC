1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, translucent pearly-white, and a focal dark brown/black dot.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly the erythematous component.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: Glistening and moist, with a smooth but eroded or ulcerated central area.
5. Structural Features: A central, translucent papule with fine, branching telangiectasias (arborizing vessels) on its surface. There is a small central ulceration containing a tiny dark brown dot/crust. Exogenous purple markings are present at the lesion's periphery.
