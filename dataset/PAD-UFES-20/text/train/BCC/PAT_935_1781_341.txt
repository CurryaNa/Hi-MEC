1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), red, and pearly-white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Translucent and waxy with areas of fine scale and central erosion.
