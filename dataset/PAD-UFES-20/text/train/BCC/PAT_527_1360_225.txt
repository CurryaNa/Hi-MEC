1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish-yellow, and a central dark brown to hemorrhagic red focus.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming a roughly circular outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Grossly symmetrical in its overall round shape, though internal features are asymmetrically distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally hyperkeratotic, crusted, and verrucous, with a smoother, firm-appearing peripheral rim.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, dome-shaped papule with a central keratin-filled crater containing a hemorrhagic crust, surrounded by a raised erythematous shoulder.
