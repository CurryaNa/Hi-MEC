1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), violaceous (purple-blue), whitish, yellow-brown, and dark gray-black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with a raised, inflammatory rim that blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in shape, color, and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Heterogeneous, featuring areas of yellow-brown crusting, central smooth atrophy, and surrounding induration.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration with a whitish, atrophic floor, surrounded by a raised, violaceous, and erythematous border. Foci of crusting and a superior area of dark pigmentation are present.
