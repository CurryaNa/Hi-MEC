1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous base), brown, black, and whitish-yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated in some areas, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Rough, dry, and crusted with adherent, keratotic scale.
5. Structural Features: Erythematous plaque with a central, thick, multi-colored (brown, black, white) crust and scale. Foci of dark pigmentation are visible within the crust.
