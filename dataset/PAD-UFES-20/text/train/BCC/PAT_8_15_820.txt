1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, translucent pearly-white, and a central area of reddish-brown and yellow crusting with dark red specks.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, raised, and rolled with a relatively distinct demarcation.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed, with a waxy/pearly texture on the raised border and a crusted, ulcerated texture in the center.
5. Structural Features: A central depression with ulceration and hemorrhagic crusting, a prominent elevated and translucent rolled border, and fine, branching blood vessels (telangiectasias) on the surface of the border.
