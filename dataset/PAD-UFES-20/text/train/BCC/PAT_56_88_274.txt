1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to deep red), with focal areas of yellow-orange.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined overall; the central ulcerated component has a more distinct, raised, and rolled border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Moist, glistening, and ulcerated with areas of erosion and overlying serosanguinous crusting.
5. Structural Features: A prominent central ulcer with a translucent, rolled border. Multiple smaller, satellite erythematous papules and erosions are present in a clustered configuration.
