1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red (erythematous), white, and focal reddish-brown crusts.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined in some areas, with a slightly raised, translucent quality in others.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Rough, with overlying white scale and areas of hemorrhagic crusting.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration, fine arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels), and a pearly, slightly rolled border.
