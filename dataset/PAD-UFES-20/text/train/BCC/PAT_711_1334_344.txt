1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red) and violaceous (purple), with areas of central pallor and small foci of brown-black crusting.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and moderately defined, with some areas appearing more distinct and others blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
