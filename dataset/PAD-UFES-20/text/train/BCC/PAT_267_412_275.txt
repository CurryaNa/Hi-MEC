1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), yellowish-white, and dark brown/black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, with a raised, rolled edge on one side.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed, featuring a central crusted and ulcerated area adjacent to a smoother, nodular portion.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration with hemorrhagic and serous crusting, a raised, somewhat translucent nodular component, and fine telangiectasias (small blood vessels) on the surface of the nodule.
