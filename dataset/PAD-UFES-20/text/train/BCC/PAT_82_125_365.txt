1. Primary Colors: Pink, violaceous, dark brown, and black with some yellowish-white areas.
2. Border Appearance: The border is well-demarcated but has an irregular, scalloped contour. The peripheral edge appears rolled and translucent.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed texture; the periphery is smooth and glossy (pearly), while the central area is ulcerated, crusted, and friable.
5. Structural Features: This is a raised, nodular lesion featuring prominent, arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels) on the translucent periphery. The central portion exhibits ulceration, hemorrhagic crusting, and irregular dark brown to black pigmentation.
