1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), brown, black, and yellowish-white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and relatively well-demarcated, with a peripheral erythematous halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated and covered with a thick, friable, keratotic crust; the peripheral border is smoother.
5. Structural Features: Raised, firm nodule with a rolled, somewhat translucent border and central ulceration with hemorrhagic and serous crusting.
