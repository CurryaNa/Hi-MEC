1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), translucent pearly white, and flesh-toned.
2. Border Appearance: The borders are well-defined and regular, with a round shape.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is generally symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is smooth and glistening with a pearly quality.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule with fine, branching blood vessels (telangiectasias) visible on the surface and a subtle central indentation or early ulceration.
