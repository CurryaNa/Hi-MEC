1. Primary Colors: Translucent pearly white, skin-toned pink, violaceous/purple, and red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly at the inferior margin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and waxy over the central elevation.
5. Structural Features: A central elevated papule with a slight central depression, fine arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels) on the surface, and surrounding purplish discoloration. Small dark purple specks are present at the periphery.
