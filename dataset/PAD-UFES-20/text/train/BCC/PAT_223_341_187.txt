1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red, light brown, and a central dark reddish-brown to black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, particularly on the left and inferior aspects.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted and slightly scaly, especially in the central area.
5. Structural Features: A central hemorrhagic crust overlying a possible erosion or ulceration, with surrounding erythema and a few fine telangiectasias (small, dilated blood vessels) at the periphery. The lesion appears slightly elevated (papular).
