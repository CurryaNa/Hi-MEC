1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (bright red) with a focal area of dark red to black hemorrhagic crust.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and largely regular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Symmetrical with a round to oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: Moist, glistening, and friable with evidence of erosion and crusting.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, dome-shaped papule with a vascular, fleshy appearance and surface ulceration.
