1. Primary Colors: Red, pink/skin-toned.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with a stellate (star-like) pattern formed by the radiating vessels.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, non-scaly.
5. Structural Features: A prominent network of fine, branching, and arborizing telangiectasias (small, dilated blood vessels) radiating from a central area.
