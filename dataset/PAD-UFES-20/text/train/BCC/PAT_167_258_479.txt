1. Primary Colors: Erythematous pink and red, with a central pale-yellowish and translucent white base.
2. Border Appearance: The central ulcer has a well-demarcated, slightly elevated border. The surrounding erythema has ill-defined borders that blend into the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The central ulcerated area is ovoid and largely symmetrical. The surrounding erythematous component is asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: The central base of the lesion is moist, glistening, and non-crusted. The surrounding skin appears smooth.
5. Structural Features: The lesion is characterized by a central, shallow ulceration with a translucent, "pearly" or waxy, and slightly rolled border. It is surrounded by a zone of erythema.
