1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink/red), brown, black, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with an erythematous halo that blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted and scaly; a thick, dark, adherent crust is present centrally, surrounded by fine, white scales.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous plaque with a central, irregularly shaped, dark brown-to-black hyperkeratotic crust/eschar and peripheral scaling.
