1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) and whitish (hypopigmented).
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with areas that are somewhat indistinct and blend with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and elongated.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and atrophic (thinned), with a slightly shiny or waxy appearance.
5. Structural Features: A central erythematous patch situated within a larger area of atrophic, scar-like skin. Fine telangiectasias (tiny, dilated blood vessels) may be present within the erythematous component.
