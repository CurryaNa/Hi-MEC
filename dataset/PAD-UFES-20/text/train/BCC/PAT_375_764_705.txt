1. Primary Colors: Black, dark red (hemorrhagic), and pink (erythematous).
2. Border Appearance: The central dark component has a slightly irregular but defined border; the surrounding erythematous halo is well-demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the central crusted area is ovoid and irregular within a more circular erythematous ring.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and friable, consistent with a hemorrhagic crust over an ulceration or erosion.
5. Structural Features: A central, dark, ulcerated papule or nodule covered by a hemorrhagic crust, surrounded by a distinct erythematous halo. There are minute dark specks superior to the central crust.
