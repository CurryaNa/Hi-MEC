1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red), with some lighter, skin-toned areas.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and slightly crusted, with areas of superficial erosion.
