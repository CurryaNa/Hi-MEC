1. Primary Colors: Translucent pink, red, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular with a round-to-oval outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its overall round shape, though the internal color distribution is asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, glistening, and taut.
5. Structural Features: A raised, dome-shaped, translucent papule containing multiple, discrete, dark brown to black pigmented globules (lacunae).
