1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, blue-gray, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, composed of two distinct, unequally sized nodules.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and glistening with areas of focal crusting.
5. Structural Features: A multilobular nodule with fine, arborizing telangiectasias (branching vessels) on the surface and focal areas of dark pigmentation.
