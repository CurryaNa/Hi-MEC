1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pinkish-red), dark brown/black, and whitish-yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and lobulated.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted and nodular, with areas of ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A complex lesion with a raised, somewhat translucent pinkish nodule, central dark hemorrhagic crusting, and peripheral whitish scale-crust.
