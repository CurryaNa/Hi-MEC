1. Primary Colors: Pearly pink, translucent white, erythematous red, and a focal yellow-white area.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, with a distinct, raised, and rolled peripheral margin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, glistening, and waxy with a small central crust.
5. Structural Features: A translucent papule with a rolled border, central depression with early ulceration, and prominent arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels) over the surface.
