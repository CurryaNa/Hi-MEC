1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, and subtle violaceous (purple-ish) hues.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with the lesion's color fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, with an irregular, somewhat clustered or lobulated outline.
4. Surface Texture: Slightly uneven with areas of subtle scale and possible early atrophy.
5. Structural Features: A patch composed of multiple, partially confluent erythematous papules, with some foci of more intense erythema.
