1. Primary Colors: Pearly white, erythematous (pink-red), and specks of brown-black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with some areas blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: Waxy and slightly translucent, with areas of fine scaling and atrophic (thinned, shiny) skin.
5. Structural Features: Prominent arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels), central scar-like white areas suggestive of regression, and multifocal, blotchy pigmentation.
