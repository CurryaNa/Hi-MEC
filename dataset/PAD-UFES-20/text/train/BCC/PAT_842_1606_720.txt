1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) and hypopigmented (white), with overlying fine silvery-white scale.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and polycyclic (scalloped), with well-demarcated borders separating the lesion from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly on the erythematous plaques; the central hypopigmented area appears smoother and slightly atrophic.
5. Structural Features: The lesion consists of multiple coalescing erythematous plaques forming a polycyclic pattern around a large central area of hypopigmentation. Fine scale is visible on the surface of the erythematous areas.
