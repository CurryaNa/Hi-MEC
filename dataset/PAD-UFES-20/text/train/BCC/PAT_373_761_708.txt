1. Primary Colors: Fleshy red, dark brown/black, and pearly white/translucent areas within a pinkish-red base.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, featuring a slightly raised, rolled periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated and moist, with focal areas of crusting.
5. Structural Features: A central depression (ulceration) with a glistening base, a pearly, translucent, and rolled border, and fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) on the adjacent skin surface.
