1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), violaceous (purplish-blue), dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, merging with surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Glistening, moist, and crusted.
5. Structural Features: Multiple coalescing, flaccid, and ruptured bullae (blisters) on an erythematous and eroded base. Central areas within the bullae show dark, hemorrhagic, and necrotic crusting.
