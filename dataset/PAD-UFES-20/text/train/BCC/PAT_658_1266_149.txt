1. Primary Colors: Pink, white, skin-tone, and red.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic, smooth, and slightly waxy in appearance, without significant scale or crust.
