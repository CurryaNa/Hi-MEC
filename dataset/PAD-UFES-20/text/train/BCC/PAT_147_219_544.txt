1. Primary Colors: Bright red in the center, surrounded by a pink to erythematous halo, with pearly white translucent areas and focal dark red to black specks.
2. Border Appearance: The peripheral border is somewhat ill-defined and irregular, while the border of the central erosion is sharply demarcated but also irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: Moist, glistening, and eroded centrally, with a surrounding waxy or pearly texture and evidence of minute crusting.
5. Structural Features: A central, irregularly shaped ulceration is present, surrounded by a raised, rolled, translucent border. Arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels) are visible on the surface of the border.
