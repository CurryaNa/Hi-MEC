1. Primary Colors: Translucent pink, pearly, with erythematous (red) linear vessels.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated with an irregular, slightly scalloped outline and a raised, rolled edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the overall shape is roughly round but not uniform.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, glistening, and waxy.
5. Structural Features: A raised, dome-shaped papule with prominent, fine, branching telangiectasias (arborizing vessels) on its surface and a subtle central depression.
