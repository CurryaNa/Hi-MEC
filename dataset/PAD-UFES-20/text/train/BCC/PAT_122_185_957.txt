1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, with an overall ovoid but uneven outline that is not sharply demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular surface characterized by central hemorrhagic crusting and ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A raised, erythematous nodule with central ulceration, crust, and focal areas of black pigmentation. The periphery has a slightly translucent, fleshy appearance.
