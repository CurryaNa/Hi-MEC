1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink/red), white, dark red, and black.
2. Border Appearance: The outer erythematous border is ill-defined and fades into the surrounding skin, while the central ulcerated area has a more distinct but irregular border.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is roughly round but asymmetrical due to the irregular central ulceration and crusting.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated, with a surrounding smooth, non-scaly erythematous halo.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule or small nodule with central ulceration, hemorrhagic crusting (dark red/black areas), and whitish macerated or keratotic material within the ulcer bed, surrounded by a prominent erythematous halo.
