1. Primary Colors: Red (erythema and fresh blood), dark red/black (hemorrhagic crust), and yellowish (serous exudate).
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with a slightly raised, rolled superior margin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Ulcerated and covered with moist hemorrhagic crust.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration with evidence of friability (recent bleeding) and a trickle of dried blood.
