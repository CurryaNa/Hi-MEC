1. Primary Colors: Variegated with shades of gray-brown, black, violaceous, and focal yellowish-white areas.
2. Border Appearance: The borders of the individual nodules are relatively well-defined but irregular in contour; the overall cluster has an irregular border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Markedly asymmetrical; the lesion cluster lacks symmetry across any axis.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed; featuring smooth, glistening, and translucent surfaces suggestive of vesicles or bullae, alongside nodular areas with rough, crusted components.
5. Structural Features: Multiple, clustered, raised (papulonodular) lesions. Key features include significant color variegation, a translucent bullous-like component (possibly hemorrhagic), and solid nodules with central yellowish crusting/keratinous material.
