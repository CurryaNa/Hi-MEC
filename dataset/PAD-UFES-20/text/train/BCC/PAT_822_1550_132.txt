1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) base with central white and yellow adherent scale-crust.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both its overall roundish shape and the distribution of surface features.
4. Surface Texture: Rough, with a prominent, centrally located, adherent, hyperkeratotic (thickly scaled) and crusted area.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule with central, thick, adherent scale-crust. There are fine radial fissures within the central keratotic material.
