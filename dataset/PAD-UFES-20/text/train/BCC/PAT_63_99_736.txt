1. Primary Colors: Translucent pink, erythematous red, and a central area of yellowish-brown and dark red-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-defined, raised, and rolled with a regular, ovoid contour.
3. Shape Symmetry: Grossly symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and slightly bosselated at the periphery with central erosion and crusting.
5. Structural Features: A pearly, translucent papulonodule with central ulceration and overlying serosanguineous crust.
