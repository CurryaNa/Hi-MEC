1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red (erythematous), white, and focal reddish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly at the periphery which blends into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted, with a rough, uneven surface.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous plaque with central ulceration and hemorrhagic crusting, accompanied by fine white scale (hyperkeratosis) and surrounding atrophic changes.
