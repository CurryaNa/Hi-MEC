1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with pink, violaceous, brown-black, and whitish-grey areas.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and not sharply demarcated from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both contour and distribution of color and texture.
