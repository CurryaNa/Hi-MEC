1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), pearly white, and focal brown pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming a raised, rolled edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical and circular.
4. Surface Texture: The border appears smooth and somewhat waxy; the central area is atrophic with fine wrinkling and slight crusting.
5. Structural Features: A central depression (atrophy/ulceration) is surrounded by a raised, pearly, rolled border. Fine telangiectasias may be present along the rim.
