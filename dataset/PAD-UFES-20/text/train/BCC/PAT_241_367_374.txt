1. Primary Colors: Erythematous pink, red, white, yellow, and dark brown/black.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly, crusted, and rough.
5. Structural Features: A central linear ulceration with adherent yellowish crust and scale, flanked by erythematous and pearly papulonodular components. There are areas of hyperkeratosis and some dark, hemorrhagic crusting.
