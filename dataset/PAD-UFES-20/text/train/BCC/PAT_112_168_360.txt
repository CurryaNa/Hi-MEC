1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with shades of brown, red, yellow, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Highly asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Exophytic, verrucous (wart-like), and heavily crusted.
5. Structural Features: A multi-lobulated nodule with areas of hyperkeratosis (white scale), ulceration (red areas), and serosanguinous crust (yellow-brown).
