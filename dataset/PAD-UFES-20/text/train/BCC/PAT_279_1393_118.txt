1. Primary Colors: Erythematous pink, dark red, black, and yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined; the central component's border is distinct but uneven, while the surrounding erythema blends into the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in shape, color distribution, and overall structure.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated with a moist-appearing hemorrhagic and serous crust.
5. Structural Features: A centrally ulcerated papule/nodule covered by a dark crust, surrounded by an erythematous halo. Hairs are present within and around the lesion.
