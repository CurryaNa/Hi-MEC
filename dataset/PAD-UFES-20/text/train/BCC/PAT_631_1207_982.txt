1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), translucent pearly white, and a central yellowish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular peripherally, with a more distinct, raised, and rolled border encircling the central component.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated; the surrounding border is smooth and waxy.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration with an adherent serosanguineous crust, surrounded by a raised, translucent, pearly, and rolled border.
