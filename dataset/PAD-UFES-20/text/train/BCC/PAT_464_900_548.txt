1. Primary Colors: Dark red, black, bright red, and a peripheral pink-violaceous halo.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-defined and circular, but with an irregular, rolled edge and a surrounding indistinct erythematous halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: Grossly symmetrical in its round shape, but asymmetrical regarding color and internal structure.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated with a glistening, hemorrhagic crust.
5. Structural Features: A raised, crateriform (volcano-like) nodule with a central depression containing a dark crust and an area of active bleeding, surrounded by a rolled border.
