1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red (erythematous) and translucent, with a central area of white-yellow crust.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and somewhat irregular, with the lesion appearing to blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is not uniform in shape or elevation.
