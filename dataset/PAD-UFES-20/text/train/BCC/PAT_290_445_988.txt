1. Primary Colors: Red, pink, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a surrounding erythematous halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and the distribution of color and texture.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular, with a friable-appearing surface covered by a central hemorrhagic crust and some peripheral scale.
5. Structural Features: A raised, nodular papule with central ulceration and dark crusting.
