1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-tan, whitish-grey, with scattered black specks and a surrounding erythematous (pinkish-red) halo.
2. Border Appearance: The border of the central keratotic component is irregular and well-demarcated, with a subtle collarette of scale at the base.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the shape is irregular and elongated.
4. Surface Texture: Hyperkeratotic, verrucous (wart-like), and crusted with a dry, adherent scale.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, firm papule with a central keratotic core, resembling a cutaneous horn. Small black dots are present within the crusted surface. The lesion arises from an erythematous base.
