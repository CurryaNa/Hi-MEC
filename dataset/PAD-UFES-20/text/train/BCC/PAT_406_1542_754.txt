1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red (erythematous), violaceous, and a central yellowish-white adherent crust.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated but slightly irregular, forming a round to oval outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its overall circular shape, but asymmetrical in the distribution of color and surface features.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly; the surrounding rim appears slightly raised and firm.
5. Structural Features: A firm papule or small nodule with central ulceration or erosion covered by a crust. The peripheral border is raised and has a subtle translucent quality.
