1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), dark brown-to-black, and pearly white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and raised, creating a rolled border that is relatively well-demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and the distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed texture featuring a centrally crusted and ulcerated area, with a smoother, somewhat translucent peripheral rim.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration with hemorrhagic crusting, a raised and rolled peripheral border, and a distinct pearly papule on the inferior margin.
