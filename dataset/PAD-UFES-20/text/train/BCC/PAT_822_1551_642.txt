1. Primary Colors: Black, dark brown, red, and pearly translucent white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a distinctly raised and rolled edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated with a glistening, moist appearance; the surrounding rim is smooth and waxy.
5. Structural Features: A central, dark, hemorrhagic crust within an ulcerated depression. The lesion possesses a raised, pearly, and rolled border. Some fine telangiectasias are visible on the periphery.
