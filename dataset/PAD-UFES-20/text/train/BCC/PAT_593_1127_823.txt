1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), brown-black, and pearly white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated and crusted, with surrounding areas appearing translucent and slightly scaly.
5. Structural Features: A central erosion with hemorrhagic crusting, a surrounding raised and rolled pearly border, and fine arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels).
