1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), white (atrophic), and yellowish-brown (scale-crust).
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, with some sections being well-demarcated and others ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven and mixed, with areas of smooth, atrophic skin, scattered adherent yellowish scales, and focal crusting.
5. Structural Features: Central atrophy (thinning of the skin), a focal erosion covered by a hemorrhagic crust, and surrounding erythema. Hairs are visible growing through the lesion.
