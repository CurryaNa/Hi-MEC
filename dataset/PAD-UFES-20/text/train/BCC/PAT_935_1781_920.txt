1. Primary Colors: Pearly pink, translucent, and erythematous (red), with a small central whitish-yellow crust.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular yet relatively well-demarcated, with a distinct raised and rolled quality.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is an irregularly shaped papule.
4. Surface Texture: Waxy and glistening with a focal area of central crusting.
5. Structural Features: Raised papulonodular lesion with prominent arborizing telangiectasias (fine, branching blood vessels) coursing over its surface and a pearly sheen; a small central erosion or ulceration is noted.
