1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), with central hemorrhagic (dark red) crusting and yellowish-white scale.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated and regular, with a raised, rolled quality.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical, with a round to oval configuration.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular and crusted, particularly in the center, with some peripheral scaling.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule or nodule with a somewhat pearly or translucent appearance, featuring central ulceration and hemorrhagic crust.
