1. Primary Colors: Black, dark brown, and a surrounding pink-red (erythematous) halo.
2. Border Appearance: The border of the central pigmented component is irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is irregular with a central dark crust (eschar).
5. Structural Features: The lesion exhibits central ulceration covered by a hemorrhagic crust, surrounded by an erythematous and slightly indurated rim. There are also small, dark satellite macules adjacent to the primary lesion.
