1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-brown, and dark brown/black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with the erythematous component blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly; the surrounding erythematous plaque is smoother and appears slightly atrophic and shiny.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration covered by an adherent yellowish-brown serohemorrhagic crust. This is surrounded by an erythematous plaque containing fine telangiectasias (arborizing vessels) and a few small, peripheral erosions.
