1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish-yellow, and central brown-black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, with a raised, rolled, and somewhat translucent edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and distribution of features.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated and covered with an adherent crust; the peripheral rim appears smooth and waxy.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration with crusting, a pearly and rolled border, and fine, arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels) visible on the surface.
