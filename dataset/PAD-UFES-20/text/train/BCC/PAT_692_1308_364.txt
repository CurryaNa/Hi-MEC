1. Primary Colors: Erythematous pink and red, with foci of whitish-yellow scale.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, gradually blending with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly, with a rough, slightly crusted texture in some areas.
