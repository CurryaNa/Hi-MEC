1. Primary Colors: Erythematous pink, dark brown/black, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated with some peripheral fine scaling.
5. Structural Features: A central, dark, crusted ulceration surrounded by an erythematous, slightly raised rim. Fine, thread-like vessels (telangiectasias) may be present on the periphery.
