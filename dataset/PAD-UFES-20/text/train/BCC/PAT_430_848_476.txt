1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, whitish-yellow, and dark brown/black.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion's overall border is irregular and ill-defined, merging with the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
