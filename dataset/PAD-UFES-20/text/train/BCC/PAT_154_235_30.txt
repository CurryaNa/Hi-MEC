1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), yellowish-brown, and whitish.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion's overall border is circular but somewhat irregular. It is defined by a distinct erythematous halo that appears slightly raised. The inner border of the central feature is irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly symmetrical in its round shape, but the central crusting and underlying ulceration are asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: The surface is characterized by central crusting, which appears thick, yellowish, and somewhat friable or waxy.
5. Structural Features: A central, shallow ulceration is covered by a prominent honey-colored to yellowish crust. The lesion is surrounded by a distinct ring of erythema, creating a "punched-out" appearance. There is a central depression under the crust.
