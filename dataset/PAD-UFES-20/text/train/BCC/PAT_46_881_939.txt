1. Primary Colors: Red, pink, and white (hypopigmented).
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with the central red area being more demarcated than the surrounding white patch which blends into the skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: The central red area is raised, shiny, and focally eroded with crusting; the surrounding white area appears atrophic and smooth.
5. Structural Features: A central erythematous papule featuring fine, arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels) and a small central ulceration, all situated within a larger, atrophic, scar-like white plaque.
