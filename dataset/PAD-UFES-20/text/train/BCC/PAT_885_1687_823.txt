1. Primary Colors: Pink, pearly white, with focal dark red and violaceous dots.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular but relatively well-demarcated with a slightly raised, rolled edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven and slightly waxy/pearly with areas of fine scaling and central crusting.
5. Structural Features: Raised papule with central shallow erosion/ulceration and fine, dot-like vascular structures (telangiectasias).
