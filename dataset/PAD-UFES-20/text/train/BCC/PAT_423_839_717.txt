1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red, bright red, and a central whitish-yellow crust.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and slightly waxy/translucent with a central area of erosion and crusting.
5. Structural Features: Prominent, branching, and tortuous blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) on the surface, with a small central ulceration.
