1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, brown, black, and white-yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of features.
4. Surface Texture: Multi-textured with areas of ulceration, crusting, and scaling.
5. Structural Features: Raised nodular lesion with central erosion, multiple foci of hemorrhagic and keratotic crusts (including a prominent black crusted area), and fine telangiectasias.
