1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), pearly white-yellow, brown, and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a raised, rolled quality.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and ulcerated.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration with hemorrhagic and serous crusting, a pearly-translucent border, and prominent, arborizing telangiectasias on the surrounding erythematous base.
