1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous (red), brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Shiny and waxy with central ulceration and dark crusting.
5. Structural Features: A slightly raised, pearly, and translucent border with visible fine, arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels) and a central crusted ulcer.
