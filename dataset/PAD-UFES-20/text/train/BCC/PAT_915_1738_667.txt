1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red (erythematous) and pearly white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular; partially ill-defined with a distinct, raised, thread-like (serpiginous) edge on one side.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth with some areas of atrophy (thinning of the skin).
5. Structural Features: A raised, pearly, serpiginous border is the most prominent feature. The center of the lesion appears slightly depressed and atrophic.
