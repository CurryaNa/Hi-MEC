1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), with central yellowish-white and focal dark brown-black areas.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated but irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly with a peripherally smoother, slightly shiny surface.
5. Structural Features: Raised papule with central ulceration covered by a serosanguineous crust, focal pigmentation, and a translucent periphery.
