1. Primary Colors: Pink, violaceous, with focal areas of dark brown and black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated with an irregular, slightly scalloped outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, slightly mammillated (cobblestone-like), and glistening.
5. Structural Features: Raised papulonodular lesion featuring multiple colors, punctate red vascular dots (telangiectasias), and peripherally located dark brown/black globules.
