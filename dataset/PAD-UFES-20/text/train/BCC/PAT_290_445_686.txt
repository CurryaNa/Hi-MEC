1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined in some areas, with a surrounding erythematous halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Multi-textured with areas of hemorrhagic crusting, ulceration, and a waxy/pearly sheen.
5. Structural Features: A raised, nodular lesion with central ulceration, scattered dark pigmentation, and fine, visible telangiectasias (blood vessels) on the surface.
