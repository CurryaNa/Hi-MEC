1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, translucent white, and violaceous.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding sun-damaged skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and shiny (waxy/pearly) with subtle atrophy and scattered small papules.
5. Structural Features: Prominent arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels), small, raised, translucent papules, and areas of mottled erythema.
