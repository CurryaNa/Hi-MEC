1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-tan, and focal brown/violaceous areas.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with the erythematous component blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted with a surrounding slightly scaly and uneven surface.
5. Structural Features: A central erosion or ulceration covered by a yellowish-tan crust, situated within an erythematous papule/plaque. Fine, branching telangiectasias are visible on the surface of the lesion.
