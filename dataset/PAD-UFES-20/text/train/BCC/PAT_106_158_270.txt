1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, and violaceous/purple.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and linear to irregularly oval.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic (thinned) with areas of fine scale and central crusting.
5. Structural Features: Central erosion or early ulceration with serosanguinous crust, and visible fine, linear telangiectasias (small blood vessels).
