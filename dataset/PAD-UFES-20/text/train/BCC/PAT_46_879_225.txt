1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous red, and pearly white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Atrophic and slightly waxy with focal central crusting and micro-ulceration.
