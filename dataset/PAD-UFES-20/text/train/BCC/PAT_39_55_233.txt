1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), violaceous, with focal areas of brown-black pigmentation and a central white-grey scale.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin with an erythematous halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both contour and distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly with an adherent, flaky white scale over a friable base.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule or small plaque with evidence of central erosion or ulceration covered by a scale-crust; fine telangiectasias may be present at the periphery.
