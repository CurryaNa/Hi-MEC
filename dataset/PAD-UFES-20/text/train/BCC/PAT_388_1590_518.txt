1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, with a focal point of bright red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, gradually blending with the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry with fine scaling and mild central crusting.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous patch exhibiting central atrophy (thinning of the skin) and a small peripheral erosion with pinpoint bleeding. Fine telangiectasias may be present within the lesion.
