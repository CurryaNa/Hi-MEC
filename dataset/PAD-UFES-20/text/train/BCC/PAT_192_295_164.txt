1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red, translucent/pearly, with adjacent violaceous and ecchymotic areas.
2. Border Appearance: The primary nodule has a relatively well-defined, rounded border.
3. Shape Symmetry: The primary nodule is largely symmetrical and ovoid.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth, waxy, and glistening.
5. Structural Features: A raised, firm-appearing papule/nodule with prominent, fine, arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels) on its surface.
