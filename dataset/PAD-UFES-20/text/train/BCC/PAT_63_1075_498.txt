1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, translucent white, and bright red.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated but slightly irregular and indistinct in some areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its round-to-oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: Shiny and translucent with fine scaling and small foci of crusting.
