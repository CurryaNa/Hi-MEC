1. Primary Colors: Red, pink, pearly white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, with a raised and rolled edge that is distinct in some areas and blends with the surrounding skin in others.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated, moist, and friable; peripherally smooth and waxy.
5. Structural Features: A central, non-healing ulceration with a hemorrhagic base, a surrounding translucent, pearly, elevated border, and visible fine, branching telangiectasias on the lesion's margin and adjacent skin.
