1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), with areas of white scale and a central dark brown/black punctum.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat indistinct, with a faint, dark, circular marking on the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both outline and internal structure.
4. Surface Texture: Rough and scaly, with visible keratotic debris.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule with central umbilication (depression) containing a dark, keratotic plug or small ulceration.
