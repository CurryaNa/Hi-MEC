1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), violaceous, with a central area of brown, yellow, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, particularly where the erythema fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in terms of shape, color distribution, and surface characteristics.
4. Surface Texture: The periphery appears smooth and firm (nodular), while the central portion is ulcerated and covered with an adherent crust.
5. Structural Features: A raised, solid nodule with an elevated, somewhat pearly or "rolled" border surrounding a central ulceration with hemorrhagic and serous crusting.
