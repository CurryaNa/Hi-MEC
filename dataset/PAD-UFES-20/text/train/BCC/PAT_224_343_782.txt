1. Primary Colors: Translucent pearly white, pink, and red.
2. Border Appearance: The borders of the central papule are relatively defined but not sharply demarcated, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The central papular component is largely symmetrical and ovoid.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and waxy with a prominent glistening or pearly sheen.
5. Structural Features: A central, raised, translucent papule with prominent, fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) on its surface and periphery.
