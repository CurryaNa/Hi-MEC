1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous (red), and pearly translucent white, with some areas of skin-tone yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with the lesion's edge blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and shiny with an atrophic (thinned) appearance.
5. Structural Features: Fine, arborizing telangiectasias (small, branching blood vessels) are visible on the surface, particularly in the erythematous areas. The lesion has a slightly raised, papular quality and a waxy, translucent appearance. A few minute, dark specks are present centrally.
