1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), translucent pearly pink, and a central focus of dark red to black hemorrhagic crust.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, with a raised, rounded, and rolled peripheral margin that is somewhat well-demarcated from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Biphasic; centrally ulcerated with a rough hemorrhagic crust, surrounded by a smooth, waxy, and pearly surface.
5. Structural Features: A central, crusted ulceration is present, along with a distinct pearly, rolled border and fine, arborizing telangiectasias (visible blood vessels) on the surface of the lesion.
