1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, presenting in a linear or serpiginous configuration.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted, with areas of erosion.
5. Structural Features: Erythematous plaque with multiple small erosions and adherent whitish scale-crust, particularly in the central portion.
