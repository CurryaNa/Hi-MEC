1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), translucent pearly-pink, with central yellowish-brown crust and fine red vessels.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, with a distinct, round, and slightly elevated ("rolled") edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Glistening and smooth at the periphery, with central ulceration covered by a rough, crusted, and scaly surface.
5. Structural Features: A raised, dome-shaped papule (nodule) exhibiting prominent, fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) on its surface and a central depression consistent with ulceration.
