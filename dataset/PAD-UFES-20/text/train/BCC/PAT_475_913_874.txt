1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pearly-white, and yellowish, with foci of brownish-red crust.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated, raised, and rolled (indurated) with a slightly irregular outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the overall shape is roughly ovoid but the internal structures and border are not uniform.
4. Surface Texture: Glistening and moist centrally due to ulceration, surrounded by a pearly, partially crusted surface.
5. Structural Features: A central, "punched-out" ulcer with a yellowish base, a translucent, elevated peripheral rim, and subtle telangiectasias visible on the border.
