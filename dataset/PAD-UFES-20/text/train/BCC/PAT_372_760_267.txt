1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), red (hemorrhagic), black, and yellowish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Ulcerated, with extensive hemorrhagic and serosanguineous crusting.
5. Structural Features: A raised, exophytic nodule with central ulceration, necrosis (black crust), and surrounding erythema and edema.
