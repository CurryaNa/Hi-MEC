1. Primary Colors: Red, pink, and whitish.
2. Border Appearance: The ulcerated areas have sharply demarcated, slightly raised borders, while the overall lesion's periphery is ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion consists of two non-identical, vertically aligned ulcerated components.
4. Surface Texture: Moist and crusted within the ulcerations, with a smooth, slightly atrophic texture in the intervening skin.
5. Structural Features: Two distinct, "punched-out" ulcerations are present on a subtle, flesh-colored to pinkish papule. The inferior ulceration is larger and more erythematous than the superior one.
