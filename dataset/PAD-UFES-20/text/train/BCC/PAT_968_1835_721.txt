1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) with a translucent, pearly-white hue.
2. Border Appearance: The border is regular, round, and appears as a distinct, raised, rolled edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical with a round to oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: Shiny and waxy, with a slightly atrophic (thinned) and finely textured appearance in the center.
5. Structural Features: Fine, arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels) are visible on the surface. The lesion presents with a characteristic raised, pearly border and a slightly depressed central area.
