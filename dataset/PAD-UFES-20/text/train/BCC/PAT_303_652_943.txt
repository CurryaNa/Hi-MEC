1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-violaceous, white, and yellowish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and keratotic; peripherally appears somewhat translucent and waxy.
5. Structural Features: A raised nodule with a rolled, pearly border and central ulceration covered by a thick, adherent, yellowish-white crust and eschar.
