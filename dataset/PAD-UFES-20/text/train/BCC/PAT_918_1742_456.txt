1. Primary Colors: Black, dark brown, gray, and an erythematous (pink-red) base.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with an erythematous halo blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and hyperkeratotic, covered by a thick, friable, adherent crust.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, nodular lesion with central ulceration concealed by a large, dark, hemorrhagic crust.
