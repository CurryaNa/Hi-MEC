1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) base with overlying yellowish-white and light-brown scale.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of scale.
4. Surface Texture: Markedly dry, scaly (hyperkeratotic), and crusted; the scales are thick, adherent, and lamellar.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous plaque with prominent, thick, adherent scale and crust. Fissuring is evident within the scaly areas. Some smaller, satellite scaly papules are visible.
