1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), dark brown-to-black, and pale pinkish-white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with indistinct margins blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted, with multiple erosions and areas of atrophy (thin, shiny skin).
5. Structural Features: A heterogeneous plaque characterized by multifocal erosions, ulcerations, and adherent hemorrhagic crusts on an erythematous and atrophic base.
