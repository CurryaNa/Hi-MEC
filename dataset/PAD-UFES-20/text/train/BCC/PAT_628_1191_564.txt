1. Primary Colors: Pearly pink, skin-toned, yellowish-white, and red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a slightly raised, rolled quality.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed; pearly and waxy at the periphery with central ulceration and yellowish crusting.
5. Structural Features: A raised, translucent border, central depression with ulceration, and fine telangiectasias (arborizing vessels) at the periphery.
