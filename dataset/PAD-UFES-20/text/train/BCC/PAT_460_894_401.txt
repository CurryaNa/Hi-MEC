1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red, translucent white, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and not sharply demarcated from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed, with smooth, glistening (pearly) areas and regions of ulceration and crusting.
