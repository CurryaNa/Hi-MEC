1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red) and a central whitish-yellow focus.
2. Border Appearance: The erythematous halo has an ill-defined border, while the central papule's border is more distinct but irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and eroded, with a smooth but inflamed surrounding area.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous papule with central umbilication (indentation) containing a whitish-yellowish plug or crust, suggestive of ulceration.
