1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) with a central white focus and surrounding violaceous-brown discoloration.
2. Border Appearance: The central raised portion is relatively well-demarcated, but surrounded by an ill-defined erythematous halo; the overall shape is round.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven, with a smooth, tense periphery and a central area of crust or a pustule.
5. Structural Features: A solitary, indurated erythematous papule or nodule located in a hair-bearing area, featuring a central white pustule or keratotic plug.
