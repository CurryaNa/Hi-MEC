1. Primary Colors: Red, black, brown, and tan-yellow with surrounding pink erythema.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly defined, with some sections appearing raised and rolled.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and distribution of colors and structures.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular and complex, featuring areas of moist ulceration, crusting, and peripheral scaling.
5. Structural Features: A central ulcer, blotches of variegated pigmentation (black and brown), and an erythematous, raised periphery.
