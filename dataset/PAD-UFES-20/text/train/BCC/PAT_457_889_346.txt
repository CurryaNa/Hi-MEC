1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-white, and dark brown/black.
2. Border Appearance: The erythematous base has an ill-defined and irregular border, while the central component is more sharply demarcated but still irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: The overall shape is roughly round and therefore largely symmetrical, but the internal structures (crusting, ulceration) are asymmetrically distributed.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly with an adherent, verrucous quality; the surrounding erythematous skin appears smoother.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule with central ulceration covered by a hemorrhagic and serous crust.
