1. Primary Colors: Red, brown, black, and whitish-yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a fine peripheral scale and surrounding erythema.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Partially crusted and ulcerated, with a moist, glistening area and dry, crusted portions.
5. Structural Features: A central ulcerated papule or nodule exhibiting polychromia, including a superior, glistening red erosion and an inferior, irregular black clod.
