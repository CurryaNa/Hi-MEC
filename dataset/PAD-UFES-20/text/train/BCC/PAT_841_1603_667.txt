1. Primary Colors: Brown, dark brown, and a violaceous/reddish hue.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly at the superior and right-sided margins.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally rough with fine scale or crust, while the periphery is flat.
5. Structural Features: A central, darker crusted papule surrounded by a macular halo of violaceous and light brown pigmentation; color variegation is present.
