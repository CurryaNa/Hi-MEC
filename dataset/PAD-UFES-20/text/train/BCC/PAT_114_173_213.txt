1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous), translucent pearly-white, with foci of yellow-brown and black crusting.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, particularly on the superior and lateral aspects.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and internal structure.
4. Surface Texture: Ulcerated and crusted, with some moist, glistening areas.
5. Structural Features: A raised, translucent ("pearly") border, multiple central ulcerations, fine arborizing telangiectasias (visible blood vessels) on the periphery, and areas of hemorrhagic and serous crust.
