1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), white, and yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly defined, with some raised edges.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally moist and ulcerated, with surrounding dry, adherent crust.
5. Structural Features: A central, raised (exophytic), erythematous nodule with surface ulceration, surrounded by a collar of whitish-yellow crust and scale.
