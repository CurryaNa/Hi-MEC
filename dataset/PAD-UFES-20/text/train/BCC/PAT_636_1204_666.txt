1. Primary Colors: Pink, flesh-toned, erythematous, with a focal dark brown to black area.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Predominantly smooth and slightly waxy or pearly, with a focal, firm-appearing dark crust.
5. Structural Features: A central, raised, translucent papule with an eccentric, dark brown/black crusted nodule at its inferior edge. The entire lesion is situated in a field of atrophic skin with prominent radial wrinkling. Fine telangiectasias may be present on the papule's surface.
