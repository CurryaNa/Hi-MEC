1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Crusted and eroded/ulcerated.
5. Structural Features: The lesion presents as two distinct foci within an erythematous patch: a superior dark brown crusted papule and an inferior, larger, irregularly shaped area of ulceration with hemorrhagic crusting.
