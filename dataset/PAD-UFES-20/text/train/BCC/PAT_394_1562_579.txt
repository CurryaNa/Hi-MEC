1. Primary Colors: Pink, red, and peripheral whitish/hypopigmented areas.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and stellate (star-shaped), with ill-defined, fine, scar-like projections radiating into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally atrophic (thinned) and slightly scaly, with surrounding induration (firmness).
5. Structural Features: A central erythematous (red) plaque with evidence of atrophy, surrounded by a whitish, indurated rim that forms radiating, finger-like extensions, creating a scar-like or starburst pattern.
