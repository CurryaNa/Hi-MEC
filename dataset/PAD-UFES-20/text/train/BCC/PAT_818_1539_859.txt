1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, reddish-brown, and pearly translucent white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular overall, with distinct raised, rolled borders around central components.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed, with areas of central hemorrhagic crusting and ulceration, surrounded by smooth, somewhat waxy or pearly surfaces.
5. Structural Features: Multiple foci of ulceration, raised pearly borders, and prominent, fine, branching telangiectasias on the surface.
