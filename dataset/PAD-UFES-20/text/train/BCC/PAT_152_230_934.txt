1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous (red), and a central pale whitish area.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, with a distinct, elevated, rolled, and translucent (pearly) border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical with a regular oval shape.
4. Surface Texture: Glistening and moist, with a smooth surface over the rolled border and a centrally ulcerated texture.
5. Structural Features: A central depression or ulceration, a prominent raised, pearly, rolled border, and visible fine, branching telangiectasias (small blood vessels) on the surface.
