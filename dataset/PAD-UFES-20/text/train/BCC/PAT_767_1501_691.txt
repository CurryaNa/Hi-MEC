1. Primary Colors: Central black/dark violaceous area surrounded by a pinkish-red (erythematous) to purplish halo.
2. Border Appearance: The erythematous halo has a relatively well-defined inner border and an ill-defined outer border that fades into the surrounding skin; the overall shape is round.
3. Shape Symmetry: Symmetrical and circular.
4. Surface Texture: Central area appears crusted and slightly depressed; the surrounding erythematous ring is smooth.
5. Structural Features: A central necrotic eschar or crusted punctum with slight umbilication, surrounded by an edematous, erythematous papule. Fine hairs are visible within the lesion.
