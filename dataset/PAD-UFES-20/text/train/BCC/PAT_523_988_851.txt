1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink), hypopigmented (white), and skin-toned.
2. Border Appearance: Annular (ring-shaped), slightly raised, and somewhat ill-defined with slight irregularity.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its roughly ovoid configuration.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly, with a fine, whitish scale most notable on the raised peripheral border.
5. Structural Features: The lesion presents as a plaque with a raised, scaly border and a slightly atrophic, clearer center. Fine, linear telangiectasias (small blood vessels) are visible.
