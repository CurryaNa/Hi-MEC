1. Primary Colors: Red (erythema and hemorrhage), pink, brown-black, and translucent pearly-white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Ulcerated, crusted (hemorrhagic), and friable with some shiny areas.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration, arborizing telangiectasias (fine branching blood vessels), active bleeding points, hemorrhagic crusting, and a somewhat pearly, translucent quality in some areas.
