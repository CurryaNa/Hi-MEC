1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), white, and focal brown-to-black.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated but with irregular and indistinct margins that blend into the surrounding scaly skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesion is grossly symmetrical in its ovoid shape, but asymmetrical in the distribution of surface features like scale and crust.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted, with fine, adherent white scales and focal areas of rough, dark crust.
5. Structural Features: A raised erythematous papule with central erosion covered by a serosanguineous crust and surrounded by overlying scale.
