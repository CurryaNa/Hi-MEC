1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), translucent pearly white, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, indistinct in some areas, and features a raised, rolled quality.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both contour and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Primarily crusted and scaly in the center, with a smoother, slightly shiny texture at the periphery.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration covered by a hemorrhagic crust, a raised and translucent (pearly) border, and focal pigmentation.
