1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), black, blue-grey, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with some areas blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Varied, featuring a shiny, atrophic (thinned) area and a slightly eroded, focally crusted surface.
5. Structural Features: Multicoloration, focal black pigmented globules, a white scar-like atrophic area, a small central erosion, and fine telangiectasias (arborizing vessels) at the periphery.
