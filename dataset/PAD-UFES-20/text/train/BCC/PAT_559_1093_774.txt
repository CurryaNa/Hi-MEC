1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, white, and yellowish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with indistinct margins blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and structures.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly, with some surrounding atrophic (thinned and shiny) areas.
5. Structural Features: Central ulceration covered by an adherent yellowish crust, surrounded by an erythematous halo and peripheral areas of white, scar-like atrophy. Hairs are present within the lesion.
