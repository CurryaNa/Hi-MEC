1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) with a central dark red and brownish crust, and a surrounding translucent, pearly-white quality.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, particularly on the lower and left aspects.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both its overall shape and the distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: The surface appears smooth and slightly glossy or pearly, with a central area of ulceration and crusting.
5. Structural Features: A raised, rolled border surrounds a central, umbilicated depression that contains a hemorrhagic crust.
