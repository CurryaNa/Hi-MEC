1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red/pink), white, yellowish-brown, and dark brown/black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with a scaly periphery.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Heterogeneous, featuring a thick, centrally located, adherent crust and surrounding fine, flaky scaling on an erythematous base.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous plaque with prominent central ulcero-crusting and peripheral desquamation.
