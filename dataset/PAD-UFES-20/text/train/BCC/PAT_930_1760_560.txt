1. Primary Colors: Pink, erythematous (red), pearly white, and central yellowish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and partially ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and scaly, with some areas appearing waxy or pearly.
5. Structural Features: Raised, nodular lesion with central ulceration covered by a hemorrhagic crust and a pearly, translucent, rolled border.
