1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, white, pink/erythematous, and red-orange.
2. Border Appearance: The overall affected area is ill-defined; the individual raised lesions have irregular but discernible borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Markedly hyperkeratotic, verrucous (wart-like), and crusted; the surrounding skin is dry and scaly.
5. Structural Features: Multiple elevated papules with thick, adherent, yellowish-white scale forming cutaneous horn-like structures. The base of the lesions and surrounding skin is erythematous and atrophic. One larger lesion shows central pinpoint hemorrhage beneath the crust. A separate, smaller, eroded erythematous papule is also visible.
