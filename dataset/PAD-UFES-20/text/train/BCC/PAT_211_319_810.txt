1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), violaceous (purplish), and light brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with indistinct margins that blend into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed; predominantly smooth with a slight sheen, accompanied by areas of fine scale and subtle crusting.
5. Structural Features: A slightly elevated plaque with central atrophy (thinning of the skin), areas of translucency, and possible early erosion.
