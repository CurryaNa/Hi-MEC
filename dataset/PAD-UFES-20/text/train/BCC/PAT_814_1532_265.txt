1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), yellowish-brown, and whitish.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and moderately defined, with some indistinct areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted with an adherent, firm-appearing crust, and peripherally dry and scaly.
5. Structural Features: Raised erythematous plaque with central ulceration covered by a thick, yellowish-brown keratotic crust and fine peripheral scale.
