1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red) and white.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated but irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is roughly ovoid but not symmetrical across any axis.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and keratotic, with a dry and rough appearance.
5. Structural Features: An elevated plaque with a central area of adherent white scale (hyperkeratosis) over an erythematous base.
