1. Primary Colors: Variegated with areas of pink, brown, black, grey-violaceous, and yellow-white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined in some areas, but generally demarcated from the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular and lobulated with areas of crusting and a subtle waxy sheen.
5. Structural Features: A raised, nodular lesion with significant color variegation, focal yellowish crust/keratin, and possible early ulceration.
