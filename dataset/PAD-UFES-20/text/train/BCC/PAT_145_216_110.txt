1. Primary Colors: Central black and dark red, surrounded by translucent amber-yellow and peripheral pink erythema.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with the edges blending into the surrounding inflamed skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed; a central, thick, hemorrhagic crust with a surrounding moist, glistening, and exudative surface.
5. Structural Features: A central ulcerated area covered by a dark, eschar-like crust. The lesion is surrounded by an erythematous halo with serous exudate. Fine, branching telangiectasias are visible on the adjacent skin.
