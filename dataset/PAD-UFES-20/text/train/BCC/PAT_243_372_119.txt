1. Primary Colors: Dark reddish-black, violaceous (purplish-red), and erythematous (pinkish-red).
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with a surrounding halo of erythema that blurs the transition to normal skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, composed of two distinct, vertically aligned components of different sizes.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed texture featuring a central hemorrhagic crust on the larger inferior component and an eroded, moist surface on the superior component.
5. Structural Features: The lesion consists of two distinct papules. The inferior papule is larger, violaceous-to-black, with central crusting suggestive of thrombosis or hemorrhage. The superior papule is smaller, red, and appears as a small erosion or ulceration. A subtle erythematous halo surrounds the entire complex.
