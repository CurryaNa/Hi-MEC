1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red (erythematous) with a small, darker red central area.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively regular and round, but indistinct and poorly demarcated, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Grossly symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and shiny (pearly/translucent) with a small area of central erosion and crusting.
5. Structural Features: Raised, dome-shaped papule with fine, branching telangiectasias (blood vessels) visible on the surface, particularly around the periphery, and a small central ulceration.
