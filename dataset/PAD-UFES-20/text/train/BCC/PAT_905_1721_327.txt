1. Primary Colors: Dark brown to black, red, and violaceous.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with a central dark focus and a surrounding erythematous halo that blends into the adjacent skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and eroded; the surrounding skin appears slightly edematous.
5. Structural Features: A central, dark, crusted ulceration or depression within a slightly raised, erythematous papule or nodule.
