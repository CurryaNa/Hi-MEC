1. Primary Colors: Red, dark brown, black, and pink.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, with the central erosion having a slightly more demarcated but jagged edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally moist and ulcerated with overlying hemorrhagic crust; surrounding skin is erythematous.
5. Structural Features: A central ulceration with adherent dark crust (eschar) and evidence of bleeding, surrounded by an erythematous halo. Telangiectasias are visible on the adjacent skin.
