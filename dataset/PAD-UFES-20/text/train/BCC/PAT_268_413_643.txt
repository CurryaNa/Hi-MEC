1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), black, white, and yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted with areas of ulceration.
5. Structural Features: A central, dark hemorrhagic crust and ulceration surrounded by a raised, erythematous border with adherent white-to-yellow hyperkeratotic scale.
