1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish/hypopigmented, and dark red/brown.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and texture.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed, featuring areas of atrophy (smooth, slightly depressed) and multiple foci of erosion and hemorrhagic crusting.
5. Structural Features: Central cluster of small ulcerations and crusted papules within a larger patch showing signs of atrophy and erythema.
