1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red), with focal areas of yellow-brown crust and fine, silvery-white scale.
2. Border Appearance: Variable; one lesion is ill-defined, while another adjacent lesion is well-demarcated with an annular (ring-like) border.
3. Shape Symmetry: The lesions are asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Mixed; the surface presents with areas of adherent yellow-brown crusting as well as dry, fine scaling on a raised plaque.
5. Structural Features: Two primary morphologies are visible: an erythematous patch with central erosions and crusting, and an annular erythematous plaque with a raised, scaly border and some central clearing.
