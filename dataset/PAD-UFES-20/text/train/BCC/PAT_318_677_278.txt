1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pinkish-red), focal bright red, and whitish.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted; fine, whitish scale is visible on one side with a central area of hemorrhagic crust.
5. Structural Features: A central erosion or shallow ulceration with overlying crust, surrounded by an erythematous papule with peripheral scaling.
