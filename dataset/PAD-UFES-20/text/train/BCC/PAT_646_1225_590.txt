1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, with foci of amber and yellowish-white crust.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated and crusted, with the surrounding area appearing somewhat shiny and atrophic.
5. Structural Features: Prominent arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels), central ulceration, and a subtly raised, translucent border.
