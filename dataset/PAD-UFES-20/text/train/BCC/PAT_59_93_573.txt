1. Primary Colors: Erythematous pink and red, with areas of whitish scale and scattered dark brown to black crusted foci.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and irregularly shaped.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, with fine scaling (desquamation) and areas of crusting and erosion.
5. Structural Features: A thin plaque characterized by erythema, scattered small erosions, and fine scale. Some central atrophy may be present. Small, dark, crusted specks are visible on the surface.
