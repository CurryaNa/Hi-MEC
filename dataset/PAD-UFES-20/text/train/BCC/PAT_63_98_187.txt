1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), whitish, and focal dark brown/black pigmentation.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, particularly in the erythematous and whitish areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: Varied, appearing atrophic and slightly scarred in some areas, with no significant scale or crust.
5. Structural Features: Presence of fine, linear telangiectasias (arborizing vessels), focal dark brown to black pigmented globules, and whitish, scar-like areas suggestive of regression.
