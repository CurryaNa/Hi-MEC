1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red, with areas of lighter pink and subtle whitish tones.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with the color gradually fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Slightly scaly and atrophic (thinned), with fine surface wrinkling and a small central erosion or crust.
5. Structural Features: Central atrophy with white, shiny, linear streaks (chrysalis structures). Fine, short, linear and dot-like vessels (telangiectasias) are visible, particularly in the center.
