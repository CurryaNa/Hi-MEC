1. Primary Colors: Pink-red (erythematous) and pearly white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat indistinct, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in color and structure, with an uneven distribution of pink and white areas.
4. Surface Texture: Slightly scaly and rough, with effacement of normal skin lines.
5. Structural Features: A central, pale, possibly atrophic area with surrounding peripheral erythema; potential for fine telangiectasias (small, dilated blood vessels) within the erythematous portions.
