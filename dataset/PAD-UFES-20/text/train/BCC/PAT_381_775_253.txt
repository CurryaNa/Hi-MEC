1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, translucent white, brown, and focal black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and indistinct, with a peripheral collarette of fine scale.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Glistening and lobulated with areas of hemorrhagic crusting.
5. Structural Features: A raised, polymorphic nodule with central ulceration, multiple colors (polychromia), and distinct black globules.
