1. Primary Colors: Pink, translucent pearly-white, and red.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated with a distinct, raised, and rolled border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical, presenting as a roughly circular papule.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and waxy/pearly along the raised border, with a centrally atrophic and slightly depressed surface.
5. Structural Features: A prominent raised, annular (ring-like) border, fine arborizing telangiectasias (branching blood vessels) visible on the surface, and a central umbilication or depression.
