1. Primary Colors: Variegated with areas of pink-red, blue-black, and a central greenish-yellow region.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, with notching and indistinct margins.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and structure.
4. Surface Texture: Irregular and polymorphic, featuring smooth nodular areas, scaling, and a central crusted exudate.
5. Structural Features: A multi-component pattern with a raised nodular structure, areas of blue-black pigmentation, central ulceration covered by a crust, and fine, arborizing telangiectasias on the erythematous portions.
