1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, yellowish-white, and tan.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, blending into the surrounding erythematous skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and the distribution of the central crust.
4. Surface Texture: Predominantly crusted and scaly, with a thick, adherent, waxy-appearing central scale.
5. Structural Features: Raised erythematous papule with significant central hyperkeratotic crusting; located in a hair-bearing area with hairs emerging from and around the lesion.
