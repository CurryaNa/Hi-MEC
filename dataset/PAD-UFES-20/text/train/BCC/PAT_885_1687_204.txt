1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, translucent pearly-white, and focal reddish-purple.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and relatively well-defined but not sharply demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both overall shape and distribution of internal features.
4. Surface Texture: Primarily smooth and waxy/pearly with central ulceration and small, white, firm-appearing structures.
5. Structural Features: A raised, translucent papule with a subtle rolled border, fine telangiectasias (small, visible blood vessels), and a central depression containing white, keratinous material.
