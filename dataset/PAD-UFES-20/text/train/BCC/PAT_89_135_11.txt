1. Primary Colors: Translucent pink, erythematous (red), with a central focus of dark red to brownish-black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular, with a distinct, raised, and rolled edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and roughly ovoid.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and pearly at the periphery with central hemorrhagic crusting and erosion.
5. Structural Features: A raised papule (nodule) with a translucent, rolled border, central ulceration, and potential fine telangiectasias (tiny blood vessels) on the surface.
