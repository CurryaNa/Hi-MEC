1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat indistinct, blending into the surrounding erythema.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Ulcerated and crusted, with areas of hemorrhagic and serous crusting.
5. Structural Features: A central, multi-focal ulceration with overlying hemorrhagic crust, surrounded by an erythematous papule/plaque.
