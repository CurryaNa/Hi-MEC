1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), pink, and translucent white, with central areas of dark red and black hemorrhagic crusting.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular yet relatively well-demarcated, with a distinct, raised, and rolled (pearly) peripheral edge.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in its overall ovoid shape and the distribution of the central ulceration.
4. Surface Texture: Biphasic; the central area is ulcerated, moist, and crusted, while the peripheral border is smooth and waxy.
5. Structural Features: A central, shallow ulceration with evidence of bleeding, surrounded by a classic elevated, pearly, and rolled border.
