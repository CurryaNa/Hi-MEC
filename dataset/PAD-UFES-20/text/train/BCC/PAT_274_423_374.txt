1. Primary Colors: Pinkish-red, violaceous, yellowish-green, and central brownish-red with black specks.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, forming a circular outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its overall circular shape.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and taut at the periphery with a centrally crusted and ulcerated surface.
5. Structural Features: A raised nodule with central umbilication containing a keratinous plug or exudate, ulceration, and a slightly translucent, rolled border.
