1. Primary Colors: Pink-red, translucent pearly-white, dark brown-black, and yellow.
2. Border Appearance: The lesion's overall border is ill-defined, but the central ulceration has a well-demarcated, raised, and irregular C-shaped border.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally ulcerated with hemorrhagic crusting; the surrounding border is smooth, waxy, and pearly.
5. Structural Features: A central, non-healing ulcer with a raised, rolled border and prominent, branching telangiectasias (fine blood vessels) on the surface of the surrounding papule.
