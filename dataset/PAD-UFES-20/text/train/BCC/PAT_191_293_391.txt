1. Primary Colors: Black, brown, white, yellowish, and erythematous red.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, partially obscured by scale and crust.
3. Shape Symmetry: Markedly asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Heavily crusted, hyperkeratotic, and scaly; the surrounding skin is also xerotic and scaly.
5. Structural Features: An elevated, verrucous plaque with a central, adherent, multi-colored eschar (crust). There is peripheral erythema and evidence of erosion or ulceration.
