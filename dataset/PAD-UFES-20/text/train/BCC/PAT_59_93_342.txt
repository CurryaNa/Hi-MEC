1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink to red) with small, scattered, dark brown to black crusted foci.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, blending into the surrounding skin in some areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and elongated.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, with fine, whitish, adherent scaling and areas of superficial crusting and erosion.
5. Structural Features: An atrophic-appearing patch with focal erosions, fine scale, and scattered small, dark, crusted papules.
