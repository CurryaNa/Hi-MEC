1. Primary Colors: Pearly white, pink, and erythematous (red).
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Waxy and smooth with some surface irregularity.
5. Structural Features: A raised, translucent, rolled border with prominent, fine, branching blood vessels (arborizing telangiectasias) and a central atrophic, slightly depressed, scar-like area.
