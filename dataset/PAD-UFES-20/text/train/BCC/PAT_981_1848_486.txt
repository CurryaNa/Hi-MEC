1. Primary Colors: Red, pink, violaceous (purple), and yellowish-brown on a pale skin background.
2. Border Appearance: The overall area of change is ill-defined and diffuse, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: The distribution of the lesions is amorphous and asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Smooth and slightly shiny, consistent with atrophic skin.
5. Structural Features: Multiple vascular structures are visible, including numerous fine, branching telangiectasias, a distinct spider angioma (stellate lesion with a central red punctum and radiating vessels), and a violaceous macule consistent with purpura or ecchymosis.
