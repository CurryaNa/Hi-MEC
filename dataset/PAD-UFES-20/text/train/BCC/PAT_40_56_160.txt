1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink-red), white, and pinpoint dark red/brown spots.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, blending with the surrounding skin in some areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and texture.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted, with a focal area of thicker, white, adherent scale (hyperkeratosis).
5. Structural Features: Raised plaque with central scaling and multiple small erosions or ulcerations.
