1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pinkish-red), white, and a central dark red/brown focus.
2. Border Appearance: The border is relatively well-defined but irregular in shape.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical and roughly round.
4. Surface Texture: Scaly and crusted, with an adherent white scale over the surface.
5. Structural Features: An erythematous papule or nodule with a central area of ulceration or hemorrhagic crusting. Hairs are seen emerging from the lesion.
