1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (pink), brown (multiple shades), and white-yellow.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined for both the pigmented and erythematous components.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: The erythematous area is eroded with an overlying white scale-crust; the pigmented area appears macular (flat).
5. Structural Features: A multicomponent lesion consisting of a superior, irregularly pigmented brown macule and an inferior erythematous plaque with central ulceration and adherent crusting.
