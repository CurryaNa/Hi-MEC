1. Primary Colors: Pearly translucent, pink, red, and focal dark brown/black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and fairly well-demarcated, with a distinctly raised and rolled quality.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Glistening and waxy at the border, with central crusting and slight depression.
5. Structural Features: A prominent pearly, rolled border, fine branching telangiectasias (blood vessels), central ulceration, and focal areas of dark crust.
