1. Primary Colors: Dark grey, black, and hints of dark blue.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated but irregular and lobulated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like), cerebriform (brain-like), and keratotic with a slightly crusted surface.
5. Structural Features: Raised (exophytic) nodule with a multi-globular surface, dark pigmentation, and visible keratin plugging within the crevices, creating a "stuck-on" appearance.
