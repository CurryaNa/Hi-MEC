1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with shades of gray-brown, black, and violaceous with whitish hyperkeratotic flecks.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated with an irregular, scalloped outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and keratotic, appearing dry and rough.
5. Structural Features: Raised (exophytic) papule with a "stuck-on" appearance, featuring multi-component pigmentation and a faint peripheral erythematous halo.
