1. Primary Colors: Heterogeneous, with shades of gray-black, brown, and whitish-gray.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated with an irregular, slightly scalloped outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like), lobulated, and appears waxy or greasy.
5. Structural Features: Elevated papule with a cerebriform (brain-like) surface, multiple small pits and comedo-like openings (horn cysts), and a "stuck-on" quality.
