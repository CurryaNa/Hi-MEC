1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with shades of gray, brown, and dark gray to black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and notched; the borders are fairly well-demarcated but not sharply defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is composed of at least two distinct, non-symmetrical, coalescing lobules.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous and keratotic; the surface appears dry, rough, and slightly scaly.
5. Structural Features: Elevated (papular/nodular) with a multi-lobulated or cerebriform (brain-like) surface; exhibits a "stuck-on" appearance with heterogeneous pigmentation.
