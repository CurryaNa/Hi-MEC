1. Primary Colors: Light brown, tan, and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated; borders are generally regular, though some lesions have slightly irregular outlines.
3. Shape Symmetry: Individual lesions are mostly symmetrical (round to oval), but there is a high degree of variation in size and shape among the multiple lesions.
4. Surface Texture: Varied; ranging from smooth to waxy, slightly mamillated, or verrucous (wart-like) on the raised papules.
5. Structural Features: Multiple scattered and clustered macules (flat spots) and papules (raised bumps). Many papules have a "stuck-on" appearance, and several lesions, particularly within the skin fold, are pedunculated (on a stalk).
