1. Primary Colors: Gray-brown, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Well-defined and sharply demarcated, with a regular overall circular shape but a minutely irregular, lobulated contour.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its overall dome shape, but asymmetrical in surface detail.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and cerebriform (brain-like), with a dry, fissured surface.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic papule with a mamillated surface composed of multiple lobules; exhibits dark, comedo-like openings and keratin-filled crypts within the surface fissures.
