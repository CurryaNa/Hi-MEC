1. Primary Colors: Uniform light brown to tan.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and sharply defined, with a slightly irregular, scalloped outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical in its overall ovoid or slightly heart-shaped form.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and papillomatous, creating a cerebriform (brain-like) and slightly greasy or waxy-appearing surface.
5. Structural Features: Elevated plaque with a "stuck-on" appearance. The surface is characterized by multiple crypts, fissures, and comedo-like openings.
