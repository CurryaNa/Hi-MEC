1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with shades of light brown, tan, dark brown, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular but mostly well-demarcated, giving a "stuck-on" impression.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and texture.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and keratotic, with a dry, scaly, and crusted surface.
5. Structural Features: A slightly elevated plaque with variegated pigmentation, a central darker and more crusted area, and visible comedo-like openings (keratin plugs).
