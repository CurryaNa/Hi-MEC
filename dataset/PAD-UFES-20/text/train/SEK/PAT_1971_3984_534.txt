1. Primary Colors: Yellowish-white, with a focal area of grayish-blue discoloration.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular, with a distinct, "stuck-on" appearance.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical, with an overall round to ovoid shape.
