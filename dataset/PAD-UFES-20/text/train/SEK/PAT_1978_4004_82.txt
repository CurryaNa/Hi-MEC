1. Primary Colors: Dark brown, black, and tan.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and mostly well-demarcated, with some areas showing a subtle, lighter brown halo.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like), dry, and crusted with prominent fissures.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic (raised) papule with a cerebriform (brain-like) surface pattern, variegated pigmentation, and a "stuck-on" appearance.
