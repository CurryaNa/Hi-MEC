1. Primary Colors: Tan, grey, and light brown.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and papillomatous with a dry, cerebriform (brain-like) surface.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, lobulated papule with a "stuck-on" appearance, featuring multiple fissures and small horny cysts (milia-like cysts).
