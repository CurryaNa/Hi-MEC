1. Primary Colors: Light brown, tan-yellow, with dark brown to black punctate specks.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular in shape with margins that are somewhat indistinct and not sharply demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and keratotic with a slightly scaly, rough, and uneven surface.
5. Structural Features: A slightly elevated papule with multiple dark brown punctate dots and small, keratin-filled pits (comedo-like openings) giving it a cerebriform (brain-like) pattern in areas.
