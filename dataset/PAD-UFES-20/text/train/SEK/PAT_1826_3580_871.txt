1. Primary Colors: Multiple shades of brown (tan, dark brown, black) with areas of hypopigmentation and normal flesh tones.
2. Border Appearance: The overall area of change is ill-defined; individual pigmented macules have irregular and indistinct borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the distribution and shape of the pigmented areas lack any symmetry.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and atrophic with fine wrinkling and accentuated skin lines.
5. Structural Features: Mottled hyperpigmentation consisting of numerous, confluent, flat macules of varying size and color, characteristic of extensive sun damage (solar lentigines and dermatoheliosis).
