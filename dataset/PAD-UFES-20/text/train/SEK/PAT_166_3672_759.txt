1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with shades of brown, tan, gray, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and poorly demarcated, with some areas fading into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Highly asymmetrical in both contour and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Uneven, with areas of scaling and a dry, crusted, and warty (verrucous) feel.
5. Structural Features: Elevated papule/plaque with significant color variegation, a central crusted area, and a distinct, darker, almost black nodular component.
