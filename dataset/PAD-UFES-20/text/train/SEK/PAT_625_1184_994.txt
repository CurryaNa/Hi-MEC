1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), violaceous (blue-purple), and black.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Mostly smooth with a central area of crusting.
5. Structural Features: A raised, nodular papule with color variegation and a focal, central black crust or ulceration.
