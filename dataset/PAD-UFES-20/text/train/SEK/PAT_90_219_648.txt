1. Primary Colors: Tan, light brown, dark brown, grey, and white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with the lesion's edge fading into the surrounding skin in some areas.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color and texture.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally verrucous (wart-like) and keratotic, with a granular, crusted surface. The peripheral area of the lesion is flatter.
5. Structural Features: A centrally elevated plaque with a "stuck-on" appearance, featuring multiple whitish-grey keratin plugs (comedo-like openings). The entire lesion is surrounded by a subtle, lighter tan halo.
