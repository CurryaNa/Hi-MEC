1. Primary Colors: Tan, light brown, with focal areas of dark brown to black in the center.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated with a fusiform (spindle-shaped) outline and tapered ends.
3. Shape Symmetry: Largely symmetrical along its longitudinal axis.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and scaly with a slightly verrucous (wart-like) quality and fine fissures.
