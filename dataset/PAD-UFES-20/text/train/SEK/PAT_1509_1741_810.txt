1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with shades of brown, tan, pinkish-red, and scattered black specks.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with some scalloped and notched areas where the pigment fades into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.

5. Structural Features: Multiple colors, scattered black pigment dots (globules), and effacement of normal skin lines, with a slightly raised or papular central component.
