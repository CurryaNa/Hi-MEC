1. Primary Colors: Light brown, tan, medium brown, and dark brown.
2. Border Appearance: Generally well-demarcated with regular, round to oval borders.
3. Shape Symmetry: The individual lesions are largely symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Variable; ranging from smooth and macular to slightly raised and warty (verrucous).
5. Structural Features: Multiple, discrete, scattered papules and macules of varying sizes, with some exhibiting a classic "stuck-on" appearance.
