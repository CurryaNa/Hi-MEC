1. Primary Colors: Grey, brown, and off-white/tan.
2. Border Appearance: Relatively well-demarcated with a slightly irregular, scalloped outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical; the lesion is roughly ovoid but lacks mirror-image symmetry.
