1. Primary Colors: Variegated with shades of light brown, dark brown, and a central black/violaceous area.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and somewhat ill-defined, with notching observed.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of color.
4. Surface Texture: Slightly scaly with disruption of normal skin lines (dermatoglyphics).
5. Structural Features: A pigmented macule with a central, darker, slightly elevated (papular) component.
