1. Primary Colors: Blue-gray, black, and whitish-gray.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated but irregular and lobulated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and mammillated, with a dry, keratotic quality.
