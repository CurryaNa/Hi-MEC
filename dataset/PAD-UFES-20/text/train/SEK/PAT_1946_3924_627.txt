1. Primary Colors: Light brown and tan, with a focal area of darker brown at the inferior pole.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated with an irregular, lobulated outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical, with an elongated, bilobed form.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and mamillated, not smooth.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic, sessile to slightly pedunculated papule composed of two fused lobules with variegation in pigmentation.
