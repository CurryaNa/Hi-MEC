1. Primary Colors: Erythematous (red), yellowish-white, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and regular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Generally symmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Centrally crusted and hyperkeratotic.
5. Structural Features: A raised, erythematous, rolled border surrounding a central, crater-like depression filled with a firm, yellowish keratin plug containing small black specks.
