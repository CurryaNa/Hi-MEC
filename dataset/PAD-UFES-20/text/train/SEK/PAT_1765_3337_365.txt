1. Primary Colors: Pink, violaceous, brown, and yellowish-white.
2. Border Appearance: Ill-defined and irregular, blending into the surrounding skin.
3. Shape Symmetry: Roughly symmetrical in its round shape, but asymmetrical in color and surface structure.
