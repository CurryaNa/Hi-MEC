1. Primary Colors: Variegated; shades of dark brown, light brown, black, and gray-white.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with scalloped and notched edges.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and scaly, with some areas appearing slightly crusted.
5. Structural Features: A multi-component papule with a complex surface pattern, exhibiting both raised areas with a cobblestone or cerebriform texture and flatter pigmented sections. There is notable pigment variegation and areas of gray-white scale-crust.
