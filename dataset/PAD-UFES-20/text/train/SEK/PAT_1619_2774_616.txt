1. Primary Colors: Light brown and tan with scattered whitish and dark brown specks.
2. Border Appearance: Well-defined and regular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Symmetrical and round.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (warty) and papillomatous with a dry, "stuck-on" quality.
5. Structural Features: Raised (exophytic) papule with a lobulated, cerebriform surface and multiple small, whitish keratin plugs (horn cysts or comedo-like openings).
