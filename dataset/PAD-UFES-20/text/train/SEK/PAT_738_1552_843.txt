1. Primary Colors: Light brown, tan, and reddish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined with a scalloped or geographic outline.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and finely scaly, with a reticulated or cracked appearance.
5. Structural Features: Multiple flat, coalescing patches with a net-like pattern of pigmentation and scale. Some smaller, discrete macules and papules are also present in the vicinity.
