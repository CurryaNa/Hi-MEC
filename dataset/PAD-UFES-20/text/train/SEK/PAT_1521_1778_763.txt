1. Primary Colors: Light brown, tan, and grey-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated with an irregular, multilobulated (scalloped) contour.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like) and cerebriform (brain-like) with fine fissures and ridges.
5. Structural Features: Raised (papillomatous) lesion with a "stuck-on" appearance, featuring multiple lobules and dark, comedo-like openings (horn cysts) within the furrows.
