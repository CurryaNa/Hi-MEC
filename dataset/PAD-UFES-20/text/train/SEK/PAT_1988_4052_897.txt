1. Primary Colors: Skin-toned, pinkish-brown, with grayish-brown pigmentation concentrated within the surface clefts.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated with a slightly irregular, scalloped contour.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical due to the non-uniform arrangement of surface lobules.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous and papillomatous, with a dry, lobulated, and cerebriform (brain-like) appearance.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic (outward-growing) papule composed of multiple confluent lobules separated by deep clefts or fissures.
