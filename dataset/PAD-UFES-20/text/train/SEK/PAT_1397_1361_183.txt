1. Primary Colors: Variegated, with multiple shades of brown (from tan to dark brown), pink-red, and a focal blue-grey area.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and ill-defined, with notched and scalloped segments.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Dry and slightly scaly with a finely wrinkled to pebbly texture; skin lines (dermatoglyphics) are partially effaced.
5. Structural Features: A polychromatic patch with areas of darker brown pigmentation, a focal blue-grey nodular component in the lower left, and areas of pinkish-white regression. The central surface has a slightly mamillated or cerebriform appearance.
