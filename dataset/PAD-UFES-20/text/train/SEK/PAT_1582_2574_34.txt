1. Primary Colors: Dark brown, black, and blue-gray.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular and scalloped, but relatively well-demarcated.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both its ovoid shape and color distribution.
4. Surface Texture: Papillomatous and cerebriform (brain-like), with a pattern of fine grooves and ridges.
5. Structural Features: Raised (exophytic) papule with multiple lobules, fissures, and comedo-like openings (milia-like cysts).
