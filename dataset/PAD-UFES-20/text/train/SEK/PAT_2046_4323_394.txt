1. Primary Colors: Dark brown, black, and tan.
2. Border Appearance: Generally well-demarcated but irregular and, in some lesions, scalloped.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both shape and distribution of pigment.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like), dry, and crusted with a granular, pebbly surface.
5. Structural Features: Multiple pigmented papules and a larger patch with a "stuck-on" appearance, exhibiting variegated pigmentation and multiple dark, keratin-filled plugs (comedo-like openings).
