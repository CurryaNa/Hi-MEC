1. Primary Colors: Dark brown, black, and greyish-brown.
2. Border Appearance: Irregular in shape and relatively well-demarcated, though not uniformly sharp.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical in both its overall contour and distribution of color.
4. Surface Texture: Dry, rough, and verrucous (wart-like) with fine fissures and a slightly scaly quality.
5. Structural Features: Raised (papular) lesion with a "stuck-on" appearance, heterogeneous pigmentation, and a cerebriform (brain-like) surface pattern.
