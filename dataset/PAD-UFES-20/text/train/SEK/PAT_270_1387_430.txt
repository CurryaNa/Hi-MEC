1. Primary Colors: Dark brown, gray, and black.
2. Border Appearance: Well-demarcated and irregular.
3. Shape Symmetry: Asymmetrical.
4. Surface Texture: Verrucous (wart-like), keratotic, and dry with a "stuck-on" quality.
5. Structural Features: Exophytic lesion composed of multiple coalescing, pigmented papules creating a cerebriform (brain-like) or cobblestone surface.
