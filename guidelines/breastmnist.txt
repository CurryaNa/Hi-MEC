Breast Ultrasound Diagnostic Criteria
0 — Malignant
Irregular shape
Non-parallel orientation, also described as taller-than-wide
Not circumscribed margins, including indistinct, angular, microlobulated, or spiculated margins
Possible posterior shadowing
Possible markedly hypoechoic echogenicity or calcifications
1 — Benign / Normal
If a lesion is present:
Oval or round shape
Parallel orientation, also described as wider-than-tall
Circumscribed or smooth margins
Anechoic or hypoechoic echogenicity
Posterior enhancement or no posterior acoustic change
If no lesion is present:
Normal fibroglandular tissue
No discrete mass
No architectural distortion or focal abnormality
