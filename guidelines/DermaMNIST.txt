Dermatology Diagnostic Criteria
ACK — Actinic Keratosis
Pink, red, or brown color
Ill-defined borders
Rough or scaly plaque-like surface
BCC — Basal Cell Carcinoma
Pearly or translucent nodules
Raised or rolled borders
Central crusting or ulceration
Abnormal vessels
BKL — Benign Keratosis-like Lesions
Yellow, brown, or black color
Oily, verrucous, or stuck-on surface
Adhering texture
Millet-like cysts
DF — Dermatofibroma
Peripheral reticular lines
Central white fibrous plaque
Firm fibrotic-looking structure
MEL — Melanoma
Multicolored appearance, such as black, brown, gray, or mixed colors
Asymmetry
Blurred or irregular borders
Lack of obvious regular structure
NEV — Melanocytic Nevus
Brown or black color
Regular and smooth borders
High symmetry
Uniform pigment distribution
VASC — Vascular Lesions
Red or purple color
Solid lacunae
Red vascular masses
